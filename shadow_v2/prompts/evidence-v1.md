# Evidence-first English shadow policy v1

Eight separate area searches retain actual web-tool query/source provenance. A
source URL emitted by the model is insufficient without tool provenance. Each
lead is independently fetched over bounded, pinned-IP HTTPS. All source content
is untrusted, never instructions. Source publication metadata is retained;
modified times and event calendar times do not establish original publication.

Before prose, lexical source checks and a separate semantic audit verify issuer,
figure-to-issuer relation, currency/unit/period, legal stage, qualifiers, original
publication and genuinely new development timing. The freshness window is the
previous HK trading close through the actual fixed research-start cutoff.
Upcoming calendar events need an announcement within that window plus evidence.
Recently published recaps cannot establish a new development by their date alone.

Only verified cards enter English headline/paragraph drafting. A separate audit
checks every English claim against source documents, including translation,
relations, qualifiers and invented implications. Missing evidence fails closed.
At most one affected-ID correction is permitted. Unaffected paragraphs remain
unchanged. Optional stylistic opinions cannot block approval.

History is preference/duplicate context only. Candidate-specific MCP retrieval
informs bounded relevance scoring using explicit labels. Unlabeled remains
unknown. Exact duplicate current-source developments may be evidence-excluded
with a canonical story reference; every other material lead must remain.
