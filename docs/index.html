Morning Market Memo | 13 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 12 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 13 Aug 2026 07:21 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    PBOC signals further support: The second-quarter monetary-policy report said the central bank will prepare practical incremental measures, strengthen counter-cyclical adjustment and keep liquidity ample. It highlighted a RMB1tn relending facility for private enterprises and said June new corporate-loan rates were about 3.0%, roughly 20bp lower year on year. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    Liquidity operations – tax-period support: The PBOC will offer overnight reverse repos on 14 August and 17–19 August, with a daily ceiling of RMB600bn. The new operations are intended to manage tax-payment pressure and the RMB1tn six-month outright reverse-repo maturity due on Friday. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Tencent – revenue tops RMB200bn: Second-quarter revenue rose 11% to RMB204.8bn while attributable profit increased 0.7% to RMB56.0bn. AI infrastructure prepayments contributed to negative free cash flow of RMB13.8bn; excluding those payments, free cash flow was RMB37.6bn, and Tencent said it has substantially increased third-quarter computing-power procurement. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Tencent buybacks and user base: The company spent HK$24.4bn repurchasing shares during the first half. Combined WeChat and Weixin monthly active accounts reached 1.439bn at quarter-end, up 2% year on year, supporting the distribution case for its AI products and advertising tools. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Pop Mart – Duan Yongping vehicle raises stake: A post-close HKEX disclosure showed H&H International Investment, managed by Duan Yongping, increased its Pop Mart holding from 5.55% to 7.70% on 6 August. The newly disclosed accumulation is relevant to the consumer-IP name at today’s open. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Antong Holdings – control may shift to China Merchants: Sinotrans Container Lines and China Merchants Port have nominated more than half of the company’s proposed board. If shareholders approve the slate, Sinotrans would become the controlling shareholder and China Merchants Group the ultimate controller, providing a fresh restructuring catalyst for shipping and logistics. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    CSPC Innovation – AstraZeneca payment received: Subsidiary Jushi Biotech received a US$30mn upfront payment from AstraZeneca under their July agreement covering novel extrahepatic-delivery siRNA candidates. Receipt of the payment confirms progress on the small-nucleic-acid partnership. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Baofeng Energy – profit and dividend growth: Baofeng Energy (600989 CH) reported first-half net profit of RMB9.73bn, up 70.14%, on revenue of RMB30.20bn, up 32.33%. It proposed a RMB0.42-per-share interim dividend, with polyolefin output rising 23.64% to 2.97mn tonnes. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Shemar Electric – overseas grid wins: Shemar Electric (603530 CH) reported first-half net profit of RMB215mn, up 34.01%, as revenue increased 26.98% to RMB895mn. The company won composite-insulator framework orders from the UK’s National Grid and Belgium’s Elia, adding an overseas power-grid read-through. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Zhongding – cloud partnership clarification: Zhongding said its three-year framework agreement with Tencent Cloud covers computing clusters, data-centre liquid cooling, cloud procurement and AI applications, but currently has no substantive implementation or revenue contribution. The clarification limits the near-term earnings relevance of the previously circulated cooperation story. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    China Baoan – shareholder accumulation: Chengxing Investment purchased 23.61mn shares between 10 and 12 August, equal to 0.9153% of China Baoan’s capital. Chengxing and concert party Kunpeng New Industries now hold 23.37%; the purchases implement an existing plan and do not change control. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    US inflation – core pressure moderates: July core CPI rose 0.2% month on month and 2.5% year on year, while headline CPI increased 0.1% month on month and 3.4% year on year. Treasury yields fell and gold and silver held gains as investors reduced expectations for a September rate increase, supporting duration-sensitive HK technology stocks. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Overnight US markets: The Dow slipped 0.04% to 53,770.27, while the S&P 500 gained 0.26% to 7,748.50 and the Nasdaq rose 0.54% to 26,588.49. Nvidia added 3.03%, AMD 1.82% and TSMC’s ADR 1.68%; Tesla fell 1.59%, Microsoft 2.26% and Meta 3.38%. A verified final Nasdaq Golden Dragon close was not available in the retrieved wrap by cutoff, so no China-ADR percentage is stated. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    AI hardware – mixed after-hours signals: Supermicro rallied during regular trading after a stronger outlook, while Cisco and Coherent fell after hours despite above-consensus quarterly results and forecasts; Cerebras declined after revenue missed expectations. The split leaves a mixed read-through for HK/A-share servers, optical components and AI infrastructure suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Hormuz remains closed: Iran’s Persian Gulf Strait Authority said overnight that the Strait of Hormuz remains shut and would not reopen unless the United States accepts Iran’s conditions. The latest status keeps shipping, airlines, refiners and energy names exposed to supply-route uncertainty. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    Former premier Zhu Rongji dies: Xinhua announced after the Hong Kong close that former premier Zhu Rongji died in Beijing on 12 August at the age of 98. The official obituary highlighted his role in fiscal, financial, state-enterprise and housing reforms and China’s WTO accession. [Eastmoney]
