Morning Market Memo | 25 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 24 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 25 Sep 2026 06:10 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    China–US summit: Xi Jinping and Donald Trump met at the White House and endorsed a new joint economic arrangement reached by their negotiating teams. Both sides also backed continued dialogue on artificial intelligence, while Xi urged Washington to handle Taiwan carefully; Alibaba, Tencent, exporters and technology hardware names are the main read-through. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    PBOC policy signal: The central bank’s third-quarter monetary-policy committee reiterated an “appropriately accommodative” stance, stronger counter-cyclical adjustment and ample liquidity. It called for financing support for domestic demand, technology, small businesses and the “six networks,” while keeping overall financing costs low and the renminbi broadly stable. [PBOC]

  3. 03

    Beijing property reform: New residential projects applying for presales must generally complete structural topping-out, while projects on land offered after 28 August should prioritise completed-home sales. Mortgage funds for affected presale projects will be released only after completion filing, while land payments may be spread over two years; the rules raise funding requirements for developers but strengthen delivery protection. [Beijing Government] [CLS]

  4. 04

    Domestic fuel prices: The NDRC raised regulated petrol and diesel prices by RMB395 and RMB385 per tonne respectively from midnight, versus formula-based increases of RMB830 and RMB800, as authorities continued to cushion the domestic impact of higher crude prices. This supports refiners’ pricing but limits the pass-through to transport and consumer costs. [NDRC]

  5. 05

    Shanghai index rules: The Shanghai Stock Exchange and CSI will cap any single constituent’s weight at 15% in the affected Shanghai composite-index series and accelerate the removal of risk-warning securities. The revised methodology takes effect on 26 October and may prompt rebalancing in linked index products. [SSE]

  6. 06

    China Life technology fund: China Life Insurance (2628 HK/601628 CH) plans to commit up to RMB4.5bn to a partnership with total commitments of up to RMB6bn. The fund will target unlisted artificial-intelligence and semiconductor companies, adding another large pool of long-duration insurance capital to domestic technology investment. [NBD] [Sina]

  7. 07

    Hengtong Optical-Electric financing: Hengtong Optical-Electric (600487 CH) proposed a private placement of up to RMB6.636bn for next-generation optical fibre, CPO packaging, AI optical interconnect, data communications and offshore-energy projects. The plan provides a fresh capacity catalyst for the mainland fibre, optics and AI-infrastructure chain. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Sinnet data-centre expansion: Beijing Sinnet Technology (300383 CH) will invest up to another RMB1.265bn in its Horinger intelligent-computing centre, taking total investment to about RMB2.5bn. Planned IT load rises to 90–120MW from 60–100MW and the cabinet count to 7,000–10,000, supporting server, power and cooling suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Shandong Gold cuts guidance: Shandong Gold (1787 HK/600547 CH) reduced its 2026 mined-gold target to 36–38 tonnes from at least 49 tonnes. It expects output to fall by roughly 11–13 tonnes year on year and said the decline would weigh on revenue, profit and attributable earnings despite elevated gold prices. [Sina] [Shanghai Securities News]

  10. 10

    Putailai advances Hong Kong listing: Battery-materials producer Putailai (603659 CH) received CSRC filing clearance for a proposed issue of up to 269m H shares and Hong Kong listing. The filing adds another battery-chain issuer to the HK IPO pipeline and creates a future A/H valuation reference. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    InnoLight completes buyback: InnoLight (300308 CH) completed a RMB4.997bn repurchase covering 5.6531m shares, equal to 0.48% of its capital. The unusually large completed programme is a fresh capital-return signal for the optical-transceiver and AI-hardware complex. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Jifeng seating award: Jifeng Auto Parts (603997 CH) said its Grammer unit won a complete-seat nomination from a leading passenger-vehicle manufacturer. Production is scheduled from May 2028, with a seven-year lifecycle and estimated total value of RMB9.2bn, although there is no material impact on current-year earnings. [Yicai]

  13. 13

    Tencent buyback: Tencent (700 HK) repurchased 230,000 shares for about HK$101m at HK$433.40–439.60 and plans to cancel them. The post-close filing extends the group’s regular capital-return programme ahead of today’s open. [Sina]

  14. 14

    Southbound flows: Mainland investors bought a net HK$2.90bn of Hong Kong shares on Thursday. Tencent drew roughly HK$650m of net buying, while Tracker Fund and Hang Seng Tech ETF products were also among the leading inflows, providing limited support after the Hang Seng Index’s 0.29% decline. [Securities Times]

  15. 15

    Overnight US markets: The Dow fell 0.31%, the S&P 500 slipped 0.02% and the Nasdaq edged up 0.01%. Meta gained 4.50%, but Western Digital fell 4.94%, SanDisk 3.47% and Seagate 1.86%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index lost 0.67%, with JD down 1.51% and Baidu 1.89%. WTI rose 2.82% to US$94.76, while spot gold fell 0.29% to US$4,274.71 and silver lost 0.90%, favouring HK oil names but leaving storage hardware and China internet ADR read-through softer. [Eastmoney]
