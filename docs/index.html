Morning Market Memo | 06 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 05 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 06 Oct 2026 06:10 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Sihuan Pharmaceutical – five new skin-booster specifications approved:** China’s NMPA approved five additional specifications of Sihuan Pharmaceutical’s (460 HK) Dongyan injectable sodium-hyaluronate compound solution. The product, described as China’s first facial compound skin booster containing L-carnosine, expands the group’s offering across the mass-market light medical-aesthetics segment. [Stockstar]

  2. 02

    **CSPC Pharmaceutical – eight studies selected for ESMO:** CSPC Pharmaceutical (1093 HK) will present eight oncology clinical-trial updates at the European Society for Medical Oncology congress on 23–27 October, including two late-breaking abstracts. The programme comprises one preferred oral presentation, three rapid oral presentations and four posters across breast, lung, gastric, liver, colon and pancreatic cancers, providing a fresh pipeline catalyst. [Sina]

  3. 03

    **Forward Fashion – Pop Mart Macau agreement to end:** Forward Fashion (2528 HK) said its agreement to manage Pop Mart stores in Macau will terminate from 21 December. Those stores generated about 22% of group revenue for the nine months ended September, and the board expects a material short-term impact; mitigation includes opening other Macau stores and introducing new brands. [Investing.com]

  4. 04

    **XtalPi – HK$15.69 million post-close buyback:** XtalPi Holdings (2228 HK) repurchased 2.004 million shares on Monday for HK$15.69 million, paying HK$7.78–7.88 per share. The purchase is the largest identified post-close HK buyback in the retained announcement set and provides a direct reference for the AI-drug-discovery name. [Investing.com]

  5. 05

    **China Resources Beverage – buyback continues:** China Resources Beverage (2460 HK) spent HK$1.466 million repurchasing 201,600 shares at HK$7.22–7.30 each. The post-close filing adds modest support for the consumer-staples name while mainland markets remain shut for the National Day holiday. [Stockstar]

  6. 06

    **Pagoda Group – 530,000 shares repurchased:** Shenzhen Pagoda Industrial Group (2411 HK) bought back 530,000 shares for HK$815,100, at prices between HK$1.52 and HK$1.55. The shares will be held as treasury stock, extending the fruit retailer’s recent repurchase activity. [Stockstar]

  7. 07

    **Nobel medicine prize – optogenetics recognised:** The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine went to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel for discoveries involving light-gated ion channels and optogenetics; the three will share SEK12 million. The award raises attention on neuroscience research tools, brain-circuit mapping and related life-science instrumentation, though near-term commercial read-through remains indirect. [China Securities Journal]

  8. 08

    **Saudi-led defence grouping activates collective mechanism:** Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkey announced activation of the Mecca Defence Alliance’s collective-defence mechanism after an emergency meeting in Riyadh. The statement called for rapid deployment of agreed forces and capabilities inside Saudi Arabia, increasing overnight attention on Middle East defence, aviation and energy infrastructure. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Houthis claim strikes on Saudi refinery and airports:** Yemen’s Houthi movement said it conducted three waves of ballistic-missile, cruise-missile and drone attacks targeting Riyadh’s King Khalid International Airport, Saudi Aramco’s Rabigh refinery, Abha Airport and several military facilities. The refinery claim is relevant for oil and petrochemical names despite crude finishing lower before the full late-night escalation was assessed. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **US markets – Nasdaq record, AI hardware leads:** The Dow rose 0.18%, the S&P 500 gained 0.66% and the Nasdaq advanced 1.05% to a record close. Nvidia and TSMC rose more than 2% to records, while Broadcom and Tesla also gained more than 2%; the positive hardware read-through favours HK semiconductors, optical components, PCB and AI-server names. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **China ADRs – Golden Dragon up 1.71%:** The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index gained 1.71%, with all constituents higher. Futu rose 7.37%, Alibaba 4.68%, PDD 3.34%, Baidu 3.17%, JD 2.25% and Bilibili 2.15%; XPeng, Li Auto and NIO added 1.62%, 1.50% and 1.48%, respectively, setting a firmer reference for their HK listings. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Cross-assets – oil below US$90, silver outperforms:** WTI crude fell 1.99% to US$89.30 a barrel despite the developing Saudi security headlines. COMEX gold edged up 0.13% to US$4,167.60 an ounce and silver gained 1.63% to US$61.40, while spot gold was effectively flat; the mix is neutral for gold names but keeps oil producers sensitive to further Saudi developments. [Eastmoney]
