Morning Market Memo | 24 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 23 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 24 Sep 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Xi arrives in Washington:** President Xi Jinping arrived for his US state visit and was welcomed at Andrews Air Force Base by President Donald Trump. The visit puts US–China trade, technology controls and broader bilateral relations at the centre of today’s market agenda. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    **PBOC adds medium-term liquidity:** The central bank will conduct RMB800bn of one-year MLF today, versus RMB600bn maturing this month, for a net RMB200bn injection. September’s medium-term operations therefore return to net injection after August’s RMB100bn MLF withdrawal. [The Paper]

  3. 03

    **Holiday liquidity support expanded:** The PBOC will offer overnight reverse repos from 28 September through 8 October, with a daily ceiling of RMB1tn, up from RMB600bn in the previous three rounds. The 11-day facility is designed to smooth quarter-end, holiday cash-withdrawal and government-bond settlement pressures. [CLS]

  4. 04

    **New communications infrastructure:** MIIT Vice Minister Yu Xiaohui said China will systematically advance next-generation networks during the 15th Five-Year Plan, including “dual-10-gigabit” broadband, low-orbit satellite internet, higher-quality computing infrastructure and accelerated 6G research and standards. The programme is relevant to telecom equipment, optical networking, data-centre and satellite-communications suppliers. [Economic Observer]

  5. 05

    **Satellite-internet launch:** China launched the 26th group of low-orbit internet satellites at 21:32 HKT from the Hainan commercial spacecraft launch site using a Long March-8A rocket. The payloads entered their designated orbit, extending the high-frequency deployment cycle for the domestic satellite-internet chain. [Xinhua]

  6. 06

    **Xiaomi flagship launch:** Xiaomi introduced the 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max after the close, starting at RMB5,999 and RMB6,999 respectively, with sales beginning immediately. The phones debut Qualcomm’s new 2nm flagship platforms and Xiaomi’s MiMo-powered Super XiaoAI 2.0, providing a fresh catalyst for Xiaomi 1810 HK and the Android component chain. [Beijing Daily]

  7. 07

    **Shanghai RAAS buys biologics plant:** Shanghai RAAS plans to acquire 100% of Boehringer Ingelheim Biopharmaceuticals China, using a RMB1 base price plus closing adjustments capped at an expected RMB20m. The Zhangjiang facility is intended to give the company in-house development and commercial-production capacity for SR604 and other innovative-drug pipelines; overseas antitrust approval remains required. [Sina]

  8. 08

    **Linglong Tire adds Egypt capacity:** Linglong Tire will invest US$270m in an Egyptian plant with annual capacity for six million semi-steel radial tyres. Construction is planned over two years, subject to regulatory approvals, expanding overseas capacity closer to European, Middle Eastern and African customers. [CNR]

  9. 09

    **Low-altitude testing investment:** Sichuan Jiuzhou’s controlled subsidiary will invest about RMB360m in equipment for a ground-based testing and verification centre covering complete-aircraft performance, subsystems and structural strength. Its shareholders will inject RMB165m proportionately, supporting the listed company’s low-altitude aircraft testing and certification platform. [Zhitong]

  10. 10

    **AI-hardware materials capex:** Sidike approved a RMB1.652bn project adding 103,000 tonnes of high-end functional BOPET base-film capacity for MLCC, optical, PCB and smart-device applications. Yibo Technology separately approved up to RMB800m for phase two of its Zhuhai PCB plant, targeting high-speed, multilayer and advanced-HDI products. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Yuexin Semiconductor subscription:** The Guangdong 12-inch foundry opens its ChiNext IPO subscription today at RMB12.01 per share, issuing 512.65m shares for gross proceeds of roughly RMB6.16bn. Yuexin is Guangdong’s first locally developed 12-inch wafer manufacturer to enter mass production, making the deal a key test of appetite for domestic foundry assets. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **HK buybacks:** Tencent repurchased 227,000 shares for HK$100.4m, its 28th consecutive buyback session since 17 August; year-to-date spending reached HK$30.94bn. Baidu bought back 561,800 shares for about HK$50.0m, providing post-close support signals for the internet complex. [STCN] [Securities Star]

  13. 13

    **Southbound flow recap:** Mainland investors remained net buyers of HK$3.45bn on Wednesday, led by Tencent at HK$2.04bn and Xiaomi at HK$457m. Kingboard Laminates saw HK$2.17bn of net selling, while Alibaba and SMIC recorded net outflows of HK$241m and HK$331m respectively. [STCN]

  14. 14

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow fell 0.68%, S&P 500 0.75% and Nasdaq 1.13%; Nvidia lost 1.47%, Google 3.80%, and SanDisk, Coherent and Ciena fell more than 3%, while Western Digital gained about 2%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon index dropped 1.43%, with Alibaba down 4.74% and PDD down 1.43%, versus a 2.45% gain for New Oriental. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Oil rises as Hormuz remains shut:** Iran’s top national-security official said the Strait of Hormuz would not reopen until Tehran’s conditions were met. WTI rose 2.42% to US$92.71 and Brent 3.12% to US$98.39; meanwhile the dollar index gained 0.52%, offshore yuan weakened to 6.7115, spot gold fell 1.71% and silver dropped 3.95%. [Eastmoney] [NBD]
