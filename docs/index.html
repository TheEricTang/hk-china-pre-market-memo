Morning Market Memo | 26 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 25 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 26 Nov 2026 09:18 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **US–China tariffs:** USTR proposed Section 301 tariffs of up to 20% on most Chinese goods, with a 10% rate for a separate product list and exemptions for selected goods. The proposal remains subject to comments and a January 25 hearing, but puts renewed attention on Chinese exporters, EVs, solar, appliances and Southeast Asian supply chains. [USTR] [Reuters]

  2. 02

    **New-generation communications network:** MIIT, the cyberspace regulator and NDRC issued a State Council-approved plan targeting a comprehensive, performance-leading network by 2030. Tasks include nationwide 5G-A expansion, timely 6G construction, broader 10-gigabit optical coverage and faster low-orbit satellite-internet deployment, relevant to telecom operators, ZTE, optical equipment and satellite names. [MIIT]

  3. 03

    **Buyback and shareholder-increase lending:** CCB, ICBC and Bank of China signalled faster deployment of specialised loans supporting listed-company share purchases. Disclosed contracts and commitments exceed RMB460bn: CCB has signed over RMB100bn, while ICBC and BOC each reported close to or above RMB180bn. [CLS]

  4. 04

    **Goldman raises Hong Kong:** Goldman Sachs upgraded Hong Kong equities to overweight and forecast 2027 gains of 23% for MSCI China and 10% for CSI 300, alongside 4.5% Chinese GDP growth. Its preferred themes include AI, shareholder returns and earnings recoveries in consumption and cyclical sectors. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **US inflation and growth:** October headline PCE inflation accelerated to 3.8% year-on-year and core PCE reached 3.3%, while third-quarter GDP growth was revised sharply higher to an annualised 4.3% from 3.4%. The combination reinforces pressure on rate-sensitive Hong Kong growth stocks ahead of the December Fed meeting. [BEA] [BEA]

  6. 06

    **Li Auto 2015 HK:** Third-quarter revenue rose 5.4% to RMB28.8bn and gross margin recovered to 16.7% from 11.0% quarter-on-quarter. Net loss narrowed to RMB309.7m; fourth-quarter delivery guidance is 110,000–115,000 vehicles, implying 0.7%–5.3% year-on-year growth. [Li Auto]

  7. 07

    **XPeng 9868 HK technology day:** XPeng said its IRON humanoid robot will enter hardware mass production in January 2027, while initial deliveries of its second-generation VLA system are targeted for the first half. The company also said certification of its piloted eVTOL is in the final stage, adding fresh robotics, autonomous-driving and low-altitude-economy catalysts. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **CATL 300750 CH/3750 HK:** CATL repurchased 474,400 A-shares for approximately RMB99.997m on November 25, paying RMB210.13–212.22 per share. The shares are designated for cancellation under its RMB20bn–40bn repurchase programme. [Yicai]

  9. 09

    **GDS 9698 HK:** Third-quarter revenue increased 10.1% to RMB3.18bn and adjusted EBITDA rose 2.8% to RMB1.38bn, although the adjusted EBITDA margin narrowed to 43.4%. Committed and pre-committed data-centre area grew 25.5%, while utilisation rose to 81.2%, supporting the domestic computing-infrastructure read-through. [GDS]

  10. 10

    **SHEIN 625 HK:** Third-quarter revenue fell 5.9% to US$10.27bn, partly reflecting weaker European sales after removal of the low-value parcel duty exemption. Operating income rose 17.4% to US$743m and adjusted net income increased 26.8% to US$790m; the reported US$4.65bn net profit included a US$4.08bn preferred-share fair-value gain linked to the IPO. [HKEX]

  11. 11

    **Zhuhai Duty Free consolidation:** Zhuhai Duty Free Group 600185 CH proposed issuing shares and paying cash to acquire the remaining 49% of Zhuhai Duty Free, comprising 26% and 23% stakes from two state-owned counterparties. The target will become wholly owned after the major asset restructuring; trading resumes today. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Poly Developments 600048 CH:** The developer approved a public company-bond programme of up to RMB20bn with maturities of no more than ten years. Proceeds may refinance interest-bearing debt, replenish working capital, fund projects and finance property acquisitions. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Dongyi Risheng 002713 CH:** The controlling shareholder will donate an 85% stake in Huazhu Intelligent Computing to the listed company without consideration, followed by a RMB20m cash injection into the target. Dongyi Risheng said the transaction marks its entry into computing-centre operations and computing-power services, though the acquired business remains early-stage. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Keda Industrial 600499 CH:** Shareholder Liang Tongcan agreed to sell 95.89m shares, or 5% of Keda, to Hong Kong-based Sunda Group at RMB15 per share for RMB1.438bn. The strategic investment requires exchange confirmation and will leave Keda without a controlling shareholder or actual controller. [Sina]

  15. 15

    **Copper-foil capacity:** Defu Technology 301511 CH’s subsidiary plans to invest approximately RMB1.61bn in a 30,000-tonne annual high-performance copper-foil project in Anhui. The expansion is relevant to battery-material suppliers and lithium-battery equipment names. [Yicai]

  16. 16

    **Shanying International 600567 CH:** Paper-industry investor Cheng Jun launched a partial tender for 310.27m shares, equal to 5% of the company, at RMB1.18 per share. The RMB366m offer carries a roughly 28% premium to the pre-suspension close, is fully cash-backed and does not seek delisting; the stock resumes today. [NBD]

  17. 17

    **Hong Kong buybacks:** A total of 126 issuers repurchased HK$578m of shares on Wednesday. COSCO Shipping Holdings spent HK$85.4m, Pop Mart HK$49.7m and Miniso HK$30.0m; Miniso has now spent HK$1.06bn on buybacks this year. [Eastmoney]

  18. 18

    **Overnight US markets:** Dow +0.33%, S&P 500 −0.25% and Nasdaq −0.62%; SOX fell 0.65%, with Nvidia −3.91%, Micron −4.53% and AMD −1.75%. The Golden Dragon index gained 1.87%, led by Li Auto +11.03%, NIO +4.26% and Alibaba +1.65%; WTI rose 2.51% to US$84.11, spot gold gained 0.43% to US$3,571 and the US 10-year yield fell to 4.87%. S&P separately placed Nvidia’s AA ratings on negative watch over governance concerns; US markets are closed today for Thanksgiving. [Eastmoney] [AP] [Eastmoney]
