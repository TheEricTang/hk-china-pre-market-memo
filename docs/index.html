Morning Market Memo | 10 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 09 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 10 Sep 2026 08:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Financial-policy briefing today:** The State Council Information Office will hold a 15:00 HKT briefing on implementing the 15th Five-Year Plan and building a stronger financial system. Speakers include PBOC Deputy Governor Lu Lei, NFRA Deputy Director Cong Lin, CSRC Vice Chairman Li Chao and SAFE Deputy Administrator Li Bin, putting banks, brokers and the renminbi in focus. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **China–US AI dispute:** MOFCOM rejected claims by three US security agencies that six Chinese AI companies conducted unauthorised “industrial-scale” model distillation, calling distillation a normal industry practice. The ministry said China would take countermeasures if the US uses the allegations to suppress Chinese AI companies. [MOFCOM]

  3. 03

    **Beijing high-tech plan:** Beijing released its 2026–30 high-tech industries plan, calling for reusable-rocket and high-thrust engine breakthroughs, greater satellite and component mass-production capacity and participation in national constellations. The plan also backs advanced chips, new materials and six future-industry clusters, supporting the commercial-space and semiconductor supply chains. [Beijing Government] [CLS]

  4. 04

    **Beijing digital-economy targets:** A separate 2026–30 plan targets roughly 8% annual growth in Beijing’s digital economy and a share above 50% of local GDP by 2030. It calls for domestic computing clusters, commercial deployment of embodied intelligence, more than 100,000 robots across manufacturing and public-service scenarios, and 100% green-power use at new intelligent-computing centres. [Mysteel]

  5. 05

    **Medical-consumables procurement:** China’s seventh national bulk procurement round for medical consumables opened in Tianjin at 07:30 HKT. It covers 23 digestive-intervention products used mainly in endoscopic surgery, making submitted prices and winning suppliers relevant for domestic device makers and hospital suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **Apple enters foldables:** Apple launched the iPhone Duo, its first foldable handset, at RMB15,999 for the base model and RMB26,499 for the top 2TB version, with sales starting October 23. The company also unveiled the 2nm A20 Pro-powered iPhone 18 Pro range, providing a fresh read-through for HK/A-share display, hinge, casing, optics and component suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    **Enflame Technology listing:** Chinese cloud-AI chip developer Enflame Technology (688801 CH) confirmed it will list on Shanghai’s STAR Market on Friday at RMB142.18 per share. The IPO comprises 43.04 million shares, while only 17.90 million shares, or 4.16% of post-issue capital, will be freely tradable initially. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **Shenzhen Kaifa advanced packaging:** Shenzhen Kaifa Technology (000021 CH) approved a RMB1.85bn expansion of high-end memory-chip packaging and testing. The plan includes facilities capable of handling 90,000 conventional wafers and 10,000 2.5D wafers per month, plus a 2.5D/3DS advanced-packaging research line. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Hand Enterprise Solutions drops H-share plan:** Hand Enterprise Solutions (300170 CH) terminated its proposed H-share issuance and Hong Kong listing, citing the macro environment, capital-market conditions and its development plans. The company said the decision would not affect normal operations, but it removes a prospective HK IPO from the pipeline. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **Optical and copper theme warnings:** PCB maker Xunjiexing (688655 CH) said optical modules are not a major revenue source and its micron-scale products remain at the technical-discussion and sample stage. Huamai Technology (603042 CH) said data-centre and computing-related revenue is small, while Jingyi (002295 CH) warned that higher copper prices increase working-capital requirements rather than directly benefiting earnings. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **HK megacap buybacks:** Tencent (700 HK) disclosed a HK$100.43m repurchase of 231,000 shares at HK$432.80–438.20, with the stock to be cancelled. Xiaomi (1810 HK) bought back 1.90 million shares for approximately HK$50.08m, maintaining capital-return support for the internet and handset complex. [Eastmoney] [Tiger Brokers]

  12. 12

    **Southbound flows:** Mainland investors recorded HK$3.72bn of net buying on Wednesday, extending the recent accumulation despite a 0.17% Hang Seng decline. Baidu (9888 HK) attracted HK$1.74bn and Alibaba (9988 HK) HK$438m of net inflows, while Changfei Fiber Optical (6869 HK) was the largest active name but saw net selling. [STCN] [CLS]

  13. 13

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow fell 0.77%, S&P 500 0.48% and Nasdaq 0.64%. Storage and optical names outperformed—SK Hynix rose 7.05%, Micron more than 2% and Marvell more than 4%—but the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index lost 2.09%; Li Auto fell 4.41%, XPeng 3.29%, Alibaba 2.89%, JD.com 2.46% and NIO 2.37%. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **US Treasury buyback:** The US Treasury raised the maximum size of Thursday’s 10-to-20-year bond buyback to US$6bn, three times the originally indicated US$2bn. Long yields nevertheless rose: the 10-year finished at 4.8305% and the 30-year at 5.2868%, a firmer discount-rate backdrop for growth equities. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Oil above the geopolitical premium:** WTI gained 3.91% to US$96.67 and Brent traded above US$100 intraday as US–Iran retaliatory attacks around the Strait of Hormuz escalated. The move supports PetroChina, Sinopec and CNOOC upstream earnings but raises fuel-cost pressure for airlines, logistics operators and chemical producers. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    **Precious metals and FX:** Spot gold rose 1.07% to US$4,402 an ounce and spot silver advanced 2.31% to US$67.28; COMEX gold and silver settled 0.18% and 1.38% higher respectively. The dollar index edged up 0.03% to 98.817, while offshore renminbi was quoted at 6.70612 at 18:00 HKT Wednesday. [Eastmoney]
