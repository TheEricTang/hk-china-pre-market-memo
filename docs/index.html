Morning Market Memo | 22 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 21 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 22 Sep 2026 06:37 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **PBOC – monetary stance and financial opening:** Governor Pan Gongsheng told 15 foreign financial institutions that the PBOC will implement an appropriately accommodative monetary policy, steadily expand two-way financial-market opening and improve cross-border payment services and the convenience of international renminbi use. Bank of America, JPMorgan, HSBC, Standard Chartered, Goldman Sachs and UBS were among the participants. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    **Ant reorganises Alipay around agent commerce:** Ant Group CEO Han Xinyi announced that the Alipay, digital-payments and Sesame Credit units will be combined into a new Alipay business group. Wu Minzhi will become its president while continuing as Ant’s chief people officer, with the restructuring intended to accelerate commercial deployment of AI agents. [Sina]

  3. 03

    **Unitree launches lower-priced dexterous hand:** Unitree released the Dex5-S robotic hand with 22 degrees of freedom, one-to-one human-hand dimensions and a starting price of RMB39,900. The hand can carry 2kg, sustain a 1kg working load and offers tactile sensing on the Pro version, providing a fresh product catalyst for the A/H-share humanoid-robot supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    **CATL executes RMB1.1bn A-share buyback:** CATL bought 3.69m A shares on Monday at RMB296.26–301.00 each, spending approximately RMB1.1bn; the shares are intended for cancellation. The purchase falls under its previously approved RMB20bn–40bn programme, supporting both 300750 CH and 3750 HK sentiment. [Yicai] [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **Keda Manufacturing – RMB4.5bn graphite-anode expansion:** Keda Manufacturing 600499 CH plans a 500,000-tonne-a-year integrated graphite-anode project in Baotou, with construction expected to begin in October and take 10–12 months. Completion would raise its integrated artificial-graphite capacity to 680,000 tonnes; the company said its RMB3.68bn cash balance cannot fully cover the investment and bank financing will be required. [NBD] [China Fund News]

  6. 06

    **Sinomine starts Kitumba copper trial production:** Sinomine Resources 002738 CH said mining and processing works at its Zambia Kitumba project have begun full-line trial production, initially producing copper concentrate. The project is designed to process 3.5m tonnes of ore annually; the smelter is scheduled for trial preparation in 4Q26 and formal production in 1Q27, adding a fresh copper-volume catalyst. [Sina] [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    **Chaoying Electronics files for Hong Kong listing:** High-end PCB maker Chaoying Electronics 603175 CH submitted its H-share application to the HKEX, with CICC and Guolian Securities International as joint sponsors. Proceeds are intended for capacity expansion, intelligent manufacturing upgrades and R&D, adding another automotive, AI-server and data-storage PCB name to the A+H pipeline. [Sina] [Stockstar]

  8. 08

    **Galaxy Microelectronics – RMB1.08bn semiconductor project:** Galaxy Microelectronics 688689 CH approved investment of about RMB1.08bn in a high-end core-device industrialisation and innovation project. The three-year programme, scheduled to begin in January 2027, will expand production capacity for integrated-circuit discrete devices; environmental and other approvals remain outstanding. [Sina]

  9. 09

    **Zhongfu Circuit expands Thailand capacity:** Zhongfu Circuit 300814 CH plans to inject up to RMB500m into its wholly owned Thai subsidiary WTT to support construction and operations at its Thailand manufacturing base. The move extends the overseas footprint of the PCB supplier amid customer demand for geographically diversified electronics production. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **Hubei Yihua adds iron-phosphate capacity:** Hubei Yihua 000422 CH plans to invest approximately RMB1.15bn in a 150,000-tonne-a-year iron-phosphate project through its wholly owned new-energy materials subsidiary. The project extends its battery-materials chain and is relevant to domestic lithium-battery precursor suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Tencent resumes daily buyback:** Tencent 700 HK repurchased 235,000 shares for HKD100.5m at HKD423.20–434.20 per share, with the stock intended for cancellation. Total purchases under the current mandate have reached 46.48m shares, equivalent to 0.51% of shares outstanding when the mandate was approved. [Stockstar] [Investing.com]

  12. 12

    **Overnight US markets – semis and China ADRs higher:** The Dow rose 0.71%, S&P 500 gained 1.49% and Nasdaq climbed 2.26% to a record close; the SOX jumped 4.29%, led by Arm +17.16%, Intel +12.14%, AMD +9.95% and Qualcomm +9.29%, while Nvidia added 2.30% and Tesla 3.03%. The Golden Dragon Index gained 0.71%, with Tencent ADR +3.62%, Baidu +2.54%, Alibaba +2.22% and Bilibili +1.88%, although Li Auto fell 5.11%; WTI dropped 4.28% to US$91.97, while spot gold fell 0.79% to US$4,343.73. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Fed official keeps further tightening in view:** Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee warned that persistent inflation risks from energy, tariffs, strong demand and other supply shocks could require renewed policy action if demand overheats. The comments leave US-rate sensitivity elevated despite the overnight technology rally. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **EU introduces data-centre efficiency ratings:** The European Union unveiled an energy-efficiency rating system for AI data centres as it seeks to triple regional capacity over seven years without overloading power and water infrastructure. The rules are relevant to Chinese optical, power-management and cooling-equipment suppliers serving overseas AI infrastructure. [Eastmoney]
