Morning Market Memo | 21 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 20 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 21 Aug 2026 07:02 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    US markets – bonds weigh on indices: The Dow fell 1.32% to 52,759.21, the S&P 500 lost 0.87% and the Nasdaq dropped 1.00%. Storage and optical names outperformed—SK Hynix rose over 4%, Micron nearly 4% and Lumentum over 6%—while the Nasdaq Golden Dragon index fell 1.15%; Bilibili lost 3.76% and NetEase 5.85%, versus Li Auto’s 1.65% gain. [Eastmoney] [AP]

  2. 02

    Rates and commodities: The US 10-year yield rose 4.35bp to 4.6902% and the 30-year added 3.97bp to 5.2298%, reversing much of the relief from the Treasury’s expanded bond-buyback plan. WTI gained 2.16% to US$86.21 a barrel, COMEX gold rose 0.66% to US$4,575.10 and silver climbed 3.50% to US$68.13, supporting the oil and precious-metals complexes while higher long yields remain relevant for growth-stock valuations. [Eastmoney] [AP]

  3. 03

    Private investment policy: NDRC chairman Zheng Shanjie said the commission will make full use of existing measures and promptly plan practical incremental policies, while improving private-company access to major national projects. January–July private investment fell 0.5% year on year, keeping infrastructure, new-energy and advanced-manufacturing support in focus. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Shanghai property easing: Six municipal departments introduced eight measures covering provident-fund withdrawals, mortgage rules, housing vouchers, purchases of second-hand homes for subsidised rental housing and “sell old, buy new” subsidies of up to RMB80,000. The package takes effect today and is relevant to mainland developers, brokers, property managers and home-appliance demand. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Alibaba – cloud accelerates, profit misses: Alibaba 9988 HK reported fiscal first-quarter revenue of RMB268.95bn, up 9% and broadly in line with estimates, while adjusted net profit of RMB20.72bn missed the RMB25.58bn consensus cited by Eastmoney. External Alibaba Cloud revenue grew 45%, AI-product revenue reached RMB12.38bn and management expects AI and cloud growth to accelerate over coming quarters. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    China Ping An results: Ping An 2318 HK/601318 CH posted first-half net profit of RMB92.59bn, up 36.1%, with operating profit rising 8.3% to RMB84.20bn. Life and health new-business value increased 11.2%, and the insurer raised its interim dividend 3.2% to RMB0.98 per share. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Pop Mart – guidance and buyback: Pop Mart 9992 HK reported first-half revenue of RMB17.17bn, up 23.8%, but net-profit growth slowed to 8.9% and overseas revenue fell 11.1%. Chairman Wang Ning said the company will probably miss its 20% full-year growth target and announced a six-month RMB2bn–5bn share-buyback plan. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    CITIC Securities – record interim profit: CITIC Securities 6030 HK/600030 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB49.69bn, up 50%, and net profit of RMB23.34bn, up 69.6%, its best interim result. The board proposed a cash dividend of RMB4.27 per ten shares, with brokerage, investment and underwriting revenue all recording double-digit growth. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    GigaDevice buyback: GigaDevice 603986 CH approved a RMB1bn–2bn repurchase at no more than RMB750 per share over six months. The repurchased shares, estimated at 1.33m–2.67m at the price ceiling, will be cancelled, providing a fresh capital-return catalyst for the mainland memory and semiconductor-design chain. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Eoptolink equity incentives: Eoptolink 300502 CH proposed granting 6.48m restricted shares at RMB212 each to up to 961 employees. Vesting targets require at least RMB50bn of 2026 revenue, RMB140bn cumulatively for 2026–27 and RMB290bn for 2026–28, setting aggressive targets for the optical-module supplier. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    CICC merger review: The Shanghai Stock Exchange’s M&A committee will meet on 27 August to review CICC’s proposed share-swap absorption of Dongxing Securities and Cinda Securities. The timetable is a fresh catalyst for CICC 3908 HK/601995 CH and the wider mainland brokerage-consolidation theme. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Han’s CNC expands overseas: Han’s CNC 301200 CH plans to invest up to US$180m through a Hong Kong subsidiary in a Malaysian PCB-equipment production, R&D and service base. The two-year project is designed for annual capacity of 400 high-end PCB equipment units, extending the company’s exposure to overseas AI-server and electronics supply chains. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Shanghai Sinyang raises semiconductor investment: Shanghai Sinyang 300236 CH increased planned investment in its Shanghai Chemical Industry Park project from RMB580m to RMB1.05bn. The revised plan adds 5,000 tonnes of annual chip copper-interconnect electroplating-solution capacity and targets production by end-2028, reflecting stronger semiconductor-material orders. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Wuhan Tianyuan storage project: Wuhan Tianyuan 301127 CH’s controlled subsidiary plans to invest RMB404m in a 100MW/400MWh electrochemical energy-storage station in Jiaozhou. Management expects the project to complement its existing waste-to-energy assets and expand green-energy operations. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Tengyuan Cobalt adds DRC processing: Tengyuan Cobalt 301219 CH plans to invest US$18m in a 160,000-tonne sulphuric-acid facility and 5.3MW waste-heat power unit at its Democratic Republic of Congo site. Production is scheduled to begin during August–October 2027, allowing greater self-supply of key processing inputs and reducing transport and diesel-generation costs. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    Waymo moves to an in-house chip: Alphabet’s Waymo said its custom ASIC is already in mass production in its latest Robotaxi generation, accelerating sensor-data processing and reducing reliance on Nvidia and AMD hardware. The development is relevant to the cost and vertical-integration debate around Baidu 9888 HK and China’s listed autonomous-driving ecosystem. [Eastmoney]
