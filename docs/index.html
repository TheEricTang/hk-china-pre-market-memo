Morning Market Memo | 16 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 15 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 16 Sep 2026 08:49 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Technology lending definitions tighten:** Authorities are preparing an industry casebook that will narrow which projects banks may classify as technology-company loans, potentially excluding innovation projects tied to traditional industries. The framework is expected by year-end and could reduce reported technology-loan balances at listed banks. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **NDRC presses private-sector investment:** Chairman Zheng Shanjie met executives from Delixi, Runze Intelligence, Blue Arrow Aerospace and businesses in tourism and elderly care. The NDRC said it will open more application scenarios, improve project financing and regularly recommend investments in emerging industries, new infrastructure and consumption upgrades. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **Online hotel platforms face competition review:** The State Administration for Market Regulation and the Ministry of Culture and Tourism summoned Meituan, Douyin, JD.com, Trip.com, Tongcheng and Fliggy. Platforms were told to examine exclusive-cooperation and “lowest price across the internet” practices, creating fresh compliance scrutiny for 3690 HK, 9961 HK and 780 HK. [SAMR]

  4. 04

    **Steelmakers call for output and inventory cuts:** China Baowu, Ansteel, HBIS, Shougang and other leading mills jointly urged the industry to comply with production controls, reduce elevated inventories and stop selling below cost. The initiative targets the sector’s combination of weak demand, low prices and falling core-steel profitability. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **Geely considers consolidating battery assets:** A Geely executive said the group is reorganising several battery businesses and may eventually inject them into Geely Automobile 175 HK once the operations become profitable. The executive described CATL’s proposed acquisition of a Chongqing battery project as an individual capacity transaction rather than a wider disposal programme. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **Xingyun Technology triples server-purchasing ceiling:** Xingyun Technology 300209 CH proposed adding RMB130bn to its 2026 server and component budget, raising the annual ceiling from RMB70bn to RMB200bn, subject to shareholder approval. The company said the purchases support AI-computing and cross-border digital-trade operations; its five-year computing-order framework stood at RMB16.0bn as of 8 September. [Eastmoney] [jrj]

  7. 07

    **Runze commits RMB10bn to AI fund:** Runze Intelligence 300442 CH will take a 20.41% limited-partner interest in the RMB49.0bn Xiamen Shidai Shenyuan venture fund using its own capital. The fund targets artificial intelligence, embodied intelligence, technology applications and other frontier sectors. [Sina]

  8. 08

    **Hangcha seeks RMB22.59bn for robotics expansion:** Hangcha Group 603298 CH proposed a six-year convertible bond to finance a forklift-robot smart factory, new-energy forklift capacity and logistics-robot research, with part of the proceeds allocated to working capital. Its intelligent-logistics and robotics revenue rose 30.81% to RMB796m in the first half. [NBD]

  9. 09

    **Jinzhou Pipe makes large liquid-cooling commitment:** Jinzhou Pipe 002443 CH will inject RMB6.8bn into its wholly owned liquid-cooling subsidiary, increasing registered capital from RMB20m to RMB7.0bn. Funds will support production-line expansion, research, operations and market development, although the company warned of technology-selection and capacity-utilisation risks. [Sina]

  10. 10

    **Fenghua rejects MLCC relabelling claims:** Fenghua Advanced Technology 000636 CH said its high-end multilayer ceramic capacitors are independently developed and manufactured, rejecting social-media claims that overseas products were being relabelled. The post-close clarification matters after strong recent attention on domestic MLCC substitution and capacity expansion. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Tencent maintains daily buyback:** Tencent 700 HK repurchased 229,000 shares on Tuesday for HK$100.7m at HK$428.00–445.80 per share, extending its buyback run to 22 consecutive trading days. Purchases since 17 August total 9.47m shares and HK$4.21bn. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **CICC sees longer optical-component order visibility:** CICC said orders at some high-speed optical-chip companies extend into 2028, while optical-module visibility covers 2027 and beyond. It identified advanced-process DSPs as a potential bottleneck and expects continuing demand for high-speed optical chips, mSAP PCBs and related HK/A-listed AI-infrastructure suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **US equities and China ADRs close lower:** The Dow fell 0.63%, S&P 500 0.45% and Nasdaq 0.78%; Nvidia gained 0.57%, while Amazon lost 2.02%, Microsoft 1.64% and Tesla 0.67%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon index fell 1.14%, led by XPeng −4.48%, Li Auto −3.37%, NIO −1.91%, Bilibili −1.78% and Baidu/PDD −1.64% each; Alibaba edged up 0.10%. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Oil and bond yields remain the main external pressure:** WTI rose 4.03% to US$105.48 a barrel and Brent gained 2.65% to US$108.48, supporting energy names but raising fuel-cost exposure for airlines and downstream manufacturers. The US 10-year yield ended near 5.00%, DXY rose to 99.63, COMEX gold fell 0.43% to US$4,333.40 and offshore renminbi softened to about 6.712 per dollar. [Eastmoney]
