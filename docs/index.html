Morning Market Memo | 27 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 26 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 27 Nov 2026 09:10 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    AI data rules: The National Data Administration opened consultation on rules supporting legally obtained, generated, derived and contract-acquired data for AI training and inference, while encouraging payment for high-quality data. The draft also sets privacy, copyright, commercial-secret and output-risk requirements, with comments due by 6 December. [National Data Administration]

  2. 02

    Guangdong computing plan: Guangdong targets more than 1,000 EFlops of intelligent computing capacity by 2028 and over 2,500 EFlops by 2030, with an ambition to reach 4,000 EFlops. The province will encourage data centres to prioritise domestic AI chips, develop a Greater Bay Area cross-border computing corridor and explore subsea and space-based computing infrastructure. [Mysteel] [NBD]

  3. 03

    Buyback lending exceeds RMB600bn: ICBC, Agricultural Bank of China, Bank of China and China Construction Bank have signed more than RMB600bn of stock-repurchase and shareholder-increase loan contracts. The update follows the PBOC’s conversion of its supporting relending facility into a permanent arrangement and the rate cut to 1%, reinforcing funding availability for listed-company capital returns. [Sina] [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Domestic fuel prices cut: China reduced benchmark gasoline and diesel prices by RMB440 and RMB425 per tonne respectively from midnight, equivalent to roughly RMB0.35–0.37 per litre for common grades. The second November reduction lowers transport and logistics costs while trimming domestic refining and fuel-retailing margins. [Beijing News] [Hunan DRC]

  5. 05

    Lithium derivatives: Guangzhou Futures Exchange starts trading lithium-hydroxide options today on the LI2706–LI2711 futures months, adding a dedicated hedging instrument for high-nickel battery materials. The underlying futures debuted Thursday, expanding the exchange’s新能源-metals complex alongside lithium carbonate, silicon, polysilicon, platinum and palladium. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Commercial space: China launched the Yaogan-49 01 group satellite at 17:38 from Xichang aboard a Long March-2D rocket, placing it into the planned orbit. The satellite will support scientific testing, land-resource surveys, crop estimation and disaster mitigation; it was the Long March series’ 681st mission. [Xinhua]

  7. 07

    Weimob expands into AI infrastructure: Weimob Group (2013 HK) agreed to buy 5,000 Nvidia RTX PRO 5500 Blackwell GPUs and related equipment for approximately RMB430.5m. The company plans to support its own AI products and open spare capacity to third-party customers, extending from SaaS and digital marketing into computing services. [Xinhua Finance]

  8. 08

    HK biotech filings: HUTCHMED (13 HK) said China’s NMPA accepted the application for Orpathys combined with osimertinib as first-line treatment for EGFR-mutated, MET-overexpressing advanced lung cancer. Boan Biotech (6955 HK) separately secured acceptance for BA1104, its nivolumab biosimilar covering several tumour types and the first domestic Opdivo biosimilar submitted for approval. [HSTong] [Zhitung]

  9. 09

    China Shenhua capital actions: China Shenhua (1088 HK/601088 CH) authorised an H-share issuance under its existing general mandate, with timing, size, pricing and investors to depend on market conditions. The board also approved a RMB1.96bn cash injection into its wholly owned overseas investment subsidiary and elected Zhang Changyan chairman. [Sina]

  10. 10

    A-share buybacks: Tianqi Lithium (9696 HK/002466 CH) approved a RMB400m–800m A-share repurchase at no more than RMB53.08 per share for incentives or employee ownership. Songcheng Performance, Jingce Electronic and Sunwoda separately announced or proposed RMB100m-plus programmes, with several companies planning cancellation and capital reduction. [Securities Times]

  11. 11

    CATL and Pop Mart repurchases: CATL (3750 HK/300750 CH) bought 147,700 A shares for RMB29.99m at RMB202.39–204.34, with the shares intended for cancellation. Pop Mart (9992 HK) spent HK$44.31m repurchasing 320,000 shares at HK$137.90–139.50, also for cancellation. [HKEX] [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Moutai upgrade: JPMorgan upgraded Kweichow Moutai (600519 CH) from neutral to overweight and set a RMB1,800 target, around 23% above the referenced market price. The reversal from its May downgrade provides a fresh external catalyst for Moutai and the wider premium-baijiu group. [21st Century Business Herald]

  13. 13

    Nvidia shipment restrictions: Nvidia reportedly told manufacturing partners to suspend AI-server deliveries to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia under new US licensing controls; Dell and Supermicro customers were among those informed. No resumption date was given, creating uncertainty for Southeast Asian data centres serving China-linked offshore computing demand. [Eastmoney] [Anadolu]

  14. 14

    Oil supply risk: Saudi Arabia said its Jizan refinery was attacked, causing a fire and a temporary suspension of some operations. Separately, OPEC+ is expected to maintain current production targets through at least the first quarter of 2027 when it meets Sunday, according to people familiar with the talks. [Xinhua] [Reuters]

  15. 15

    Overnight markets: US cash equities were closed for Thanksgiving, so there were no Thursday closes for the Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq, SOX or Nasdaq Golden Dragon/China ADRs; US Treasuries were also shut. Germany’s DAX rose 2.08%, the FTSE 100 gained 1.75% and Euro Stoxx 50 added 2.04%; DXY gained 0.06% to 103.78, spot gold fell 0.26% to US$3,567.39, silver rose 0.28% to US$41.52, WTI gained 0.90% to US$84.92 and Brent rose 1.17% to US$91.69. [Jin10] [Eastmoney]
