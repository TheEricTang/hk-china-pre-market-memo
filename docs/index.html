Morning Market Memo | 21 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 18 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 21 Sep 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Overnight US markets:** The S&P 500 gained 0.17%, Nasdaq 0.39% and Dow fell 0.18% on Friday. Semiconductors led: the sector ETF rose 2.21%, SanDisk gained about 11%, Seagate over 6%, Western Digital over 4%, Micron over 3% and Nvidia 1.34%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index added 0.76%, with Alibaba +4.33%, Bilibili +3.47%, PDD +1.51% and JD +1.05%. The US 10-year yield finished at 4.9961%, spot gold rose 0.84% to US$4,378.23/oz, silver gained 1.63%, DXY closed at 100.222 and Eastmoney’s continuous WTI contract fell 1.81% to US$95.47/bbl. [CLS] [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **China–US trade talks:** The two countries’ economic and trade teams began consultations in New York on Sunday morning local time. Vice Premier He Lifeng is leading the Chinese delegation during a September 19–23 US visit, making any negotiating update relevant to exporters, technology names and broader China risk sentiment. [Xinhua]

  3. 03

    **US secondary-tariff risk:** China’s Commerce Ministry responded after Washington enacted legislation allowing tariffs of up to 100% on third countries importing Russian oil or gas. Beijing opposed unilateral and secondary sanctions, said normal trade should not be subject to third-party coercion and reserved the right to take necessary measures. [MOFCOM] [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    **Iran offers terms to end the conflict:** Iran said it had submitted conditions to the US through Qatar, including an end to hostilities, the release of frozen Iranian assets and termination of the maritime blockade. Washington’s response remains pending; the development is relevant to oil, airlines, shipping and chemicals after recent energy-price volatility. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **State Council targets silver-economy and healthcare capacity:** Premier Li Qiang’s Friday-evening State Council meeting called for more inclusive elderly-care services, new consumption scenarios for older people and stronger links between home, community and institutional care. It also ordered an expansion of medical rehabilitation and nursing capacity, support for smart rehabilitation equipment, more high-quality sporting events and stronger protection for traditional Chinese medicine knowledge. [Gov.cn]

  6. 06

    **Regulators intensify action against low-price competition:** The State Administration for Market Regulation said authorities had opened 29,000 cases involving pricing, competition, quality and certification violations under the campaign against low-quality, low-price practices. The regulator wants competition to shift from price-cutting toward quality and value, with implications for consumer goods, autos, internet platforms and industrial supply chains. [SAMR] [Shanghai Securities News]

  7. 07

    **PBOC liquidity calendar:** RMB272bn of seven-day reverse repos mature over the shortened September 21–24 week, with no maturities today or Tuesday, followed by RMB110bn Wednesday and RMB162bn Thursday. A further RMB180bn three-month Treasury cash deposit matures Tuesday; economists expect flexible operations to keep quarter-end funding broadly stable. [Yicai] [China Securities Journal]

  8. 08

    **AI plus manufacturing:** Vice Minister of Industry and Information Technology Xin Guobin told the World Manufacturing Convention that China will advance “AI+manufacturing,” develop open-source foundation and industry-specific models, expand smart-factory development and strengthen next-generation intelligent-manufacturing hardware and software. The message supports domestic AI infrastructure, industrial software, automation and robotics suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **ChangXin Memory begins G5 production:** ChangXin Technology said its fifth-generation DRAM platform has entered mass production, increasing dies produced per wafer by at least 50% from the previous generation. A 24GB LPDDR5X product based on the platform is also in production for mainstream flagship phones, providing a fresh catalyst for the domestic memory, packaging and equipment chain. [STCN] [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **RV-consumption measures:** The State Council General Office forwarded a ten-ministry package intended to remove bottlenecks across recreational-vehicle consumption. Nine measures cover product quality, registration and scrappage, driving tests, road access and parking, campground construction, tourism products, rentals, financing and promotional activity, benefiting auto aftermarket, tourism and campsite operators. [Ministry of Culture and Tourism]

  11. 11

    **Xinhua Media to acquire CLS parent:** Xinhua Media (600825 CH) plans to issue shares at RMB3.93 each to purchase 100% of Shanghai Jiemian CLS Technology, constituting a major and connected restructuring. The company resumes trading today; the deal adds financial news, commercial-information and internet-advertising businesses while leaving Shanghai United Media Group and the Shanghai SASAC in control. [CLS]

  12. 12

    **Tencent buyback:** Tencent (700 HK) repurchased 237,000 shares after Friday’s close for HK$100.45m at HK$425.20–430.20 per share, with the shares designated for cancellation. This was the company’s 25th consecutive trading day of repurchases. [STCN]

  13. 13

    **FII completes RMB1bn repurchase:** Foxconn Industrial Internet (601138 CH) completed its buyback after purchasing 15.83m shares, equal to 0.08% of issued capital, at RMB59.91–66.40 per share. The shares are intended to support company value and shareholder interests and may be sold after the required 12-month holding period. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Aihua plans high-end capacitor expansion:** Aihua Group (603989 CH) proposed a private placement of up to RMB1.88bn, allocating RMB1.5bn to an integrated high-end capacitor capacity project and RMB380m to working capital. The announcement is relevant to the domestic capacitor and AI-electronics component chain. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Shenghe Resources denies control-sale report:** Rare-earth producer Shenghe Resources (600392 CH) said its controlling shareholder has no plan to transfer control, rejecting overseas-media reports that an industry group could acquire the company and indirectly gain interests in its foreign assets. The clarification addresses a weekend rumour in a standing priority sector. [Eastmoney]
