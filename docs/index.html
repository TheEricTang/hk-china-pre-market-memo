Morning Market Memo | 27 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 26 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 27 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    PBOC – new bond-market liquidity backstop: The central bank established a Bond-market Non-bank Large-redemption Swap Facility, or BNSF, for periods of systemic stress. Eligible bank wealth-management units and public-fund managers may borrow central-bank bills for repo financing, allowing them to purchase qualifying bonds or fund units facing redemptions; this adds a formal liquidity channel for the asset-management sector. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    Active ETFs – three more funds hit their caps: Active ETFs managed by Huatai-PineBridge, Ping An and ChinaAMC reached their RMB2bn fundraising limits and will close early, following first-day sell-outs at several other managers. The first batch may list together in late November, extending the pipeline of incremental equity products. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    WuXi AppTec – guidance raised again: WuXi AppTec (2359 HK/603259 CH) reported nine-month revenue of RMB45.54bn, up 38.6%, and net profit of RMB19.16bn, up 62.4%. It lifted 2026 group revenue guidance to RMB61.0bn–61.9bn from RMB58.5bn–60.5bn and raised expected continuing-business growth to 40%–42%, a fresh catalyst for the CXO sector. [CLS] [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    CITIC Securities – strong nine months, weaker third quarter: CITIC Securities (6030 HK/600030 CH) posted nine-month net profit of RMB31.02bn, up 32.7%, on revenue of RMB66.59bn. Third-quarter net profit fell 20.2% year on year to RMB7.68bn and declined 41% sequentially, focusing attention on trading and investment-banking momentum across the broker sector. [CLS] [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    ZTE – quarterly profit jumped from a low base: ZTE (763 HK/000063 CH) reported third-quarter revenue of RMB38.81bn, up 34.0%, while net profit rose 394.5% to RMB1.31bn. The release is relevant to telecom equipment, computing infrastructure and the broader communications supply chain. [CLS] [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Cambricon – chairman rejected shipment speculation: Cambricon (688256 CH) chairman and general manager Chen Tianshi said at the company’s third-quarter briefing that it had never communicated the market claim that 2027 chip shipments would exceed one million units. The denial removes an unsupported figure that had been circulating around the domestic AI-accelerator trade. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Shannon Semiconductor – storage demand remained strong: Shannon Semiconductor (300475 CH) said nine-month revenue exceeded RMB100bn and net profit reached RMB5.34bn, with third-quarter net profit near RMB1.7bn. Management expects the storage-supply shortage to persist through 2028, reinforcing the overnight strength in global memory names and the read-through to Chinese distributors and module suppliers. [STCN] [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    A-share buybacks – cancellation plans led the announcements: Kingfa Sci. & Tech. (600143 CH) proposed an RMB300m–500m repurchase at no more than RMB19.57 per share, with all shares to be cancelled; the company secured a loan commitment covering up to RMB450m. Rijiu Optoelectronics proposed an RMB100m–200m cancellation-linked repurchase, while Dajin Heavy Industry’s chairman proposed buying back up to 10% of its issued H shares. [STCN]

  9. 09

    CICC – three-way brokerage combination completed: CICC (3908 HK/601995 CH) completed its share-exchange absorption of Dongxing Securities and China Securities Credit Investment. It registered 3.104bn new A shares, of which 1.136bn will begin trading on 29 October; the remaining 1.968bn shares held by designated shareholders are subject to a 36-month lock-up. [China Fund News]

  10. 10

    Anthropic – public IPO filing submitted: Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 with the US SEC and plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading the offering. The share count, price range and listing date remain unset; the filing is a new valuation marker for listed AI, cloud and data-centre infrastructure names. [SEC] [STCN]

  11. 11

    Overnight US markets – semis and optics led: The Dow rose 0.28%, the S&P 500 gained 0.70% and the Nasdaq added 0.82%. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index climbed 2.82%; Nvidia rose 2.39%, SK Hynix gained more than 4%, Western Digital and Corning added more than 3%, Micron rose nearly 3%, Lumentum gained more than 8% and Coherent more than 5%. Tesla advanced 5.21%, while Apple fell 1.10%. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    China ADRs – modestly positive but mixed: The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index rose 0.63%. Hesai gained 4.27%, Baidu added 0.84% and Alibaba rose 0.61%, while XPeng fell 0.98% and PDD declined 1.15%, implying a mixed opening signal for Hong Kong internet, autonomous-driving and EV names. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Cross-assets – oil fell while gold and short yields rose: WTI crude declined 1.96% to US$101.25 a barrel; COMEX gold gained 0.58% to US$4,031 an ounce and spot gold rose 0.54% to US$4,017.20. The US ten-year yield was broadly unchanged at 4.995%, the two-year yield rose 3.6bp to 4.718%, and the DXY gained 0.15% to 101.959; offshore renminbi was around 6.668 per dollar in the early-evening reference. [Eastmoney]
