Morning Market Memo | 20 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 19 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 20 Aug 2026 07:00 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    NDRC – “six networks” coordination: The National Development and Reform Commission established a “2+3+N” coordination mechanism linking two power-grid groups, three telecom operators and computing-related companies to accelerate computing, next-generation communications and new-power-grid projects. The announcement is relevant to data centres, optical networks, grid equipment and telecom infrastructure. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    JD.com – China blocks assistance with EU subsidy probe: The Ministry of Justice, working with MOFCOM and other agencies, ruled that cross-border measures used under the EU Foreign Subsidies Regulation in its JD.com investigation constitute improper extraterritorial jurisdiction. Organisations and individuals are prohibited from implementing or assisting with the measures, providing a fresh legal response around JD.com’s proposed European acquisition. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Medical insurance – support for innovative drugs: The National Healthcare Security Administration’s 15th Five-Year Plan sets 26 measures across eight areas, including procedures to add eligible innovative drugs to reimbursement lists and improve commercial-insurance coverage. It also proposes a launch-pricing mechanism that recognises clinical value and allows highly innovative products to maintain relatively stable initial pricing, relevant to the HK/A biotech and medical-device sectors. [China Securities Journal]

  4. 04

    CATL – five-year auto partnership: Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor and CATL signed a five-year strategic cooperation agreement, extending CATL’s engagement with domestic vehicle manufacturers. The agreement is a fresh read-through for CATL 3750 HK/300750 CH and the battery supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Hengrui Pharma – new RMB1–2bn buyback: Hengrui Pharma reported first-half revenue of RMB15.46bn and net profit of RMB4.47bn; innovative-drug sales rose 16.4% to RMB8.81bn. After completing a RMB1.00bn A-share repurchase, the company approved another RMB1–2bn programme at up to RMB81.78 per share for employee ownership or incentives, relevant to 1276 HK/600276 CH. [Securities Times]

  6. 06

    Foxconn Industrial Internet – first shares repurchased: Foxconn Industrial Internet 601138 CH bought 2.41m shares for RMB149m on Wednesday, its first purchase under a previously announced RMB1–2bn programme. Teradyne-related charging-equipment name TGOOD and smart-grid supplier Willfar Information also conducted their first repurchases, adding fresh support for the A-share hardware complex. [China Securities Journal]

  7. 07

    Saiyi Information – RMB6.45bn computing contract: Saiyi Information 300687 CH signed two five-year contracts to provide high-performance computing services to an unnamed customer; the combined value equals 311% of 2025 revenue. Deployment is expected within three months, but the company has also disclosed up to RMB60bn of server-purchase commitments, making funding and execution key watchpoints. [CLS]

  8. 08

    Litong Electronics – RMB5bn placement for AI capacity: Litong Electronics 603629 CH plans to raise up to RMB5bn, allocating RMB4bn to a computing centre in Zhongwei, Ningxia, and RMB1bn to working capital. The RMB5.91bn project has a six-month construction period and will purchase GPU servers and networking equipment for integrated training and inference services. [China Fund News]

  9. 09

    MEMS and optical components – Minxin acquisition: Minxin Microelectronics 688286 CH will spend RMB177m through share purchases and a capital injection to obtain 54% of Pudan Optoelectronics. The target’s products are intended to expand Minxin’s semiconductor and optoelectronic component portfolio, although the deal carries a valuation premium of roughly 195%. [China Fund News]

  10. 10

    Nanjing state assets – large-scale consolidation: Nanjing SASAC is injecting stakes in municipal construction, transport and investment groups into Nanjing State-owned Assets Investment Management, involving assets of about RMB486.3bn. Nanjing Gaoke, Nanjing Tourism and Nanjing Public Utilities said their controlling shareholders remain unchanged, but the enlarged platform should have greater capacity for investment and asset restructuring. [Shanghai Securities News]

  11. 11

    SK Hynix – record buyback and cancellation: SK Hynix approved a KRW40tn, approximately US$28.3bn, repurchase and cancellation programme covering about 24m shares or 3.3% of its issued stock. The action supports Asian memory sentiment, although US-listed storage names fell overnight, leaving a mixed read-through for Hua Hong 1347 HK, SMIC 981 HK and A-share memory suppliers. [Science and Technology Daily]

  12. 12

    Google–Marvell – custom AI-chip partnership expands: Google received warrants to acquire up to 59m Marvell shares at US$206.58, with a maximum value of US$12.2bn, as the companies deepen cooperation on TPU accelerators, storage controllers and networking chips. Marvell rose as much as 14% intraday; the agreement reinforces demand for custom silicon, advanced packaging and high-speed interconnects. [CLS]

  13. 13

    Cancer vaccine – positive phase III result: Moderna and Merck said their personalised mRNA melanoma vaccine met its objective in a large phase III trial. Moderna surged 176.97%, Merck gained 12.6% and the Nasdaq Biotechnology Index rose 6.39%, providing a strong overnight read-through for HK-listed vaccine and innovative-drug developers. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Fed and US Treasury – rates remain the macro swing factor: Federal Reserve minutes showed a sizeable group of officials supported raising rates if inflation failed to improve; three officials had favoured a 25bp increase at the July meeting. Separately, the US Treasury doubled the maximum size of long-duration bond buybacks from US$2bn to at least US$4bn, pushing the 10-year yield down 6.1bp to 4.641% and the 30-year yield down 9.3bp to 5.191%. [Xinhua] [CLS]

  15. 15

    Overnight US markets – indices edge higher; China ADRs outperform: The Dow rose 0.22%, S&P 500 0.21% and Nasdaq 0.16%; Tesla gained 4.23% while Nvidia fell 0.99%. Storage and optics were weak—Seagate −7.87%, Western Digital −6.87%, Lumentum −5.23% and Coherent −6.19%—but the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index gained 1.51%, led by Li Auto +4.87%, PDD +3.36%, Baidu +2.20%, Bilibili +1.88% and JD.com +1.84%. Spot gold jumped 4.35% to US$4,523, silver 5.79% to US$67.01, WTI added 0.40% to US$84.40 and the DXY fell 0.83% to 98.833. [Eastmoney]
