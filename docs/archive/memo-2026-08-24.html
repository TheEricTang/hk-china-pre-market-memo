Morning Market Memo | 24 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 21 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 24 Aug 2026 07:05 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Alibaba placement priced: Alibaba (9988 HK) priced 710mn new shares at HK$112.70 each, raising HK$80bn, with closing expected on 26 August. All net proceeds are earmarked for full-stack AI capabilities and infrastructure; this is the group’s first Hong Kong share placement since its 2019 listing. [Alibaba]

  2. 02

    New-generation communications network: Premier Li Qiang’s Friday-evening State Council meeting called for moderately advanced construction across terrestrial, space, international and converged networks. The government will support smart terminals, industrial internet, satellite connectivity, smart cities and manufacturing through land, power and spectrum provision. [China Government]

  3. 03

    Corporate arrears clean-up: The State Council also ordered faster settlement of overdue corporate accounts, directing local governments to use special-purpose bonds and relending facilities to break payment chains. Regulators will tighten disclosure and enforcement against large companies delaying payments to smaller suppliers, relevant to construction, equipment and government-service contractors. [China Government]

  4. 04

    YMTC STAR Market IPO accepted: Shanghai Stock Exchange accepted Yangtze Memory Technologies’ application for a proposed RMB33bn offering, potentially the STAR Market’s largest IPO. Of the planned proceeds, RMB20.8bn will upgrade mass-production lines and RMB12.2bn will fund R&D, providing a fresh valuation reference for domestic NAND, equipment and materials names. [Caixin]

  5. 05

    Inner Mongolia green-computing build-out: Saturday’s Green Computing Conference produced 13 agreements worth a combined RMB136.1bn across Hohhot and Ulanqab. Projects include ByteDance’s Volcano Engine GW-scale campuses, a Cambricon chip laboratory, GCL Energy Technology’s power-computing integration project and a China Telecom data centre. [China News]

  6. 06

    DeepSeek changes API pricing: From Sunday, DeepSeek classified all Saturday and Sunday API usage as off-peak, priced at half the weekday peak rate. V4-Pro output is RMB13.50 per million tokens off-peak versus RMB27 at peak, supporting weekend batch workloads while retaining the broader price increases introduced on 17 August. [Sina]

  7. 07

    Zhongji Innolight earnings: Zhongji Innolight (300308 CH) reported first-half revenue of RMB41.78bn, up 182.5%, and net profit of RMB13.65bn, up 241.7%. Second-quarter profit rose 38% sequentially to RMB7.92bn, extending the strong earnings signal for the optical-module and AI-interconnect chain. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Dongshan Precision raises optical investment: Dongshan Precision (002384 CH) posted first-half net profit of RMB2.96bn, up 290.1%, on revenue of RMB27.80bn. It also lifted planned investment in optical chips, modules and overseas facilities by US$500mn, taking total project investment from US$1.2bn to US$1.7bn. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    YOFC profit jumps: Yangtze Optical Fibre and Cable (601869 CH/6869 HK) reported first-half revenue of RMB9.81bn, up 53.6%, and net profit of RMB2.93bn, up 888.9%. The company proposed a RMB1.06-per-share interim dividend, with the result reinforcing the fibre and communications-equipment read-through from Friday’s State Council meeting. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Zijin Mining earnings: Zijin Mining (601899 CH/2899 HK) recorded first-half net profit of RMB39.17bn, up 68.2%, on revenue of RMB194.18bn. It proposed an interim dividend of RMB0.42 per ten shares, giving the copper and gold complex an additional company-specific catalyst after the weekend surge in metals. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    ZTE profit pressure: ZTE (000063 CH/0763 HK) reported first-half revenue of RMB78.03bn, up 9.1%, but net profit fell 45.6% to RMB2.75bn. Second-quarter profit improved 10% sequentially, but the headline decline may temper the broader policy support for communications infrastructure. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    China Tungsten High-Tech expansion: China Tungsten High-Tech (000657 CH) reported first-half revenue of RMB16.39bn, up 108.5%, and net profit of RMB2.08bn, up 280.5%, helped by higher tungsten-product prices. Its subsidiary also approved three precision micro-tool capacity projects serving multilayer and high-speed AI PCBs, with combined planned investment of about RMB526mn. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Tencent buyback: Tencent (0700 HK) repurchased 667,000 shares on Friday for HK$300mn at HK$445.20–457.00. This marked a fifth consecutive session of buybacks, taking the five-day total to 3.36mn shares and HK$1.50bn. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Overnight US markets: The Dow rose 0.98%, S&P 500 0.43% and Nasdaq 0.43% on Friday, although all three declined for the week. Tesla gained 5.14% while Nvidia fell 0.98%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index added 0.4%, with Alibaba down 8.59%, PDD down 1.27%, NetEase up 6.98%, Futu up 9.68%, Bilibili up 2.4% and NIO up 2.1%. [Sina]

  15. 15

    Cross-assets: Spot gold rose 1.88% to US$4,604.09/oz and silver gained 1.32% to US$68.99, while LME copper climbed US$179 to US$14,216/tonne; WTI slipped 0.22% to US$86.64/bbl. The US 10-year yield ended at 4.7339%, the two-year at 4.2359%, and DXY eased 0.09% to 98.801, leaving gold and copper miners with the clearest commodity read-through. [Eastmoney]
