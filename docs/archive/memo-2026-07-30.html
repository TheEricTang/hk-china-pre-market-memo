Morning Market Memo | 30 Jul 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 29 Jul 2026 16:00 HKT close → 30 Jul 2026 07:42 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Source note – Eastmoney’s same-day 经济早餐 was not retrievable from the column index by the cutoff; its 06:15 global-market wrap was used for final overnight closes and cross-assets. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    Overnight US markets – broad indices fell but China ADRs outperformed: the Dow lost 2.19%, S&P 500 1.52% and Nasdaq 1.74%. Micron fell nearly 10%; Coherent lost more than 8%; Credo, Lumentum and SanDisk dropped over 7%; Marvell fell over 6%; and Intel and AMD lost over 5%, a negative read-through for HK/A storage and optical names. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index gained 1.73%, with New Oriental up over 15%, PDD over 3% and Bilibili and Tencent ADRs over 2%. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Fed – hold accompanied by three tightening votes: the FOMC kept the federal-funds target at 3.5%–3.75% for a fifth meeting, passing the decision 9–3. Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack, Minneapolis Fed President Neel Kashkari and Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan preferred a 25bp increase, the first three same-direction dissents since 2016. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Microsoft – Azure growth beat expectations: fiscal fourth-quarter revenue rose 18% to US$90.0bn, operating income reached US$40.6bn and adjusted EPS was US$4.74. Azure and other cloud-services revenue increased 43%, while annual Azure revenue exceeded US$100bn for the first time; the shares gained more than 2% after hours, supporting cloud-computing demand but contrasting with the overnight hardware sell-off. [Microsoft] [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Meta – higher spending and softer guidance hit the shares: second-quarter revenue rose 28% to US$60.8bn, but net income fell 14% to US$15.8bn. Third-quarter revenue guidance was US$61bn–64bn and Meta lifted the lower end of its full-year capital-expenditure range to US$130bn–145bn; the stock fell more than 9% after hours, relevant to Chinese internet and AI-capex sentiment. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Oil and precious metals – Middle East escalation lifted energy sharply: WTI settled 6.74% higher at US$84.60 a barrel after President Trump threatened retaliation following an Iranian attack on US forces in Jordan. COMEX gold added 0.66% to US$4,065.50 an ounce and spot gold gained 0.94%, favouring Chinese oil producers and gold miners while raising input-cost pressure for airlines and transport. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Tinavi Medical – orthopaedic acquisition and resumption: Tinavi Medical 688277 CH plans to acquire 62% of Shanghai MicroPort Orthopedics through a share issue and raise matching funds; pricing remains undetermined. The target brings joint implants plus established US, European and Japanese operations, extending Tinavi’s surgical-robot business into an integrated robot-and-implant platform; trading resumes today. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Haoneng – robot reducer capacity: Haoneng 603809 CH plans a RMB1bn robot-joint reducer production base in Luzhou, designed for annual capacity of five million units. The post-close announcement adds a fresh capacity catalyst for the A/H robotics and precision-transmission complex. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    COSCO Shipping Development – 15 new bulk carriers: subsidiary Hainan COSCO Shipping Development plans to order fifteen 210,000-deadweight-tonne bulk carriers from shipyards under CSSC and Xiamen Xiangyu. The pre-tax contract value totals RMB7.92bn, or RMB528m per vessel, supporting shipbuilding order visibility and dry-bulk fleet renewal. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Hengxing New Material – two specialty-chemical projects: Hengxing New Material 603276 CH plans approximately RMB600m of investment across projects in Shandong and Jiangsu. The projects cover natural organic ester and aldehyde-acid derivatives, specialty fine chemicals, silicones and wet electronic chemicals, expanding its higher-end materials mix. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Base metals – copper prices drove strong first-half earnings: Western Mining 601168 CH reported revenue of RMB39.44bn, up 25%, and net profit of RMB4.17bn, up 123%, citing higher copper, molybdenum, lead, zinc and precious-metal prices. Shengtun Mining 600711 CH posted net profit of RMB1.80bn, up 71.4%, and proposed a RMB0.05-per-share dividend, adding company-level support to the copper complex. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Lianchuang Electronic – local state capital to take control: the current controlling shareholder agreed to sell 76.93m shares at RMB8.19 each, for RMB630m, to an investment platform under Shouxian County. Completion would transfer control of 002036 CH to the Shouxian finance bureau; the stock resumes trading today. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    A-share buybacks – several fresh proposals: Huakai Yibai’s chairman proposed a RMB30m–50m buyback, ArcSoft’s controller proposed RMB100m–150m, and Guangdian Electric approved RMB100m–200m. Shanghai Phoenix separately plans to spend RMB117m–195m repurchasing B shares for cancellation, providing fresh capital-return catalysts across the small-cap group. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Tianhe Magnetics – southern precision-magnet platform: Tianhe Magnetics 603072 CH will establish a 90%-owned Guangdong subsidiary focused on magnetic materials and assemblies for humanoid robots, low-altitude applications and consumer electronics. The company said the unit will strengthen precision processing and customised assembly while improving customer coverage in southern China. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Sinosun Technology – potential control change: Sinosun Technology 300333 CH said its controlling shareholder is negotiating a stake transfer to Shanghai Jingheheng Technology. The buyer would hold about 14.18% if completed; the stock is suspended from today for no more than two trading days. [Eastmoney]
