Morning Market Memo | 15 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 14 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 15 Sep 2026 08:59 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **GAC–FAW restructuring:** Guangzhou Automobile Group (2238 HK/601238 CH) signed an intention agreement to acquire part of an unnamed FAW joint-venture automaker through a share issue and raise matching funds. FAW would become GAC’s second-largest and strategically influential shareholder without changing GAC’s controller; the transaction is expected to constitute a major asset restructuring and connected transaction. [NBD] [Shanghai Securities News]

  2. 02

    **PBOC six-month liquidity:** The central bank will conduct RMB500bn of six-month outright reverse repos today through fixed-quantity, interest-rate bidding, with maturity on 15 March 2027. The operation provides medium-term funding support as the banking system enters the September tax-payment period. [PBOC via Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **Industrial economy – fourth-quarter support:** MIIT Vice Minister Xin Guobin convened a meeting with provincial officials after Monday’s close, calling for supply- and demand-side measures focused on key industries and regions to stabilise fourth-quarter industrial activity. The ministry also urged faster AI-industry development and application, supporting domestic software, automation and intelligent-manufacturing names. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    **Brain–computer interfaces:** China’s drug regulator approved the world’s first brain–computer-interface medical-device standard governing the quality and evaluation of EEG datasets processed with AI. The standard takes effect on 1 September 2027 and provides a regulatory framework for product development, manufacturing and quality control. [Xinhua]

  5. 05

    **Xiangshan adds AI-computing equipment:** Xiangshan (002870 CH) plans to acquire 100% of Wuluo Zhihui through shares and cash, adding AI-computing equipment to its existing automotive-components operations. The proposed transaction would create a dual automotive-parts and computing-hardware business. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **TCL Smart Home expands in Thailand:** TCL Smart Home (002668 CH) said phase two of its Thai production base, designed for 1.4m refrigerators annually, has entered trial production. Phase one’s 300,000-unit freezer line is already close to full utilisation, adding overseas capacity for the home-appliance supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    **Qipai Technology guides to profit:** Semiconductor packager Qipai Technology (688216 CH) expects to return to profit in the third quarter as AI-related demand supports industry utilisation and packaging orders. The company said quarterly revenue rose 61.26% year on year, providing a fresh read-through for domestic testing and packaging peers. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **Lianke Technology’s Egypt project:** Lianke Technology (001207 CH) plans an integrated silica and carbon-black production project in Egypt’s Suez Canal Economic Zone, with intended annual capacity of 100,000 tonnes for each product. The overseas investment targets tyre and industrial-material customers and expands the company’s non-China manufacturing footprint. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Shenzhen Airport traffic:** Shenzhen Airport (000089 CH) reported August passenger throughput of 6.10m, up 5.1% year on year, while cargo and mail volume fell 5.31% to 162,700 tonnes. The figures offer mixed read-through for mainland aviation and air-logistics operators. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **A-share buybacks:** Bathroom-products maker Aifenda (301575 CH) announced a RMB50m–100m repurchase, while logistics-automation supplier Damon Technology (688360 CH) proposed buying back RMB30m–60m of shares. Both programmes are intended for employee incentives or share-ownership plans. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow fell 0.29%, the S&P 500 lost 0.48% and the Nasdaq declined 0.56%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index sank 5.86%, with Nvidia down 3.36%, Micron 5.25%, TSMC 3.52%, Coherent 12.73% and Lumentum 9.92%. The Golden Dragon Index gained 0.36% as Li Auto rose 3.05%, PDD 2.02% and XPeng 1.61%; WTI settled 1.84% higher at US$101.89, while spot gold fell 1.14% to US$4,299.15 and the US 10-year yield briefly crossed 5%. [Eastmoney] [AP] [Yahoo Finance]

  12. 12

    **Iran and Hormuz:** President Trump said he remained open to negotiations with Iran and argued that countries should compensate the US for protecting oil shipments through the Strait of Hormuz. Oil remains relevant for PetroChina, CNOOC and Sinopec, while elevated fuel costs pressure airlines and downstream chemical producers. [Xinhua]

  13. 13

    **CITIC Securities on metals:** CITIC said first-half earnings growth accelerated across metals, led by tungsten, aluminium, lead-zinc and other rare metals, while lithium returned to profit. It highlighted gold, copper, strategic metals, rare earths, aluminium and lithium, noting that selected subsectors still offer dividend yields above 5%. [First Financial]

  14. 14

    **CITIC Construction on SOE consolidation:** The broker said policy support is increasingly linking mergers with industry governance and upgrading, particularly in autos, while central SOE reform continues to reduce entity counts and concentrate capital in core businesses. The call supports attention on national and local champions with identifiable consolidation assets. [STCN]
