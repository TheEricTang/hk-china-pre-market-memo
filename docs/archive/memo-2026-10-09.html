Morning Market Memo | 09 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 08 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 09 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **PBOC – exchange-rate stance:** The central bank said market supply and demand will remain decisive in setting the renminbi, with no preset exchange-rate target and no intention to seek trade advantage through competitive depreciation. It retains macroprudential tools and direct intervention for extreme, disorderly moves, making the statement relevant to exporters, banks and other currency-sensitive names. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **Property – holiday transactions improved:** New-home sales across 25 monitored cities reached 551,000 square metres during the October 1–7 holiday, up 33.8% from last year’s daily average, while second-hand transactions in 11 cities rose 42.8%. The data provide a better opening read-through for developers and property managers, although new-home volume was only broadly level with 2024. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **HK IPO – ESWIN Computing debuts:** ESWIN Computing is scheduled to begin Hong Kong trading today under code 1256 HK, with a board lot of 2,000 shares. The listing adds a new semiconductor-computing name to the local technology tape. [HKEX]

  4. 04

    **Tencent – HK$100m buyback:** Tencent (700 HK) repurchased 241,000 shares on Thursday for HK$100.38m at HK$411.40–424.40 per share; the stock will be cancelled. The filing continues the group’s regular capital-return programme after the shares weakened during the session. [Tencent]

  5. 05

    **Huada Empyrean – expands full-flow EDA capability:** Huada Empyrean (301269 CH) will invest RMB149m to acquire 9.27m shares in layout-and-routing software developer 芯行纪, giving it an expected 6.9% stake. Management said the transaction fills a core gap in its digital integrated-circuit EDA toolchain. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **Biwin Storage – packaging unit raises RMB600m:** Biwin Storage’s (688525 CH) controlled semiconductor packaging and testing subsidiary secured RMB600m from investors including Biren Technology-related capital. Investors receive 23.08% of the unit, while Biwin’s stake falls from 68.27% to 52.52% and the subsidiary remains consolidated. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    **Zhucheng Technology – connectors and liquid cooling:** Zhucheng Technology (301280 CH) plans to invest up to RMB409m in a southwest China communications-connector headquarters and manufacturing base. Proposed products include high-speed backplane and I/O connectors, liquid-cooling modules and other data-centre and communications components. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **Amlogic – strong third-quarter profit guidance:** Amlogic (688099 CH) expects nine-month revenue of RMB7.08bn–7.18bn, up 39.6%–41.6%, and net profit of RMB1.26bn–1.31bn, up 80.6%–87.7%. Third-quarter profit is guided at RMB649m–699m, representing 223%–248% year-on-year growth and supporting the domestic multimedia-chip complex. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Profit alerts – industrial technology and energy:** Xingyun Technology expects nine-month net profit of RMB240m–290m, reversing a year-earlier loss, while Guanghui Energy guides for RMB2.70bn–2.80bn, up 166.8%–176.7%. Guanghui’s third-quarter profit is estimated at RMB1.42bn–1.52bn, providing a direct read-through from stronger energy pricing. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **Optical components – Dongshan rejects “65%” rule report:** Dongshan Precision (002384 CH) said the alleged requirement for 65% US-origin materials in optical modules is an unofficial market rumour rather than an enacted FCC policy and has not affected operations. Self-produced optical chips account for no more than 10% of module bill-of-materials costs, and the company has received no related customer enquiries. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Samsung smartphones – fourth-quarter production cut:** Samsung’s mobile division reportedly told suppliers it plans to reduce fourth-quarter smartphone production by 20%–30% as higher memory prices compress handset margins. The cut is negative for smartphone-component volumes but reinforces the tight memory-pricing backdrop. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Shipping – Maersk raises fuel surcharge:** Maersk will increase its emergency fuel surcharge to 20% from October 12 for export-pickup and import-delivery services, citing higher fuel costs caused by the continuing Middle East conflict. The measure is relevant to container-shipping operators and exporters facing higher logistics costs. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Agriculture – El Niño risk strengthens:** The World Meteorological Organization said El Niño is likely to intensify into December and has a near-100% probability of lasting through February 2027, potentially becoming one of the strongest episodes since 1950. Agriculture, food processing, power demand and insurers are the principal China/HK read-throughs. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **AI spending – OpenAI revenue report hits hardware names:** Investor materials reportedly put OpenAI’s annualised revenue near US$50bn at end-September, roughly US$20bn below the US$70bn figure previously circulated. The disclosure triggered selling across Nvidia, Micron, storage and optical names overnight, adding pressure to HK/A-share AI-hardware suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Iran – US attack ruled out before midterms:** President Trump said the US would not attack Iran before the November 3 midterm elections and described talks as productive; Iran said it was still evaluating a proposal tied to reopening the Strait of Hormuz within seven days. The comments briefly eased oil and Treasury-yield pressure, but crude still closed sharply higher. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow rose 0.10%, while the S&P 500 fell 0.47% and Nasdaq lost 1.25%; the SOX dropped 3.39%, with Nvidia down 2.94%, Micron 4.79%, Seagate 4.05%, SanDisk 4.90% and Applied Optoelectronics 13.58%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon index fell 0.62%, including Alibaba -1.21%, Baidu -1.62% and NIO -3.67%. WTI gained 3.29% to US$91.18, COMEX gold rose 0.43% to US$4,158.30, the dollar index slipped 0.1% to 102.138 and the US 10-year yield closed at 5.2289%. [Eastmoney]
