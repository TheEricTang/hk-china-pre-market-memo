Morning Market Memo | 26 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 23 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 26 Oct 2026 06:40 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    State Council – family support and services: Premier Li Qiang chaired a meeting calling for stronger fiscal, social-security and housing support for families to help stabilise births. The cabinet also sought more service-sector investment and consumption, with better financing, land, talent and data support and fewer market-access barriers. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    AI and employment: The State Council issued a national framework for using AI to create jobs and upgrade traditional roles while helping workers affected by automation. Measures include AI application pilot bases, smarter recruitment and training products, employment-risk monitoring and targeted reskilling support, relevant to cloud, HR technology and vocational-education providers. [State Council]

  3. 03

    National information plan: China’s “15th Five-Year” information plan targets a fully built next-generation communications network by 2030, a basic land-sea-air-space network, an appropriate start to 6G commercialisation and a national blockchain network. The plan sets 12 indicators covering 5G-A, IPv6 traffic, data-centre efficiency, AI terminals and the digital economy, supporting telecom equipment, satellites, computing infrastructure and software. [CAC]

  4. 04

    PBOC liquidity: The central bank will conduct RMB700bn of one-year MLF operations today against RMB900bn maturing this month, implying a RMB200bn net withdrawal through this facility. Two tenors of outright reverse repos have already provided a combined RMB400bn net injection during October, leaving overall medium-term liquidity in net expansion. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Private-fund regulation: The CSRC proposed a 23-article framework for private fund-of-funds, covering classification, diversification, custody, conflicts, look-through monitoring and a negative investment list. Long-term institutional investors would receive differentiated allocation treatment, while qualifying FOF structures would be exempt from counting as an additional investment layer. [CSRC]

  6. 06

    US investment restrictions: MOFCOM opposed Washington’s proposed expansion of outbound-investment restrictions involving China, saying the rules interfere with normal corporate cooperation and that Beijing reserves the right to respond. The proposal adds uncertainty for cross-border funding and partnerships in strategic technology and life-science sectors. [MOFCOM]

  7. 07

    Shanghai index rules: Revised methodologies for the Shanghai Composite, SSE A-Share, SSE Free-Float and STAR Composite indices take effect today. The changes impose a 15% cap on any constituent’s weight and bring forward the reference date used to remove risk-warning securities, relevant to index funds and the largest Shanghai-listed names. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Memory – Demingli earnings: Demingli reported nine-month revenue of RMB28.56bn, up 329%, and net profit of RMB8.75bn, versus only RMB27m a year earlier. Third-quarter profit reached RMB2.73bn as memory pricing, enterprise-storage sales and the company’s in-house controllers lifted its product mix. [Eastmoney] [10jqka]

  9. 09

    Lithium earnings: Tianqi Lithium posted third-quarter revenue of RMB6.04bn, up 135%, and net profit of RMB1.36bn, up 1,324%, helped by higher lithium-product prices and stronger income from SQM. Huasheng Lithium separately reported quarterly profit of RMB313m, up 801%, citing higher volumes and selling prices. [Eastmoney] [CFi]

  10. 10

    Biopharma catalyst: Baili Tianheng said its T-Bren HER2 ADC met the progression-free-survival endpoint at an interim Phase III analysis in previously treated HER2-positive breast cancer. China’s drug regulator also accepted the marketing application for its EGFR×HER3 ADC in EGFR-mutated non-small-cell lung cancer. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Post-close technology announcements: Keruisi plans to acquire 100% of Honggang Optoelectronics, which makes packaging housings and heat sinks for lasers, optical communications and power semiconductors; its shares resume today. Jinyi Technology’s subsidiary also signed two computing-server component procurement contracts worth RMB35.3m and RMB183m. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Mining consolidation: Gaozheng Explosives plans to acquire 51% stakes in two Tibet mining companies and all of a third company through a share-based transaction with its controlling shareholder, with supporting funds also contemplated. The stock is suspended from today while the major asset-restructuring plan is prepared. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Capital returns: Beilu Intelligent Control proposed a RMB25m–30m share repurchase for employee incentives, capped at RMB38 per share. A subsidiary of Xingfa Group’s controlling shareholder plans to increase its holding by RMB100m–120m over three months without setting a price range. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Weekly supply calendar: Forty-four A-share companies face the release of 4.14bn restricted shares this week, worth more than RMB106bn at Friday’s close; Xi’an Yisi-U accounts for 2.84bn shares. The week has one scheduled IPO subscription, direct-drive machine-tool producer Keruisi, on 29 October. [Southern Finance]

  15. 15

    Southbound flows: Mainland investors sold a net HK$17.05bn of Hong Kong shares over last week’s four sessions. Tencent drew HK$1.23bn of net buying and Zhipu HK$1.20bn, while Alibaba recorded HK$4.30bn of net selling and Xiaomi HK$1.23bn. [Eastmoney] [CLS]

  16. 16

    Iran and oil risk: President Trump said at the weekend that the US could strike Iran again at any time but could also reach an agreement, adding that any settlement would need to prevent a permanent return to nuclear-weapons development. The comments keep Middle East supply risk in focus for PetroChina/CNOOC versus airlines, refiners and chemical producers. [CCTV]

  17. 17

    Overnight US markets: Friday’s Dow fell 1.23%, the S&P 500 0.52% and Nasdaq 0.21%; the SOX recovered 0.44%, with Micron +2.46% but Nvidia -0.59%, Western Digital -2.97%, Seagate -1.81% and SanDisk -1.82%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon index lost 0.84%; WTI rose 3.8% to US$103.27, Brent added 3.1% to above US$116, spot gold fell below US$4,000, and US two- and ten-year yields ended at 4.69% and 5.00%. [First Financial] [NBD]
