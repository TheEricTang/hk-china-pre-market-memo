Morning Market Memo | 06 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 05 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 06 Nov 2026 06:56 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Overnight US markets – AI hardware rout: The Dow fell 3.44% to 51,523.06, the S&P 500 lost 4.36% to 7,353.73 and the Nasdaq dropped 5.52% to 24,315.00. The SOX fell more than 12%; SanDisk lost 22.26%, Micron 17.46%, Western Digital 17.35%, Seagate 16.25%, Applied Optoelectronics 20.89%, Lumentum 18.68% and Tesla 10.77%, giving a negative read-through for HK/A semiconductors, storage, optics and EVs. China ADRs diverged: the Golden Dragon index rose 0.43%, with NetEase +4.37%, JD +3.28%, Alibaba +2.64% and Bilibili +2.67%. WTI rose 2.80% to $90.59, spot gold gained 0.72% to $4,089.53 and spot silver fell 0.82% to $51.81. [Eastmoney] [AP]

  2. 02

    He Lifeng – financing support for AI and new infrastructure: The vice premier called for stronger financial and factor support for artificial intelligence and computing-power networks, alongside orderly expansion of new-infrastructure investment. He also highlighted advanced manufacturing and biopharma during his Beijing inspection, relevant to the domestic AI-infrastructure and innovative-drug chains. [Xinhua]

  3. 03

    US n-propanol duties extended: MOFCOM said China will continue anti-dumping and countervailing duties on US-origin n-propanol from today for another five years after concluding that renewed dumping and injury were likely without the measures. The decision maintains protection for domestic chemical producers. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    2027 holiday calendar – nine-day Spring Festival: The State Council set the Spring Festival break for 5–13 February, with five days for Labour Day and seven days for National Day. The longer lead time and nine-day Lunar New Year window are relevant to airlines, hotels, online travel agencies and duty-free operators. [State Council]

  5. 05

    AI short dramas – regulator tightens content standards: The National Radio and Television Administration reiterated that it will support live-action short dramas while regulating AI-generated productions. Platforms must maintain full human review, while AI rewriting, unauthorised remakes and misuse of likenesses or voices will face increased enforcement, raising the compliance bar for video and short-drama platforms. [Xinhua]

  6. 06

    xFusion IPO – hearing scheduled: Shenzhen’s listing committee will review AI-server supplier xFusion’s ChiNext IPO on 12 November; the company plans to raise RMB80bn. First-half revenue was RMB37.29bn and net profit RMB2.11bn, with AI-server revenue accounting for 53.3% of its core business, putting the domestic server and computing-power supply chain back in focus. [Eastmoney] [Sina]

  7. 07

    Hua Hong Macro – profit and guidance improved: Third-quarter revenue reached RMB6.71bn, up 14.0%, while net profit rose 136.1% to RMB835m as wafer volumes and average selling prices increased. The foundry guided fourth-quarter sales to $1.08bn–$1.09bn with a 25%–26% gross margin, providing a positive domestic-foundry earnings read-through despite the overnight US semiconductor sell-off. [Sina] [Tiger]

  8. 08

    BeiGene – guidance raised again: BeiGene reported third-quarter revenue of about $1.89bn, up 34%, and net profit of $385m, helped by operating leverage and continued growth in Brukinsa. The company raised full-year revenue guidance to $6.9bn–$7.0bn, a fresh catalyst for 6160 HK and the innovative-biotech sector. [Sina] [Sina]

  9. 09

    SAIC and Momenta – intelligent-driving joint venture: Momenta, SAIC Motor and Yandian Technology will establish a RMB300m joint venture, with ownership of 51%, 25% and 24%, respectively. The venture will provide L2 and L3 advanced-driving products across SAIC’s vehicle platforms and global markets, relevant to 6880 HK and the autonomous-driving supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Battery materials – two long-dated supply agreements: Senior Material’s European subsidiary agreed to supply Volkswagen’s battery unit with an estimated 1.558bn square metres of coated wet-process separators during 2027–2034. Separately, Tinci Materials agreed to supply EVE Energy with an estimated 450,000 tonnes of electrolyte during 2027–2029, subject to a 20% volume adjustment; both are framework volumes with final pricing and orders still to be set. [Sina] [Sina]

  11. 11

    Qiaoyuan Gas – RMB3.16bn semiconductor-gas project: Qiaoyuan plans a three-phase high-purity fluorinated electronic-specialty-gas project in Zigong, including about RMB3.11bn of fixed-asset investment. The first phase requires RMB669m versus RMB383m of cash reported at end-September, making financing and execution key considerations for the semiconductor-materials expansion. [NBD]

  12. 12

    Golden Solar – five-year computing contract: The company’s controlled subsidiary signed a computing-cloud services contract with an unnamed AI customer, with estimated fees capped at RMB1.536bn and service periods of 60 months for each batch. The first two batches total RMB384m, but the company warned that later pricing, hardware funding and full contract performance remain uncertain. [Sina] [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Post-close buybacks – semiconductor equipment and optics: AMEC proposed a RMB400m–RMB500m repurchase for employee incentives, while Crystal-Optech proposed a RMB100m–RMB200m programme. The announcements add to the fresh batch of company-level support measures following this week’s reduction in the PBOC’s stock-repurchase relending rate. [NBD] [NBD]

  14. 14

    Muyuan Foods – October hog revenue declined: Muyuan sold 5.913m hogs in October, down 16.4% year on year, while the average selling price fell 16.5% to RMB9.64/kg. Sales revenue dropped 29.8% to RMB7.25bn, a weak pricing read-through for 2714 HK and the broader hog-production sector. [Eastmoney] [Sina]

  15. 15

    Shanghai port – throughput remained firm: SIPG estimated October container throughput at 4.981m TEU, up 9.8% year on year, while cargo throughput increased 5.6% to 52.01m tonnes. The figures provide a positive operating read-through for ports, shipping and export logistics. [NBD]

  16. 16

    Avary Holding – October revenue accelerated: The PCB supplier reported October consolidated revenue of RMB5.04bn, up 19.3% year on year. The print provides a company-specific offset to the overnight sell-off in US AI hardware and optical-networking stocks. [NBD]

  17. 17

    China Merchants Shekou – latest property sales tape: The state-backed developer recorded October contracted sales of RMB18.68bn across 525,400 square metres. Contracted sales for January–October reached RMB158.77bn, giving the market a fresh large-developer demand gauge for the property complex. [Sina]
