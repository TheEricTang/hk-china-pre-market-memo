Morning Market Memo | 10 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 07 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 10 Aug 2026 07:06 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    China inflation – services offset food weakness: July CPI rose 0.5% year on year and fell 0.1% month on month. Services gained 0.7% year on year, while food declined 1.5% and pork fell 13.3%, leaving a mixed read-through for consumer, restaurant and livestock names. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    PBOC gold and reserves: China’s official gold holdings increased for a 21st consecutive month, rising from 75.44m to 76.08m ounces in July. Foreign-exchange reserves edged up US$2.5bn to US$3.4188tn, relevant to gold producers and the renminbi opening tone. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Beijing property easing: From Saturday, non-Beijing households need only one year of social-security or tax payments to buy within the Fifth Ring, down from two years. The maximum housing-provident-fund loan for couples was doubled to RMB2.4m for first homes and RMB2m for second homes, with combined policy additions lifting the ceiling to RMB3.4m in qualifying cases. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    LOF delisting rules: The Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges sought feedback on mandatory delisting arrangements for commodity-futures, QDII and persistently small listed open-ended funds. A small LOF would trigger delisting after 60 consecutive trading days with on-exchange net assets below RMB10m; commodity and QDII LOFs would receive a transition period ending no later than 31 December 2027. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    New technology ETFs: Sixteen fund managers filed applications on Friday evening for ChiNext computing-infrastructure and financial-technology ETFs. The first product filings tied to the two sector indices may broaden listed funding channels for A-share computing-power and fintech constituents. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Unitree IPO subscription today: Unitree Technology opens its STAR Market online and institutional book at RMB150.80 a share, offering 40.45m new shares and targeting RMB6.10bn of gross proceeds. DeepSeek’s parent, the national social-security fund and several large internet groups feature among pre-IPO or strategic investors, keeping robotics suppliers in focus. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    WuXi AppTec court relief: A US federal court granted WuXi AppTec’s requested preliminary injunction against enforcement of its Pentagon 1260H designation while the company’s legal challenge proceeds. The ruling shields WuXi AppTec 2359 HK/603259 CH from the designation’s immediate effects during litigation. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Guotai Junan International privatisation: Guotai Haitong’s offshore unit proposed acquiring the outstanding shares of Guotai Junan International 1788 HK for HK$3 each through a scheme of arrangement, followed by delisting. The transaction would consolidate the parent’s overseas brokerage and financial-services operations. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Moore Threads seeks Hong Kong listing: The domestic GPU developer said it plans to issue H shares and list on the HKEX main board. First-half revenue rose 147.4% to RMB1.736bn, while its net loss narrowed to RMB11.6m from RMB271m as AI-cluster sales and deliveries accelerated. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Zhongji Innolight H-share disclosure: JPMorgan’s disclosed long position in Zhongji Innolight 3308 HK increased from 13.48% to 15.02% following an August 4 purchase reported after Friday’s close. The fresh filing is relevant to the recently listed H shares and the wider optical-module complex. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Sunday results – chips and renewables: electronic-component distributor Wuhan P&S Information reported first-half net profit of RMB298m, up 209.5%, citing stronger AI, industrial, NEV and automotive-electronics demand. Xinjiang Lixin Energy’s profit rose 715.8% to RMB73.0m as newly commissioned wind and independent-storage projects increased power generation. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Fresh A-share buybacks: Ningxia Building Materials proposed a RMB100m–200m repurchase at no more than RMB19.47 per share to protect shareholder value. InHand Networks proposed buying back RMB20m–30m of stock at up to RMB56 for cancellation and a corresponding capital reduction. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Healthcare approvals: Huadong Medicine said its UK subsidiary obtained EU MDR certification for KIO015, described as a non-animal chitosan injectable aesthetic product. Jichuan Pharmaceutical separately received Chinese registrations for paediatric constipation granules and mesalazine sustained-release granules. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    US critical-minerals funding: President Trump announced US$3bn of investments and financing for domestic critical-mineral and battery projects, including a US$1.4bn loan agreement for silicon-anode producer Sila Nanotechnologies. The programme targets lithium, rare-earth, scandium, copper and graphite supply, relevant to Chinese miners, refiners and battery-material exporters. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    US markets – weak payrolls lifted technology: July nonfarm payrolls unexpectedly fell 23,000 versus expectations for an 80,000 increase, while May–June were revised down by a combined 103,000; unemployment eased to 4.1% as participation fell to 61.4%. The Dow gained 0.28%, the S&P 500 rose 0.62% to a record close and the Nasdaq added 1.30%; Nvidia rose 2.3%, Broadcom 1.7% and the 10-year Treasury yield fell to 4.64%. Among liquid China ADRs, Alibaba gained 1.25%, NIO 2.83% and PDD 1.0%; a verified Golden Dragon close was not available from the opened wraps by cutoff. [AP] [Eastmoney]
