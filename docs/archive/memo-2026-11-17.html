Morning Market Memo | 17 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 16 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 17 Nov 2026 09:03 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Greater Bay Area – Xi in Zhongshan: President Xi Jinping called for deeper integration between the eastern and western sides of the Pearl River and a new phase of high-quality Greater Bay Area development after inspecting the Shenzhen–Zhongshan Link. The remarks support the regional infrastructure, transport and technology-development agenda. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    Automakers – supplier-payment scrutiny: The auto association, acting for MIIT, will annually assess 17 major automakers including BYD, Geely, NIO, XPeng and Xiaomi, covering 60-day payment compliance, payment methods and accounts payable. At least 15 suppliers per automaker will be sampled; results will be published and problem companies may be summoned for rectification. [Shanghai Securities News]

  3. 03

    Huatai – consumption indicators improving: Huatai Securities said October industry data showed initial improvement in baijiu, home appliances, medical devices, textiles, agriculture and home furnishings. It also highlighted improving indicators in wind power, automation, semiconductor equipment, communications equipment and PCB components. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Southbound flows: Mainland investors were net sellers of HK$42.27bn on Monday, buying HK$460mn of MiniMax 100 HK and HK$281mn of Tencent 700 HK while selling Alibaba 9988 HK, SMIC 981 HK and Hua Hong 1347 HK by HK$646mn, HK$454mn and HK$429mn respectively. [CLS]

  5. 05

    Victory Giant Technology 300476 CH/2476 HK – buyback and overseas capacity: The PCB maker approved an RMB1.5bn–3bn A-share buyback and up to US$170mn of additional investment in its Malaysian subsidiary for factories and equipment. It separately sought RMB70.7bn of bank credit lines, which are limits rather than committed borrowings. [Shanghai Securities News]

  6. 06

    Juncheng Technology 301106 CH – GPU purchase: The company agreed to purchase 9,000 GPU cards from an undisclosed supplier for a provisional RMB765mn, equal to at least 50% of its latest audited net assets. Shareholder approval is required, while supply, export-control and delivery risks remain. [Beijing News]

  7. 07

    Novosense 688052 CH – automotive radar acquisition: Novosense will pay RMB486.9mn for 78.152% of unprofitable microwave and millimetre-wave radar-chip developer AirTouch, partly funded by up to RMB350mn of acquisition loans. The deal expands its automotive electronics and intelligent-sensing portfolio but may create goodwill and near-term earnings dilution. [Yicai]

  8. 08

    Jiejie Microelectronics 300623 CH – power-semiconductor expansion: The company plans a RMB1.5bn vehicle-grade power-device production line covering 1,200V, 2,300V and 3,300V MOSFET chips and modules. Target applications include EV control systems, solar inverters, charging piles, energy storage and data-centre power supplies. [Securities Times]

  9. 09

    Kuang-Chi Technologies 002625 CH – major aerospace order: Its subsidiary signed an RMB1.7904bn metamaterial aerospace-structure contract, equal to 87.52% of 2025 audited core revenue. Delivery runs through June 2028, with the company expecting a positive contribution in 2027 and the first half of 2028. [CNInfo]

  10. 10

    H World 1179 HK – guidance raised: Third-quarter revenue increased 11.3% to RMB7.75bn and attributable profit rose 22.6% to RMB1.80bn, helped by hotel-network expansion and 25.7% growth in managed and franchised revenue. Full-year revenue-growth guidance was raised to 9%–10%. [The Paper]

  11. 11

    KE Holdings 2423 HK – housing transactions improve: Third-quarter GTV rose 4.5% to RMB769.7bn, with existing-home and new-home GTV up 3.0% and 5.3%. Revenue fell 8.9% to RMB21.0bn, but net profit nearly tripled to RMB2.20bn as transaction margins improved. [The Paper]

  12. 12

    Kelun Pharmaceutical 002422 CH – four products enter reimbursement list: Three drugs were newly included in the 2026 national reimbursement list and another gained two reimbursed indications. The products generated RMB543mn of sales in the first half, with the new list effective from January 1, 2027. [Sina]

  13. 13

    BeiGene 6160 HK/688235 CH – US biotech investment: BeiGene will invest US$19.5mn in Bravera Bio’s Series A and convert US$500,000 of SAFE exposure, leaving it with a 9.477% stake. The transaction is a related-party co-investment but does not require shareholder approval. [Yicai]

  14. 14

    Tesla – European Cybercab capacity: Tesla confirmed a €3bn expansion of its Berlin plant, targeting production of up to one million Cybercabs annually, with the new line expected to start in 2029. The plan is relevant to Chinese EV, autonomous-driving, battery and component suppliers competing in Europe. [Reuters]

  15. 15

    Federal Reserve – three AI scenarios: Chair Kevin Warsh said staff are modelling outcomes ranging from broad productivity gains, through growth accompanied by major job displacement, to disappointing returns that trigger lower equity valuations, slower growth and higher unemployment. He stressed these are scenarios rather than forecasts. [Federal Reserve]

  16. 16

    Overnight markets: The Dow fell 1.41%, S&P 500 1.92% and Nasdaq 2.49%; the SOX lost 5.28%, with Nvidia down 4.12%, TSMC 6.34%, Micron 7.62% and SanDisk 8.58%. The Golden Dragon index fell 1.0% as Alibaba lost 3.98%, Baidu 5.37% and NIO 6.21%, while XPeng gained 3.20%; HSI and Hang Seng Tech futures closed down 0.34% and 0.89%. WTI fell 3.47% to US$85.49, COMEX gold 0.84% to US$3,853.60, silver 4.57% to US$46.875, while DXY ended at 103.549 and US two- and ten-year yields at 4.8166% and 5.1129%. [Eastmoney]
