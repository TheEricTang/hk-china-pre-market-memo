Morning Market Memo | 12 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 11 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 12 Nov 2026 07:52 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Overnight US markets – AI hardware rebounded sharply: The Dow rose 0.49%, S&P 500 1.31% and Nasdaq 1.60%, while the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index jumped 5.66%. SanDisk gained 13.69%, Micron 11.89%, Western Digital 8.34% and Nvidia 2.87%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index nevertheless fell 0.80%, with Alibaba down 1.17%, JD.com 1.62% and XPeng 2.96%. WTI added 0.42% to US$86.40, spot gold fell 1.17% to US$3,985.90 and the US Treasury market was closed for Veterans Day. [Eastmoney] [AP]

  2. 02

    Nvidia – after-hours reversal: Nvidia erased its regular-session gain and fell about 8% after hours after reports that its board was preparing to consider removing founder and CEO Jensen Huang following fresh misconduct allegations. No board decision had been announced by the cutoff; the development adds immediate volatility for Asian AI servers, optics, PCB, memory and semiconductor-equipment names. [Eastmoney] [Axios]

  3. 03

    State financial-sector recapitalisation advanced: ICBC, Agricultural Bank of China and PICC said their A-share private-placement applications passed Shanghai Stock Exchange review, with planned fundraising caps of RMB100bn, RMB160bn and RMB15bn respectively. CSRC registration is still required; the approvals move the Ministry of Finance-backed capital injections closer to completion and support the capital position of the three groups. [Shanghai Securities News]

  4. 04

    China will issue up to US$2bn of sovereign dollar bonds in Hong Kong: The Ministry of Finance said the offering will take place during the week beginning 16 November, with detailed terms to follow. The transaction adds a fresh sovereign pricing reference for offshore Chinese dollar bonds and reinforces Hong Kong’s debt-capital-market role. [Ministry of Finance] [China News]

  5. 05

    Offshore trusts – transition period closes with RMB160bn-plus tax declarations: The State Taxation Administration said declared individual income tax linked to offshore trusts exceeded RMB160bn, including more than RMB120bn booked in October, lifting nationwide October individual-income-tax receipts by nearly 90%. Authorities will move to regular enforcement, including surcharges and investigations for incomplete filings; the disclosure is relevant to private banks, trustees and Hong Kong wealth-management platforms. [Beijing News] [Caixin]

  6. 06

    China–US communication: Foreign Minister Wang Yi and US Secretary of State Marco Rubio held a call described as positive and constructive, discussing bilateral relations and interaction between the two presidents. Wang said both sides should manage differences and support each other in hosting the 2026 APEC and G20 leaders’ meetings, a stabilising signal for China–US-sensitive technology, export and internet stocks. [CCTV]

  7. 07

    China State Shipbuilding secured a RMB17.2bn VLCC order: China CSSC Holdings’ 600150 CH subsidiary Dalian Shipbuilding signed contracts for twenty 307,000-tonne crude carriers for units of COSCO Shipping Energy and COSCO Shipping Development. The vessels are scheduled for delivery during 2029–2030, providing a large multiyear order for the shipbuilding chain and fleet expansion for the COSCO groups. [The Paper] [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Satellite Chemical – RMB20.7bn expansion: Satellite Chemical 002648 CH approved a two-phase propylene and high-end materials project in Lianyungang’s Xuwei New Area. Planned capacity includes two million tonnes a year of propane dehydrogenation, 600,000 tonnes of acrylic acid and 1.6 million tonnes of multi-carbon alcohols; shareholder approval remains required. [Company filing] [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Buybacks and insider support – metals and battery materials: CMOC obtained a bank commitment for up to RMB1.8bn of loans supporting its existing RMB1bn–RMB2bn A-share repurchase plan. Separately, Zhongwei New Materials’ controlling shareholder plans to buy RMB500m–RMB1bn of shares within six months, backed by a loan commitment of up to RMB900m; both announcements extend the policy-backed buyback and shareholder-purchase theme. [Yicai – CMOC] [Yicai – Zhongwei]

  10. 10

    Zhuhai Duty Free consolidation: Zhuhai Free Trade Group 600185 CH plans to acquire the remaining 49% of Zhuhai Duty Free through shares and cash, alongside a proposed fundraising, which would make the target wholly owned. Trading is suspended from today for up to ten trading days, placing the duty-free and travel-retail complex in focus. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    AI and robotics companies issued post-close risk warnings: Shanghai Film said AI-video revenue was below 0.5% through October; Zhiwei Robotics said embodied-intelligence revenue was under 1% in the first nine months; and Feiyada said robotics revenue was below 5%. Guangyang Bearing and other recent high-flyers also said their robotics businesses were small or unprofitable, creating stock-specific checks on the A-share AI-video and humanoid-robot themes after Wednesday’s rally. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    China launched the Pathfinder satellite: A Long March-2D rocket placed the Tanyuan programme’s Pathfinder satellite into its planned orbit from Jiuquan at 19:41 HKT-equivalent time, marking the Long March series’ 677th flight. The successful technology-verification mission is a fresh catalyst for the domestic satellite, launch-services and space-component chain. [Xinhua]

  13. 13

    China exported its “Mazu” AI weather-warning appliance: China formally launched the integrated system globally at COP31; it combines storage, computing, networking, display and AI-assisted forecasting and supports Ethernet, 5G and satellite communications. The solution was deployed in eight countries by October and tested through cloud services in more than 50, highlighting overseas applications for Chinese AI hardware and public-service technology. [CCTV]

  14. 14

    Xiaomi continued its Hong Kong buyback: Xiaomi 1810 HK repurchased 1.66m shares at HK$29.96–HK$30.12, spending HK$49.88m. The purchase provides another incremental shareholder-return signal after the company’s heavy repurchase activity this year. [Company filing]

  15. 15

    Southbound flow – mainland funds returned to Alibaba and SMIC: Southbound investors recorded net buying of HK$4.57bn despite the Hang Seng Index’s 0.98% decline. Alibaba received HK$1.20bn and SMIC HK$935m of net buying, while Tencent recorded roughly HK$515m of net selling; the flow offers selective support for internet and semiconductor leaders rather than the wider market. [Securities Times]
