Morning Market Memo | 13 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 12 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 13 Nov 2026 07:02 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Nvidia leadership change: Nvidia said co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and director with immediate effect. CFO Colette Kress becomes interim CEO while retaining her finance role, with the board starting an internal and external search; the disruption is the main overnight read-through for China/HK AI hardware, optical-module and memory names. [Nvidia]

  2. 02

    Overnight US markets: The Dow fell 0.36%, S&P 500 0.35% and Nasdaq 0.39%; the SOX lost 0.54%, Nvidia and SanDisk fell more than 4%, while AMD and Broadcom gained more than 5%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index dropped 3.09%, with Alibaba and iQiyi down more than 7%, NIO nearly 6% lower and XPeng, Li Auto and Bilibili off more than 3%. WTI rose 2.94% to US$89.07, COMEX gold fell 3.02% to US$3,879.50, the DXY closed at 103.174 and the US 10-year yield rose 12.1bp to 5.113%. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Social-security funds: Premier Li Qiang called for stronger long-term sustainability, security supervision and value preservation of social-security funds. The State Council study also urged wider enterprise-annuity coverage, greater appeal for personal pensions and support for commercial health and long-term-care insurance, relevant to insurers, banks and asset managers. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Domestic fuel-price cut: The NDRC cut benchmark gasoline and diesel prices by RMB645 and RMB625 per tonne from midnight, equivalent to average reductions of RMB0.51 per litre for 92-octane gasoline and RMB0.53 for diesel. The move lowers near-term costs for airlines, road logistics and consumers while affecting domestic refiners’ product pricing. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Exchange scrutiny of speculative stocks: The Shanghai Stock Exchange issued regulatory work letters to Dandong Commercial Group (600327 CH) and Solareast (603366 CH) over share-price volatility after both recorded five consecutive limit-up sessions. The intervention is a fresh warning for high-turnover thematic and small-cap trading. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    SMIC earnings: SMIC (981 HK/688981 CH) reported third-quarter revenue of RMB21.98bn, up 28.1%, and attributable net profit of RMB2.85bn, up 87.6%; IFRS gross margin reached 29.9%, 4.6 percentage points above the second quarter. Fourth-quarter revenue is guided to rise 4%–6% sequentially, with gross margin at 26%–28%, a key read-through for domestic foundry and semiconductor-equipment names. [HKEX] [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Semiconductor buybacks: Hua Hong Semiconductor (1347 HK/688347 CH) approved an RMB300m–500m A-share repurchase for cancellation, capped at RMB255.55 per share. Fudan Microelectronics (1385 HK/688385 CH) separately received a chairman proposal for RMB50m–100m of A-share repurchases and HK$100m–200m of H-share repurchases. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Tinci Materials capacity expansion: Tinci Materials (002709 CH) approved investment of up to RMB5bn in a 500,000-tonne annual electrolyte and supporting-materials project, with a planned 24-month construction period. Products include 400,000 tonnes of lithium-ion electrolyte and 100,000 tonnes of sodium-ion electrolyte; the company also proposed an RMB50m–100m share repurchase for cancellation. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Junda ends Oman project: Junda (002865 CH) terminated its planned 5GW high-efficiency solar-cell base in Oman, previously budgeted at about US$280m, citing changed operating conditions, trade friction and geopolitical uncertainty. HK$963m of unused H-share proceeds is proposed for cell R&D, production-line upgrades, strategic raw-material purchases and working capital. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Hengyi Petrochemical acquisition: Hengyi Petrochemical (000703 CH) will pay RMB1.201bn for 51% of Shaoxing Hengming, bringing the polyester-fibre producer into its consolidated accounts. Controlling shareholder Hengyi Group also proposed RMB3bn of unsecured, one-year liquidity support priced at the latest one-year LPR. [First Financial] [NBD]

  11. 11

    InnoLight fund investment: InnoLight Technology (300308 CH/3308 HK) will invest RMB50m for a 3.28% interest in a RMB1.526bn venture fund targeting early- and growth-stage AI, hard technology, advanced materials, medical technology and low-carbon businesses. The investment was announced after the A/H market close. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    MSCI China review: MSCI will add Huadian New Energy, Yunnan Germanium and InnoLight’s H shares to the MSCI China Index while removing 38 names, including 22 A shares. The changes take effect after the 30 November close, creating company-specific passive-flow events rather than a broad market catalyst. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Jiangfeng Electronic Materials H-share filing: Jiangfeng Electronic Materials (300666 CH) submitted an application for a Hong Kong main-board listing, with China Merchants Securities International acting as sole sponsor. The filing adds another semiconductor-materials issuer to the HK IPO pipeline, subject to regulatory and exchange approvals. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    China Resources Land sales: China Resources Land (1109 HK) reported October contracted sales of RMB15.7bn, up 3.3%, while contracted area fell 14.5%. January–October sales increased 6.3% to RMB180.3bn despite a 21.2% decline in area, indicating higher average pricing and continued relative resilience among state-backed developers. [Sina]

  15. 15

    Guoxing Aerospace listing: AI-satellite developer Guoxing Aerospace begins trading today under 6935 HK following an offering of 30.121m H shares. The new listing adds a direct commercial-space and orbital-computing name to the Hong Kong market. [HKEX]
