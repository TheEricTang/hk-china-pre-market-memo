Morning Market Memo | 07 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 06 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 07 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **US markets – semiconductors led fresh highs:** The Dow rose 0.49% to 51,521.28, the S&P 500 gained 0.58% to a record 7,818.93 and the Nasdaq added 0.45% to a record 27,599.79. Marvell rose 5.8%, AMD nearly 3% and Broadcom 3.7%, supporting the read-through for HK-listed AI hardware, optics and semiconductor names. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China index gained 0.31%; GDS rose nearly 6%, while NIO, XPeng and Bilibili gained more than 1%. [Sina] [Nasdaq]

  2. 02

    **Cross-assets – yields eased and the dollar fell:** The US 10-year Treasury yield closed at 5.266%, down about 4.5 basis points, while the DXY fell 0.33% to 101.833. Oil futures were down more than 1% during the US session and gold surrendered an early advance; lower yields provide some valuation support for HK growth stocks, though absolute rates remain elevated. [Investing.com] [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **US trade deficit widened:** The August goods and services deficit increased 13.7% month on month to US$105.6bn, the highest since March 2025, as imports rose sharply. The data reinforce the US demand and trade-policy backdrop for Chinese exporters ahead of the mainland market’s Thursday reopening. [Sina]

  4. 04

    **Google signs major nuclear-power deal:** Google and Constellation Energy agreed to add 890MW of nuclear capacity through upgrades to 11 existing units under a 20-year power-purchase agreement; Constellation said the programme represents more than US$4.3bn of investment, with the first additional capacity expected in 2028. The deal is another hard-data point on AI data-centre power demand, relevant to HK/A-listed nuclear equipment, grid and power-management suppliers. [Google]

  5. 05

    **Hong Kong retained top economic-freedom ranking:** The Fraser Institute’s 2026 report again ranked Hong Kong as the world’s freest economy. Hong Kong remained first for freedom to trade internationally and ranked second for regulation, supporting the city’s positioning for financial services, trade and inward investment. [HK Government]

  6. 06

    **Tencent continued its buyback:** Tencent (700 HK) spent HK$100.4m to repurchase 235,000 shares on Tuesday at HK$425.60–429.20 each. The company has bought back about 48.8m shares under the authorization approved in May, extending its ongoing capital-return programme. [Tiger Brokers]

  7. 07

    **J&T Express stepped up repurchases:** J&T Express (1519 HK) spent HK$31.42m to buy back 3.53m shares at HK$8.67–8.995 each after the close. The disclosure follows a HK$39.65m repurchase on Monday and is relevant after Tuesday’s high short-selling activity in the stock. [Stockstar]

  8. 08

    **Kinetic Development’s Sierra Leone rutile project entered production:** Kinetic Development (1277 HK) disclosed that the first production line is operating, with the second and third lines scheduled for completion in mid-October and early November. Phase one is designed for about 220,000 tonnes of heavy mineral sand annually, with roughly 26,000 tonnes targeted in 2026; the project adds titanium-mineral exposure beyond the group’s coal operations. [New Times] [Kinetic]

  9. 09

    **Kangning Hospital reported higher outpatient spending:** Kangning Hospital (2120 HK) said third-quarter outpatient spending per visit rose 9.8% year on year to RMB303, while average inpatient spending per bed-day increased 0.3% to RMB334. For the first nine months, outpatient spending per visit was up 7.6%, offering a modest pricing and service-mix signal for the specialist healthcare operator. [Investing.com]

  10. 10

    **Mainland and Stock Connect remain closed today:** Shanghai and Shenzhen are closed through Wednesday for the National Day holiday and reopen on Thursday, 8 October. Northbound and southbound Stock Connect are also closed today, making Thursday the first post-holiday Connect session; HK turnover will therefore remain without southbound participation at today’s open. [ChinaClear] [HKEX]

  11. 11

    **Holiday transport remained heavy in Guangdong:** Guangdong recorded about 50.03m expressway vehicle trips during 1–5 October, averaging more than 10m per day, as the return-travel peak began. The figures support continued attention on toll-road operators, service areas, EV charging and domestic tourism-related names. [Eastmoney]
