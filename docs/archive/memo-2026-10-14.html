Morning Market Memo | 14 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 13 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 14 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Hong Kong IPO enforcement – The SFC said targeted action over suspected manipulation of IPO subscription demand at ChaPanda and another issuer, whose shares were suspended, should reinforce rather than undermine market confidence. It pledged further proportionate action with HKEX against serious misconduct. [STCN]

  2. 02

    Shanghai green-development plan – Shanghai’s new 15th Five-Year energy-efficiency plan targets 3GW of new-energy storage by 2030 and more than 5GW of additional solar capacity during 2026–30. It also caps the PUE of new computing facilities at 1.2 and supports liquid cooling, subsea data centres, virtual power plants and hydrogen, green-methanol and sustainable-aviation-fuel trading platforms. [STCN]

  3. 03

    Embodied-AI deployment – Hangzhou’s national embodied-intelligence pilot base signed agreements with governments in Yunnan to test robots and related systems in real border-patrol environments. The partners aim to move from initial trials toward broader, scaled deployment, adding a fresh application case for the robotics supply chain. [STCN]

  4. 04

    CATL industry fund – CATL (300750 CH) will commit RMB400m to a roughly RMB1.50bn Shanghai venture fund, giving it a 26.64% interest. The fund will target new and future energy, AI and digital technologies, power systems and other emerging industries. [STCN]

  5. 05

    China Power Construction overseas order – PowerChina (601669 CH) signed a €1.621bn, or approximately RMB12.53bn, contract for part of Belgrade Metro Line 1. The package covers design and construction, with a 60-month project period and two-year warranty, supporting the overseas infrastructure backlog. [STCN] [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Shenhao Technology wins first computing lease – Shenhao Technology (300853 CH) secured a RMB336m central-SOE contract covering 468 PFlops of dense GPU computing capacity and maintenance over 60 months. The award marks the company’s first computing-leasing order and expands its business beyond power-grid inspection robots. [STCN]

  7. 07

    Transsion H-share pricing – Transsion Holdings set its Hong Kong offer price at HK$35.30 per H share, the bottom of the marketed HK$35.30–38.80 range. Trading in the new H shares is expected to begin on 15 October, creating another A+H consumer-technology listing. [TMTPost] [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Zoomlion disposes of RoadRover control – Zoomlion (000157 CH/1157 HK) agreed to sell 29.99% of RoadRover Technology for RMB900m, or RMB25 per share, reducing its holding to 23.83%. The transaction is expected to add about RMB288m to Zoomlion’s profit; RoadRover resumes trading today under a new controlling shareholder. [STCN] [STCN]

  9. 09

    StarPower acquisition and resumption – StarPower Semiconductor (002860 CH) plans to acquire 100% of Fanyu Technology through shares and cash at a preliminary valuation of RMB840m. The target makes precision cutting-tool equipment and PCB drill-pin components; StarPower resumes trading today. [STCN] [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    AI-server and IoT earnings – Inspur Electronic Information (000977 CH) guided for nine-month net profit of RMB3.8bn–4.1bn, up 156%–177%, citing strong AI-server demand. Espressif Systems (688018 CH) forecast third-quarter profit of RMB211m–229m, up 83%–98%, as IoT applications continued expanding beyond smart homes. [STCN] [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Tungsten and lithium earnings – China Tungsten High-Tech (000657 CH) expects nine-month profit of RMB2.9bn–3.1bn, up 219%–241%, helped by higher tungsten prices and stronger PCB micro-drill sales. Qinghai Salt Lake Industry (000792 CH) guided for RMB8.84bn–9.05bn, up 88%–92%, as its 40,000-tonne basic-lithium-salt project ramped and lithium prices recovered. [STCN] [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Battery separator turnaround – Yunnan Energy New Material (002812 CH) expects nine-month net profit of RMB1.54bn–2.05bn, reversing the prior-year loss. The guidance adds another positive read-through from the battery-materials reporting cycle. [STCN]

  13. 13

    Pien Tze Huang leadership change – Pien Tze Huang (600436 CH) said chairman and legal representative Lin Zhihui resigned because of a work reassignment and will hold no further role within the group. The change is material for a consumer-health company whose brand and pricing strategy remain closely watched. [STCN] [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Apple enters smart-home displays – Apple introduced HomeView, a US$299 seven-inch home-control hub using facial recognition to personalise information for different household members; shipments begin 13 November. The launch broadens the addressable market for smart-home displays, cameras and related component suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Overnight US markets – The Dow fell 0.35%, the S&P 500 slipped 0.02% and the Nasdaq lost 0.15%. Nvidia gained 0.97%, but Micron fell 3.62%, SK Hynix 3.19% and Western Digital 1.61%; the Golden Dragon index edged up 0.14%, with Li Auto +3.07%, NIO +2.59% and Bilibili −1.38%. WTI rose 1.12% to US$94.79, spot gold fell 0.25% to US$4,161.20 and the US 10-year yield eased to about 5.19%, leaving mixed read-through for HK technology, EV, energy and precious-metals names. [Eastmoney] [Sina]
