Morning Market Memo | 30 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 29 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 30 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Fifth Plenum – domestic demand and macro support: The CPC Central Committee called for fully using macro policies, further tapping domestic demand and ensuring completion of the year’s economic and social targets. The meeting, chaired by the Politburo with Xi Jinping delivering an address, concluded after the HK close. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    Fuel prices – state intervention limits the increase: From midnight, benchmark gasoline and diesel prices rose RMB345 and RMB335 per tonne, respectively, versus formula-based increases of RMB440 and RMB420. The NDRC said the adjustment was moderated to reduce the domestic impact of higher international crude prices, relevant to refiners, airlines, logistics and consumer costs. [NDRC] [Sina]

  3. 03

    BYD 1211 HK – third-quarter profit returned to growth: Revenue rose 13.6% to RMB221.49bn and net profit increased 28.1% to RMB10.02bn. Nine-month revenue was flat and net profit fell 4.2%, leaving the stronger quarterly result as the main read-through for the shares. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Vanke 2202 HK – losses narrowed but remain substantial: Nine-month revenue was RMB105bn and attributable net loss was RMB23.94bn, versus a RMB28bn loss a year earlier. Shenzhen Metro has provided about RMB4.52bn of shareholder loans this year, while ten public bonds involving RMB18.07bn of principal have undergone partial repayment or maturity extensions. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    China Unicom 762 HK – earnings fell despite computing growth: Nine-month attributable profit declined 33.8% to RMB5.80bn and third-quarter profit fell 31.3% to RMB1.66bn. Computing-business revenue nevertheless increased 12.2%, including 10.4% growth from intelligent-computing centres. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Optical modules – strong AI demand carried earnings: Zhongji Innolight 300308 CH reported third-quarter revenue up 171.8% and net profit up 173.7%, citing continued growth in 800G and 1.6T sales. Eoptolink 300502 CH posted nine-month net profit of RMB11.37bn, up 79.7%, keeping the AI optical chain in focus after the prior session’s sector sell-off. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Eoptolink – fourth-quarter capacity set to rise sharply: The company said deliveries are concentrated in the second half, especially the fourth quarter, and it has prepared materials and capacity for substantially higher orders. Chengdu and Thailand are both expanding rapidly, with management also optimistic on 2027 demand. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Zhongji Innolight – repurchased shares to be cancelled: The board plans to change the use of 5.65m repurchased A shares from employee incentives to cancellation. Completion would reduce total shares from 1.178bn to 1.172bn, adding a capital-return angle alongside the earnings release. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    AI interconnect – fresh capacity and order signals: Deke Digital Technology 688205 CH plans to invest RMB1.2bn in high-end optical-connectivity capacity for AI clusters and data centres. Montage Technology 688008 CH separately said fourth-quarter interconnect-chip orders scheduled for delivery total RMB2.5bn–2.79bn, 22%–36% above third-quarter revenue for the category. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Longsys 301308 CH – storage profit surged but sequential momentum slowed: Third-quarter revenue increased 123.7% to RMB14.63bn and net profit rose 436.6% to RMB3.75bn, though profit was 44% below the second quarter. The company said DRAM and NAND supply remained tight but price increases and their incremental earnings contribution had moderated. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Hua Hong 1347 HK/688347 CH – chairman proposed a buyback: Chairman and president Bai Peng proposed an RMB300m–500m A-share repurchase for cancellation, value support or future employee incentives. The proposal provides a direct read-through to the H shares after recent semiconductor volatility. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Midea 300 HK – profit grew despite lower quarterly sales: Third-quarter revenue fell 4.1% to RMB107.32bn, while net profit increased 25.9% to RMB14.95bn. Nine-month net profit rose 9.3% to RMB41.39bn, with building technology and robotics and automation revenue each growing 10%. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    COSCO Shipping 1919 HK – earnings rose, but UBS turned bearish: Third-quarter net profit increased 80.7% to RMB17.23bn as container-shipping profitability improved. UBS subsequently downgraded the A shares to Sell with an RMB14.30 target, creating a mixed read-through for the H shares. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Sinopec 386 HK – refining margins supported profit: Third-quarter net profit rose 57.2% to RMB13.36bn, while nine-month profit increased 30% to RMB38.99bn. The company cited improved refining and chemical product spreads, greater output of higher-value products and inventory gains from rising crude prices. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Overnight US markets – technology weakness led declines: The Dow fell 0.25%, S&P 500 0.30% and Nasdaq 1.38%; Alphabet lost 12.64%, Marvell more than 13%, and Micron nearly 5%. The Golden Dragon index gained 0.20%, with Alibaba down 1.41% and PDD up 2.34%; spot gold fell 1.13%, silver 3.35%, WTI was little changed at $102.86, DXY eased 0.17% to 102.037, and US two- and ten-year yields closed at 4.637% and 4.970%. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    US growth and inflation – both remained firm: Third-quarter GDP expanded at a 3.4% annualised rate, above the 3.1%–3.2% consensus range and the second quarter’s 2.2%. September headline PCE inflation accelerated to 3.6% year on year and core PCE was 3.1%, maintaining pressure on US rate expectations. [Eastmoney]

  17. 17

    US technology earnings – mixed cloud and storage signals: Amazon’s AWS revenue of $45.98bn exceeded expectations, but group sales and EPS were slightly below consensus. Intel guided fourth-quarter revenue to $15bn–$16bn versus a $17.05bn estimate, while SanDisk’s $12.2bn–$12.6bn revenue outlook topped consensus, relevant to HK cloud, server and storage names. [Eastmoney]
