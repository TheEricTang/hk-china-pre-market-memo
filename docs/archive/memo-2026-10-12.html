Morning Market Memo | 12 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 09 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 12 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    New productive-forces policy: The Communist Party Central Committee and State Council issued a broad policy package at 18:45 HKT Friday, calling for full implementation of “AI+”, national computing infrastructure and stronger support for new-energy vehicles, robotics, biopharma, aerospace and advanced materials. Future-industry priorities include embodied AI, brain–computer interfaces, 6G, quantum technology and nuclear fusion; the document also backs patient capital, technology bonds and listing and M&A support for technology companies. [MIIT]

  2. 02

    CSRC – additional policy tools under study: Chairman Wu Qing held a Saturday meeting with listed-company executives and securities and fund experts. Proposals discussed included strengthening market-stabilisation mechanisms, broadening long-term capital channels, encouraging more dividends and buybacks, optimising listing rules, tightening cross-border risk monitoring and preparing additional policy tools. [CSRC]

  3. 03

    Public funds – lower launch thresholds proposed: The CSRC proposed cutting the minimum size and units required to launch equity, mixed and fund-of-funds products to RMB50m and 50m units from RMB200m and 200m units. The draft also raises the limit for non-FOF products investing in other funds to 30% from 10% and gives fully replicating passive index funds limited exemptions from takeover-disclosure, short-swing trading and disposal restrictions. [CSRC]

  4. 04

    Autos – tougher testing proposed for new designs: MIIT and three other departments proposed mandatory validation for unconventional controls, displays, seats and door handles, including a ban on fully concealed door handles and a requirement for physical emergency controls. New models involving innovative designs would submit additional technical parameters from 1 January 2027; the draft specifies at least 30,000 kilometres of reliability testing and a full year of environmental validation. [MIIT]

  5. 05

    US patent investigation – AI-server supply chain: The US International Trade Commission opened a Section 337 investigation into vertical power-delivery systems after a complaint by Vicor, naming Luxshare Precision, Foxconn Industrial Internet and several global power and server suppliers. Luxshare and FII said the affected products remain in customer or internal validation and are not expected to have a material current-period operating impact; the ITC has made no ruling on the merits. [USITC]

  6. 06

    Focus Media–New Wave deal approved with restrictions: The State Administration for Market Regulation conditionally approved Focus Media’s acquisition of New Wave Media, the first conditionally cleared concentration case in China’s advertising sector. Conditions include price caps based on the preceding 24 months, continued service on fair and non-discriminatory terms and a prohibition on new exclusive or automatic-renewal clauses for elevator-media locations. [SAMR]

  7. 07

    AI hardware earnings – PCB profit acceleration: Jingwang Electronics forecast third-quarter net profit of RMB912m–1.089bn, up 205%–265%, citing faster shipments of high-performance PCBs for AI data infrastructure and strong orders in servers, data centres and optical modules. China Jushi forecast nine-month profit of RMB5.136bn–5.393bn, up 100%–110%, supported by stronger downstream demand and higher glass-fibre volumes and pricing. [21st Century Business Herald]

  8. 08

    Bohai Leasing – major Boeing order: Avolon, Bohai Leasing’s controlled aircraft-leasing subsidiary, agreed to purchase 140 Boeing 737 MAX-family aircraft. The catalogue value is capped at US$26.953bn before customary discounts, adding a sizeable long-dated order for Boeing and its aerospace supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Dongbai Group – optical-chip acquisition draws exchange scrutiny: Dongbai’s investment unit agreed to acquire 52.67% of Hangzhou Kuailing Optoelectronics for a provisional RMB316m, bringing loss-making optical-detector-chip producer Guangte Technology into its consolidated accounts. The Shanghai Stock Exchange issued a regulatory work letter on Sunday covering the acquisition and the responsibilities of the company, directors, senior management and controlling shareholders. [JW View]

  10. 10

    Apple supply chain – foldable order increase reported: Securities Times reported that Apple raised iPhone Duo orders by 30%, although the total remains below 10m units; iPhone 18 Pro orders were cut 10%–15%, while Pro Max orders rose by a similar amount, leaving overall Pro-series volume unchanged. The mix shift favours higher-value foldable components but does not indicate a broader increase in Apple’s total flagship orders. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Shenzhen housing support: Shenzhen’s housing authority proposed allowing provident-fund withdrawals for home renovation and property-management fees while maintaining greater rental-withdrawal support for workers without homes. The measure is incremental support for housing consumption, renovation and property services rather than a change to purchase restrictions. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Harbin Pharmaceutical – president detained: Harbin Pharmaceutical said director and president Lu Chuanyou had received investigation and detention notices from a supervisory commission. The company said its chairman and other senior executives continue to perform their duties and that board operations remain normal. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Overnight US markets: Friday’s Dow rose 0.83%, the S&P 500 0.59% and Nasdaq 0.64%; Nvidia slipped about 0.5% while Tesla gained about 2.1%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index added 2.69%, led by Li Auto +5.87%, Alibaba +5.36%, NIO +4.99%, Bilibili +4.31%, PDD +3.95% and XPeng +3.66%. Spot gold rose 1.47% to US$4,194.24/oz, silver gained 2.77%, WTI added 0.19% to US$91.66/bbl, DXY closed at 102.216 and the US 10-year yield ended at 5.2378%. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Week ahead – data, reporting and industrial events: China’s September CPI and PPI and the first nine-month trade briefing are scheduled for Wednesday, while September financing data may also be released this week. The Shanghai International Industry Fair opens today, A-share third-quarter reporting accelerates, and 37 companies face approximately RMB42.61bn of scheduled share unlocks this week, led by Shaanxi Energy. [Eastmoney]
