Morning Market Memo | 28 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 27 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 28 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Source availability – The 28 October Eastmoney 经济早餐 was not available on the column index by the cutoff; the latest visible edition remained dated 27 October. The memo therefore uses the specific underlying articles checked below. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    Overnight US markets – The Dow rose 0.03%, S&P 500 gained 0.60% and Nasdaq advanced 1.18%. Western Digital and Coherent rose more than 6%, Lumentum over 5%, Marvell over 4% and Tesla 4.04%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon index slipped 0.11%, with XPeng up 1.86%, JD.com up 1.31% and NetEase down 3.58%. WTI fell 1.53% to US$100.20/bbl, while spot gold gained 1.07% to US$4,060.33/oz and silver rose 0.82%. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Storage – Seagate reported fiscal first-quarter revenue of US$4.2bn, up 60%, and adjusted EPS of US$7.68 versus the US$7.39 consensus; adjusted gross margin reached 57.8%. The print and overnight US storage rally provide a positive read-through for mainland memory, PCB and data-centre hardware suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Robot memory demand – Elon Musk said robots would eventually require more memory than other computing applications, specifying that humanoids primarily use low-power DRAM rather than HBM. The comments add a fresh demand narrative for Chinese memory, robotics-controller and semiconductor-component names. [Shanghai Securities News]

  5. 05

    Shanghai intelligent manufacturing – The city’s new action plan targets 500 advanced smart factories, more than 2,000 industrial-AI applications, 300-plus high-level agents and deployment of 8,000 humanoid robots in factories by 2028. Shanghai also wants AI-enabled manufacturing-equipment output to exceed RMB200bn, supporting robotics, industrial software, machine tools and sensors. [Shanghai Government] [Shanghai Securities News]

  6. 06

    Pop Mart (9992 HK) – Third-quarter unaudited revenue fell 45%–50% year on year, including declines of 25%–30% in China and 70%–75% in the Americas. Management said China revenue could still post full-year growth, but the release indicates continued pressure from the tough LABUBU-led comparison base and overseas inventory adjustment. [NBD] [The Paper]

  7. 07

    Hengrui Pharma (600276 CH/1276 HK) – Third-quarter revenue rose 20.55% to RMB8.95bn and net profit increased 88.92% to RMB2.46bn. The company received RMB4.34bn of licensing upfront and milestone cash during the period, mainly including the US$600m Bristol Myers Squibb upfront payment. [NBD] [CLS]

  8. 08

    Wuliangye (000858 CH) – Third-quarter revenue dropped 37.68% to RMB4.44bn and net profit fell 48.25% to RMB958m. The company cited weak market demand and lower product volumes, providing a cautious read-through for the broader premium-baijiu complex despite sequential profit improvement. [The Paper] [Beijing Daily]

  9. 09

    Hudian (002463 CH) – Third-quarter revenue increased 90.02% to RMB9.54bn and net profit rose 160.77% to RMB2.70bn; its Thai subsidiary moved from ramp-up into scaled operations and turned profitable over the first nine months. The results reinforce the earnings momentum in high-end PCB and overseas AI-hardware capacity. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Sugon (603019 CH) – Third-quarter revenue rose 60.68% to RMB4.77bn and net profit climbed 217.19% to RMB753m on stronger domestic computing-equipment demand. The company also proposed a RMB300m–500m share buyback for an employee ownership or equity-incentive programme. [NBD] [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Huaxin (600621 CH) – Shanghai Yidian plans to transfer 26.99% of the company at RMB13.85 per share to Guosheng Group and Guomao Holdings for RMB3.97bn. Guosheng will become the controlling shareholder while Shanghai SASAC remains the ultimate controller; trading resumes today. [NBD] [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Rainbow Heavy Industries (002483 CH) – Its offshore-engineering subsidiary signed contracts with a European shipowner for six 13,000-DWT stainless-steel chemical tankers, including option vessels. The RMB1.50bn–1.65bn total value equals 21.9%–24.1% of 2025 revenue, adding material order visibility for the shipbuilding business. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    V-Test Semiconductor (688372 CH) – The company plans to invest RMB1bn in phase two of its Shanghai headquarters base in Pudong. The project will expand integrated-circuit testing capacity and increase coverage and scaled delivery capabilities for mid- to high-end chips. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Haike Xinyuan (301292 CH) – The company plans to invest up to RMB630m through a subsidiary in a 200,000-tonne-per-year battery-grade ethylene-carbonate plant and supporting facilities. The project expands upstream capacity for lithium-battery electrolyte solvents. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Yangjie Technology (300373 CH) – Third-quarter revenue rose 40.93% to RMB2.67bn and net profit increased 24.22% to RMB462m. Revenue from automotive, AI data-centre and silicon-carbide operations each more than doubled during the first nine months, supporting the domestic power-semiconductor chain. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    Hong Kong IPO – Dongshan Precision (2384 HK) is scheduled to begin Main Board trading today with a board lot of 100 shares. The A-to-H listing adds a major PCB, optical-module and precision-component manufacturer to the Hong Kong AI-hardware universe. [HKEX]
