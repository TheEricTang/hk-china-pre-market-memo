Morning Market Memo | 23 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 22 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 23 Sep 2026 06:50 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Overnight US markets – storage and semis lead:** The Dow fell 0.36%, the S&P 500 was flat and the Nasdaq gained 0.45% to a record close. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose more than 2%; SanDisk gained over 6%, Micron 5%, Seagate over 4%, Western Digital over 3%, Nvidia 0.66% and Tesla 0.96%, supporting the HK/A storage and semiconductor chain. ETF proxies showed USO down 2.77%, energy-sector XLE down 1.09% and gold ETF GLD up 0.43%; no verified final Golden Dragon or major China ADR print was available by cutoff. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **US–Iran talks – Hormuz conditions discussed:** Iran’s foreign minister met US special envoy Steve Witkoff in New York for three hours, with Trump calling the talks constructive and saying another meeting had been arranged. Iran said reopening the Strait of Hormuz would require an end to the maritime blockade, the unfreezing of Iranian assets and an end to regional wars; oil, airlines and shipping remain sensitive. [CCTV]

  3. 03

    **Digital trade – foreign leaders confirmed for Hangzhou event:** China’s Foreign Ministry said Malaysia’s Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim and Kyrgyzstan’s Prime Minister Adylbek Kasymaliev will attend today’s launch of the fifth Global Digital Trade Expo. The event runs through 27 September and keeps digital trade, cloud, AI and cross-border commerce themes in focus. [Foreign Ministry]

  4. 04

    **CICC – A shares resume trading:** CICC’s 601995 CH shares resume today following completion of the dissenting-shareholder cash-option process for its share-swap absorption of Dongxing Securities and Cinda Securities. The reopening provides a fresh price-discovery event for the brokerage sector and CICC’s 3908 HK shares. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **GPU infrastructure – two fresh spending commitments:** Xingyun Technology 300209 CH signed three five-year contracts to acquire 43 sets of GPU computing services for RMB453m. Xianhe Environmental 300137 CH separately proposed buying 63 GPU-server systems through finance leasing for RMB168m, adding fresh orders for domestic computing infrastructure. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **Semiconductor equipment – capacity funding:** Fuxin Technology 688662 CH proposed raising up to RMB270m for micro semiconductor thermoelectric-cooling devices, factory upgrades and working capital. Kerui Technology 002957 CH proposed a separate placement of up to RMB752m for ultra-high-precision components and equipment expansion. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    **Huace Film & TV – AI fund investment:** Huace Film & TV 300133 CH plans to invest RMB100m in a RMB704m Hangzhou Junlian private-equity fund, taking a 14.21% interest. The company said the investment is intended to accelerate its AI business positioning and ecosystem cooperation. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **Dongguan Holdings – low-altitude platform acquisition:** Dongguan Holdings 000828 CH agreed to acquire 65% of a municipal low-altitude-economy company for RMB6.05m. The target is Dongguan’s sole city-owned operating platform focused on government, transport, logistics and insurance-related flight services. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Baiyang Pharmaceutical – cardiovascular fund:** Baiyang Pharmaceutical 301015 CH will commit RMB156m to a proposed RMB400m Beijing medical-industry fund, representing 39% of total capital. The fund will target breakthrough drugs, medical devices and projects filling domestic technology gaps, with an emphasis on cardiovascular innovation. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **Yuexin Semiconductor – IPO pricing:** Yuexin Semiconductor 301660 CH priced its ChiNext IPO at RMB12.01 per share. The implied 2025 price-to-sales multiple is 13.39 times before any greenshoe exercise and 13.74 times if the option is used in full. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Sanwei Chemical – new specialty-chemical unit:** Sanwei Chemical 002469 CH completed construction, installation and trial-production readiness checks for a 50,000-tonne-a-year isooctanoic-acid unit. The project extends its aldehyde-alcohol-acid-ester chain, although the company cautioned that ramp-up and utilisation remain uncertain. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Yinglite – PVC weakness prompts shutdown:** Yinglite 000635 CH will temporarily halt its PVC production line and supporting carbide and power-generation facilities because product prices remain below expectations. The stoppage will reduce PVC, caustic-soda and calcium-carbide output and lower revenue. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Vanke – post-rally clarification:** Vanke A 000002 CH said operations remain stable and it has found no undisclosed information after its A shares accumulated a price-deviation gain exceeding 20% over three sessions. The post-close notice is a fresh caution for Vanke 2202 HK and the broader property complex. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Chuanda Zhisheng – CSRC investigation:** Chuanda Zhisheng 002253 CH received a formal CSRC investigation notice over suspected disclosure violations. The company said production and operations remain normal, but the investigation creates a fresh regulatory overhang. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Mindong Electric – hot-theme exposure denied:** Mindong Electric 000993 CH said it has no computing-power coordination, green-power direct-supply or smart-grid operations and no cooperation with computing or data centres. Its virtual-power-plant business remains pre-commercial, while its offshore-wind projects have not started construction or production. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    **Insider buying – two fresh plans:** Pulutong 002769 CH’s controlling shareholder plans to buy RMB25m–48m of shares over six months without a fixed price range. United Power 301656 CH’s chairman separately plans to purchase at least RMB20m of company stock within three months. [Eastmoney]

  17. 17

    **Source check:** The same-day Eastmoney 经济早餐 was not yet published or indexed by the research cutoff; the latest verified edition remained 22 September and its headlines were not reused as new overnight items. [Eastmoney]
