Morning Market Memo | 09 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 08 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 09 Sep 2026 08:38 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    US markets – indices lower, semis and optics outperform: The Dow fell 1.18% to 52,786.07, the S&P 500 lost 0.58% and the Nasdaq declined 0.32%. The SOX gained 1.30%, with Intel +9.05%, AMD +5.90%, Qualcomm +3.17% and TSMC +2.35%; Nvidia fell 2.01% while Tesla rose 3.98%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon dropped 1.73%, led by Baidu -6.96%, PDD -2.99% and JD -2.05%, although Bilibili gained 6.37%. WTI’s continuous contract rose 3.03% to US$94.25/bbl, spot gold fell 1.15% to US$4,355.55/oz and the US 10-year yield added 1.2bp to 4.7942%. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    HK night session – limited rebound: September Hang Seng futures closed 59 points higher at 25,281, still 36 points below the cash index. The contract’s modest 0.23% gain points to a restrained start after Tuesday’s 0.38% cash-market decline. [Investing.com]

  3. 03

    China–UK opening agenda: Xi Jinping told newly appointed UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham that China will further open education, healthcare and financial services during the 15th Five-Year Plan. Xi also called for protection of Chinese investors’ legal rights, putting financial and commercial cooperation at the centre of the first leader-level contact with the new government. [Xinhua]

  4. 04

    City renewal – fiscal and credit support: Premier Li Qiang said China should increase government investment, coordinate policy funding with finance and improve city-renewal funds and other sustainable financing tools. The State Council study also prioritised old districts, infrastructure resilience and conversion of legacy industrial areas, relevant to contractors, building materials, property managers and banks. [State Council DRC]

  5. 05

    Middle East escalation lifts the energy focus: Houthi forces attacked Saudi energy and military facilities, while the US military said it destroyed five Iran-linked oil tankers; Iran subsequently warned tankers in Kuwaiti and Bahraini ports to leave. The widening threat to production and shipping routes keeps PetroChina, Sinopec, CNOOC, refiners, airlines and shippers in focus after oil’s overnight rise. [HK01] [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Critical minerals – Zimbabwe halts antimony and tungsten exports: Zimbabwe immediately suspended exports of antimony and tungsten, including ores and concentrates, as it seeks more domestic refining. The restriction adds another supply-side catalyst for Chinese tungsten and antimony miners, smelters and recycling companies. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Qualcomm–Amazon AI infrastructure deal: Qualcomm and Amazon agreed to develop multiple generations of customised silicon for AI inference and high-performance optical connectivity running at up to 1.6T. The deal, which helped Qualcomm rise 3.17%, provides a direct read-through for HK/A-share optical modules, DSPs, SerDes, PCB and data-centre power suppliers. [Qualcomm] [IT之家]

  8. 08

    Youdi Robot IPO – listing today: Youdi Robot 3231 HK priced 45 million H-shares at HK$14.45, the bottom of its range, for estimated net proceeds of HK$576.6 million. The Hong Kong tranche was subscribed 140.02 times but the international tranche only 0.99 times; trading begins at 09:00 HKT. [Sina] [观点网]

  9. 09

    HK buybacks – Tencent, Miniso and Li Auto: Tencent 700 HK spent HK$100 million buying 230,000 shares, extending its repurchase run to 17 sessions and HK$3.71 billion since 17 August. Miniso 9896 HK bought HK$24.85 million of shares for a seventh session, while Li Auto 2015 HK repurchased HK$26.63 million. [STCN] [STCN] [STCN]

  10. 10

    Southbound flows – Alibaba and optical fibre bought: Southbound investors recorded net buying of HK$61.28 billion on Tuesday. Changfei Fiber 6869 HK and Alibaba 9988 HK led individual purchases at HK$654 million and HK$627 million, while SMIC 981 HK saw HK$662 million of net selling. [Yicai]

  11. 11

    Benchuang Intelligent – major AI-PCB expansion: Benchuang Intelligent 300964 CH plans a RMB20 billion Nanjing project with annual capacity of 1.5 million square metres of high-layer and high-density AI circuit boards, scheduled over five years. Its Zhuhai subsidiary separately plans to invest up to RMB10 billion in a second high-end interconnect manufacturing base. [Eastmoney] [Sina]

  12. 12

    Zhifei Biological – semaglutide filing accepted: Zhifei Biological 300122 CH said its subsidiary’s semaglutide injection production application was accepted by the drug regulator. The diabetes and cardiovascular-risk product would enter a market where, according to the announcement, no domestically developed semaglutide injection has yet been approved. [Sina]

  13. 13

    Shennan Circuits – AI project funding retained: Shennan Circuits 002916 CH reduced its proposed private-placement fundraising ceiling to RMB4.367 billion from RMB4.882 billion. The RMB3.6 billion allocation for its Wuxi AI-computing circuit project was unchanged, with the reduction entirely in working-capital funding. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    A-share repurchases – medical devices and solar equipment: Yuyue Medical 002223 CH plans a RMB200–400 million buyback at no more than RMB40 per share for employee incentives. Deye 605117 CH’s chairman separately proposed a RMB100–200 million repurchase, also for employee ownership or equity incentives. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Longsys – large post-listing buyback: Longsys 301308 CH spent RMB448 million on its first buyback, acquiring 1.2495 million A-shares at RMB353.52–365.38 each. The purchase followed Tuesday’s Hong Kong listing of 29.99 million H-shares and provides a fresh capital-management signal for the storage complex. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    Longban Media – trading suspended today: Longban Media 605577 CH will be suspended from today pending a trading review after six consecutive limit-up sessions through 7 September and another 9.12% gain on Tuesday. The company said the roughly 93.47% cumulative rise had seriously diverged from fundamentals. [Eastmoney]

  17. 17

    Nanda Environment – potential control change: Nanda Environment 300864 CH’s controlling shareholder plans to seek a buyer for at least 15% of the company under reforms governing enterprises affiliated with centrally administered universities. Its holding would fall to no more than 14.23%, potentially changing the environmental-services company’s controller. [Eastmoney]

  18. 18

    Three’s Company – cross-sector data-centre investment: Three’s Company 605168 CH plans to invest RMB285 million for a 33% stake in Wuhu Yungang, which provides equipment hosting, customised data rooms and rack resources. The target is not yet profitable and Three’s Company will not obtain control, making execution and utilisation the key follow-ups. [Eastmoney]
