Morning Market Memo | 18 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 17 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 18 Nov 2026 08:04 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow fell 1.64%, S&P 500 lost 1.03% and Nasdaq declined 0.74%; the SOX reversed an intraday drop of more than 3% to close 0.34% higher, with TSMC down 3.70% while Lumentum and Coherent gained 2.56% and 3.69%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon index fell 1.89%—JD.com lost 7.66%, Alibaba 4.38% and Baidu 3.77%, while Tencent Music rose 6.64%. By 07:01 HKT, Nasdaq and S&P futures were up 1.38% and 0.75%; the US 10-year yield ended at 5.0987%, DXY at 103.485, spot gold at US$3,849.59 and silver at US$47.28. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney Futures]

  2. 02

    **Nvidia beats; China excluded from guidance:** Nvidia reported fiscal-Q3 revenue of US$115.8bn, up 103% year on year, including US$107.6bn from data centres. It guided fiscal-Q4 revenue to US$130bn, plus or minus 3%, but assumed no China data-centre compute revenue; the shares rose more than 4% after hours, supporting the read-through for HK/A-share AI hardware, optical and PCB names. [Nvidia] [Eastmoney Futures]

  3. 03

    **China–US semiconductors:** MOFCOM said Washington’s new restrictions on remote access to advanced semiconductors interfere with normal trade and threaten global chip supply chains. China urged the US to return to consultations and said it would take countermeasures if the rules proceed, relevant to Chinese AI-cloud operators and overseas computing arrangements. [MOFCOM]

  4. 04

    **Semiconductor-equipment standard:** China released its first dedicated quality-management standard for semiconductor equipment and components, covering ultra-clean production, supply-chain resilience, equipment reliability, nanometre-level process control and full-lifecycle traceability. The standard takes effect on 1 May 2027 and supports further standardisation across domestic equipment and component suppliers. [Xinhua] [MIIT]

  5. 05

    **Digital Guangdong plan:** Guangdong set a 2030 target for digital-economy core industries to reach 18% of provincial GDP and for large internet and related-service companies to generate RMB1.1tn of revenue. Priorities include integrated-circuit manufacturing, advanced packaging, optical-communications and AI chips, high-end robotics and domestic software ecosystems. [Southcn] [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **APEC bilateral cooperation:** Xi Jinping’s meetings with Mexico and Chile highlighted deeper trade and investment ties, including manufacturing, new energy, agriculture and AI with Mexico, and energy, mining, infrastructure and AI with Chile. The statements are relevant to exporters, industrial supply chains and China–Latin America metals and energy cooperation. [Xinhua] [Xinhua]

  7. 07

    **JD.com (9618 HK):** Third-quarter revenue rose 3.5% to RMB309.6bn, while non-GAAP operating profit increased to RMB7.3bn from RMB0.2bn and non-GAAP net profit rose 44% to RMB8.3bn. Management cited resilient retail operations, narrowing losses in new businesses and completion of the CECONOMY acquisition. [HKEX] [JD.com]

  8. 08

    **JD Logistics (2618 HK):** Third-quarter revenue increased 16.1% to RMB63.95bn and adjusted operating profit rose 37.9% to RMB2.10bn. Revenue from external integrated-supply-chain customers grew 17.3% to RMB10.5bn, with both customer numbers and average revenue per customer increasing. [HKEX] [CLS]

  9. 09

    **Tencent Music (1698 HK):** Revenue rose 5.6% to RMB8.94bn, led by 10.2% growth in music-related services; adjusted EBITDA increased 2.7% to RMB3.02bn. The company repurchased US$300m of ADSs during the quarter, while artist merchandise and offline performances supported consumption-related revenue. [HKEX] [Xinhua Finance]

  10. 10

    **Miniso (9896 HK):** Third-quarter revenue increased 14.3% to RMB6.63bn, with mainland Miniso-brand revenue up 29.6% but overseas revenue only 1.7% higher. Global stores reached 8,665, although quarterly profit fell 34.4% to RMB291m, leaving the focus on the balance between domestic same-store growth and profit investment. [HKEX] [The Paper]

  11. 11

    **ZTO Express (2057 HK):** Third-quarter parcel volume rose 7% to 10.2bn pieces and market share reached 20.1%. Revenue increased 21% to RMB14.36bn, while adjusted net profit rose 2.6% to RMB2.57bn, providing an overnight read-through for express delivery and e-commerce logistics. [HKEX] [PR Newswire]

  12. 12

    **Fosun Pharma (2196 HK/600196 CH):** Chairman Chen Yuqing proposed an A-share repurchase of RMB250m–500m through competitive bidding. The maximum price would be 150% of the average A-share price during the 30 trading days before board approval, with funding from internal or raised resources. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Yunnan Germanium (002428 CH):** The company increased investment in its high-quality indium-phosphide wafer project from RMB188.6m to RMB270.0m, citing higher equipment costs and a shift toward larger products. The revised plan adds 294 equipment sets and retains an ultimate annual-capacity target equivalent to 450,000 four-inch wafers, relevant to optical-communications materials. [CNInfo] [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **OKE Precision Cutting Tools (688308 CH):** OKE proposed raising up to RMB950m, including RMB500m for an industrial-park upgrade, RMB120m for high-precision inserts and RMB57m for an engineering platform producing ultrafine carbide rods for AI-PCB drill bits. The transaction extends its integration from upstream carbide rods into PCB micro-drills following its acquisition of control of Yongxin Precision. [China Fund News] [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Tongguang Cable (300265 CH)–YOFC venture terminated:** Tongguang and YOFC agreed to dissolve their planned RMB600m optical-fibre joint venture. Tongguang had committed RMB339m for a 56.5% stake but had paid only RMB400,000, making the cancellation a fresh company-specific item for the fibre and cable complex. [NBD] [Eastmoney]
