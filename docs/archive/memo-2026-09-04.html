Morning Market Memo | 04 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 03 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 04 Sep 2026 08:25 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Policy finance – RMB800bn tool begins deployment: China Development Bank disclosed after the close that the 2026 new policy-based financial instrument made its first RMB460m of project-capital injections, covering three private-investment projects. These include RMB245m for CALB’s 50GWh Sichuan battery and energy-storage base, alongside a green nickel-chromium materials project and a Wuhan ring-road section. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    Innovative drugs – national negotiations start Saturday: The 2026 national reimbursement-drug negotiations and bidding process will begin on 5 September, with price consultations for the commercial-insurance innovative-drug catalogue running in parallel. Pharmaceutical companies completed preliminary communications with the National Healthcare Security Administration last week; the process is relevant to innovative-drug and biotech names across Hong Kong and the A-share market. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Agriculture and insurance – El Niño set to strengthen: The World Meteorological Organization confirmed on 3 September that El Niño is firmly established and is expected to become very strong, with a near-100% probability of persisting through February 2027. The associated risks of drought, flooding and extreme heat bear watching for agricultural commodities, food producers, utilities and insurers. [WMO]

  4. 04

    JCET – RMB6.5bn advanced-packaging placement: JCET Group (600584 CH) plans to raise up to RMB6.5bn through a private A-share placement. Proceeds will fund high-performance-computing and power-module advanced packaging, wafer-level process expansion, higher-density memory-chip testing and packaging, and balance-sheet purposes. [NBD]

  5. 05

    Jingzhida – RMB1.58bn semiconductor-equipment order: Jingzhida (688627 CH) signed a RMB1.576bn tax-inclusive contract for semiconductor testing equipment and related fixtures, with delivery expected over two years. The company said successful execution should contribute to results from 2026 through 2028; the contract is nearly twice its first-half revenue of RMB804m. [NBD]

  6. 06

    Yangtze Memory – STAR Market IPO reaches inquiry stage: The Shanghai Stock Exchange changed Yangtze Memory parent Changcun Holdings’ IPO review status to “under inquiry” on Thursday evening. The move advances the domestic 3D-NAND producer into the substantive exchange-review process and is a fresh catalyst for China’s memory, semiconductor-equipment and materials supply chain. [Economic Observer]

  7. 07

    Enflame – RMB6.12bn AI-chip IPO allocation completed: Enflame Technology published its STAR Market allocation and lottery results after the close, with 20,657 winning numbers entitled to subscribe for 500 shares each. The company is selling 43.04m shares at RMB142.18, implying gross proceeds of approximately RMB6.12bn and keeping domestic AI accelerators in focus. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Computing-power contracts – matching RMB922m agreements: Xingyun Technology (300209 CH) signed a five-year RMB922m contract to provide computing resources, while Yakang Technology (301085 CH) separately disclosed a five-year RMB922m purchase from an unnamed Hunan supplier. Matching amounts, terms and locations suggest the companies may be counterparties, but neither has confirmed the other’s identity. [CLS] [Yicai]

  9. 09

    Shuanghuan Driveline – robot-joint unit listing withdrawn: Shuanghuan Driveline (002472 CH) approved the termination and withdrawal of subsidiary Huandong Technology’s proposed STAR Market listing, citing changes in market conditions and its broader capital planning. The decision remains subject to shareholder approval; the company said it would not materially affect existing operations or finances. [Sina]

  10. 10

    Zhongji Innolight – third consecutive A-share buyback: Zhongji Innolight (300308 CH/3308 HK) repurchased 233,100 A-shares for RMB190m on 3 September at RMB806.93–819 per share. Since starting the programme on 1 September, it has bought roughly 960,000 shares for RMB803m, providing a fresh capital-return signal for the optical-transceiver complex. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Tencent – HKD100m daily repurchase continues: Tencent (700 HK) bought back 229,000 shares for approximately HKD100m at HKD433–445.40 after Thursday’s session. This was its 14th consecutive repurchase day since 17 August, taking purchases over that period to 7.63m shares and HKD3.41bn. [STCN]

  12. 12

    US data and Fed – services firm, rate decision remains data-dependent: US August ISM services rose to 55.4, although its employment component remained in contraction and the prices index climbed to 72.6; weekly jobless claims were 206,000. Fed Governor Christopher Waller said he would favour holding rates if inflation continues cooling but could support another increase if upcoming inflation data are hot, prompting some reduction in near-term tightening bets. [Xinhua Finance]

  13. 13

    Overnight US markets – equities rallied; ADRs lagged and metals jumped: The Dow gained 1.18% to 53,686.11, the S&P 500 rose 1.06% to 7,747.71 and the Nasdaq added 1.40% to 26,584.06; Tesla finished more than 5% higher, while Dell and Oracle gained more than 4% and 5%, respectively. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index fell 0.81%, with Hesai +11.27%, Baidu +1.31%, Alibaba flat and Trip.com -5.11%. COMEX gold rose 2.39% to US$4,520.30/oz, WTI gained 0.73% to US$91.67, the DXY fell 0.56% to 99.00, and the US 10-year yield eased 1.4bp to 4.766%. [Eastmoney] [Sina] [Xinhua Finance]

  14. 14

    Southbound flows – HKD33.95bn net buying: Mainland investors bought a net HKD33.95bn of Hong Kong shares on Thursday. The largest net purchases were CSOP Hang Seng Tech at HKD528m, YOFC at HKD464m and MiniMax at HKD411m; Alibaba, Kingboard Holdings and SMIC saw net selling of HKD760m, HKD183m and HKD148m, respectively. [Eastmoney]
