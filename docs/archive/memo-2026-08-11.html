Morning Market Memo | 11 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 10 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 11 Aug 2026 07:35 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    PBOC five-year plan: The central bank issued its “15th Five-Year” reform and development plan with nine supporting action plans. Priorities include improving base-money delivery and market-based rate transmission, supporting technology, green and consumer finance, advancing digital renminbi and cross-border payments, and strengthening Hong Kong’s international financial-centre role. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    Coal policy – larger and smarter mines: NDRC and the National Energy Administration set 2030 targets for large modern mines to reach 87% of national capacity and intelligent mines 75%. The five main supply bases are to produce more than 80% of national output, while at least 100m tonnes a year of reserve capacity will be established; relevant names include China Shenhua, China Coal Energy and Yanzhou Coal. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Alibaba Cloud capacity expansion: Alibaba Cloud plans to more than double global modular data-centre capacity this year after cutting large AIDC delivery time to 100 days and construction costs by more than 10%. Its CUBE 5.0 architecture has reached 90% modularisation and supports both air and liquid cooling, positive for Alibaba 9988 HK and domestic power, cooling and data-centre equipment suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Kunlun Tech seeks Hong Kong listing: Kunlun Tech 300418 CH plans to issue H shares and apply for a Hong Kong main-board listing. It will separately sell its entire interest in mobile-game unit Xianlai Entertainment for RMB750m, removing the business from its consolidated accounts and sharpening focus on AI and overseas operations. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Jiemi Technology moves into optical equipment: Jiemi Technology 002859 CH agreed to acquire 100% of EFOS Technology for RMB915m through a share issue, with an additional placement planned. The deal extends the company from electronic-packaging consumables into ultra-high-precision optical-component polishing equipment. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Construction Machinery trading suspension: Construction Machinery 600984 CH plans to acquire 100% of Pucheng Clean Energy through shares and cash while raising matching funds. The target produces polyethylene and polypropylene; the buyer’s shares are suspended from today for an expected period of no more than ten trading days. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Semiconductor and rare-earth capacity: Huicheng Vacuum 301392 CH plans to raise up to RMB955m for ultra-precision semiconductor and continuous PVD equipment, while Zhongke Magnetic 301141 CH proposed a convertible bond of up to RMB609m for robot and thermal-management high-performance rare-earth magnet components. The announcements add fresh capacity catalysts for domestic semiconductor tools and robotics materials. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Gan & Lee licenses obesity drug overseas: Gan & Lee Pharmaceuticals 603087 CH granted Menarini exclusive registration and commercialisation rights for bofanglutide in obesity and overweight indications across the licensed territories. Terms include a €62m upfront payment, up to €664m in development, approval and sales milestones, and tiered royalties reaching a double-digit percentage. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Jiangbolong results and buyback: Storage supplier Jiangbolong 301308 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB24.09bn, up 136.3%, and net profit of RMB10.58bn versus a low prior-year base; second-quarter profit was RMB6.72bn. It approved an RMB400m–800m share buyback at no more than RMB735 per share for employee incentives or an ownership plan. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    ChipON returns to profit: ChipON Integrated 688469 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB4.56bn, up 30.5%, and net profit of RMB278m, reversing a RMB170m loss a year earlier. Management attributed the turnaround to higher utilisation, customer and product expansion and a better product mix, providing a fresh read-through for domestic foundry utilisation. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    A-share buybacks broaden: Beijing Ingenic 300223 CH proposed a RMB100m–200m repurchase, Zhaochi 002429 CH RMB300m–500m and Guolian Minsheng 601456 CH RMB100m–200m. Zhaochi and Guolian Minsheng said their programmes are intended to support company value and shareholder interests. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Auto suppliers secure long-cycle orders: Faway 600742 CH received seat-project nominations from a joint-venture automaker with estimated eight-year lifetime sales of RMB3.94bn, starting in late 2027 and early 2028. Sinofuse Electric 301031 CH won sole global supply status for a US EV maker’s 900V battery-pack switching platform, worth an estimated RMB200m from late 2028. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Unitree IPO allocation: Unitree Robotics 688836 CH priced its STAR Market offering at RMB150.80 per share and issued 40.45m shares. After clawback, 9.71m shares were allocated online and the final online subscription success rate was 0.0181%, keeping attention on the A/H robotics supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Overnight US markets: The Dow fell 0.11% to 53,975.98, the S&P 500 slipped 0.06% to 7,753.11 and the Nasdaq lost 0.32% to 26,605.36. Nvidia dropped 2.86% and Apple 1.53%; Coherent fell more than 14% and Lumentum more than 8%, while SanDisk gained over 2%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon index rose 1.65%, with Alibaba up over 3% and NIO over 1%. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Oil and precious metals jump: WTI crude rose 5.27% to US$82.30 a barrel as confidence in a near-term reopening of the Strait of Hormuz weakened; Brent traded above US$87 intraday. COMEX gold gained 1.11% to US$4,448.60 an ounce and silver rose 3.75% to US$65.88, supporting the HK-listed oil producers and precious-metals miners at the open. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    Nvidia infrastructure financing: Nvidia is reportedly working with Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR on more than US$500bn of financing for AI-computing infrastructure. The scale is supportive for global server demand but comes alongside weakness in US optical-networking shares, leaving a mixed read-through for Chinese AI hardware, optics and data-centre suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  17. 17

    Fed signal remains firm: Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack said a single 25bp increase may not materially affect the economy and that several rate rises could be required to return inflation to 2%. The remarks, combined with the oil-price rebound, keep US rate sensitivity relevant for Hong Kong growth stocks. [Eastmoney]
