Morning Market Memo | 16 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 13 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 16 Nov 2026 06:10 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    New World Development recapitalisation: New World Development (17 HK) proposed an 11-for-1 rights issue at HK$0.90 per share, an 81.5% discount to the pre-suspension close, to raise HK$24.9bn gross. It plans to repay 10% of HK$82bn in targeted offshore bank debt and extend the remaining HK$73.8bn by two years; the theoretical dilution is 75.3%. [HKEX] [RTHK]

  2. 02

    Overnight US markets: The Dow rose 0.10% to 52,347.55, while the S&P 500 fell 0.40% and Nasdaq lost 0.91%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 3.43%, with Nvidia down 2%, AMD off more than 8% and SanDisk down more than 5%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index gained 0.84%, with PDD up 1.86% but NIO down about 5%; gold was little changed at US$3,879.70, WTI fell 0.20% to US$88.56 and the US 10-year yield rose to 5.1413%. [Eastmoney] [Sina] [Sohu]

  3. 03

    China credit data: October renminbi loans fell RMB100bn, while new aggregate financing was RMB1.15tn; M2 growth eased to 7.1% and M1 accelerated to 4.4%. Government and corporate bond financing supported aggregate financing, but household and corporate loan demand remained soft. [Eastmoney] [Sina]

  4. 04

    PBOC liquidity: The central bank will conduct RMB200bn of six-month outright reverse repos today, against RMB300bn maturing, implying a RMB100bn net withdrawal. November’s three- and six-month operations together represent a RMB300bn net withdrawal, following a RMB400bn net injection in October. [Xinhua] [Shanghai Securities News]

  5. 05

    China data due today: The National Bureau of Statistics will release October home-price data at 09:30 HKT and industrial production, retail sales, fixed-asset investment, property and energy data at 10:00. State Council Information Office spokesman Fu Linghui will brief on the October economy at 10:00. [NBS] [SCIO]

  6. 06

    Capital-market policy: CSRC Chairman Wu Qing said social-security, insurance and pension funds have purchased more than RMB1.5tn of A-shares since 2024, while stocks and bonds accounted for nearly 50% of new aggregate financing in the first nine months. He said new-economy companies now represent more than half of A-share company numbers, revenue and market value. [CSRC]

  7. 07

    Derivatives rules take effect: China’s first CSRC departmental rules dedicated to derivatives trading become effective today, covering swaps, forwards and non-standard options. Securities and futures firms must meet capital, suitability, margin, reporting and risk-control requirements; non-compliant businesses cannot add new transactions and must run off existing positions. [CSRC]

  8. 08

    Drug reimbursement expansion: The 2026 national reimbursement list added 107 drugs, including a record 51 Class-1 innovative medicines, while the commercial-insurance innovation list added 12 drugs; both take effect on 1 January 2027. Hengrui (1276 HK), Hansoh (3692 HK) and Sunshine Lake Pharma (6887 HK) separately confirmed new products or indications were included. [NHSA] [Hengrui] [Hansoh]

  9. 09

    Hansoh European catalyst: Hansoh Pharmaceutical said the European Medicines Agency’s CHMP recommended marketing authorisation for Aumseqa, or almonertinib, in non-small-cell lung cancer. Final approval remains subject to the European Commission. [Hansoh]

  10. 10

    JW Therapeutics placement: JW Therapeutics (2126 HK) agreed to issue 61.19m shares to China Hongqiao and Novo Vista at HK$2.615 each, raising about HK$160m gross and expanding issued capital by roughly 12.8%. The proceeds mainly fund in-vivo and autologous CAR-T pipelines and manufacturing upgrades. [HKEX]

  11. 11

    Optical-chip order: Yuanjie Semiconductor (688498 CH) received a RMB1.744bn order from an unnamed customer for high-power laser chips. The company said successful execution should support earnings but warned that delivery and revenue recognition depend on its production-capacity ramp. [CNInfo] [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    China–ASEAN and Thailand: Premier Li Qiang arrived in Manila on Sunday for today’s China–ASEAN leaders’ meeting after completing his Thailand visit. China and Thailand agreed to deepen cooperation in autos, finance, semiconductors and AI and witnessed agreements covering the auto industry, agriculture, customs, vocational education and security. [Xinhua] [Xinhua]

  13. 13

    APEC Shenzhen: China’s trade-promotion agency said leaders from 12 APEC economies will attend the 17–18 November CEO Summit, including Xi Jinping and leaders from Hong Kong, Australia, Canada, South Korea, Indonesia and Vietnam. The main APEC leaders’ meeting follows on 18–19 November. [Xinhua]

  14. 14

    BYD denies acquisition rumour: BYD (1211 HK/002594 CH) said weekend claims that it planned to acquire Huawei’s digital-energy business had no factual basis. The denial removes a potential transaction catalyst for BYD and the solar-inverter and charging-equipment supply chain. [The Paper]

  15. 15

    HK buybacks: Yum China (9987 HK) repurchased 21,900 shares in Hong Kong at an average HK$387.50 and 99,033 shares in the US at an average US$50.49 on Friday. Shuangdeng Group (6960 HK) bought 55,000 H-shares for HK$252,622.50; the weekend filings showed no comparable megacap-internet repurchase. [Yum China] [Shuangdeng]

  16. 16

    Sell-side – non-bank financials: CITIC Securities said the new commercial-health-insurance drug list adds policy support for insurers and maintained recommendations on major insurers, leading brokers and HKEX (388 HK). It highlighted wealth management as a stabiliser for brokerage earnings despite weaker third-quarter A-share turnover. [Sina]
