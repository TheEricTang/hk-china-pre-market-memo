Morning Market Memo | 04 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 03 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 04 Aug 2026 07:48 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Integrated-circuit IP protection: Premier Li Qiang signed the revised Integrated Circuit Layout Design Protection Regulations, effective 15 October. The 54-article framework expands protection to integrated photonic and quantum-function circuits, tightens application procedures and strengthens infringement compensation, relevant to Chinese chip designers, foundries and semiconductor-IP owners. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    Alphamab Oncology – cervical-cancer trial: Alphamab Oncology (9966 HK) said China’s CDE agreed to a Phase III trial of JSKN033 for recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer following failure of platinum chemotherapy and PD-1/PD-L1 treatment. The company describes JSKN033 as the first high-concentration subcutaneous combination of a HER2 bispecific ADC and a PD-L1 inhibitor, adding a fresh clinical catalyst for the HK biotech sector. [AAStocks]

  3. 03

    Sungrow – RMB1bn buyback ceiling: Sungrow Power Supply (300274 CH) approved an RMB500m–1bn share-repurchase programme, with a maximum purchase price of RMB188 per share and a 12-month implementation period. Repurchased shares are intended for an employee ownership or equity-incentive plan, providing a fresh capital-return signal for the solar-inverter and energy-storage complex. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Battery materials – RMB2.81bn expansion: Shida Shenghua (603026 CH) announced three projects totalling RMB2.805bn: a 230,000-tonne annual liquid-lithium-salt facility, a 12,000-tonne additives project and a 200,000-tonne electrolyte plant. The plans expand upstream and midstream capacity across the lithium-battery electrolyte chain. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Wind and solar – Yunda project: Windey Energy Technology (300772 CH) plans to invest RMB1.45bn in the first phase of a green-power project in Fengzhen. The approved 264.5MW capacity comprises 214.5MW of wind and 50MW of solar, supporting equipment and engineering demand across the renewable-power chain. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Sunshine Co – new computing centre: Sunshine Co (000608 CH) said its controlled subsidiary plans to invest up to RMB980m in a computing-power centre in Xingning, Guangdong. The post-close announcement adds another domestic data-centre project for servers, storage, cooling, power equipment and network-infrastructure suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Gaole – five-year computing contract: Gaole (002348 CH) said a wholly owned subsidiary signed a five-year computing-services contract worth RMB3.195bn including tax. The company estimates roughly RMB200m of 2026 revenue if implementation proceeds as planned, but still expects to remain loss-making this year after accounting for equity-incentive costs. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    A-share shareholder returns: Dongwu Securities’ controlling shareholder plans to add RMB100m–200m of shares, while Shandong Expressway and Boke New Materials each approved RMB100m–200m repurchases for cancellation. Shandong Expressway also secured a repurchase-loan commitment of up to RMB180m, reinforcing the use of bank-supported buybacks and controlling-shareholder purchases. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Overnight US markets: The Dow rose 1.32% to a record 53,178.41, the S&P 500 gained 1.48% and the Nasdaq added 2.13%. Nvidia rose nearly 3%, Alphabet almost 5%, Amazon more than 4%, Meta over 6% and Microsoft nearly 5%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index gained 0.75%, with Alibaba up more than 4% while NIO fell over 1%. WTI dropped 5.44% to US$80.06/bbl, COMEX gold edged up 0.09% to US$4,110.90/oz and silver gained 1.05% to US$58.395/oz. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    US AI and optics: Lumentum and Coherent rose more than 9%, Corning gained over 6% and Credo advanced more than 5%, a positive overnight read-through for HK/A optical-component names. Palantir rose over 10% after hours after quarterly revenue of US$1.94bn beat the US$1.81bn consensus and the company raised full-year revenue guidance to US$8.15bn–8.16bn. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Iran talks – Hormuz timetable: President Trump said negotiations with Iran were under way in two stages, first reopening the Strait of Hormuz and then addressing nuclear issues. He said the strait could reopen as soon as 4 August; the statement coincided with the 5.44% fall in WTI and is relevant to Chinese airlines, refiners, chemicals and oil producers. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    US tariff challenge: A coalition of 25 US states filed suit against the federal government’s latest tariffs covering goods from 60 trading partners. The states argue the measures exceed presidential authority and improperly replace tariffs previously rejected by the Supreme Court; the litigation adds uncertainty for Chinese exporters and cross-border supply chains. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    AI-model security: The White House is due to meet leading AI companies on Tuesday to discuss a newly completed voluntary framework for testing the cybersecurity capabilities of frontier models. The initiative follows a June presidential order and is relevant to global AI governance, model-security requirements and Chinese platform companies’ overseas operations. [Eastmoney]
