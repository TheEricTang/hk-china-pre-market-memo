Morning Market Memo | 26 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 25 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 26 Aug 2026 07:15 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Alibaba – founder and management buy shares:** Yicai reported, citing sources, that Jack Ma bought more than HK$600mn of Alibaba 9988 HK shares over several days. Together with purchases by chairman Joe Tsai and CEO Eddie Wu, recent management buying exceeded HK$800mn, following the group’s HK$80bn AI-focused placement. [Yicai]

  2. 02

    **Infrastructure funding – NDRC studies “six networks” support:** Vice chairman Yue Xiuhu chaired a post-close meeting on financing mechanisms for the six-network programme. Officials discussed combining fiscal, financial, investment and pricing support to accelerate projects spanning computing, power, communications and other strategic infrastructure. [36Kr]

  3. 03

    **Shanghai trade plan – metals, autos and offshore business:** Shanghai’s new 2026–30 international trade-centre plan calls for larger copper and aluminium trading markets and new lithium, cobalt and nickel futures products. It also supports auto-consumption reform, integrated-circuit and AI exports, foreign-company reinvestment and further offshore-trade pilots; the city targets more than US$3tn of goods trade during the five years. [Shanghai Government]

  4. 04

    **Agricultural distribution – 2030 market-system target:** The Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs issued its 15th Five-Year Plan for agricultural wholesale and origin markets. By 2030 it aims to improve market layouts, cold-chain and logistics facilities, monitoring systems and links between producers and domestic and international buyers. [Jiemian]

  5. 05

    **Nuclear fusion – Yangtze River Delta magnet consortium:** China Fusion Energy and eight partners will jointly develop high-temperature superconducting magnets for the China Circulation-4 tokamak. The programme targets a 25-tesla magnet development and testing line before 2028 and completion of a prototype magnet before 2030, relevant to listed fusion-material and superconducting-equipment suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **North Huachuang – semiconductor-equipment revenue up 25%:** North Huachuang 002371 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB20.16bn, up 24.9%, and net profit of RMB3.37bn, up 5.1%. Its new 12-inch high-end ICP etching tool has completed customer validation and entered the mass-production introduction stage, while equipment coverage expanded across etching, deposition, cleaning and ion implantation. [Shanghai Securities News]

  7. 07

    **Hudian – AI PCB mix lifts margins:** Hudian 002463 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB13.69bn, up 61.2%, and net profit of RMB2.92bn, up 73.7%. Revenue from PCB products with 32 or more layers rose 190.8%, helping PCB gross margin increase about 4.1 percentage points to 40.5%; the read-through is positive for high-layer PCB and copper-clad-laminate suppliers. [China Securities Journal]

  8. 08

    **Kuaishou – another HK buyback:** Kuaishou 1024 HK repurchased 899,000 shares for HK$29.98mn on Tuesday at HK$33.24–33.46 per share. Purchases since the June mandate reached 28.54mn shares, equivalent to about 0.66% of the share base at the mandate date. [jrj]

  9. 09

    **Nongfu Spring – tea drives double-digit growth:** Nongfu Spring 9633 HK reported first-half revenue of RMB29.72bn, up 16.0%, and net profit of RMB8.89bn, up 16.6%. Tea-drink revenue increased 30.1% to RMB13.12bn, while packaged-water revenue rose 2.1% to RMB9.64bn, leaving the growth mix led by non-water beverages. [HSTong]

  10. 10

    **WuXi Biologics – adjusted profit outpaces reported earnings:** WuXi Biologics 2269 HK posted first-half revenue of RMB11.79bn, up 18.4%, and attributable profit of RMB2.44bn, up 4.3%. Adjusted net profit rose 38.4% to RMB3.31bn, with pre-IND service revenue up 27.2%; no interim dividend was declared. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **China Taiping – investment result reverses to profit:** China Taiping 966 HK reported first-half attributable profit of HK$12.87bn, up 90.3%, as insurance-service earnings improved and net investment results swung to a HK$9.69bn profit. Life new-business value rose 1.4% to RMB6.27bn and new premiums increased 10.6%; no interim dividend was declared. [Hong Kong Commercial Daily]

  12. 12

    **Hormuz – Iran and Oman propose safe shipping corridor:** Iran and Oman issued a joint statement proposing a mutually agreed safe maritime corridor through the Strait of Hormuz, supported by a coastguard coordination mechanism and phased operating framework. The announcement coincided with a sharp fall in oil and is relevant to PetroChina 857 HK, CNOOC 883 HK, Sinopec 386 HK and transport names. [CCTV]

  13. 13

    **Southbound flows – HK$66.05bn net selling:** Mainland investors recorded HK$66.05bn of net southbound selling on Tuesday. Kingboard Laminates 1888 HK, CSOP Hang Seng Tech ETF and Yangtze Optical Fibre 6869 HK saw the largest net outflows, while Meituan 3690 HK, MiniMax 100 HK and WuXi Biologics led net buying with HK$8.67bn, HK$3.77bn and HK$1.94bn respectively. [STCN]

  14. 14

    **Overnight US markets – tech and China ADRs higher, oil drops:** The Dow gained 0.30%, S&P 500 0.32% and Nasdaq 0.66%; Nvidia rose 2.19%, Lumentum 6.67%, Applied Optoelectronics 5.13%, Seagate 3.40%, Western Digital 3.53% and Micron 2.48%. The Golden Dragon index gained 1.11%, with Futu +8.48%, NetEase +4.75%, Baidu +1.36%, PDD +0.78%, JD +0.55% and Bilibili +0.42%. WTI fell 4.59% to US$81.11, COMEX gold rose 0.39% to US$4,715.90, DXY slipped 0.09% to 98.915, and US two- and ten-year yields fell to 4.191% and 4.635%. [Eastmoney]
