Morning Market Memo | 02 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 01 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 02 Sep 2026 08:32 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **US equities and China ADRs:** The Dow fell 0.79%, S&P 500 0.71% and Nasdaq 1.03%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index lost 2.14%, with Nvidia down 1.51%, Micron 2.64%, Lumentum 5.01% and Tesla 3.22%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index fell 0.34%; Alibaba lost 1.03%, JD.com 1.20%, XPeng 1.67%, Bilibili 2.29% and NIO 4.02%, setting a softer read-through for HK internet, EV and AI-hardware names. [Eastmoney] [Sina]

  2. 02

    **US–Iran escalation lifts oil:** US forces began fresh strikes against Iranian Revolutionary Guard targets at noon US Eastern time after alleged attempts to attack Hormuz shipping and US personnel; explosions were reported around Bandar Abbas, Qeshm Island and other southern Iranian locations. WTI rose 5.2% to US$90.22 and Brent 4.6% to US$94.65, supporting upstream oil names while increasing fuel-cost pressure for airlines and transport companies. [Xinhua] [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **Rates, dollar and precious metals:** The US 10-year yield rose 4.6bp to 4.7961% and the two-year yield 5.2bp to 4.3936%, while the DXY gained 0.25% to 99.677. Spot gold fell 2.72% to US$4,328.36/oz and silver 3.70% to US$64.09/oz, a negative overnight read-through for precious-metals equities. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    **Li Qiang meets US business delegation:** Premier Li Qiang told the US-China Business Council that bilateral relations remain broadly stable and China is willing to expand mutually beneficial cooperation, manage differences and address reasonable concerns of US companies. He highlighted intelligent and green consumption as growth areas and called for implementation of the two countries’ trade-consultation outcomes. [Foreign Ministry]

  5. 05

    **Cathay Pacific placement:** Swire completed an accelerated exchange transaction including the sale of 362.65m Cathay Pacific shares at HK$13.20 each and the repurchase of HK$4.689bn principal amount of bonds. Swire expects an approximately HK$704m overall net gain; the sizeable placement creates a near-term supply event for Cathay shares. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **Hua Hong expands 12-inch capacity:** Hua Hong Semiconductor and investment partners will fund the Wuxi Hua Hong Phase III project, targeting monthly capacity of about 55,000 12-inch wafers for specialty-process foundry production. The investment totals RMB4.163bn and remains subject to shareholder approval, adding a fresh capacity catalyst for Hua Hong and the domestic semiconductor-equipment chain. [Eastmoney] [Sina]

  7. 07

    **Shengke Communication funds next-generation switching chips:** The company announced after the close that it plans a private placement to finance research and industrialisation of next-generation high-performance Ethernet switching chips. The project strengthens the domestic data-centre networking and AI-computing supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **BYD August sales:** BYD reported August new-energy vehicle sales of 440,293 units, up 17.8% year on year and 4.15% month on month; overseas passenger-vehicle and pickup sales reached 188,746 units, up 134.6%. The overseas contribution is approaching 40%, keeping export execution central to the stock’s near-term read-through. [Sina Auto] [Sina]

  9. 09

    **Zhongji Innolight starts buyback:** The optical-module maker repurchased 374,100 shares for RMB318m on September 1, at prices between RMB838.16 and RMB870.00. The first use of its buyback account provides a fresh capital-return signal for the optical-transceiver complex after overnight US optical stocks fell. [Sina]

  10. 10

    **Tencent buyback and southbound support:** Tencent repurchased 226,000 shares for HK$100.1m, its twelfth consecutive repurchase session since August 17. Separately, southbound investors bought a net HK$77.57bn of Hong Kong stocks, including HK$1.125bn of Tencent, while selling HK$576m of Meituan. [STCN] [STCN]

  11. 11

    **CSPC Innovation wins breakthrough designation:** Drug regulator CDE added SYS6010, an EGFR-targeted antibody-drug conjugate, to its breakthrough-therapy list for recurrent or metastatic head-and-neck squamous-cell carcinoma after immunotherapy and platinum chemotherapy. A pivotal Phase III trial is underway, providing a fresh catalyst for CSPC’s oncology pipeline. [Sina]

  12. 12

    **Huachang Chemical control change:** Huana Investment agreed to transfer a 24% stake, or 229m shares, to Suzhou Xuanli for RMB1.413bn at RMB6.18 per share. Huachang resumes trading today, with Suzhou Xuanli becoming the controlling shareholder and Cheng Renjie the ultimate controller. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Guomin Technology adds NOR Flash capability:** Guomin Technology will invest RMB60m in Nanjing Youcun at a RMB590m pre-money valuation, taking an 8.3333% stake. The target has established NOR Flash development and mass-production capabilities, extending Guomin’s external-memory product offering. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Yangguang signs server-leasing contract:** A subsidiary of Yangguang signed a five-year server-leasing services agreement with an unnamed customer carrying total fees of RMB633m including tax. The contract provides a new revenue stream tied to computing infrastructure, although customer concentration and execution will require monitoring. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Hangxiao Steel wins Nigerian refinery order:** Hangxiao Steel Structure signed a US$166m contract, approximately RMB1.128bn, covering design, manufacture and supply of core equipment for the expansion of Nigeria’s Dangote refinery. The 30-month contract equals 15.87% of its latest audited revenue and adds overseas order visibility. [Sina] [GPLP]

  16. 16

    **ChiNext index expansion:** Shenzhen Securities Information will launch the ChiNext Chip Index on September 4, adding to newly announced ChiNext industrial-machinery and AI-application indices. The expansion creates additional benchmarks for future ETF products and channels capital toward new-quality productive-force sectors. [Eastmoney]

  17. 17

    **Foreign-individual dividend tax:** The Ministry of Finance and State Taxation Administration ended the exemption for dividends paid by foreign-invested enterprises to foreign individuals, applying a 20% individual income-tax rate from September 1. Paying companies must withhold the tax, changing after-tax returns for affected foreign shareholders. [State Taxation Administration] [Eastmoney]
