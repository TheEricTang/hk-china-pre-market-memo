Morning Market Memo | 17 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 16 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 17 Sep 2026 08:54 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Federal Reserve – first hike since 2023:** The FOMC unanimously raised the federal-funds target by 25bp to 3.75%–4.00%, saying inflation remains elevated and the move should support a timelier return to 2%. Median projections imply one further increase this year, tightening the external rate backdrop for Hong Kong property, growth and highly leveraged equities. [Federal Reserve]

  2. 02

    **Hong Kong rates – HKMA follows the Fed:** The HKMA raised its Base Rate by 25bp to 4.25% before the local open, in line with the Linked Exchange Rate system’s established formula. The immediate focus is whether local banks adjust prime or deposit rates, with property developers, REITs and rate-sensitive financials most exposed. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **PBOC – more emphasis on market-based finance:** Governor Pan Gongsheng said China should coordinate direct and bank financing, develop equity and bond-market “technology boards,” expand wealth management and rely more on price-based monetary tools rather than single-channel loan targets. He also called for a broader macroprudential toolkit to maintain stability in equity, bond and currency markets. [Shanghai Securities News]

  4. 04

    **China–Iran – Beijing urges reopening of Hormuz:** Foreign Minister Wang Yi met Iranian counterpart Abbas Araghchi in Beijing and urged all parties to take effective measures to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and protect international energy transport and supply chains. The post-close statement is relevant to oil, airlines, shipping and refiners amid still-elevated freight and crude prices. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **Memory chips – reported SK Hynix/Intel US talks:** SK Hynix is reportedly considering leasing part of Intel’s Ohio facilities or forming a venture with Intel and a major cloud customer to produce memory chips in the US; SK Hynix said options are being explored but no plan has been fixed. The report keeps capacity, localisation and HBM supply in focus for the HK/A semiconductor chain. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **Overnight US markets – rates hit banks and energy:** The Dow fell 1.21%, the S&P 500 0.45% and Nasdaq 0.01%; the KBW Bank Index lost 2.88%, while Lumentum gained more than 9%, Coherent nearly 7%, and Marvell and Intel more than 3%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index fell 0.55%: Bilibili lost 3.62%, Baidu 2.04% and Alibaba 1.89%, while Trip.com rose 3.01%, Li Auto 2.30% and XPeng 1.08%. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    **Cross-assets – dollar and Treasury yields rise:** WTI’s continuous contract fell 3.60% to US$102.02 a barrel, COMEX gold declined 0.70% to US$4,302.50 an ounce and the dollar index gained 0.64% to 100.252. US two-year and ten-year yields rose 7.31bp and 2.06bp respectively to 4.7359% and 5.0225%, a mixed read-through for Chinese exporters, gold miners and oil producers. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **HK physical AI and computing orders:** 51WORLD (6651 HK) said embodied-intelligence orders on hand exceeded RMB200m, with more than 90% expected to be delivered this year. Health 160 (2656 HK) separately signed an AI-computing strategic framework with an estimated cooperation value of about RMB650m, adding fresh order catalysts for smaller HK AI names. [CLS]

  9. 09

    **HK pharmaceuticals – regulatory pipeline updates:** Hengrui Pharma (1276 HK) received clinical-trial approval for injectable recaticimab; Sino Biopharm’s (1177 HK) TQH2722 IL-4Rα antibody marketing application was accepted; and CSPC Pharma (1093 HK) began a Chinese Phase II study of diabetes candidate SYH2069. The announcements provide company-specific catalysts across the innovation-drug complex. [CLS]

  10. 10

    **Leapmotor – new domestic-share issuance cleared:** Leapmotor (9863 HK) received CSRC consent to issue new domestic shares to FAW Equity and Jinyi High-Tech. The approval advances the automaker’s strategic financing and partnership arrangements and is a fresh post-close catalyst for the EV name. [CLS]

  11. 11

    **HK buybacks – Tencent and Baidu remain active:** Tencent (700 HK) spent HK$100m repurchasing 231,000 shares at HK$432.80–438.80, while Baidu (9888 HK) bought back 565,900 shares for about HK$50m. The purchases provide continued capital-return support after Chinese technology ADRs generally weakened overnight. [CLS]

  12. 12

    **A-share computing contracts – two large updates:** Saiyi Information (300687 CH) increased the combined value of two five-year computing-service contracts from RMB6.45bn to RMB6.72bn. Kanghui Pharmaceutical (603139 CH) signed a separate five-year RMB1.72bn computing-services contract, backed by an RMB1.141bn server purchase; only about RMB30m of additional 2026 revenue is expected if delivery proceeds on schedule. [Shanghai Securities News] [Securities Times]

  13. 13

    **Shanghai Yahong – control transfers to Flyco interests:** Shareholders agreed to sell 29.99% of Shanghai Yahong (603159 CH) to Flyco Investment for approximately RMB900m, or RMB21.43 per share. Flyco Investment will become the controlling shareholder and Flyco founder Li Gaiteng the effective controller, creating a fresh restructuring catalyst. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Zijin Mining – RMB1bn AI and robotics fund commitment:** Zijin’s investment subsidiary will contribute RMB1bn to a RMB4.9bn venture fund, taking a 20.4081% subscribed interest. The fund will target AI, embodied intelligence and frontier technologies, including potential applications in exploration, smart mines, processing and operational safety. [Yicai]

  15. 15

    **Sany Heavy Industry – sizeable post-selloff repurchase:** Sany (600031 CH/6031 HK) bought 16.62m A-shares for approximately RMB299m on Wednesday at RMB17.64–18.96 per share, taking cumulative purchases to RMB333m. The disclosure followed a 6.72% A-share decline and provides a fresh capital-support signal for the construction-machinery group. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    **Mindong Electric Power – denies computing-power theme:** After six consecutive limit-up sessions, Mindong Electric Power (000993 CH) said it currently has no computing-power/electricity-coordination business. The post-close clarification directly challenges the market narrative behind the recent rally and raises opening volatility risk. [Eastmoney]
