Morning Market Memo | 28 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 25 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 28 Sep 2026 06:46 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    China–US relations – eight-point outcome: China and the US announced a framework for a “constructive strategic stability relationship,” including a US$30bn reciprocal tariff-reduction arrangement, extensions to earlier trade understandings and continued work through bilateral trade and investment councils. The two sides also agreed to establish an AI dialogue, with the next session scheduled for November. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    PBOC liquidity support starts today: The central bank will conduct overnight reverse repos from 28 September through 8 October, with a daily ceiling of RMB1tn, to manage month-end and National Day funding demand. RMB660.3bn of seven-day repos and RMB600bn of MLF funds mature today, making the initial operation size a focus for banks, brokers and property names. [Securities Daily]

  3. 03

    Today’s policy and data calendar: The National Bureau of Statistics is due to publish August industrial-profit data, while the State Council Information Office will hold a briefing on central-enterprise development under the 15th Five-Year Plan. The releases provide fresh reads for industrials, materials and state-owned enterprise reform themes. [中新经纬]

  4. 04

    Zhejiang marine-energy plan: Zhejiang issued guidance targeting more than RMB300bn of marine clean-energy and equipment output by 2030. Goals include over 10GW of offshore-wind capacity, commercial-scale tidal power, combined offshore wind and solar development, green hydrogen derivatives and at least 50 leading or specialised enterprises. [券商中国]

  5. 05

    Nvidia evaluates glass substrates: Nvidia is reportedly working with equipment suppliers including Germany’s SCHMID and has asked Asian substrate makers to complete glass-substrate development within two years, with potential adoption from 2028. The report is relevant to advanced packaging, glass-through-via equipment and PCB-material suppliers, although customer certification remains incomplete. [CLS]

  6. 06

    Optical modules – Huilv Ecological expansion: Subsidiary Junheng Technology plans to invest RMB309m in a Wuhan research and production base, adding annual capacity for two million high-speed optical modules. Together with upgraded existing lines, planned Wuhan capacity would reach 3.5 million units annually, adding another capacity signal for the domestic AI-networking chain. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Battery separators – Enjie buys out Hubei venture: Enjie’s Shanghai unit agreed to pay RMB1.15bn for EVE Energy’s 45% interest in Hubei Enjie. Shanghai Enjie will own 100% after completion, consolidating the separator asset and transferring cash to EVE amid continued restructuring across the battery supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    PCB tooling – Zhongyan Dadi acquisition: Zhongyan Dadi plans to acquire and subscribe for shares giving it 60% of Xinhuanyu, a specialised producer of PCB and FPC drill bits, coated drills and milling tools. The transaction includes RMB90m of share purchases and a RMB150m capital injection, extending the company into higher-growth electronics tooling. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Battery equipment – Jinyinhe financing: Jinyinhe proposed a private placement of up to RMB1.5bn for sodium-ion, consumer-lithium, solid-liquid and new-energy-storage battery equipment, alongside dry-electrode and solid-state-battery equipment research. The plan adds another capital-spending catalyst for next-generation battery manufacturing. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Obesity drugs – Huadong Medicine Phase III: Huadong Medicine said its oral small-molecule GLP-1 agonist HDM1002 met all primary and key secondary endpoints in a Chinese Phase III weight-management study. Weight loss continued through week 52 without a reported plateau, and the company plans to submit a Chinese marketing application after completing pre-NDA discussions. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    ADC drugs – Baili Tianheng breakthrough designations: Three Baili Tianheng programmes were added to the NMPA drug-evaluation centre’s breakthrough-therapy list, covering HER2-positive biliary cancer, Claudin18.2-positive gastric cancer and recurrent or metastatic biliary cancer. The update supports the domestic antibody-drug conjugate pipeline and Hong Kong-listed biotech peers. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Hong Kong IPO pipeline – three A-share issuers price H shares: PCB producer Jingwang Electronics priced its H-share offering at HK$69.88, robotics-equipment company Robotechnik at HK$436 and electronic-materials producer Tongcheng New Materials at HK$44. All three expect to begin Main Board trading on Tuesday, 29 September. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    A-share holiday positioning: Mainland markets have only three sessions before the National Day break. Sixty-two companies face roughly 14.94bn shares of lock-ups expiring from Monday through Wednesday, with an estimated value near RMB110bn; two new stocks also open subscriptions today. [中新经纬]

  14. 14

    Overnight US markets: The Dow rose 0.93%, S&P 500 gained 0.51% and Nasdaq added 0.48% on Friday. Qualcomm rose 3.97%, Western Digital 1.44% and SanDisk 1.38%, while Tesla fell 1.54%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon index lost 0.64%, with Lufax down nearly 9% and WeRide and VNET off more than 4%. WTI fell 2.29% to US$92.44, COMEX gold gained 0.52% to US$4,320.50 and the US 30-year yield moved above 5.5%. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    US–Iran talks and oil: President Trump said he expects further negotiations with Iran this week, potentially beginning with indirect discussions as soon as Monday, while saying renewed military action remains under consideration. Indicative oil trading fell sharply on the update; Iran is seeking relief from the maritime blockade and oil sanctions in exchange for reopening the Strait of Hormuz and restarting nuclear talks. [Eastmoney]
