Morning Market Memo | 05 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 04 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 05 Aug 2026 07:46 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Overnight US markets – tech and semis rally: The Dow rose 1.71% to 54,085.88, the S&P 500 gained 1.79% to 7,736.52 and the Nasdaq jumped 2.59% to 26,584.99; the Dow and S&P closed at records. Nvidia rose 2.6%, Broadcom 6.6% and Micron 7.6%, supporting the HK semiconductor, memory and AI-hardware complex. China ADRs were mixed, with Alibaba and PDD higher while Baidu, JD, Nio and XPeng fell; Brent dropped 5.3% to US$79.36 and the US 10-year yield eased to 4.62%. [Eastmoney] [AP]

  2. 02

    Hormuz negotiations – agreement could come within two days: US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the US and Iran may reach an agreement on August 4 or 5 to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and guarantee free passage for commercial shipping. The talks accompanied the sharp oil-price decline and are relevant for Chinese airlines, refiners, chemicals companies and shipping names. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    US data-centre restrictions – optical modules targeted: The US Federal Communications Commission is reportedly drafting a ban on imports of new Chinese optical transceiver models used for high-speed data transfer inside AI data centres. Officials aim to announce and implement the measure during 2026, although the proposal may still be changed or dropped; the headline is relevant to the HK/A-share optical-module and CPO supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Anthropic – reported US$10bn compute agreement: Anthropic reportedly agreed to purchase US$10bn of computing capacity from a recently established infrastructure company. The fresh capacity commitment reinforces demand expectations for servers, networking, liquid cooling and power equipment, partially offsetting the US regulatory headline for Chinese data-centre suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    SpaceX – first listed results show rapid growth and heavy spending: Second-quarter revenue rose 92% to US$7.8bn, above the reported US$6.93bn consensus, as Starlink revenue increased 66% and AI-related revenue grew about 250%. Capital expenditure reached US$18.4bn and the shares fell more than 8% after hours, giving a mixed read-through for satellite internet, launch equipment and space-computing suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Zhongjin Lingnan – major lead-zinc mine halted: Zhongjin Lingnan (000060 CH) said its Fankou lead-zinc mine was ordered to stop production following an underground accident that killed one person. Operations can resume only after safety checks, remediation and regulatory approval; the company cannot yet quantify the duration or earnings impact. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Wanhua Chemical – 1.1mt MDI facility enters maintenance: Wanhua Chemical (600309 CH) will shut its 1.1m-tonne-per-year Yantai MDI unit and supporting facilities from August 10 for about 45 days. The company described the shutdown as scheduled annual maintenance with no material operating impact, but the duration is relevant for regional MDI supply and pricing. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Jiangnan New Materials – placement funds liquid cooling: Jiangnan New Materials (603124 CH) plans to raise up to RMB1.6bn through a private placement. Proceeds will fund high-purity electronic-grade copper oxide powder and liquid-cooling module and component projects, adding a fresh A-share financing catalyst for AI thermal-management materials. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Meili Technology – intelligent-suspension expansion: Meili Technology (300611 CH) proposed raising up to RMB585m through a private placement. The funds will support annual capacity for two million intelligent-suspension components and ten million electrically or hydraulically driven elastic components, relevant to smart-EV chassis suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Zhongke Sanhuan – rare-earth magnet acquisition: Zhongke Sanhuan (000970 CH) signed an initial agreement to acquire control of Ningbo Zhongdian Cisheng Electronics in cash. The target is one of the limited domestic manufacturers with large-scale consumer-electronics magnetic-component assembly capability, extending Zhongke Sanhuan’s rare-earth permanent-magnet exposure downstream. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Zhidongli – RMB300m optical and thermal-material project: Zhidongli (300686 CH) signed an investment agreement covering materials for optical communications, computing thermal management and ITO applications, plus indium, tin, gallium and bismuth products and recycling. The approximately RMB300m project will be implemented in two phases through a newly established subsidiary in Yunnan. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Industrial orders – ships and energy storage: Dajin Heavy Industry (002487 CH) signed contracts worth about RMB1bn to build two 211,000-DWT bulk carriers for a Norwegian shipowner, with delivery scheduled in 2029. Separately, Zhiguang Electric (002169 CH) secured a RMB217m contract to supply high-voltage cascaded energy-storage systems. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    AI connectors and subsea cables – interim earnings: Dingtong Technology (688668 CH) reported first-half revenue up 31.17% and net profit up 59.24% to RMB184m, citing strong communications-connector demand, higher orders and new-product production. Oriental Cable (603606 CH) reported revenue up 30.85% and net profit up 19.41% to RMB565m as higher-value subsea and high-voltage cable sales increased. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Jiangsu Bank – profit growth with lower bad-loan ratio: Jiangsu Bank (600919 CH) reported first-half revenue of RMB48.95bn, up 9.11%, and net profit of RMB21.88bn, up 8.09%. Its non-performing-loan ratio fell three basis points from year-end to a post-listing low of 0.81%, while the provision-coverage ratio stood at 304.83%. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    A-share buybacks – new programmes and bank financing: Huiyun Titanium and ZKTeco proposed buybacks of RMB20m–30m and RMB30m–60m respectively, while several companies obtained dedicated bank facilities, including up to RMB144m for Jutze Intelligence and RMB90m each for Invengo and Huayuan Holdings. The cluster extends the use of stock-repurchase lending support among smaller listed companies. [Eastmoney]
