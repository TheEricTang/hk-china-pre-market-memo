Morning Market Memo | 31 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 28 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 31 Aug 2026 08:51 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Iran–US escalation:** Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said early Monday that it fired missiles at a US base following US strikes on military facilities on Larak Island; separate reports cited launches toward US vessels around the Strait of Hormuz. Oil moved higher and US equity futures weakened in early Asian trading, putting China oil producers, airlines and shipping names in focus. [Sina] [HSTong]

  2. 02

    **Property policy package:** Housing authorities now require presold residential buildings to have completed their main structure, with down payments and mortgage proceeds deposited into supervised accounts; new projects will be encouraged to adopt completed-home sales. Separate financial rules extend maximum personal mortgage terms to 40 years and introduce a project-centred main-bank system for development lending, supporting developers, banks and construction contractors. [Xinhua] [NFRA] [Suzhou Government]

  3. 03

    **Property capital-market support:** The CSRC issued 12 measures supporting listed developers’ refinancing and acquisitions using shares, directional convertible bonds and cash, alongside company bonds, commercial mortgage-backed securities and property ABS. The framework says reasonable financing needs should be met regardless of ownership type, broadening funding options for private and state-owned developers. [CSRC]

  4. 04

    **Restricted-share tax rules tightened:** The Ministry of Finance, State Taxation Administration and CSRC retained the 20% individual-income-tax rate on gains from restricted shares but expanded coverage to bonus and transferred shares generated after lock-up expiry. Taxpayers must complete final reconciliation by June 30 of the following year, while the previous deemed-cost treatment is being tightened. [State Taxation Administration] [Sina]

  5. 05

    **Token economy and data labelling:** National Data Administration chief Liu Liehong said tokens are becoming a pricing and settlement unit linking data, models and computing power, and called for work on commercial models, industry applications and supporting rules. The administration will also deploy a second round of data-labelling pilots in 32 cities, taking the total to 39 and benefiting AI-data, cloud and model-service providers. [National Data Administration] [Xinhua]

  6. 06

    **ChangXin LPDDR6 enters mass production:** ChangXin Memory said its LPDDR6 memory has entered mass production and will debut in Xiaomi’s 18 Fold, due in September; Xiaomi’s in-house Xring O3 processor will be the first chip supporting the product. The device offers peak speed of 12,800Mbps and capacity of up to 16GB, a fresh catalyst for ChangXin Technology 688825 CH, Xiaomi 1810 HK and the domestic memory chain. [Securities Times] [The Paper]

  7. 07

    **Liquid-cooling orders remain strong:** CCTV reported that domestic liquid-cooling server and component producers are operating at high utilisation, with one CDU production line booked through December and a Guangdong supplier’s orders, output and deliveries all nearly doubling year on year. The report supports AI-server cooling, pumps, heat exchangers and data-centre infrastructure suppliers. [CCTV]

  8. 08

    **BYD first-half profit falls:** BYD 1211 HK/002594 CH reported revenue of RMB344.82bn, down 7.13%, and attributable profit of RMB12.33bn, down 20.5%. Overseas exports rose 67.8% to 792,000 vehicles and R&D spending reached about RMB28.9bn, leaving overseas growth as the main offset to weaker group earnings. [First Financial] [NBD]

  9. 09

    **AI servers and chip packaging:** Inspur Information 000977 CH posted first-half revenue of RMB84.38bn, up 5.22%, and profit of RMB2.95bn, up 269.59%; second-quarter profit reached RMB2.35bn. Tongfu Microelectronics 002156 CH reported revenue up 23.03% to RMB16.04bn and profit up 316.77% to RMB1.72bn, citing AI computing, storage, automotive and domestic-customer demand. [First Financial] [Securities Times]

  10. 10

    **Optical-chip earnings:** Yuanjie Technology 688498 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB925mn, up 351.4%, and attributable profit of RMB607mn, up 1,212.2%, with operating cash flow rising to RMB488mn. The company proposed a RMB10 cash dividend for every ten shares, reinforcing the earnings read-through for optical chips and data-centre connectivity. [ITHome] [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **BOE profit recovery:** BOE Technology 000725 CH recorded first-half revenue of RMB103.13bn, up 1.83%, while attributable profit rose 61.62% to RMB5.25bn. Second-quarter profit more than doubled sequentially to roughly RMB3.54bn, supporting the display-panel and consumer-electronics supply chain. [ITHome] [Sina]

  12. 12

    **Youyan Silicon plans semiconductor consolidation:** Youyan Silicon 688432 CH plans to acquire 71.89% of Shandong GRINM Aisi and 14.98% of Shandong GRINM Semiconductor through shares and cash, making both wholly owned subsidiaries. The transaction constitutes a major and connected restructuring; the stock is suspended from today for no more than five trading days. [Securities Times] [Sina]

  13. 13

    **Tencent buyback:** Tencent 700 HK repurchased 659,000 shares on Friday for HK$300.28mn at HK$443.80–461.60 per share, with the shares intended for cancellation. Purchases under the current mandate have reached 42.79mn shares, or about 0.47% of the share count when the mandate was approved. [NBD] [HKEX filing summary]

  14. 14

    **Xi Jinping attends SCO summit:** President Xi arrived in Bishkek on Sunday for the 2026 Shanghai Cooperation Organisation summit and a state visit to Kyrgyzstan, followed by a visit to Egypt. Regional transport, energy and digital-infrastructure agreements will be monitored, but no major commercial package had been announced by the cutoff. [Foreign Ministry] [Xinhua]

  15. 15

    **US markets and cross-assets:** Friday’s Dow slipped 0.02%, the S&P 500 lost 0.25% and the Nasdaq fell 0.52%; the SOX dropped 3.47%, with Nvidia down 4.57%, Marvell 10.28% and Intel 2.85%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon index gained 0.44%, while COMEX gold fell 3.43% to US$4,504.10/oz, spot silver lost 4.16% and WTI settled 0.11% lower at US$83.44 before the weekend’s Middle East escalation. [Kiplinger] [Nasdaq] [Eastmoney]
