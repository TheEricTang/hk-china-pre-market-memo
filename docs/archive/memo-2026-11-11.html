Morning Market Memo | 11 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 10 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 11 Nov 2026 06:43 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **US inflation – December tightening priced:** October headline CPI rose 0.4% month on month versus 0.3% expected, while core CPI increased 0.3% versus 0.2% expected; year-on-year figures were unavailable because of the October 2025 government shutdown data gap. Fed Governor Christopher Waller said another hot reading could justify a 50bp December hike, pushing Treasury yields higher and reinforcing rate pressure on HK growth stocks. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **Overnight US markets – tech and China ADRs lower:** The Dow fell 0.30%, S&P 500 0.38% and Nasdaq 0.74%; Nvidia lost 5.47%, Microsoft 2.20%, SK Hynix 3.51% and SanDisk 2.99%, although Broadcom gained 3.91% and TSMC 2.02%. The Golden Dragon index fell 1.21%, with Alibaba down 2.52%, Baidu 1.63% and PDD 1.37%; WTI slipped 0.64% to US$86.29, COMEX gold fell 1.84% to US$4,038.80 and spot gold lost 2.66%. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **Alibaba – T-Head spin-off moves forward:** Alibaba 9988 HK said HKEX has confirmed it may proceed with a separate Main Board listing of wholly owned data-centre chip unit T-Head, which confidentially filed its A1 application on 11 September. The proposed global H-share offering would leave T-Head as an Alibaba subsidiary, creating a potential standalone valuation marker for China’s full-stack data-centre chip sector. [HKEX]

  4. 04

    **Zhipu – up to HK$4bn H-share buyback:** Zhipu 2513 HK proposed open-market repurchases of up to HK$4bn, capped at 1% of total issued shares, under the mandate granted in June. Execution remains subject to market conditions and the company retaining sufficient resources for operations and growth. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **Hesai – lidar shipments and robotics modules scale:** Hesai 2525 HK reported third-quarter revenue of RMB1.068bn, up about 34% year on year, and net income of RMB80.8m. Lidar shipments reached 729,673 units, while robotic actuation-module shipments rose 97% sequentially to 23,405, supporting the physical-AI and autonomous-driving supply-chain read-through. [Hesai]

  6. 06

    **Shanghai marine economy – equipment and data infrastructure:** Shanghai’s new five-year implementation plan calls for faster development of marine sensors, intelligent robots, submersibles, satellite communications and underwater navigation. The city will also study a marine-data trading platform, encourage marine vertical AI models and promote large-scale offshore wind equipment, relevant to marine engineering, robotics and renewable-energy suppliers. [Securities Times]

  7. 07

    **CGN Power – Cangnan Unit 4 construction starts:** CGN Power 1816 HK/003816 CH poured first concrete for Cangnan Unit 4, marking the start of full construction and civil works. The Hualong One reactor has 1,215MW of capacity, adding a fresh project milestone for domestic nuclear equipment and engineering contractors. [Yicai]

  8. 08

    **Huali Group – up to US$500m optical investment:** Huali Group 300979 CH plans to invest as much as US$500m for no more than 25% of O-Net Technologies, whose businesses develop and manufacture optical-communications products globally. The footwear manufacturer described the deal as a financial investment using idle internal funds, giving it exposure to the AI optical-connectivity cycle. [NBD]

  9. 09

    **Rongqi Technology – semiconductor-parts acquisition:** Rongqi 301360 CH agreed to pay RMB562m for 60% of Deqishun, a supplier of precision polymer and metal parts used in CMP, wet-cleaning and electroplating equipment. The target has passed first-article validation with a leading semiconductor-equipment customer; part of the consideration will be funded with RMB486m of remaining IPO proceeds. [CLS]

  10. 10

    **Qingdao Port – consolidating ore-terminal assets:** Qingdao Port 601298 CH/6198 HK plans to acquire the remaining 70% of Dongjiakou Ore Terminal through a public bid with a floor price of RMB1.918bn. The target operates four large ore berths with more than 100m tonnes of annual handling capacity, giving Qingdao Port full control of a core dry-bulk asset. [NBD]

  11. 11

    **Qingdao Foods – Tsingtao group to take control:** Qingdao Foods 001219 CH’s current parent agreed to transfer a 29.24% stake to Tsingtao Brewery Group for RMB965m, or RMB16.9246 per share. Tsingtao Brewery Group will become the controlling shareholder, with the transaction positioned as municipal state-asset consolidation and food-and-beverage supply-chain coordination. [NBD]

  12. 12

    **CTF Services – RMB1.98bn toll-road disposal:** CTF Services 659 HK agreed after the close to sell three mainland toll-road interests for aggregate cash consideration of about RMB1.979bn, including 100% of Wanjue and 25% stakes in the Jingzhu and Yue-Zhao roads. The transaction extends the group’s capital recycling from mature infrastructure assets. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **China Suntien Green Energy – power up, gas down:** China Suntien 956 HK/600956 CH generated 1.60m MWh in October, up 68.22% year on year, led by a 68.09% increase in wind generation. Gas transmission and sales fell 38.12% to 245m cubic metres, leaving a sharply divergent read-through across its renewable-power and natural-gas businesses. [Sina]

  14. 14

    **Gemdale – October sales decline narrowed:** Gemdale 600383 CH reported October contracted sales of RMB1.75bn, down 8.85% year on year, while contracted area fell 40.22% to 110,000 square metres. January–October sales remained down 41.19% at RMB15.35bn, keeping the focus on property-sector volume and cash collection. [Jiemian]

  15. 15

    **Southbound flows – net selling but Alibaba accumulated:** Southbound investors sold a net HK$2.739bn on Tuesday, while active-stock data showed net purchases of HK$868m in Alibaba, HK$216m in SMIC and HK$206m in Hua Hong. Tencent saw HK$614m of net selling, indicating continued rotation within the internet and semiconductor complexes. [Securities Times]

  16. 16

    **A-share capital returns – new buyback plans:** Dier Laser 300776 CH proposed an RMB50m–100m buyback for employee incentives, Chengda Biotechnology 688739 CH proposed RMB25m–50m for cancellation, and Jereh 002353 CH proposed RMB100m–200m for incentives. Bosi Software 300525 CH separately announced an RMB50m–100m value-support buyback. [Eastmoney]
