Morning Market Memo | 02 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 30 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 02 Nov 2026 06:38 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Capital-market support tools: Media reports say the PBOC and CSRC will meet securities and fund firms at around 09:00 HKT today to discuss capital-market support tools. The PBOC, NFRA and CSRC will separately meet banks on optimizing stock-repurchase and shareholding-increase relending; no meeting outcome was available at cutoff. [21st Century Business Herald]

  2. 02

    PBOC policy-rate anchor shifts overnight: From today, the central bank will conduct overnight reverse repos every working day through fixed-rate, quantity tenders, with the current rate at 1.25%. Seven-day operations become fixed-quantity, multi-price tenders mainly used around tax periods and month-end, making the overnight rate the clearer short-end policy anchor for banks and bonds. [PBOC via Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    China PMI falls back below 50: October’s official manufacturing PMI dropped 0.8 points to 49.3, with production at 49.8 and new orders at 49.4. Large manufacturers remained marginally expansionary at 50.2, while medium and small firms fell to 48.4 and 47.5; non-manufacturing activity also slipped to 49.7. [NBS]

  4. 04

    Consumption infrastructure policy: Five ministries issued 20 measures covering commercial-facility upgrades, community retail, tourism, sport, elderly care, childcare, charging facilities, cold-chain logistics and digitalized shopping venues. The plan allows policy-based financial tools, REITs, bank lending and private capital to support projects, relevant to retail property, logistics, equipment and service-consumption names. [NDRC] [Securities Times]

  5. 05

    Apple–Alibaba AI partnership: Apple confirmed that Alibaba’s Qwen will be integrated into Siri, writing tools and visual intelligence for Chinese users. Developer beta testing starts on 4 November, with the formal China release scheduled for iOS 27.3 in December, providing a fresh catalyst for Alibaba 9988 HK and Apple’s China ecosystem. [Apple] [Economic Observer]

  6. 06

    DeepSeek’s Super-agent architecture: DeepSeek Harness disclosed a dual-layer Router-and-Worker system designed to run multiple tasks continuously through event-driven, asynchronous workflows. The team said an internal instance is already handling problem reproduction, code reviews and system iteration, keeping domestic AI agents, software and cloud infrastructure in focus. [IT Home]

  7. 07

    Nvidia’s next-generation optical interconnect: Nvidia displayed an Optics-on-Interposer prototype that places optical engines directly on the silicon interposer carrying the GPU, bypassing the organic package substrate. The OOI design remains at the research stage with no commercialization timetable, but is relevant to the A/H-share CPO and optical-component complex. [CLS] [Sina]

  8. 08

    October EV deliveries diverge: Leapmotor 9863 HK delivered 106,100 vehicles, up 51% year on year, while XPeng 9868 HK reached a record 50,200, up 20%, including 13,300 overseas deliveries. Xiaomi exceeded 50,000, but NIO fell 15.2% to 34,200 and Harmony Intelligent Mobility dropped 50.8% to 33,500, sharpening the read-through across HK-listed EV makers. [The Paper via Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Huada Jiutian EDA acquisition: Huada Jiutian 301269 CH plans to pay up to RMB220 million for control of Qijie Technology, with the proposed agreement covering 50.38% for RMB219 million. The target develops digital-logic optimization EDA tools used by nearly 100 chip-design companies but remained loss-making through September. [National Business Daily]

  10. 10

    Aojing Medical faces CSRC investigation: Aojing Medical 688613 CH said it was placed under investigation for suspected false financial information in periodic reports. The company had already missed its third-quarter reporting deadline, and warned that a finding of major illegality could trigger mandatory delisting provisions. [Shenzhen Economic Daily]

  11. 11

    Hengtong Optic-Electric buyback proposal: Chairman Cui Wei proposed a RMB100 million–RMB200 million share repurchase, funded with internal or raised capital and priced at no more than 150% of the preceding 30-session average. Shares would be used for employee incentives, an employee ownership plan or cancellation, providing a fresh signal for the optical-fibre and subsea-cable leader. [China Fund News]

  12. 12

    China Hongqiao extends buybacks: China Hongqiao 1378 HK disclosed after Friday’s close that it bought 17.79 million shares on 29 October for approximately HK$354.4 million. Repurchases from 23–29 October totalled 85.22 million shares, reinforcing capital-return support for the aluminium major. [Stockstar] [Tiger Brokers]

  13. 13

    A-share earnings scorecard: The CSRC said 5,224 Shanghai- and Shenzhen-listed companies generated RMB56.6 trillion of revenue and RMB5.1 trillion of net profit in the first nine months, up 5.8% and 8.5% respectively. Seventy-three percent were profitable, with technology and advanced-manufacturing cohorts leading earnings growth. [CCTV]

  14. 14

    Property remains volume-led but price-soft: October new-home prices across 100 tracked cities rose 0.18% month on month, while second-hand prices fell 0.61%. Second-hand transactions in 20 major cities increased 20% year on year; Shanghai recorded more than 24,000 deals, up 32%, although new-home volumes remained weaker. [Securities Daily]

  15. 15

    OPEC+ holds December supply targets: Saudi Arabia, Russia and five other core producers agreed to maintain September’s required production levels through December. The group will meet again on 29 November, keeping supply policy steady after Friday’s oil-price rise and amid continuing Middle East disruption. [OPEC] [Xinhua]

  16. 16

    Overnight US equities: The Dow fell 1.09%, S&P 500 1.82% and Nasdaq 2.86%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 7.2%. Intel plunged 28.58%, Qualcomm 11.12%, AMD 10.88%, Nvidia 4.22% and TSMC 5.95%; the Golden Dragon Index fell 0.27%, with Baidu down 2.86% and NIO down 3.31%, pointing to pressure on HK semis, optics and EVs. [CLS]

  17. 17

    Cross-assets: Spot gold fell 3.63% to US$3,875.55 an ounce and silver lost 5.79% to US$49.15, while December WTI rose 2.08% to US$104.12 and Brent gained 2.4% to US$117.64. The DXY eased 0.16% to 101.878; US 10-year and two-year yields ended at 4.9289% and 4.5876%, while onshore and offshore yuan stood at 6.6557 and 6.6505. [Eastmoney] [RTHK]
