Morning Market Memo | 30 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 29 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 30 Sep 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow fell 0.26%, S&P 500 lost 0.17% and Nasdaq slipped 0.09%. Nvidia fell 0.72%, Tesla 1.29% and Apple 2.66%, while Meta rose 3.26%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon index dropped 1.55% and Alibaba ADR lost 0.91%. The US 10-year yield reached 5.293%, WTI fell 3.48% to US$89.38, Brent declined 2.56% to US$102.59, spot gold rose 1.35% to US$4,170.54 and the DXY gained 0.17% to 101.37. [HK01]

  2. 02

    **US demand indicators softened:** August job openings fell to 7.08m from a revised 7.34m, while September consumer confidence dropped 6.7 points to 81.9, its lowest level since 2014. The data add a weaker US consumer and employment read-through for Chinese exporters even as elevated Treasury yields remain the main valuation constraint. [AP] [AP]

  3. 03

    **China–Japan business ties:** Vice Premier He Lifeng told a visiting Japan International Trade Promotion Association delegation that China continues to welcome Japanese companies and wants deeper bilateral economic cooperation. The message is relevant to Japanese-invested manufacturing, automation, autos and consumer supply chains in China. [Xinhua]

  4. 04

    **Tencent buyback:** Tencent (700 HK) spent HK$100.63m repurchasing 232,000 shares on Tuesday. The post-close filing extends the company’s regular shareholder-return programme and provides a modest support signal for the internet complex. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **Wangsu invests in video-generation AI:** Wangsu Science & Technology (300017 CH) will invest RMB300m in Sand.ai’s A+ preferred shares, giving it a 4.3963% stake in the video-model developer. The deal expands Wangsu’s exposure from content delivery and edge infrastructure into generative-video applications. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **Xingyun lands compute-server orders:** Xingyun Technology (300209 CH) disclosed a roughly RMB573m server sale through its Singapore subsidiary and a separate RMB239m domestic server contract. Customer names were abbreviated, but the combined orders are a fresh revenue catalyst for the AI-computing hardware chain. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    **Kesi AI-server contract:** Kesi Technology (688788 CH) signed a RMB160m contract to supply high-compute servers carrying its proprietary AI-model software. The hardware uses an unnamed original vendor’s computing products, with Kesi responsible for software deployment and system integration. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **Huazhijie enters semiconductor testing:** Huazhijie (603400 CH) plans to acquire 83.5052% of Geliming through shares and cash, with the transaction price still to be determined; trading resumes today. The target makes supporting products for chip testing, giving Huazhijie direct exposure to semiconductor-test interface localisation. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Changyingtong fibre order:** Changyingtong (688143 CH) received a provisional RMB147m tax-inclusive order from one customer for polarization-maintaining optical fibre. The order is a material fresh catalyst for the domestic specialty-fibre and optical-component chain. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **Dongyangguang buyback proposal:** Dongyangguang’s (600673 CH) controlling shareholder proposed a company share repurchase of RMB600m–RMB1.2bn using internal or raised funds. The proposal is among the larger post-close A-share shareholder-return announcements. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **BOMESC wins FPSO work:** BOMESC Offshore Engineering’s (603727 CH) subsidiary signed SBM for construction of topside modules for an FPSO destined for South America. The contract is valued at roughly RMB1.6bn–RMB2.1bn and is scheduled for completion in the second half of 2028, supporting offshore-equipment order visibility. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Anning titanium project starts trial production:** Anning Iron and Titanium (002978 CH) said its 60,000-tonne-per-year energy-grade titanium and alloy project produced its first approximately 13-tonne batch of sponge titanium. Product quality met design requirements, although the company cautioned that full ramp-up will take time. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Sichuan Development Lomon lithium licence:** The company’s controlled subsidiary completed renewal of the exploration licence for the Simancuogou spodumene project in Sichuan. The licence now runs to 29 March 2031, preserving a longer-dated upstream lithium option for Sichuan Development Lomon (002312 CH). [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Shaangu equipment R&D investment:** Shaangu Power (601369 CH) plans to invest up to RMB673m in a high-end equipment innovation centre. The project will consolidate research, testing, process control, prototype validation and commercial incubation, with relevance to industrial machinery and localisation. [Eastmoney]
