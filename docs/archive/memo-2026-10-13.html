Morning Market Memo | 13 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 12 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 13 Oct 2026 08:05 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **NEV exports – tax rebate cut:** The Finance Ministry and State Taxation Administration will lower the VAT export-rebate rate for new-energy vehicles from 13% to 9%, effective 1 April 2027. The change raises the cost of exporting China-built vehicles and favours manufacturers with overseas production, stronger brands or greater pricing power; export rebates for specified e-cigarette equipment will be cancelled on the same date. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    **He Lifeng meets Apple’s Tim Cook:** Vice Premier He Lifeng welcomed technology companies including Apple to deepen mutually beneficial cooperation in China and said a stable China–US relationship would improve expectations for multinational companies. Cook said Apple remained confident in its long-term China development and would support stable bilateral economic relations, relevant to Apple suppliers and the consumer-electronics chain. [Xinhua]

  3. 03

    **Goertek ends Hong Kong spin-off:** Goertek 002241 CH terminated the proposed separation and Hong Kong main-board listing of subsidiary Goermicro. The decision removes a prospective HK IPO from the pipeline and keeps the MEMS and smart-hardware subsidiary within the Shenzhen-listed group. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    **CICC consolidation enters final stage:** The Shanghai Stock Exchange approved the delisting of Dongxing Securities and Cinda Securities, which will leave the market on 19 October without a delisting-board period. Their A shares will subsequently be converted into CICC shares under the previously approved absorption merger, advancing the creation of the enlarged CICC platform. [China Securities Journal]

  5. 05

    **Seres buyback approaches the minimum target:** Seres 601127 CH had repurchased 17.54 million shares, or about 1.01% of its capital, for RMB983 million through 12 October. The cancellation-based programme has an authorised size of RMB1 billion–2 billion, leaving the company close to completing its minimum commitment. [People’s Financial News]

  6. 06

    **Hongxin Electronics signs AI-chip purchases:** A wholly owned subsidiary of Hongxin Electronics 300657 CH signed two high-performance server-computing chip procurement contracts worth RMB2.131 billion and RMB852 million including tax, respectively. The RMB2.983 billion combined commitment is intended to support AI-server supply and edge-AI demand, but represents a sizeable execution and working-capital requirement. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney Breakfast]

  7. 07

    **Semiconductor equipment – Microguide Nano financing:** Microguide Nano 688147 CH proposed a private placement of up to 139 million shares to raise no more than RMB4.944 billion. Proceeds are earmarked for semiconductor-deposition equipment capacity in Wuxi and central China, an advanced thin-film technology R&D centre and working capital. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **Electronic gases – Guoen acquisition:** Guoen 002768 CH agreed to pay RMB1.504 billion for 80% of Sichuan Zhongfuneng. The target produces high-purity sulphur hexafluoride, carbon tetrafluoride and nitrogen trifluoride, giving Guoen entry into electronic specialty gases used by semiconductor and advanced-manufacturing customers. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Optics earnings and orders:** Lianxun Instruments 688808 CH forecast nine-month net profit of RMB1.9 billion–2.1 billion as AI data-centre construction drove demand for optical-module and optical-chip testing equipment. Yuanjie Technology 688498 CH guided third-quarter profit to RMB403 million–453 million, while JPT 688025 CH disclosed about US$163 million of year-to-date orders from a North American fibre-connectivity customer. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney Breakfast]

  10. 10

    **Rare earths – Northern Rare Earth profit:** Northern Rare Earth 600111 CH expects nine-month net profit of RMB3.62 billion–3.68 billion, up roughly 135%–139%. The company cited constrained raw-material supply and demand from new-energy and high-end equipment markets, providing a fresh earnings reference for the broader rare-earth and magnetic-material chains. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Media speculation – Xinhua Media halted:** Xinhua Media 600825 CH will be suspended from 13 October while it investigates abnormal trading after ten consecutive limit-up sessions. The shares gained 159.5% between 21 September and 12 October; the company warned that the price had seriously diverged from fundamentals and could fall rapidly. [Eastmoney Breakfast]

  12. 12

    **Nvidia opens Vera Rubin infrastructure designs:** Nvidia disclosed key Vera Rubin rack and compute-tray designs at the Open Compute Project summit and promoted shared specifications for 800V power, cooling, layout and grid resilience. Nvidia and Cisco also announced the USFP pluggable optical-module format, targeting four times the bandwidth density through 400G lanes and twice the channel count—relevant to HK/A-share power, liquid-cooling and optical-component suppliers. [Eastmoney Breakfast]

  13. 13

    **Microsoft introduces Maia 300:** Microsoft unveiled its Azure Maia 300 AI accelerator, scheduled for deployment from 2027 as it reduces reliance on third-party chips. Each cabinet is planned to carry 96 chips, twice the Maia 200 configuration, with Microsoft claiming up to seven times the token throughput; the announcement adds another custom-silicon platform for server, networking and power suppliers. [Eastmoney Breakfast]

  14. 14

    **Overnight US markets and cross-assets:** The Dow rose 0.38%, while the S&P 500 slipped 0.06% and Nasdaq fell 0.44%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 2.67%. Micron lost 3.88%, SanDisk 5.97% and Lumentum 9.78%, while Nvidia declined 1.29%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index fell 0.38%, with Alibaba down 0.53%, JD 1.37%, XPeng 2.42% and NIO 3.07%. WTI gained 1.43% to US$93.16, COMEX gold fell 0.48% to US$4,195.90 and the DXY rose 0.37% to 102.590, pointing to a softer opening read-through for HK semis, optics, storage and EV names but firmer support for oil producers. [Eastmoney]
