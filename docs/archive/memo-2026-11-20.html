Morning Market Memo | 20 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 19 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 20 Nov 2026 07:48 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    China–Russia–US summit: Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump held their first trilateral leaders’ meeting from about 16:25 HKT in Shenzhen. The three governments pledged regular dialogue and cooperation on strategic stability, AI governance and energy security; their joint declaration included plans to increase refined-oil production and supply. [Xinhua]

  2. 02

    APEC Shenzhen outcome: Leaders issued the post-close “Shenzhen Outlook”, supporting regional economic integration, trade digitalisation, supply-chain resilience, cross-border data cooperation and responsible AI. The document provides policy support for Chinese logistics, digital-commerce, cloud and AI companies expanding across Asia-Pacific markets. [MFA]

  3. 03

    LPR due at 09:00 HKT: China’s November loan prime rates will be released before the Hong Kong open. Consensus expects the one-year and five-year-plus rates to remain at 3.00% and 3.50%, respectively; any change would immediately affect banks, developers and consumer finance names. [Investing.com] [Investing.com]

  4. 04

    Alibaba results and domestic chips: Alibaba 9988 HK reported fiscal second-quarter revenue of RMB271.52bn, up 10%, while adjusted net profit rose 92% to RMB19.91bn but missed the RMB26.61bn estimate cited by local media. Alibaba Cloud’s external commercial revenue increased 64%, and CEO Eddie Wu said the next-generation T-Head chip should support large-scale model training from the first quarter of 2027. [NBD] [Alibaba]

  5. 05

    PDD earnings: PDD reported third-quarter revenue of RMB108.3bn, broadly flat year on year, and attributable net income of RMB29.5bn versus RMB29.3bn previously. Marketing-services revenue grew 12% while transaction-services revenue fell 12%; the ADR closed 11.04% higher after profit beat market expectations. [PDD] [CNMO]

  6. 06

    NetEase earnings: NetEase 9999 HK posted third-quarter revenue of RMB27.9bn, down 1.8%, with games and related services revenue falling 1.7% to RMB22.9bn—its first year-on-year gaming decline in two years. Non-GAAP attributable profit was RMB9.2bn; the ADR dropped 11.39% overnight, setting a weak read-through for the H-share. [NetEase] [Beijing News]

  7. 07

    ChangXin Memory buyback: ChangXin Memory 688825 CH approved an enlarged A-share repurchase programme of RMB2.5bn–5.0bn, with a price ceiling of RMB63.78 per share. Shares may be used for incentives, employee ownership, cancellation or market-value support, providing a fresh catalyst for the domestic memory-chip complex. [SSE]

  8. 08

    Zijin Mining buyback: Zijin Mining 601899 CH/2899 HK plans to repurchase RMB2bn–3bn of A-shares at no more than RMB35 each over 12 months. The shares will primarily support employee incentives or be cancelled if unused, offering company-specific support as overseas precious-metal equities weakened. [Yicai]

  9. 09

    CATL continues cancellation buyback: CATL 300750 CH/3750 HK spent RMB199.98m buying 850,473 A-shares at RMB233.39–237 on Thursday, with all shares designated for cancellation. The purchase advances its RMB20bn–40bn programme, under which RMB5.47bn had been deployed through November 12. [Sina]

  10. 10

    Ganfeng Lithium launches buyback: Ganfeng Lithium 002460 CH/1772 HK approved a three-month A-share repurchase of RMB200m–400m, capped at RMB57.47 per share. The company cited value and shareholder-interest protection after its November 6 closing price fell below half its one-year high. [Yicai] [CNInfo]

  11. 11

    Hong Kong land award: Sino Land 0083 HK and Great Eagle 0041 HK won the URA’s To Kwa Wan Road/Wing Kwong Street development for HK$2.688bn through a 75:25 joint venture. The mixed residential-commercial project carries an estimated HK$5.87bn total capital commitment and maximum gross floor area of 49,275 square metres. [URA] [Stockstar]

  12. 12

    Hong Kong buybacks: Post-close filings showed Xiaomi 1810 HK repurchased 1.752m shares for about HK$50.0m and Baidu 9888 HK bought 450,200 shares for HK$30.0m. Chery Auto 9973 HK separately spent about HK$38.3m repurchasing 1.74m shares, extending broad corporate support across internet, hardware and autos. [AAStocks] [Stockstar]

  13. 13

    Southbound flows: Mainland investors bought a net HK$3.96bn of Hong Kong shares Thursday despite the Hang Seng Index’s 1.95% decline. Tencent attracted HK$3.84bn, SMIC HK$1.22bn and Meituan HK$650m, while Alibaba recorded a HK$457m net outflow. [CLS]

  14. 14

    Overnight US markets: The Dow fell 2.19%, the S&P 500 2.02% and the Nasdaq 2.25%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index lost 2.41%. Nvidia fell 3.3% and AMD 7.72%, while Western Digital and Seagate gained 2.98% and 1.06%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index declined 1.86%. [CLS]

  15. 15

    Cross-assets: COMEX gold fell 2.16% to US$3,660.90/oz and silver lost 2.85% to US$43.78, negative for HK-listed precious-metal miners. WTI rose 1.02% to US$83.15/bbl, the dollar index edged up to 103.895, and US two- and ten-year yields eased to 4.768% and 5.046%, respectively. [Eastmoney]
