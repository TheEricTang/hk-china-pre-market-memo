Morning Market Memo | 07 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 04 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 07 Sep 2026 08:21 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Central financial firms – RMB360bn capital injection:** Eight state financial institutions announced capital-raising plans on Sunday. ICBC and Agricultural Bank of China will raise up to RMB100bn and RMB160bn respectively through targeted A-share issues, while PICC, China Life, China Taiping, China Re and two policy institutions will also receive capital; the Finance Ministry will fund RMB300bn through special government bonds. [Xinhua] [People’s Daily]

  2. 02

    **PBOC – RMB500bn outright reverse repo today:** The central bank will conduct a three-month, 89-day outright reverse-repurchase operation on Monday to maintain ample banking-system liquidity. The operation matches RMB500bn maturing this month, leaving the outstanding balance unchanged. [People’s Daily] [Yicai]

  3. 03

    **Private funds – first comprehensive fundraising rule proposed:** The CSRC issued a seven-chapter, 45-article consultation draft covering qualified-investor tests, fundraising accounts, risk disclosures and manager or distributor responsibilities. It also bans guaranteed-return claims, misleading promotion and fundraising through unqualified intermediaries, raising compliance requirements across the private-fund distribution chain. [CSRC] [21st Century Business Herald]

  4. 04

    **Data centres – seven ministries set green-computing roadmap:** The Cyberspace Administration and six other agencies released a 2026–30 digital-green transition plan after Friday’s close. Priorities include liquid cooling, waste-heat recovery, low-power chips, 800-volt direct-current architecture, higher-density server racks and increased renewable-power use by computing facilities, relevant to data-centre cooling, power equipment and grid suppliers. [CAC]

  5. 05

    **Baidu – southbound trading begins today:** Baidu’s 9888 HK shares enter Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect on 7 September following its conversion to a dual-primary Hong Kong listing. The adjustment gives eligible mainland investors direct access to the shares and adds a fresh southbound-liquidity channel. [Baidu] [SSE]

  6. 06

    **Bilibili – US$700m convertible and Tencent transactions:** Bilibili 9626 HK priced zero-coupon convertible notes due 2031 at an initial conversion price of HK$155.79, a 28.3% premium to Friday’s close; Tencent will subscribe for US$200m. The package also includes concurrent placements and repurchases, with up to US$300m authorised for the special buyback programme, while proceeds will partly support AI-driven growth. [Bilibili]

  7. 07

    **Energy storage – authorities reportedly scrutinising new capacity:** Industry sources cited by CLS said regulators are mapping existing and planned storage capacity, with battery cells a focus. Projects still at the planning stage and not yet started are reportedly being paused, while registered projects already under construction remain unaffected; watch CATL 300750 CH, EVE Energy 300014 CH and other storage suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **China State Construction – vice-president removed:** China State Construction 601668 CH said vice-president Chen Yong left his position on 5 September because he is under disciplinary review and supervisory investigation for suspected serious violations. The company said responsibilities have been reassigned and normal operations are unaffected. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Yubang New Materials – control change and Monday resumption:** Suzhou Deling agreed to acquire 29.99% of Yubang New Materials 301266 CH from its controlling shareholders and plans a partial tender offer for another 5%. Jiang Wenquan will become the ultimate controller if completed; the shares and convertible bonds resume trading today. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **Fosun Pharma – trims Gland Pharma holding:** Fosun Pharma 2196 HK/600196 CH sold 9.897m Gland Pharma shares, approximately 6% of the Indian company, through block and market trades. Its holding falls to about 45.76%, but Gland Pharma remains a controlled and consolidated subsidiary. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Zelgen – thyroid-cancer application accepted:** China’s drug regulator accepted Zelgen Biopharmaceuticals’ 688266 CH application for a new indication of recombinant human thyroid-stimulating hormone. The proposed use is alongside radioactive iodine treatment following thyroid removal in differentiated thyroid-cancer patients without distant metastasis. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Aistar – robotics expectations tempered:** After two consecutive limit-up sessions, Aistar 002403 CH said its embodied-intelligence robotics cooperation remains at an early stage and is not expected to materially affect this year’s financial results. Its core cookware and appliance operations remain dominant, while the company was loss-making in the first half. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Space industry – international cislunar constellation launched:** China announced the formal launch of an international cislunar CubeSat scientific programme at the Tiandu deep-space exploration conference. The proposed satellite network will support space-environment monitoring, gamma-ray-burst observation and lunar-resource exploration, providing a fresh policy catalyst for satellite manufacturing, payloads and ground systems. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **US markets – indices lower, semis and China ADRs outperform:** Friday’s Dow fell 0.51%, the S&P 500 0.38% and Nasdaq 0.29% after US payrolls rose 162,000, lifting September Fed-hike expectations; the SOX nevertheless gained 3.37%, with SanDisk +11.9%, Micron +6.1%, Seagate +6.34% and Western Digital +5.86%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon rose 0.89%, led by Baidu +4.07%, while Alibaba gained 1.28% and JD 1.87%; COMEX gold fell 1.38% to US$4,477.20, DXY rose 0.16 to 99.16 and the US 10-year yield reached 4.784%. US cash equities are closed Monday for Labor Day. [Sina] [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Oil – Hormuz threat lifts Monday prices:** WTI rose 0.84% to US$92.25 and Brent 0.65% to US$96.91 by 06:50 HKT after Iran said it would designate parts of the Strait of Hormuz a restricted zone. Seven OPEC+ producers separately agreed to keep October output unchanged, the first pause since the latest production increases began; the move supports attention on PetroChina 857 HK, CNOOC 883 HK and airlines. [Securities Times]

  16. 16

    **Sell-side – storage, dividends and resources favoured:** Shenwan Hongyuan expects a September rebound but prefers domestic computing, memory, high-end CCL/PCB, innovative drugs and high-dividend financials; China Galaxy highlighted the combination of stronger US employment, high oil and tighter external liquidity. Everbright Securities favours electronics, communications, defence, power equipment and upstream resources in a range-bound market. [Eastmoney]
