Morning Market Memo | 23 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 20 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 23 Nov 2026 06:15 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Margin financing:** Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing exchanges lowered the minimum financing-margin ratio from 100% to 80%, effective 30 November. Brokers may also recalculate available collateral on outstanding contracts, supporting A-share liquidity and securities names. [SSE]

  2. 02

    **Capital-market fee cuts:** Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges will waive trading-unit usage fees and extend existing listing-fee and bond-transaction-fee exemptions through 2028. ChinaClear will also continue waiving specified service and settlement fees for green, technology-innovation and private-enterprise bonds during 2027–28. [Xinhua]

  3. 03

    **ChiNext market makers:** Shenzhen Stock Exchange opened consultation on introducing market makers to ChiNext, covering broker registration, quoting obligations, performance assessment and risk controls. Feedback closes 6 December; the mechanism is intended to improve liquidity, pricing efficiency and market stability. [SZSE]

  4. 04

    **Active ETFs:** China’s first 18 active ETFs list in Shanghai and Shenzhen today after all reached their RMB2bn fundraising caps, raising RMB36bn in aggregate. The launch adds a transparent, exchange-traded active-equity channel and brings incremental demand for holdings including Kweichow Moutai, Midea and CATL. [NBD] [Beijing Daily]

  5. 05

    **Hang Seng Tech expansion:** The index will expand from 30 to 50 constituents, adding CATL, Luxshare, Zhongji Innolight, Victory Giant, Hesai, Biren, ASMPT and UBTech, among others. Changes take effect on 7 December; MiniMax and Zhipu join the HSCEI while Postal Savings Bank and Shenzhou International exit. [Hang Seng Indexes] [RTHK]

  6. 06

    **Fiscal and property pulse:** January–October general public-budget revenue rose 4.9% to RMB19.57tn, while securities transaction stamp-tax receipts increased 54.1% to RMB251.1bn. Land-sale revenue fell 25.8% to RMB1.85tn, maintaining pressure on local-government and property-linked finances. [Ministry of Finance] [NBD]

  7. 07

    **China–Australia cooperation:** A post-close joint statement established a bilateral artificial-intelligence dialogue and included cooperation on clean-energy transition, trade and investment. The countries also designated 2027 as the China–Australia Tourism Year, relevant to commodities, EVs, renewables, airlines and travel platforms. [Xinhua]

  8. 08

    **US telecom restrictions:** The FCC voted through rules intended to bar China Mobile, China Telecom and China Unicom from directly obtaining US internet-telephone numbering resources. MOFCOM urged an immediate withdrawal and said China would take necessary measures to protect its interests. [MOFCOM]

  9. 09

    **Capital-market rumours:** Securities regulators and police disclosed four enforcement cases involving fabricated tax policy, a false IPO suspension, forged regulatory notices and a fictitious 30% corporate layoff. All named perpetrators received administrative punishment, signalling tighter scrutiny of AI-generated and social-media market claims. [Xinhua]

  10. 10

    **AI-server pricing:** Dell, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro are reportedly seeking 25%–45% price increases on some Nvidia-based AI-server orders, including contracts signed months earlier, as memory and component costs rise. The report is relevant to Lenovo 992 HK and the storage, PCB and data-centre supply chains. [Eastmoney] [Intelwire]

  11. 11

    **Tongwei buyback:** Tongwei 600438 CH approved an RMB1bn–2bn share repurchase at no more than RMB17.20 per share, with the stock intended for employee ownership or incentive plans. The six-month programme provides a fresh company-specific catalyst for the pressured solar sector. [China Fund News]

  12. 12

    **Zijin buyback execution:** Zijin Mining 601899 CH/2899 HK disclosed its first purchase under the new A-share programme, spending RMB100.1m on 4.41m shares at RMB22.58–22.82. The full programme targets RMB2bn–3bn of purchases for employee ownership or incentives. [Sina]

  13. 13

    **PetroChina:** CNPC party-group member and deputy general manager Xie Jun is under disciplinary review and supervisory investigation for suspected serious violations, according to the central anti-corruption authorities. The announcement is a governance headline for PetroChina 857 HK/601857 CH. [CCDI]

  14. 14

    **Huanrui Century:** Regulators said the company’s 2022 annual report overstated net profit by RMB12.88m; the stock will be suspended today and resume Tuesday as ST Huanrui. The ticker remains 000892 CH with a 10% daily price limit. [Economic Information Daily]

  15. 15

    **Regional stabilisation measures:** Taiwan raised short-selling collateral from 130% to 150% and prohibited sales below the previous close, while its NT$500bn stabilisation fund remains authorised to intervene. South Korea prepared its market-stabilisation fund, relaxed corporate-buyback limits for three months and intensified illegal-short-selling surveillance. [Taipei Times] [Korea Herald]

  16. 16

    **Nvidia investigation:** Reuters reported that SEC enforcement staff are examining potential accounting issues at Nvidia; the scope and timing remain unclear, and an investigation does not establish wrongdoing. The development adds uncertainty for global AI hardware and HK-listed suppliers exposed to Nvidia demand. [Reuters]

  17. 17

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow fell 1.93%, S&P 500 3.70% and Nasdaq 5.28%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 10.73% and Nvidia lost 14.01%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index fell 3.37%, with XPeng down 13.86%, NIO 7.89%, Li Auto 7.56% and Baidu 6.75%, setting a weak read-through for HK technology, semiconductors and EVs. [Xinhua] [Investing.com]

  18. 18

    **Cross-assets:** US 10-year yields fell 10.24bp to 4.9438%; COMEX gold dropped 4.37% to US$3,524.30/oz and silver 7.89% to US$40.86/oz. WTI rose 1.75% to US$83.88/bbl, DXY slipped 0.08% to 103.808, and onshore renminbi closed at 6.6735 per dollar. [Eastmoney]
