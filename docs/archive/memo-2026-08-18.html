Morning Market Memo | 18 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 17 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 18 Aug 2026 07:05 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    Policy finance – private investment support: The NDRC said it will accelerate deployment of 2026’s new policy-based financial instruments and increase support for privately funded projects. China Development Bank, Export-Import Bank of China and Agricultural Development Bank officials attended the working meeting, giving infrastructure, construction machinery and private capex names a fresh policy catalyst. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    Unitree – STAR Market listing date confirmed: Unitree Robotics (688836 CH) announced after Monday’s close that its shares will begin trading on 19 August. The company reported first-half revenue of RMB1.15bn; the listing is the main near-term catalyst for the A/H robotics, components and embodied-AI complex. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    Huayang Group – potential state-owned change of control: Automotive-electronics supplier Huayang Group (002906 CH) will halt trading from today after its controlling shareholder began talks to transfer shares to Sichuan Jiuzhou Investment Holding. The transaction could change Huayang’s controller and bring it under a state-owned group spanning communications, electronic systems and intelligent manufacturing. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Ruifeng Polymer Materials – electronic-resins acquisition: Ruifeng (300243 CH) plans to spend RMB400m–500m for at least 51% of Mitop New Materials. The target makes electronic-grade epoxy, low-dielectric resins and photosensitive materials used in copper-clad laminates, adding a fresh semiconductor and high-end PCB materials angle. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Yingxin Development – advanced packaging and memory fundraising: Yingxin (000620 CH) proposed a private placement of up to RMB1.779bn for advanced packaging, memory-module manufacturing and memory-controller chip R&D, with part of the proceeds allocated to debt repayment and working capital. The announcement supports the domestic packaging, storage and controller-chip supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    Printable OLED materials – TCL CSOT technology licence: Ruilian New Materials (688550 CH) signed a licence agreement with TCL CSOT’s printable-display unit covering patents and technical know-how for printable OLED materials. The companies will jointly own new intellectual property arising from development, with licence fees payable once products enter mass production. [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Embodied AI funds – two fresh commitments: Kaichuang Electric (301448 CH) agreed to invest RMB15m in a RMB100m fund focused on embodied intelligence, robot joints and intelligent equipment. Haomei New Materials (002988 CH) separately plans to commit RMB100m to a proposed RMB1bn AI venture fund, extending private-capital participation in the robotics and AI-hardware chain. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    800G switches – Gongjin flags limited current exposure: Gongjin Electronics (603118 CH) said revenue from its 800G data-centre switches remains a small share of its core business and will have limited short-term financial impact. The post-close warning is relevant after strong trading in domestic high-speed networking and optical-interconnect concepts. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Hisense Home Appliances – first-half profit falls: Hisense Home Appliances (000921 CH/921 HK) reported revenue of RMB46.77bn, down 5.2%, and attributable profit of RMB1.66bn, down 20.2%. The result is a fresh read-through for white-goods demand, margins and the broader home-appliance subsidy trade. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Fudan Microelectronics – profit rebound: Fudan Microelectronics (688385 CH/1385 HK) reported first-half revenue of RMB2.23bn, up 21.0%, and attributable profit of RMB849m, up 338.6%. The post-close result is relevant for the domestic FPGA, security-chip and semiconductor-design complex. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Optical components – Guangku profit and orders accelerate: Guangku Technology (300620 CH) reported revenue up 72.9% to RMB1.03bn and net profit up 186.1% to RMB148m. It said bulk lithium-niobate modulators remain in volume production while some newer thin-film products have received small orders from leading customers, supporting the AI optical-interconnect read-through. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Electronic films – Jiemi expands release-film capacity: Jiemi Technology (002859 CH) reported first-half profit up 39.9%, while electronic-grade film revenue rose 79.0%. It plans to increase annual release-film capacity at its North China base from 480m to 768m square metres, adding RMB428m to project investment. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Overnight US markets – indices lower, storage and optics outperform: The Dow fell 0.51%, S&P 500 0.52% and Nasdaq 0.32%; Microsoft lost 3.0% and Meta 3.5%, while Kioxia’s ADR rose more than 13%, SanDisk over 8%, Western Digital over 5%, Micron over 4% and Coherent over 7%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon index gained 0.37%, with XPeng and VNET up more than 4% and PDD over 2%. WTI rose 3.09% to US$84.95, COMEX gold gained 0.81% to US$4,473.10 and the US 10-year yield climbed to 4.72%, leaving a mixed open signal: supportive for HK storage, optics and oil names, but higher rates for long-duration growth stocks. [Eastmoney] [AP]

  14. 14

    US–Iran – negotiation window expires without an extension: President Trump said the US is not seeking to extend its memorandum of understanding with Iran and that Tehran is unlikely to accept the agreement he considers necessary. Washington and Tehran also issued conflicting claims over control of the Strait of Hormuz, keeping oil producers, refiners, airlines and shipping names sensitive to further headlines. [Eastmoney]
