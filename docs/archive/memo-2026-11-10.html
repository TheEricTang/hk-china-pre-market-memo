Morning Market Memo | 10 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 9 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 10 Nov 2026 08:58 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Overnight US equities:** The Dow rose 2.15%, S&P 500 2.35% and Nasdaq 2.74%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index gained nearly 7%, with Coherent +16.79%, AMD +13.26%, Lumentum +10.69%, Broadcom +8.78% and Micron +6.25%, while Nvidia fell 4.27%. China ADRs lagged: the Golden Dragon Index lost 1.32%, Alibaba fell 3.72%, PDD 3.05%, JD 2.51% and Baidu 1.03%, leaving a mixed read-through for HK internet versus semis and optical hardware. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **Oil falls as Iran tone softens:** Trump said on November 9 that renewed US military action against Iran was “quite unlikely” and the conflict was moving toward an end. WTI settled 4.38% lower at US$86.85 and Brent fell 5.85% to US$92.57; COMEX gold gained 1.83% to US$4,149.50, the dollar index fell 0.63% to 101.66 and the US 10-year yield rose 2.9bp to 4.886%. Lower crude is negative for Chinese oil producers but supportive for airlines and fuel-intensive manufacturers. [Xinhua Finance] [China Financial Information Network]

  3. 03

    **China–US maritime measures:** MOFCOM extended for two months the suspension of countermeasures against five US-linked subsidiaries of South Korea’s Hanwha Ocean, effective November 10, citing implementation of the latest China–US trade consultations. The decision is relevant to shipbuilding, marine equipment and logistics names. [MOFCOM]

  4. 04

    **Dual-use export controls:** MOFCOM will also continue suspending for two months the restrictions imposed on 16 US entities under its April 2025 export-control list. Chinese exporters must still apply for licences for dual-use shipments, but the extension reduces near-term friction for affected industrial and technology supply chains. [MOFCOM]

  5. 05

    **Swap-facility funds enter equities:** CITIC Construction Investment, Huatai and Zheshang Securities disclosed that they completed repo financing under the third SFISF operation and made their first stock purchases on November 9. At least 10 brokers have now deployed funds from the facility, with Huatai saying it will increase allocations to A-shares and equity ETFs; this is a direct liquidity signal for brokers and large-cap A-shares. [Sina Finance] [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **Xinde Semiconductor IPO:** Xinde Semiconductor (7672 HK) priced at HK$8.88 and begins trading today, raising net proceeds of about HK$881.6m. The Hong Kong public tranche was 9.84 times subscribed, versus 1.13 times for the international tranche, providing a fresh test of demand for semiconductor packaging and testing listings. [HKEX]

  7. 07

    **Denglin Technology IPO:** AI-chip developer Denglin Technology-P (9917 HK) priced at the bottom of its range at HK$231, with estimated net proceeds of HK$1.31bn. The public and international tranches were subscribed 2.11 and 1.34 times respectively; trading starts today with 612,500 shares over-allocated. [HKEX]

  8. 08

    **Frontage privatization:** A consortium including Tigermed proposed taking Frontage Holdings (1521 HK) private at HK$2.37 per share, a 59.1% premium to its last closing price, followed by delisting. The CRO resumes trading at 09:00 HKT today; completion remains subject to shareholder, court and regulatory approvals. [HKEX] [CNInfo]

  9. 09

    **Pop Mart buyback:** Pop Mart (9992 HK) repurchased 340,000 shares on November 9 for HK$48.75m at HK$142.60–144.00 per share; the shares will be cancelled. The company has bought back 3.06m shares under its current mandate, reinforcing shareholder-return support for the consumer name. [HKEX] [Sina Finance]

  10. 10

    **Hudian share repurchase:** PCB maker Hudian (002463 CH) approved an RMB500m–RMB1bn A-share buyback at up to RMB156.70 per share. Half of the shares are intended for employee ownership or incentives and half for protecting company value and shareholder interests, relevant to the AI-server and high-speed PCB complex. [Eastmoney] [Sina Finance]

  11. 11

    **Electronic fibreglass expansion:** Kingboard-related materials supplier Jinan Guoji (002636 CH) will invest about RMB500m in a new plant with annual capacity of 130m metres of electronic-grade fibreglass cloth. Construction is expected to take 12 months, adding upstream supply for copper-clad laminates, AI servers and high-speed PCBs. [NBD] [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Hongda Electronics placement:** Hongda Electronics (300726 CH) plans to raise up to RMB1bn from no more than 35 investors. RMB530m is earmarked for polymer tantalum-capacitor capacity and RMB470m for ceramic thin-film circuits and SLC products, targeting AI servers, storage and optical-module applications; the deal still requires regulatory and shareholder approvals. [NBD] [Sina Finance]

  13. 13

    **Cambridge Industries optical modules:** Cambridge Industries (603083 CH) rejected market talk of order cuts, saying high-speed optical-module demand remains strong and production is proceeding to plan. It intends to add at least 10m units of annual capacity by end-2027 across China, Malaysia, Mexico and the US for 800G, 1.6T and NPO products, supporting the optical-interconnect chain. [Sina Finance] [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Tianbang restructuring:** Tongwei Group was selected as Tianbang Food’s (002124 CH) industrial restructuring investor and will acquire 1.47bn converted shares at RMB0.98 each for RMB1.441bn. Tongwei is expected to become the controlling shareholder after completion and has committed to a 60-month lock-up, linking the pig-farming restructuring with Tongwei’s agriculture platform. [Securities Times] [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **Electronics-adhesive consolidation:** Weiteou (301319 CH) signed a preliminary agreement to acquire at least 51% of Xinya New Materials in cash after Xinya Process transfers its principal electronics-adhesive operations, assets and personnel into the target. Valuation and final terms remain subject to due diligence and a definitive agreement. [CLS] [Sina Finance]

  16. 16

    **Huaru Technology procurement ban:** Huaru Technology (301302 CH) received a final decision barring it from all military materials, engineering and services procurement through November 22, 2028. The military-simulation company said it has received no orders through that channel since November 2023 and does not expect the decision to threaten continued operations, although the restriction remains a clear defence-IT overhang. [NBD] [Sina Finance]

  17. 17

    **Ausnutria leadership change:** Ausnutria Dairy (1717 HK) said Ren Zhijian resigned as executive director and CEO for personal reasons, effective November 9. Shao Jianbo, a 28-year veteran of controlling shareholder Yili, was appointed CEO and will oversee strategy, operations and development, making this a fresh management catalyst for the dairy name. [HKEX]
