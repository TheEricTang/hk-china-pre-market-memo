Morning Market Memo | 14 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 13 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 14 Aug 2026 07:24 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **PBOC liquidity:** The central bank will conduct RMB1tn of six-month outright reverse repos today, matching the RMB1tn maturing this month. Together with the August five operation, three- and six-month outright repos have delivered a net RMB200bn injection this month, supporting government-bond issuance and bank funding. [Securities Daily] [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **Next-generation communications:** China has built 136 pilot projects since launching 10-gigabit optical-network trials in 2025, while 5G base stations are upgrading toward 5G-A. MIIT’s 6G coordination programme aims to form a batch of domestically developed technical solutions by 2029; telecom equipment, optical-fibre and satellite-internet names are relevant. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **Indian optical fibre:** MOFCOM will continue anti-dumping duties on single-mode optical fibre imported from India for another five years from today. The measure supports domestic fibre pricing and capacity utilisation across China’s optical-communications supply chain. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    **US drone tariffs:** President Trump signed tariffs of 10%–100% on imported drones and components, including a 100% rate for drones above 25kg or with thermal-imaging functions and 25% for certain smaller products. Most drone tariffs take effect in 21 days, with some non-sensitive component duties delayed 180 days; Chinese drone exporters and suppliers face the clearest read-through. [CCTV]

  5. 05

    **DeepSeek API pricing:** DeepSeek will introduce peak/off-peak API pricing from 17 August, with off-peak rates set at half the peak level. DeepSeek V4 Pro peak output pricing rises to RMB27 per million tokens from RMB6, while ordinary web and app users remain free; the change directly affects enterprise AI deployment costs and domestic cloud demand. [Yicai] [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    **SMIC and Hua Hong:** SMIC’s second-quarter revenue reached US$3.0bn, up 20% quarter-on-quarter, with a 25.3% gross margin; it guided third-quarter revenue up 2%–4% and margin at 26%–28%. Hua Hong reported record revenue of US$717.5m and a 16.5% margin, while guiding third-quarter revenue to US$770m–780m and margin to 16%–18%. [Xinhua Finance]

  7. 07

    **China Mobile:** China Mobile 941 HK reported first-half revenue of RMB538.0bn, down 1.1%, and net profit of RMB78.9bn, down 6.3%. It declared an interim dividend of RMB2.51 per share, or RMB54.43bn in total, while computing-service revenue reached RMB52.9bn, up 14%. [China Securities Journal] [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **JD.com:** JD’s second-quarter revenue reached RMB346.4bn, with service revenue of RMB79.3bn accounting for 22.9% of the total; non-GAAP net profit rose 20.3% to RMB8.9bn. First-half R&D spending increased 53.2%, with investment focused on JoyAI, robotics, autonomous delivery and physical-AI supply-chain applications. [Economic Information Daily] [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Domestic computing chips:** Hygon Information reported first-half revenue of RMB9.10bn, up 66.5%, and net profit of RMB1.80bn, up 49.7%. Sugon’s revenue rose 27.0% to RMB7.43bn and net profit increased 34.2% to RMB978m, supporting the domestic CPU, accelerator and intelligent-computing complex. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **Zhongji Innolight–Jones Tech:** Zhongji Innolight 300308 CH will pay RMB1.747bn for a 10.47% stake in thermal-materials supplier Jones Tech 300684 CH at RMB55.70 per share. Zhongji cited rising heat-dissipation requirements as 800G and 1.6T optical-module shipments scale, giving the deal a direct AI-infrastructure rationale. [NBD] [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Longcheer acquisition:** Longcheer Technology 603341 CH agreed to buy 80% of data-centre infrastructure supplier Anruike for RMB1.12bn. The valuation represents a 425% premium to book value; the sellers committed to cumulative 2026–2028 net profit of at least RMB480m, with payment conditions linked to receivables collection. [NBD] [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Senior Technology Materials:** The lithium-battery separator maker will pay RMB242m for another 59.98% of Bangci Electronics, increasing its holding to 73.48% and taking control. Bangci produces multilayer piezoelectric ceramic components used in semiconductor equipment, satellite laser communications, electronics and new-energy applications. [China Fund News]

  13. 13

    **Joyson Electronics:** Two financial asset investment companies will inject a combined RMB1.5bn into Joyson’s automotive-safety subsidiary, with CIB Investment contributing RMB500m and China Post Investment RMB1bn. The funds will repay shareholder loans and reduce listed-company bank debt, supporting Joyson’s target of lowering its overall gearing below 60%. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Hengtong copper foil:** Hengtong 600226 CH proposed a private placement of up to RMB3.6bn for a green-energy and electronic-circuit high-end copper-foil industrial park and working capital. The project adds another fresh capacity catalyst for high-end copper foil used in electronics and energy applications. [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    **SanDisk storage outlook:** SanDisk said agreements with eight customers cover roughly half of fiscal-2027 bit volumes and about two-thirds of fiscal-2028 volumes. Its 2028–2030 framework targets an approximately 80% non-GAAP gross margin and 50% adjusted free-cash-flow margin, with all residual cash after investment returned to shareholders; the shares gained 13.7%. [CLS]

  16. 16

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow rose 0.13%, the S&P 500 gained 0.65% to a record and the Nasdaq advanced 0.81% after July PPI rose 4.7% year-on-year versus 4.9% expected. Storage rallied—SanDisk +13.7%, Western Digital +7.3%, Seagate +4.9% and Micron +4.2%—but the Golden Dragon index fell 1.84%, with JD -7.3%, PDD -5.5% and Alibaba -2.4%; WTI fell 2.47% to US$81.21, spot gold lost 1.30% to US$4,351 and the US 10-year yield dropped 4.6bp. [Eastmoney]
