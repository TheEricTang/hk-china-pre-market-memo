Morning Market Memo | 03 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 02 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 03 Sep 2026 08:30 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **FTSE China A50 rebalance:** FTSE Russell will add AMEC and Shengyi Technology to the benchmark while removing Muyuan Foods and Wanhua Chemical, effective after the 18 September close. The changes point to passive inflows for semiconductor equipment and AI-server materials names. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **Suiyuan Technology IPO:** The domestic AI-accelerator developer priced its STAR Market offering at RMB142.18 per share for 43.04m shares. After clawback, the online tranche was 10.33m shares with a final lottery rate of 0.0246%, keeping the domestic GPU supply chain in focus. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **Anji Microelectronics seeks H-share listing:** Anji Microelectronics (688019 CH) confidentially filed an application to issue H shares and list in Hong Kong, with a CSRC filing still required. The move adds another semiconductor-materials company to the HK IPO pipeline. [Eastmoney] [Shanghai Securities News]

  4. 04

    **China CSSC wins car-carrier order:** China CSSC Holdings’ subsidiary Guangzhou Shipyard International and China Shipbuilding Trading signed contracts for ten 8,200-car LNG dual-fuel vessels worth more than US$1bn. Deliveries are scheduled for 2029–31, extending order visibility for shipbuilding and marine-equipment suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **Huafon Chemical’s RMB6.85bn related-party acquisition:** Huafon Chemical (002064 CH) plans to buy 100% of Huafon Synthetic Resin and Huafon Thermoplastic through cash and shares, entering leather-use polyurethane resins and thermoplastic polyurethane elastomers. The proposed issuance equals about 12.2% of enlarged share capital. [Eastmoney] [Shanghai Securities News]

  6. 06

    **Large-cap A-share buyback updates:** Midea Group had spent RMB8.02bn buying 99.8m A shares, while Foxconn Industrial Internet, Sungrow and Wuliangye reported cumulative spending of RMB887m, RMB325m and RMB1.10bn respectively. The disclosures provide fresh capital-return support across appliances, AI hardware, renewables and consumer staples. [Securities Times]

  7. 07

    **Tencent extends daily repurchases:** Tencent (700 HK) bought 229,000 shares for HK$100.2m at HK$435.20–441.00 on Wednesday, its 13th consecutive repurchase session since 17 August. Year-to-date repurchases reached HK$29.43bn. [Securities Times]

  8. 08

    **Huada Jiutian backs the domestic EDA ecosystem:** Huada Jiutian (301269 CH) will invest RMB90m as a limited partner in a RMB451m semiconductor fund. Management said the vehicle will strengthen upstream and downstream cooperation and support development of domestic electronic-design-automation tools. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    **Zhongke Electric scales 6C anode production:** Zhongke Electric (300035 CH) said it has achieved mass production of 6C fast-charging anode material and is supplying the launch product of a leading battery manufacturer. The update is relevant to battery-material and ultra-fast-charging names. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    **Quartz Co. passes DRAM-vendor certification:** Quartz Co. (603688 CH) said high-purity quartz furnace tubes made from internally sourced material passed certification at a domestic IDM capable of mass-producing DRAM. The company cautioned that certification does not yet mean volume supply. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **Guangdong Hongtu expands into magnesium die casting:** Guangdong Hongtu (002101 CH) plans to establish a wholly owned subsidiary in Zhaoqing for a RMB274m magnesium-alloy die-casting project. The investment adds capacity relevant to automotive lightweighting and structural components. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **Precious-metals operating catalysts:** Xingye Silver & Tin said the processing plant at its Yinman mine will resume production today after safety rectification, although mining operations remain suspended. Separately, Shengda Resources enlarged the licensed area of its Shidi silver-lead-zinc mine to 5.24 square kilometres following mining-right consolidation. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    **Aishida flags robot-order shortfall:** Aishida (002403 CH) said Honglu Steel Construction purchased 920 industrial robots from subsidiary Qianjiang Robot during the one-year framework period, versus the planned 1,888 units. Fulfilment reached 49%, with the customer adjusting procurement plans as its external market changed. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    **Broadcom’s AI-chip revenue accelerates:** Broadcom reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of US$29.6bn, up 86%, with AI semiconductor revenue rising 221% to US$16.7bn. It expects AI semiconductor revenue of US$21.7bn next quarter, a positive demand read-through for HK/A optical, PCB, server and data-centre supply chains, although total revenue guidance of US$34.8bn was slightly below consensus. [Broadcom] [NBD]

  15. 15

    **US labour and Fed signals:** August ADP private employment increased by 38,000, below the 47,000 consensus. The Fed’s Beige Book said economic growth remained moderate but increasingly depended on projects including AI data centres, while price pressures persisted and consumer conditions became more divided. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow rose 0.56%, S&P 500 gained 0.46% and Nasdaq advanced 0.45%. Dell jumped more than 15% and Nvidia over 3%, while MongoDB fell more than 13%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon index slipped 0.06%, with NIO down nearly 5% and Bilibili more than 2% lower. [NBD] [AP]

  17. 17

    **Cross-assets:** WTI rose 0.45% to US$90.63 and Brent gained 0.62% to US$95.24. Spot gold climbed about 1.4% to US$4,388, DXY eased 0.08% to 99.595, and US two- and ten-year yields finished near 4.73% and 4.79%, respectively. [Eastmoney] [NBD]

  18. 18

    **Southbound flow:** Mainland investors recorded a net HK$4.09bn purchase on Wednesday. AIA received HK$966m, Zhongji Innolight HK$452m and Tencent HK$336m, while Alibaba was a HK$765m net sell, providing the main positioning read-through for today’s HK open. [Securities Times]
