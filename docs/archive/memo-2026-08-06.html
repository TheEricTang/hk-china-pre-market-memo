Morning Market Memo | 06 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 05 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 06 Aug 2026 07:50 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    China–US countermeasures: MOFCOM announced a package responding to US restrictions immediately after Wednesday’s close. Measures include tighter case-by-case controls on US-bound drones and key components, sanctions on seven US entities, suspension of certain US factory inspections under China’s CCC system and a national-security investigation into imported printing and copying equipment; MOFCOM warned that further US restrictions would trigger additional retaliation. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    Critical-minerals recycling: The US will later this month impose a one-year export ban on tungsten scrap and lithium-ion battery “black mass,” requiring the materials to be sold domestically unless exporters obtain exemptions. The move may tighten overseas access to recycled tungsten, lithium, nickel and cobalt feedstock and is relevant to China’s battery-recycling and strategic-metals chains. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    SME policy pipeline: Chinese media reported that MIIT and other departments have completed a draft 15th Five-Year Plan for small and medium-sized enterprise development and that publication is approaching. The plan is expected to strengthen the system for cultivating specialised and innovative “little giant” companies and industrial clusters, keeping SME financing and advanced-manufacturing suppliers in focus. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    Shenzhen market-data access: A brokerage source said the Shenzhen Stock Exchange plans to switch its market-data access method from local-area to wide-area network lines on Friday, August 7. Institutions have been competing for scarce server space near the Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges ahead of the change, relevant to brokers, financial-IT vendors and data-centre operators. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Unitree IPO: Unitree Technology (688836 CH) scheduled its STAR Market online roadshow for August 7 from 14:00 to 17:00. The robotics company plans to issue 40.446434 million new shares, equal to 10% of enlarged capital, providing a fresh price-discovery catalyst for the A/H humanoid-robotics supply chain. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney]

  6. 06

    BeiGene results: BeiGene (688235 CH) reported second-quarter global revenue of US$1.7 billion, up 30%, including US$1.2 billion from Brukinsa, up 31%. The company raised full-year revenue guidance to US$6.6 billion–US$6.8 billion; first-half mainland filings showed revenue of RMB22.22 billion and attributable profit of RMB3.27 billion. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    RemeGen turns profitable: RemeGen (688331 CH) forecast first-half attributable net profit of about RMB4.7 billion, reversing the prior-year loss. Domestic sales of telitacicept and disitamab vedotin continued to grow, while recognition of income from the AbbVie licensing agreement for RC148 provided the main earnings uplift. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    Electronic cloth remains tight: Ping An Electrical disclosed after the close that high-end specialty electronic cloth remains in short supply and said the tight market could continue through 2027–2028. AI-driven demand for high-frequency, high-speed and low-loss materials supports the read-through to copper-clad laminate, PCB and AI-server material suppliers. [Eastmoney]

  9. 09

    Indium-phosphide capacity: Meishike Technology (001229 CH) authorised preparatory work for an indium-phosphide semiconductor-material project through a newly established subsidiary. Phase one comprises a RMB12 million laboratory, while phase two is expected to require RMB200 million–RMB260 million for production equipment, adding a new domestic project in optical-chip substrate materials. [Eastmoney]

  10. 10

    Zhaori Technology transaction: Zhaori Technology (300333 CH) is planning a share-and-cash acquisition of Shenzhen Bainei Technology and an associated fundraising. Trading is suspended from today, with the company targeting disclosure of the transaction plan by August 12; pricing and final terms have not yet been announced. [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    Loan-backed buybacks and insider buying: Qianli Technology (601777 CH) announced a RMB100 million–RMB150 million repurchase backed by a bank facility of up to RMB135 million. China Tianying’s controlling shareholder separately plans to buy RMB50 million–RMB100 million of shares using a facility of up to RMB90 million, extending the use of dedicated bank lending for listed-company capital returns. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Hormuz shipping arrangements: Iran’s deputy foreign minister said Iran and Oman are close to finalising a new temporary system for commercial ships using the Strait of Hormuz. Existing northern and southern routes would close and replacement lanes would partly cross Iranian waters for an estimated two to four months, relevant to tanker operators, insurers and China’s oil importers. [Eastmoney]

  13. 13

    Overnight US markets: The Dow rose 0.49% to another record, while the S&P 500 fell 0.17% and Nasdaq lost 0.83%. Nvidia gained more than 3%, but AMD dropped more than 7%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index fell 1.09%, with NIO and Bilibili down over 2% and Tencent’s ADR nearly 2% lower, setting a softer read-through for HK internet and EV names. [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Storage earnings: SanDisk reported fiscal fourth-quarter revenue of US$8.96 billion, up 372%, and authorised a US$14 billion buyback, but its shares fell more than 4% after hours as next-quarter revenue guidance topped out around consensus. Western Digital fell more than 10% after hours despite quarterly revenue rising 44% to US$3.75 billion, a mixed signal for the HK/A memory, controller and storage-hardware complex. [Eastmoney] [Eastmoney]

  15. 15

    Gold and cross-assets: Spot gold surged 4.16% to US$4,247.02 an ounce and COMEX gold gained 3.74% to US$4,308, while spot silver rose 4.25%. WTI fell 0.91% to US$75.08, the dollar index slipped 0.18% to 99.676 and the US 10-year yield edged up to 4.6188%, favouring a strong opening read-through for HK/A precious-metals producers while oil names face a weaker crude close. [Eastmoney]
