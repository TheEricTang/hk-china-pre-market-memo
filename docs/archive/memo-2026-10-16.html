Morning Market Memo | 16 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 15 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 16 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **Overnight US markets:** The Dow rose 1.08%, the S&P 500 gained 0.33% and the Nasdaq added 0.08%, recovering from early technology-led losses. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index still fell 0.88% for a seventh decline; Nvidia and TSMC lost nearly 2%, while Qualcomm gained more than 3% and Micron rose over 1%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index advanced 1.96%; NetEase, Trip.com, JD.com, NIO and XPeng gained 2%–3%, while Alibaba was flat. [Eastmoney]

  2. 02

    **US inflation and cross-assets:** September producer prices rose 1.3% month on month and 6.3% year on year, versus expectations of 0.5% and 5.3%, keeping US rate expectations firm. WTI gained 2.59% to US$97.55 a barrel, COMEX gold rose 2.06% to US$4,192.50 and silver climbed 3.09%, relevant to PetroChina/CNOOC and HK-listed precious-metal producers. [Eastmoney]

  3. 03

    **Diesel supply response:** International Energy Agency members agreed to release more than 20mn barrels of diesel as soon as possible and accelerate the remaining portion of the emergency oil-stock release promised in March. The volume is less than one day of global diesel consumption, leaving refining margins, transport costs and oil-linked inflation in focus. [Eastmoney]

  4. 04

    **Alibaba options trading:** Cboe Options Exchange will add early-morning and post-close trading sessions for selected equity and ETF options from Monday. Alibaba, TSMC, Nvidia, Apple and Tesla are among the covered names, extending price discovery around Asian trading hours. [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    **Apple–China engagement:** Commerce Minister Wang Wentao met Apple executive chairman Tim Cook after the close to discuss China–US trade, artificial intelligence and Apple’s China operations. Cook said Apple remained positive on China’s prospects and would further expand its business in the country; the meeting is relevant to Apple suppliers including Luxshare Precision and AAC Technologies. [MOFCOM]

  6. 06

    **Premium EV safety review:** MIIT, the Cyberspace Administration and the State Administration for Market Regulation formed a joint team to investigate online reports of a brake-pedal bracket fracture in the Maextro V800. The authorities said the matter would be handled under applicable rules, putting the vehicle and component supply chain under scrutiny. [Xinhua]

  7. 07

    **Eoptolink buyback proposal:** Eoptolink Technology’s controlling shareholder and chairman proposed an RMB800mn–1bn A-share repurchase, funded with company or borrowed resources. The proposed programme would run for 12 months after board approval and provides a fresh catalyst for the optical-module chain. [China News Service]

  8. 08

    **Piotech acquisition and repurchase:** Semiconductor-equipment maker Piotech plans to acquire Wuxi Shangji and two holding vehicles for RMB2.5bn, funded through shares and cash, and raise up to RMB1.82bn in supporting capital. Its chairman separately proposed an RMB200mn–300mn repurchase for incentives, employee ownership or capital reduction. [National Business Daily]

  9. 09

    **Domestic semiconductor earnings:** Fudan Microelectronics forecast nine-month net profit of RMB950mn–1.15bn, while Hygon Information guided to RMB2.71bn–2.98bn. Xi’an Yisi estimated third-quarter net profit of about RMB441mn as wafer sales reached roughly 8.3mn units in the first nine months, supporting the domestic FPGA, AI-computing and semiconductor-material chains. [CLS]

  10. 10

    **Lithium-material recovery:** Minmetals New Energy reported nine-month revenue of RMB12.50bn and net profit of RMB462mn, reversing the prior-year loss as orders, utilisation and product margins improved. Management attributed the recovery to expanding battery-material demand and better pass-through of higher raw-material prices, although third-quarter profit moderated sequentially. [CLS]

  11. 11

    **Ganfeng Lithium results:** Ganfeng Lithium reported nine-month revenue of RMB36.16bn and net profit of RMB5.01bn; third-quarter net profit rose 35.6% year on year to RMB755mn. The company cited higher lithium-product prices and increased battery sales, while noting that its battery operations remained near full utilisation. [Securities Times]

  12. 12

    **Optical-components earnings:** Advanced Fiber Resources guided to nine-month net profit of RMB279mn–302mn, up 142%–162%, with third-quarter profit estimated at RMB131mn–154mn. The company cited AI data-centre investment, stronger high-speed optical-module demand and scale benefits; it also updated its plan to acquire 99.97% of Suzhou Agixun. [CLS]

  13. 13

    **Mainboard payouts and industrial earnings:** China Shenhua proposed RMB21.2bn of cash distributions, while Haitong Development reported nine-month net profit of RMB818mn, up 224%, on stronger domestic and overseas shipping markets. CICC Gold and Tongkun also issued higher profit guidance, linking gains to precious-metal prices and improved polyester-sector spreads respectively. [China Fund News]

  14. 14

    **Luxshare repurchase progress:** Luxshare Precision disclosed that it had repurchased 17.67mn A-shares for approximately RMB1bn as of October 15, reaching the lower end of its RMB1bn–2bn programme. The company said communications/data-centre and automotive-electronics revenue grew faster than its core consumer-electronics business in the first half. [National Business Daily]

  15. 15

    **China–Togo cooperation:** Premier Li Qiang and Togo’s Faure Gnassingbé witnessed agreements covering supply chains, digital trade, customs inspection and human resources. Li called for deeper cooperation in infrastructure, agricultural modernisation and finance, with China also offering wider access for Togolese products. [Xinhua]

  16. 16

    **Source check:** The same-day Eastmoney *财经早餐* could not be date-verified by the cutoff; the column index remained stale despite repeat checks, so no unverified breakfast items were used. [Eastmoney]
