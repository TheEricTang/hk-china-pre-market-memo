Morning Market Memo | 24 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 23 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 24 Nov 2026 09:20 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    NVIDIA leadership: NVIDIA’s board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director, effective immediately, after receiving input from senior US government officials that his return posed no national-security risk. The independent investigation and enhanced export-control oversight will continue; the reversal is a major overnight catalyst for HK/A-share AI hardware and semiconductor names. [NVIDIA]

  2. 02

    US technology surge: The Dow rose 3.01%, S&P 500 4.73% and Nasdaq 6.51%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index jumped 14.03%. NVIDIA gained 31.48%, Dell 20.46%, Micron 19.04% and Tesla about 11.5%, providing a strong read-through for semiconductors, servers, storage, optics and CPO names. [Xinhua] [Nasdaq]

  3. 03

    China ADRs and HK futures: The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index closed about 2.4% higher; Alibaba gained 6.12%, Baidu 5.54%, XPeng 7.43% and NIO 4.90%. Hang Seng November futures rose 0.70% to 22,527, finishing 143 points above Monday’s cash close. [Eastmoney] [HKEJ]

  4. 04

    Cross-assets: The dollar index added about 0.4% to 104.32; the US 10-year yield was around 4.94% and the two-year near 4.70%. Spot gold gained roughly 2.8%, silver 4.8%, while WTI fell about 4% and Brent around 3.5%, setting up firmer precious-metal shares but weaker oil producers. [HSTong] [Eastmoney]

  5. 05

    Margin financing implementation: Regulators told brokers that the forthcoming reduction in the minimum financing margin ratio to 80% can apply directly to existing contracts without closing positions. During volatility, brokers may flex maintenance-margin and forced-liquidation arrangements, while avoiding concentrated selling near the open or close; the new ratio takes effect on 30 November. [NBD]

  6. 06

    A-share buybacks: InnoLight (300308 CH/3308 HK) approved a RMB20bn–40bn A-share repurchase at no more than RMB600 per share, for incentives and shareholder-value support. Moore Threads (688795 CH) approved a RMB500m–1bn programme capped at RMB511 per share and secured a Bank of China loan commitment of up to RMB900m. [CNStock] [Eastmoney]

  7. 07

    Xiaomi buyback: Xiaomi (1810 HK) repurchased 1.815m shares on Monday for HK$49.93m at HK$27.44–27.58 and will cancel them. Repurchases under the current mandate have reached 159.77m shares, or 0.62% of the share count when the mandate was approved. [HKEX]

  8. 08

    Swire/Cathay placement: Swire Pacific (19/87 HK) agreed to place 252.655m Cathay Pacific (293 HK) shares at HK$14.23, an 8.5% discount to Monday’s close. Net proceeds are estimated at HK$3.575bn; Swire’s Cathay holding will fall from 39.15% to about 35%, with the group saying it has no plan for further reductions. [RTHK]

  9. 09

    China Kepei profit warning: China Kepei Education (1890 HK) expects FY2026 revenue and gross profit to rise 10%–15%, but net profit to fall 35%–40% from RMB748.4m. The decline mainly reflects additional tax expenses and late-payment charges arising from a local tax reassessment. [Investing.com]

  10. 10

    Sinotau Pharma IPO: Sinotau Pharma-B (9941 HK) priced its 6.561m-share offering at HK$52, raising estimated net proceeds of HK$276.6m ahead of today’s debut. The Hong Kong tranche was subscribed 5,228.77 times versus 3.01 times internationally; the company warned that concentrated H-share ownership could produce sharp moves on limited turnover. [HKEX]

  11. 11

    Cobalt supply: Democratic Republic of Congo will suspend all cobalt exports from 30 November until further notice, covering industrial and artisanal output. The regulator plans a review after four months but said this would not automatically restart shipments, making the decision relevant for CMOC (3993 HK/603993 CH) and the battery-materials chain. [Reuters] [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    Monoclonal-antibody procurement: Company and product information filing for the national monoclonal-antibody alliance procurement opens at 09:00 today and runs through 18:00 on 27 November. Applicants face production-experience, GMP, intellectual-property and drug-traceability requirements, advancing nationwide price and volume negotiations for biologic drugs. [NHSA]

  13. 13

    6G conference: The 2026 6G Development Conference opens in Shanghai today for a two-day programme focused on a globally unified standard and industrial-ecosystem construction. The event is organised by the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology and the IMT-2030 6G Promotion Group, keeping telecom equipment, satellite communications and advanced components in focus. [The Paper] [Eastmoney]

  14. 14

    Social-insurance fund supervision: The human-resources ministry opened consultation through 23 December on regulations covering the payment, management and investment of pension, unemployment and workplace-injury funds. The draft strengthens supervisory powers and penalties for fraudulent claims, with implications for institutions involved in fund administration and investment operations. [Xinhua]

  15. 15

    Electricity demand: China’s electricity consumption rose 3.7% in January–October; October demand increased 1.6% to 870.9TWh. High-tech and equipment-manufacturing demand grew 8.5% year to date, while charging services and internet-data services rose 55.9% and 40.8%, respectively, supporting grid, charging and data-centre equipment demand. [NEA]

  16. 16

    PBOC–Europe dialogue: PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng met representatives from the ECB, European Commission and Eurogroup to discuss the Chinese and euro-area economies, monetary policy, exchange rates and G20 cooperation. Pan called for managing differences through dialogue and expanding practical monetary and financial cooperation. [PBOC]
