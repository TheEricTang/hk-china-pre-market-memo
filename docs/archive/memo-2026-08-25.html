Morning Market Memo | 25 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open

(covers 24 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 25 Aug 2026 07:01 HKT research cutoff)

  1. 01

    **PBOC – month-end liquidity support:** The central bank will conduct RMB500bn of one-year MLF on 25 August, a RMB100bn net withdrawal against maturities. It also scheduled overnight reverse repos from 27 August to 1 September, capped at RMB600bn daily; combined with August buyout-repo operations, medium-term liquidity still shows a RMB100bn net injection for the month. [Shanghai Securities News]

  2. 02

    **Shanghai – “15th Five-Year” industrial blueprint:** Shanghai targets industrial value-added at more than 20% of GDP and strategic emerging industries at over 50% of large industrial output by 2030. The plan prioritises integrated circuits, biopharma and AI, while seeking more than RMB100bn of incremental output from future industries including quantum technology, brain-computer interfaces, fusion, 6G, silicon photonics and fourth-generation semiconductors. [Shanghai Government] [Shanghai Securities News]

  3. 03

    **Beijing property – central-government provident-fund easing:** Dual-contributor households can now obtain up to RMB240m for a first home, with policy uplifts taking the maximum to RMB340m for qualifying families. The changes also broaden home-status recognition, permit mortgaged-home transfers and add a renovation-withdrawal facility, supporting Beijing housing demand and brokers’ property names. [Government Offices Administration]

  4. 04

    **IPO policy – tightening rumour rejected:** An authoritative source said exchanges have asked sponsors to improve project quality and avoid a rush of weak filings, but have not imposed a new blanket restriction on loss-making applicants. The clarification removes part of the overnight uncertainty around the A-share IPO pipeline and broker underwriting businesses. [China Financial Information Network]

  5. 05

    **Alibaba – senior executives buy after placement:** Chairman Joe Tsai and CEO Eddie Wu acquired about HK$120m of Alibaba 9988 HK shares following the group’s HK$80bn placement announcement. Tsai bought 720,000 shares and Wu bought 350,000, providing a post-close confidence signal after Monday’s placement-driven sell-off. [21st Century Business Herald]

  6. 06

    **Orient Securities – Shanghai Securities merger advances:** Shareholders approved the Orient Securities 600958 CH/3958 HK merger plan and related resolutions by more than 99%. The evening announcement completes a key corporate-governance step in the Shanghai state-owned brokerage consolidation, although regulatory approvals remain outstanding. [Securities Times] [Xinhua Finance]

  7. 07

    **Suiyuan Technology – STAR IPO timetable:** The domestic GPU developer will issue about 43.04m shares, equal to 10% of post-IPO capital, with online and offline subscriptions scheduled for 2 September. The fresh timetable is relevant for the domestic AI-chip complex and listed investors or suppliers linked to Suiyuan. [Eastmoney]

  8. 08

    **Eoptolink – optical-module earnings:** Eoptolink 300502 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB20.91bn, up 100.34%, and net profit of RMB7.53bn, up 90.98%. Second-quarter profit reached RMB4.75bn, about 70% above the first quarter, confirming continued earnings delivery from AI data-centre optical demand. [Jiemian] [Yicai]

  9. 09

    **Luxshare Precision – third-quarter guidance:** Luxshare 002475 CH expects nine-month net profit of RMB13.25bn–14.40bn, up 15%–25%. First-half revenue rose 40.16% to RMB174.50bn, while profit increased 18.04%, with communications/data centres and automotive electronics helping improve the business mix. [Shanghai Securities News] [Sina Finance]

  10. 10

    **Far East Smarter Energy – liquid-cooling acquisition:** Far East 600869 CH agreed to pay RMB216m cash for 80% of Fu De Wang Wang, a specialist supplier of liquid-cooling components and thermal-management products. Management says the deal completes a “power-interconnect-cooling” infrastructure offering, adding another listed-company transaction in the AI data-centre cooling chain. [Shanghai Securities News] [Eastmoney]

  11. 11

    **TGOOD – data-centre orders accelerate:** TGOOD 300001 CH said first-half contract value from computing-centre customers doubled year on year as it deepened work with ByteDance, Alibaba, Tencent and China’s three major telecom operators. First-half net profit rose 30.73% to RMB428m, providing an equipment read-through for power distribution around AI infrastructure. [Eastmoney]

  12. 12

    **HK buybacks – Tencent and Xiaomi:** Tencent 700 HK repurchased 680,000 shares for HK$300m at HK$438.60–450.20, while Xiaomi 1810 HK spent HK$50.78m on 1.82m shares. Both programmes continued after Monday’s technology-sector decline; Xiaomi’s year-to-date repurchases reached HK$11.77bn. [Sina Finance] [STCN]

  13. 13

    **Southbound flows – buying into HK weakness:** Mainland investors recorded HK$11.57bn of net southbound buying as the Hang Seng fell 1.89%. Alibaba received HK$2.18bn and Tencent HK$1.54bn of net buying, while SMIC and Hua Hong saw net selling of HK$1.90bn and HK$871m respectively. [STCN] [Sina Finance]

  14. 14

    **Nvidia – Groq inference racks enter production:** Nvidia said Groq 3 LPX racks are in full production and will be deployed with Vera CPUs and Rubin GPUs at Nebius data centres later this year. Each rack integrates 256 Groq 3 chips, sharpening competition in low-latency AI inference and supporting demand for servers, high-speed interconnects and cooling. [CLS]

  15. 15

    **Overnight US markets – tech and China ADRs lower:** The Dow rose 0.26%, while the S&P 500 fell 0.28% and Nasdaq lost 0.76%. Storage and optics sold off—SanDisk -6.45%, Seagate -6.51%, Micron -5.83%, Western Digital -5.24% and Lumentum -4.22%—while Nvidia fell 2.91% and Tesla 3.83%; the Nasdaq Golden Dragon index dropped 1.66%, with XPeng -8.53%, Bilibili -3.28%, PDD -1.48% and Baidu -1.11%. [Eastmoney]

  16. 16

    **Cross-assets – oil falls, gold advances:** WTI dropped 2.39% to US$84.98 a barrel, easing the immediate cost read-through for airlines and downstream chemicals despite new US measures targeting Iran. COMEX gold gained 0.63% to US$4,710 an ounce and spot gold rose 1.03% to US$4,651.38, keeping precious-metals shares in focus. [Eastmoney]
