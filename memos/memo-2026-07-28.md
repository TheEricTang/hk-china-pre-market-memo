Beijing said it would take all necessary measures against actions that materially damage Chinese interests, keeping export-control and sanctions risk in focus for the domestic AI complex. - **US adds 12.5% tariff under forced-labour probe:** MOFCOM confirmed that the US final measure covers 60 economies and imposes an additional 12.5% Section 301 tariff on China. China called the action unilateral and protectionist and said its existing countermeasures against US fentanyl-related and reciprocal tariffs remain in effect. [[MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_475f1fdfee3141ba9e68d6f88ebf7fb3.html)]

- **Orient Securities – Shanghai Securities deal terms fixed:** Orient Securities (600958 CH) priced its acquisition of 100% of Shanghai Securities at RMB25.12bn, equivalent to roughly 1.25 times book value. It will pay RMB23.55bn through approximately 2.289bn new A-shares at RMB10.29 each and RMB1.57bn in cash; combined assets could exceed RMB600bn. [[NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-27/4517814.html)] [[Shanghai Securities News](https://paper.cnstock.com/html/2026-07/28/content_2249209.htm)]

- **Industrial Fulian – up to RMB2bn buyback:** Industrial Fulian (601138 CH) approved a three-month share repurchase of RMB1bn–RMB2bn at no more than RMB103 per share. The shares will initially be held to protect company and shareholder value and may subsequently be sold or cancelled, providing a fresh capital-return catalyst for the AI-server supply chain. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://www.cnstock.com/commonDetail/751200)] [[China Fund News](https://www.chnfund.com/article/AR69e5cd69-cb97-4fb1-01ff-3a22b542d20a)]

- **Exchange market-data access – local links to close:** Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges are moving exchange-room market-data connections onto wide-area-network lines, with existing local-area-network links due to close on 31 July. A regulatory source described this as a routine technical upgrade rather than a measure targeting quantitative trading, while saying it removes potential advantages arising from physical deployment differences. [[21st Century Business Herald](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260727/herald/724d683e7b7631b68dd33757ae49ca67.html)]

- **Smart vehicles – MIIT preparing five-year plan:** MIIT equipment-industry official Guo Shougang said the ministry will accelerate a “15th Five-Year” plan for intelligent connected NEVs. Priorities include core technologies, mandatory safety standards, vehicle-road-cloud pilots and expanded production and road-testing approvals, relevant to automakers, autonomous-driving suppliers and roadside-infrastructure companies. [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103293868.html)]

- **Moutai – direct-store Flying Fairy price raised:** Kweichow Moutai’s directly operated stores have increased the retail price of standard Flying Fairy Moutai to RMB1,719 a bottle, while the iMoutai online price is RMB1,639. Stores reported limited physical inventory; the two-tier pricing marks a further adjustment of the company’s direct-sales structure and is relevant across the baijiu sector. [[NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-27/4517432.html)]

- **Changxin Broadcom – RMB4.5bn optical-fibre agreement:** A controlled subsidiary signed a long-term supply agreement with an overseas customer carrying estimated sales of RMB4.5bn through 31 December 2030. The amount equals approximately 178% of the company’s 2025 audited revenue, giving the optical-connectivity chain a large fresh order catalyst while execution remains subject to customer demand. [[CFi](https://www.cfi.net.cn/p20260727001981.html)]

- **Robotechnik – optical-component automation order:** Robotechnik’s ficonTEC subsidiary signed a €16.74m, approximately RMB129m, contract with a subsidiary of a New York-listed customer. The order covers automated equipment and services for mass production of fibre arrays and other optical components, supporting CPO and high-speed optical-manufacturing suppliers. [[JRJ](https://finance.jrj.com.cn/2026/07/27202557911501.shtml)]

- **CPO – Nvidia and Broadcom begin shipments:** TrendForce said Nvidia has started supplying its next-generation Spectrum-X CPO switches to selected partners, while Broadcom continues small-volume shipments of its 51.2T Bailly product. CPO has therefore entered the production phase, although optical-engine yields and advanced-packaging capacity remain key constraints. [[TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.cn/presscenter/news/20260727-13152.html)]

The project will focus on process optimisation, product trials and customer validation, adding an onshore catalyst for glass-substrate and advanced-packaging names. [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/vip/news/103294053.html)]
