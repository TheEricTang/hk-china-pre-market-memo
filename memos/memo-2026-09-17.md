Morning Market Memo | 17 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 16 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 17 Sep 2026 08:54 HKT research cutoff)

- **Federal Reserve – first hike since 2023:** The FOMC unanimously raised the federal-funds target by 25bp to 3.75%–4.00%, saying inflation remains elevated and the move should support a timelier return to 2%. Median projections imply one further increase this year, tightening the external rate backdrop for Hong Kong property, growth and highly leveraged equities. [[Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm)]

- **Hong Kong rates – HKMA follows the Fed:** The HKMA raised its Base Rate by 25bp to 4.25% before the local open, in line with the Linked Exchange Rate system’s established formula. The immediate focus is whether local banks adjust prime or deposit rates, with property developers, REITs and rate-sensitive financials most exposed. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609173876719623.html)]

- **PBOC – more emphasis on market-based finance:** Governor Pan Gongsheng said China should coordinate direct and bank financing, develop equity and bond-market “technology boards,” expand wealth management and rely more on price-based monetary tools rather than single-channel loan targets. He also called for a broader macroprudential toolkit to maintain stability in equity, bond and currency markets. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609173876448182.html)]

- **China–Iran – Beijing urges reopening of Hormuz:** Foreign Minister Wang Yi met Iranian counterpart Abbas Araghchi in Beijing and urged all parties to take effective measures to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and protect international energy transport and supply chains. The post-close statement is relevant to oil, airlines, shipping and refiners amid still-elevated freight and crude prices. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609163876390814.html)]

- **Memory chips – reported SK Hynix/Intel US talks:** SK Hynix is reportedly considering leasing part of Intel’s Ohio facilities or forming a venture with Intel and a major cloud customer to produce memory chips in the US; SK Hynix said options are being explored but no plan has been fixed. The report keeps capacity, localisation and HBM supply in focus for the HK/A semiconductor chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609163876390814.html)]

The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index fell 0.55%: Bilibili lost 3.62%, Baidu 2.04% and Alibaba 1.89%, while Trip.com rose 3.01%, Li Auto 2.30% and XPeng 1.08%.
