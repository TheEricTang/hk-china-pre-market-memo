- **PBOC – RMB2.1tn month-end liquidity operation:** The central bank said at 17:02 Friday that it will conduct RMB600bn of overnight reverse repos on each of 29–31 July and RMB300bn on 3 August, using fixed-rate, quantity tenders. The operation is intended to match short-term banking-system liquidity demand around month-end. [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103291258.html)]

- **Trip.com – RMB5.18bn antitrust penalty:** The market regulator fined and confiscated RMB5.179bn from Trip.com for requiring hotel exclusivity and “lowest price” terms enforced through traffic restrictions and reserve deductions. Trip.com must return RMB122m of hotel reserves; the penalty comprises RMB1.658bn of confiscated gains and a RMB3.521bn fine, directly affecting the online-travel platform and hotel sector. [[SAMR](https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2026/art_46d2c74cbd7249f189622dd030e3c3a7.html)]

- **Insider trading – revised judicial interpretation starts today:** The first major revision since 2012 brings the disclosure of an initial transaction intention to closely connected persons into the insider-information sensitive period and tightens available defenses. The changes apply from 27 July and increase compliance risk around controlling shareholders, corporate actions and pre-announcement trading. [[China Fund News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607263821257930.html)]

- **CATL – RMB20bn–40bn cancellation buyback:** CATL reported first-half revenue of RMB276.92bn, up 54.8%, and net profit of RMB43.28bn, up 42.0%. It plans to repurchase RMB20bn–40bn of shares for cancellation, with a maximum purchase price of RMB573 per share; the scale is material for both its A- and H-share lines and the battery complex. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607243820651915.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607263821212088.html)]

- **Lens Technology – Intel advanced-packaging cooperation:** Lens Technology’s Hong Kong subsidiary signed a memorandum with Intel focused on through-glass-via advanced packaging. The parties will assess glass drilling, precision laser processing, metallised vias and multilayer interconnects; it remains a potential rather than binding commercial arrangement but is relevant to glass substrates and domestic packaging equipment. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607243820651915.html)]

- **Sunwoda – strategic investors enter battery unit:** Sungrow and Tianqi Lithium’s Shehong subsidiary will invest a combined RMB805m in Sunwoda Power for a 2.93% stake. Sunwoda’s ownership falls from 27.18% to 26.38% while retaining control; the transaction links storage-cell demand with longer-term lithium supply. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607243820651915.html)]

- **Hikvision – profit recovery and RMB5bn interim dividend:** First-half revenue rose 12.0% to RMB46.82bn and net profit increased 39.6% to RMB7.90bn, the strongest first-half profit growth in nearly five years. Hikvision proposed RMB5.50 per ten shares, implying a RMB5.04bn interim cash distribution, with AI-enabled smart-IoT and overseas localisation cited as growth drivers. [[STCN](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607263821185668.html)]

- **Yitian Intelligent – up to RMB2bn AI-server procurement:** The appliance maker’s subsidiary plans to buy servers and supporting equipment from multiple suppliers to provide computing services and accelerate its AI-compute build-out. The company cautioned that the new business remains at an early stage and its core operation is still integrated kitchen appliances. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607263821248395.html)]

- **Power projects – new coal unit and wind investment:** Hubei Energy said the second 660MW unit at its Jiangling phase-two plant completed its 168-hour full-load trial, bringing the project fully into commercial operation. Chenfeng Technology separately approved RMB1.18bn for two Inner Mongolia wind projects totalling 300MW, supporting equipment and grid-investment names. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607263821248395.html)]

- **Xunyou Technology – shareholder group plans an increase:** Two parties acting with Xunyou’s 5%-plus shareholders plan to buy RMB40m–80m of shares over six months without a fixed price range. The announcement provides a fresh support signal for the small-cap gaming-network acceleration company. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607263821248395.html)]

- **Control-change activity – three small caps halt:** Xintian Technology, Pulian Software and Beiken Energy said their controlling shareholders are considering stake transfers that may change control. Their shares halt from today, generally for no more than two trading days, putting potential restructuring terms in focus when trading resumes. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607243820651915.html)]

- **ChangXin Memory – RMB57.9bn STAR Market debut:** Domestic DRAM manufacturer ChangXin Technology begins trading today at RMB8.66, with base gross proceeds of about RMB57.9bn and 4.50bn shares immediately tradeable. The listing is the STAR Market’s largest IPO by base proceeds and provides a new direct benchmark for mainland memory, semiconductor-equipment and materials valuations. [[21st Century Business Herald](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607263821259048.html)]
