- Social-security funds: Premier Li Qiang called for stronger long-term sustainability, security supervision and value preservation of social-security funds. The State Council study also urged wider enterprise-annuity coverage, greater appeal for personal pensions and support for commercial health and long-term-care insurance, relevant to insurers, banks and asset managers. - Domestic fuel-price cut: The NDRC cut benchmark gasoline and diesel prices by RMB645 and RMB625 per tonne from midnight, equivalent to average reductions of RMB0.51 per litre for 92-octane gasoline and RMB0.53 for diesel. The move lowers near-term costs for airlines, road logistics and consumers while affecting domestic refiners’ product pricing. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611123929437071.html)]

- Exchange scrutiny of speculative stocks: The Shanghai Stock Exchange issued regulatory work letters to Dandong Commercial Group (600327 CH) and Solareast (603366 CH) over share-price volatility after both recorded five consecutive limit-up sessions. The intervention is a fresh warning for high-turnover thematic and small-cap trading. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611123929948193.html)]

- SMIC earnings: SMIC (981 HK/688981 CH) reported third-quarter revenue of RMB21.98bn, up 28.1%, and attributable net profit of RMB2.85bn, up 87.6%; IFRS gross margin reached 29.9%, 4.6 percentage points above the second quarter. Fourth-quarter revenue is guided to rise 4%–6% sequentially, with gross margin at 26%–28%, a key read-through for domestic foundry and semiconductor-equipment names. Products include 400,000 tonnes of lithium-ion electrolyte and 100,000 tonnes of sodium-ion electrolyte; the company also proposed an RMB50m–100m share repurchase for cancellation. - Junda ends Oman project: Junda (002865 CH) terminated its planned 5GW high-efficiency solar-cell base in Oman, previously budgeted at about US$280m, citing changed operating conditions, trade friction and geopolitical uncertainty. HK$963m of unused H-share proceeds is proposed for cell R&D, production-line upgrades, strategic raw-material purchases and working capital.
