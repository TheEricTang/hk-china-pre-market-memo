Nvidia lost 4.40%, AMD, Intel and SanDisk fell more than 8%, and Micron and Western Digital dropped more than 6%, setting a weak read-through for HK/A-share semiconductors, optics and AI hardware. - **China ADRs and cross-assets:** The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index fell 1.19%; XPeng dropped 3.10%, Alibaba 2.48%, Tencent ADR 1.59%, NIO 1.49%, Baidu 1.07% and Bilibili 0.96%. WTI rose 2.01% to US$99.10 a barrel, while COMEX gold fell 2.66% to US$4,039.80 and silver slid 5.34%, relevant to PetroChina/CNOOC, Zijin and other HK-listed resources names. - **Optical hardware companies address market concerns:** Eoptolink (300502 CH), Innolight (300308 CH), TFC Optical (300394 CH) and Dongshan Precision (002384 CH) said production and order deliveries remain normal after Thursday’s sector sell-off. Eoptolink said 1.6T modules are in continuous volume ramp-up, while TFC said CPO/NPO expansion plans have not materially changed. [[STCN](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904447479.html)]

- **AI forced-liquidation report denied:** China Securities Journal reported after midnight that Shenzhen-listed AI leaders did not experience the rumoured chain of large forced liquidations. Only isolated liquidation activity of less than RMB100,000 was identified, making the clarification relevant to Friday’s open after Thursday’s sharp technology correction. [[China Securities Journal](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904477662.html)]

- **Zijin Mining takes control of Chifeng Gold:** Zijin Mining (2899 HK/601899 CH) completed the transfer and placement steps giving its subsidiaries 571.66 million Chifeng Gold shares, equal to 25.85% of the enlarged capital. Zijin Gold is now Chifeng Gold’s controlling shareholder, consolidating another major gold producer as bullion equities face a weaker overnight metal price. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]

- **Battery electrolyte capacity – Yongtai Technology:** Yongtai Technology (002326 CH) said vinylene-carbonate capacity currently stands at 16,666 tonnes a year and is expected to reach 30,000 tonnes by year-end. A further 50,000-tonne project is planned for completion next year, adding a fresh capacity signal for the lithium-battery materials chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]

- **Thermal management M&A:** Zhengan Technology (300767 CH) signed an initial agreement to acquire at least 51% of Shenzhen Sanwei, a producer of heat-dissipation products, tooling and precision components. The cash transaction is expected to constitute a major asset restructuring and would expand Zhengan’s manufacturing exposure to thermal management. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]

- **Quantum-computing order:** QuantumCTek (688027 CH) said its Shanghai subsidiary plans to supply a quantum computer and related technical services to controlling shareholder China Telecom Quantum Group for approximately RMB64.77 million. The company also plans separate superconducting-chip and signal-amplifier development contracts with the University of Science and Technology of China. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]

- **JCET – AI demand supports packaging results:** JCET Group (600584 CH) reported third-quarter revenue of RMB11.43 billion, up 13.53%, and net profit of RMB773 million, up 59.99%. The company cited growing AI infrastructure and edge-device demand, domestic semiconductor substitution, higher orders and strong capacity utilisation. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]

- **China Telecom earnings soften:** China Telecom (728 HK/601728 CH) reported third-quarter revenue of RMB121.09 billion, down 3.01%, and net profit of RMB6.62 billion, down 14.65%. Nine-month revenue fell 3.59% and profit declined 14.84%, providing a weaker company-specific read-through for the mainland telecom complex. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]

- **Tonghuashun – stronger fintech earnings:** Tonghuashun (300033 CH) posted third-quarter revenue of RMB2.15 billion, up 45.01%, and net profit of RMB1.08 billion, up 53.97%. Management attributed the nine-month improvement to business expansion and higher value-added telecommunications, advertising and online-promotion revenue. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]

- **Asymchem – emerging CDMO businesses accelerate:** Asymchem Laboratories (6821 HK/002821 CH) reported third-quarter revenue growth of 24.04% and net-profit growth of 81.49%. Nine-month emerging-business revenue rose 50.2%, led by chemical-macromolecule, biologics and formulation operations, offering a fresh read-through for HK-listed contract-research and manufacturing names. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]

- **G-bits declares large interim distribution:** G-bits Network Technology (603444 CH) proposed a cash dividend of RMB60 for every ten shares after nine-month net profit rose 30.55%. Third-quarter revenue and profit nevertheless fell 10.07% and 13.34%, respectively, leaving shareholder returns as the main near-term catalyst. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223904316464.html)]
