Morning Market Memo | 19 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 18 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 19 Nov 2026 07:34 HKT research cutoff)

- **China–US / APEC:** President Xi Jinping welcomed US President Donald Trump at Shenzhen Bao’an airport at 17:06 HKT. Xi said he expected in-depth exchanges on China–US relations, Asia-Pacific cooperation and global affairs, putting today’s APEC meetings at the centre of the bilateral agenda. [[Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/politics/leaders/20261118/5db289af84bc4f5e843dab5e527edc2d/c.html)]

- **Water-wind-solar policy:** The National Energy Administration released guidance to advance integrated river-basin hydropower, wind and solar development. It targets progress at the Yalong, upper Jinsha, upper Lancang and southeast Tibet bases by 2030, with integrated development becoming a major route for upgrading hydropower by 2035; relevant names include hydro operators, grid equipment, storage and renewable developers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611183934100022.html)]

- **Hong Kong banking regulation:** The Legislative Council passed amendments introducing oversight of bank holding companies, strengthening enforcement powers and simplifying the current three-tier banking system into two tiers. Implementation begins in phases from 1 January 2027, with existing deposit-taking companies receiving a five-year transition period. [[HKMA](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/gb_chi/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261118-4/)]

- **Hong Kong debt capital markets:** The Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation announced a HK$22.5bn-equivalent three-currency benchmark issue comprising HK$8bn three-year, CNH5bn two-year and US$1.1bn five-year bonds. Peak orders reached roughly HK$55bn equivalent, including demand from Southbound Bond Connect and international institutions. [[HKMA](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/gb_chi/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261118-3/)]

- **Tencent (700 HK):** Third-quarter revenue rose 8% to RMB207.34bn, while non-IFRS attributable profit fell 6% to RMB66.52bn. Marketing-services revenue grew 19%, domestic games rose 11% and cloud-services revenue increased 35%, but higher AI investment pushed operating and administrative costs higher. [[HKEX](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1118/2026111800448.pdf)]

- **Optical-module complex:** Eoptolink (300502 CH) said orders remain sufficient, denied demand cuts and reported that 1.6T modules have entered large-scale ramp-up. Innolight’s chairman proposed a RMB2bn–4bn buyback, while TFC Optical (300394 CH) said CPO-related products are in batch delivery and disclosed RMB108m of completed repurchases. - **Pony.ai (2026 HK/PONY US):** Third-quarter revenue increased 75.2% to US$44.6m, led by 165.4% growth in Robotaxi services, while the fleet reached 2,768 vehicles. Net loss attributable to shareholders widened to US$105.6m, although the non-GAAP loss narrowed as operating scale improved. [[Pony.ai](https://ir.pony.ai/news-releases/news-release-details/pony-ai-inc-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-total)]

- **Cathay Pacific (293 HK):** Cathay Group carried more than 3.4m passengers in October, up 4% year on year, while cargo tonnage rose 8% to more than 160,000 tonnes. November–December bookings were described as firm, particularly for mainland China, Malaysia and Thailand, although management flagged continued high jet-fuel prices. [[Cathay](https://news.cathaypacific.com/%E5%9C%8B%E6%B3%B0%E9%9B%86%E5%9C%98%E5%85%AC%E5%B8%832026%E5%B9%B410%E6%9C%88%E4%BB%BD%E5%AE%A2%E8%B2%A8%E9%81%8B%E9%87%8F%E8%B3%87%E6%96%99)]

- **Xingyun Technology (300209 CH):** The company signed a 60-month cross-border AI-token service agreement with New Silkroad Holdings, carrying a RMB2bn initial order and RMB20bn cumulative settlement ceiling. The ceiling is an indicative framework rather than guaranteed revenue, with actual settlement dependent on confirmed orders and usage. [[Xingyun Technology](https://www.xingyuntech.com.cn/disclosure/72)]

- **Jiangte Motor (002176 CH):** The Qiankeng lithium-mica mine has moved into construction preparation after securing key land, safety and design approvals. Its licence permits 3m tonnes of annual production; estimated ore resources are 72.93m tonnes at 0.44% Li₂O, or 126.67m tonnes using a 0.39% grade assumption, offering a route to higher captive-feed supply. [[SMM](https://news.smm.cn/news/WT5BBFSGRAIOOAK5UJ43WWTPK5TEZ73R4NSALUI)] [[NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-18/4651500.html)]

- **Xiaomi (1810 HK) buyback:** Xiaomi spent HK$49.87m repurchasing 1.68m shares on 18 November at HK$29.62–29.70 each. The post-close disclosure provides a fresh capital-return signal after continued volatility across Hong Kong technology and hardware names. [[Zhitong](https://cn.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/article-3674286)]

- **Sun Art Retail (6808 HK):** First-half revenue fell 9.9% to RMB27.49bn and the attributable loss widened to RMB514m from RMB123m. Adjusted EBITDA declined 34.9% to RMB1.03bn, a weak consumption read-through for mainland hypermarkets despite the group maintaining a RMB10.52bn net-cash position. [[HKEX](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1118/2026111801057.pdf)]

- **Johnson Electric (179 HK):** First-half sales rose 1% to US$1.85bn, but attributable profit dropped 59% to US$55m as gross margin contracted to 20.6% from 24.0%. Automotive sales declined 2% in constant currency, while industrial-products sales grew 11%, supported by China wins, semiconductor equipment, medical devices and data-centre liquid cooling. [[HKEX](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1118/2026111800812.pdf)]
