- NVIDIA leadership: NVIDIA’s board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director, effective immediately, after receiving input from senior US government officials that his return posed no national-security risk. The independent investigation and enhanced export-control oversight will continue; the reversal is a major overnight catalyst for HK/A-share AI hardware and semiconductor names. [[NVIDIA](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Board-Reinstates-Jensen-Huang-as-President-CEO-and-Board-Director/default.aspx)]

- US technology surge: The Dow rose 3.01%, S&P 500 4.73% and Nasdaq 6.51%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index jumped 14.03%. NVIDIA gained 31.48%, Dell 20.46%, Micron 19.04% and Tesla about 11.5%, providing a strong read-through for semiconductors, servers, storage, optics and CPO names. [[Xinhua](https://www.xinhuanet.com/20261124/f6dfdb12426049c3a7e4ee59245a4d9f/c.html)] [[Nasdaq](https://indexes.nasdaqomx.com/Index/Overview/sox)]

- China ADRs and HK futures: The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index closed about 2.4% higher; Alibaba gained 6.12%, Baidu 5.54%, XPeng 7.43% and NIO 4.90%. Hang Seng November futures rose 0.70% to 22,527, finishing 143 points above Monday’s cash close. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611243937884045.html)] [[HKEJ](https://stock360.hkej.com/?code=00001)]

- Cross-assets: The dollar index added about 0.4% to 104.32; the US 10-year yield was around 4.94% and the two-year near 4.70%. Spot gold gained roughly 2.8%, silver 4.8%, while WTI fell about 4% and Brent around 3.5%, setting up firmer precious-metal shares but weaker oil producers. [[HSTong](https://www.hstong.com/news/detail/26112407000051645)] [[Eastmoney](https://emwap.eastmoney.com/a/202611243937789453.html)]

- Margin financing implementation: Regulators told brokers that the forthcoming reduction in the minimum financing margin ratio to 80% can apply directly to existing contracts without closing positions. During volatility, brokers may flex maintenance-margin and forced-liquidation arrangements, while avoiding concentrated selling near the open or close; the new ratio takes effect on 30 November. [[NBD](https://m.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-23/4656388.html)]

- A-share buybacks: InnoLight (300308 CH/3308 HK) approved a RMB20bn–40bn A-share repurchase at no more than RMB600 per share, for incentives and shareholder-value support. Moore Threads (688795 CH) approved a RMB500m–1bn programme capped at RMB511 per share and secured a Bank of China loan commitment of up to RMB900m. [[CNStock](https://www.cnstock.com/commonDetail/832583)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611233937529726.html)]

- Xiaomi buyback: Xiaomi (1810 HK) repurchased 1.815m shares on Monday for HK$49.93m at HK$27.44–27.58 and will cancel them. Repurchases under the current mandate have reached 159.77m shares, or 0.62% of the share count when the mandate was approved. [[HKEX](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1123/2026112301138.pdf)]

- Swire/Cathay placement: Swire Pacific (19/87 HK) agreed to place 252.655m Cathay Pacific (293 HK) shares at HK$14.23, an 8.5% discount to Monday’s close. Net proceeds are estimated at HK$3.575bn; Swire’s Cathay holding will fall from 39.15% to about 35%, with the group saying it has no plan for further reductions. [[RTHK](https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/ch/component/k2/1878982-20261123.htm)]

- China Kepei profit warning: China Kepei Education (1890 HK) expects FY2026 revenue and gross profit to rise 10%–15%, but net profit to fall 35%–40% from RMB748.4m. The decline mainly reflects additional tax expenses and late-payment charges arising from a local tax reassessment. [[Investing.com](https://cn.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/article-3682221)]

- Sinotau Pharma IPO: Sinotau Pharma-B (9941 HK) priced its 6.561m-share offering at HK$52, raising estimated net proceeds of HK$276.6m ahead of today’s debut. The Hong Kong tranche was subscribed 5,228.77 times versus 3.01 times internationally; the company warned that concentrated H-share ownership could produce sharp moves on limited turnover. [[HKEX](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1123/2026112301691.pdf)]

- Cobalt supply: Democratic Republic of Congo will suspend all cobalt exports from 30 November until further notice, covering industrial and artisanal output. The regulator plans a review after four months but said this would not automatically restart shipments, making the decision relevant for CMOC (3993 HK/603993 CH) and the battery-materials chain. [[Reuters](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/congo-halts-cobalt-exports-again-to-tackle-oversupply-support-prices-industry-regulator-says-ce7851dada89f121)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611233937528051.html)]

- Monoclonal-antibody procurement: Company and product information filing for the national monoclonal-antibody alliance procurement opens at 09:00 today and runs through 18:00 on 27 November. Applicants face production-experience, GMP, intellectual-property and drug-traceability requirements, advancing nationwide price and volume negotiations for biologic drugs. [[NHSA](https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2026/11/21/art_185_23491.html)]

- 6G conference: The 2026 6G Development Conference opens in Shanghai today for a two-day programme focused on a globally unified standard and industrial-ecosystem construction. The event is organised by the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology and the IMT-2030 6G Promotion Group, keeping telecom equipment, satellite communications and advanced components in focus. [[The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_34421575)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611233937615737.html)]

- Social-insurance fund supervision: The human-resources ministry opened consultation through 23 December on regulations covering the payment, management and investment of pension, unemployment and workplace-injury funds. The draft strengthens supervisory powers and penalties for fraudulent claims, with implications for institutions involved in fund administration and investment operations. [[Xinhua](https://www3.xinhuanet.com/politics/20261123/f404f69d0647481d951af25b0bee8ca7/c.html)]

- Electricity demand: China’s electricity consumption rose 3.7% in January–October; October demand increased 1.6% to 870.9TWh. High-tech and equipment-manufacturing demand grew 8.5% year to date, while charging services and internet-data services rose 55.9% and 40.8%, respectively, supporting grid, charging and data-centre equipment demand. [[NEA](https://www.nea.gov.cn/20261123/c7b24ed8e9f84139bfdb5dc4a37325e2/c.html)]

- PBOC–Europe dialogue: PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng met representatives from the ECB, European Commission and Eurogroup to discuss the Chinese and euro-area economies, monetary policy, exchange rates and G20 cooperation. Pan called for managing differences through dialogue and expanding practical monetary and financial cooperation. [[PBOC](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/goutongjiaoliu/113456/113469/2026112319381491628/index.html)]
