- China will issue up to US$2bn of sovereign dollar bonds in Hong Kong: The Ministry of Finance said the offering will take place during the week beginning 16 November, with detailed terms to follow. The transaction adds a fresh sovereign pricing reference for offshore Chinese dollar bonds and reinforces Hong Kong’s debt-capital-market role. - Offshore trusts – transition period closes with RMB160bn-plus tax declarations: The State Taxation Administration said declared individual income tax linked to offshore trusts exceeded RMB160bn, including more than RMB120bn booked in October, lifting nationwide October individual-income-tax receipts by nearly 90%. Authorities will move to regular enforcement, including surcharges and investigations for incomplete filings; the disclosure is relevant to private banks, trustees and Hong Kong wealth-management platforms. [[Beijing News](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1794390238129152.html)] [[Caixin](https://economy.caixin.com/2026-11-11/102503675.html)]

Wang said both sides should manage differences and support each other in hosting the 2026 APEC and G20 leaders’ meetings, a stabilising signal for China–US-sensitive technology, export and internet stocks.
