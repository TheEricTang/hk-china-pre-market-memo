- PBOC – new bond-market liquidity backstop: The central bank established a Bond-market Non-bank Large-redemption Swap Facility, or BNSF, for periods of systemic stress. Eligible bank wealth-management units and public-fund managers may borrow central-bank bills for repo financing, allowing them to purchase qualifying bonds or fund units facing redemptions; this adds a formal liquidity channel for the asset-management sector. [[Xinhua](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610263908645880.html)]
