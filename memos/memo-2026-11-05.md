Morning Market Memo | 05 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 04 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 05 Nov 2026 06:52 HKT research cutoff)

- **PBOC liquidity:** The central bank will conduct RMB300bn of three-month outright reverse repos today through a fixed-quantity, rate-tender and multiple-price process; maturity is 4 February 2027. The operation keeps liquidity support in place, although it is smaller than the RMB500bn reportedly maturing today. [[PBOC via Beijing Daily](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2026/11/04/12018069.shtml)]

- **Capital-market support funds:** Post-close disclosures showed 26 brokers had won allocations in the third Securities, Funds and Insurance Companies Swap Facility operation. Nine disclosed amounts totalling RMB57.5bn, led by CICC at RMB16.3bn and CITIC Securities and Guotai Haitong at RMB10bn each; the full operation was RMB100bn with a 10bp fee. [[China Fund News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611043922305630.html)]

- **PetroChina–Unitree industrial robotics JV:** PetroChina subsidiaries, Unitree and Beijing Fanjie Intelligent plan a jointly controlled company providing industrial embodied-AI robot solutions. PetroChina’s Kunlun entities will own 60%, with Unitree and Fanjie holding 20% each; the competition filing is open for comment through 13 November. [[Dahe Cailifang](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611043922063023.html)]

- **Jereh wins ADNOC awards:** Jereh Group’s 002353 CH subsidiary received three award letters from ADNOC Onshore worth US$2.697bn, or about RMB18.12bn, covering one gas-development and two oilfield-facilities projects. Shares had already hit limit-up Wednesday, but the awards were announced after close; formal contracts remain unsigned and will not materially affect 2026 earnings. [[National Business Daily](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-11-04/doc-inixqsyn9323215.shtml)]

- **CATL cooling-fluid partnership:** Unified Low Carbon Technology’s 600506 CH subsidiary signed a strategic agreement with CATL covering coolant reliability research, product lifecycle cooperation, quality standards and the NEV aftermarket. The framework runs through 2029 but contains no minimum purchase volume or transaction value, limiting the immediate earnings impact. [[National Business Daily](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-04/doc-inixqsyn9337964.shtml)]

- **Hengtong Optic-Electric buyback and shareholder increase:** Hengtong 600487 CH approved an RMB100m–200m company buyback at up to RMB84.05 per share, while controlling shareholder Hengtong Group separately committed RMB400m–600m to market purchases over 12 months. The combined plan is relevant to the optical-fibre and AI-connectivity complex. [[National Business Daily](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-04/4638510.html)]

- **Sanhuan launches fourth 2026 buyback:** Sanhuan Group 300408 CH/6951 HK approved an RMB500m–1bn A-share repurchase at no more than RMB100 per share, with a three-month execution period. The programme follows a greater-than-20% decline over 20 trading days and could cover up to roughly 10m shares, or 0.5% of capital. [[National Business Daily](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-04/4638691.html)]

- **Tianshan Materials investigated:** The CSRC opened an investigation into Tianshan Materials 000877 CH for suspected disclosure violations. The cement producer said operations and management remain normal and that its directors and senior executives continue to perform their duties. [[CNInfo](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-11-05/1225658457.PDF)]

- **Hemay Pharma delays Hong Kong IPO:** Hemay Pharma-B postponed its planned 0054 HK listing after the public offer closed, citing current market conditions, and will refund application money. The biotech had sought to issue 55.21m H shares at HK$15.50–18.90, raising up to HK$1.043bn before expenses, with listing originally scheduled for 9 November. [[The Paper](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611043922220133.html)]

- **Hong Kong buybacks:** Pop Mart 9992 HK repurchased 360,000 shares for about HK$49.46m at HK$135.80–138.40. COSCO Shipping Holdings 1919 HK bought back 3.188m H shares for HK$57.68m at HK$17.88–18.26, with the shares intended for cancellation. [[Zhito Finance](https://cn.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/article-3647756)] [[Tiger Brokers](https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/1179906124)]

- **Qualcomm–SpaceX 5G agreement:** Qualcomm signed what it called its largest-ever 5G deal, supplying platforms and software for “tens of thousands” of Starlink Mobile terrestrial base stations. Deployment is expected from 2027; financial terms were not disclosed, with potential read-through to Chinese telecom-equipment and satellite-connectivity names. [[CLS](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611043922306747.html)]

- **US semiconductor earnings:** Qualcomm guided fiscal first-quarter revenue to US$10.4bn–11.2bn versus a US$10.55bn consensus; automotive revenue rose 89% to US$1.99bn last quarter. Arm reported adjusted EPS of US$0.52 versus US$0.47 expected and guided next-quarter revenue to US$1.49bn–1.59bn, bracketing the US$1.53bn estimate. [[CLS—Qualcomm](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611053922378640.html)] [[CLS—Arm](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611053922384357.html)]

- **Europe eases proposed 2035 auto target:** European Parliament committee lawmakers backed reducing the proposed new-vehicle emissions cut from 100% to 87.5%, potentially preserving room for combustion and plug-in-hybrid sales after 2035. The proposal still requires a full parliamentary vote and negotiations with member states; it marginally reduces regulatory pressure on European automakers and Chinese EV exporters. [[CLS](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611053922356517.html)]

The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index gained 3.49%, with Alibaba +4.91%, Baidu +4.50%, Tencent ADR +3.99%, NetEase +4.33% and JD.com +3.05%. The dollar index fell 0.3% to 101.566; the US 10-year yield declined 7.4bp to 4.888% and the two-year fell 5.6bp to 4.575%, supporting precious-metals names but weighing on oil producers at the HK open.
