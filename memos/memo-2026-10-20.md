Morning Market Memo | 20 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open
(covers 16 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 20 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

- HK market reopening: Hong Kong’s cash market was closed Monday for the Chung Yeung Festival holiday and reopens today; there was consequently no Monday southbound or Hong Kong cash-market session. Monday’s post-A-share-close policy and company announcements receive their first Hong Kong pricing this morning. [[HKEX](https://www.hkex.com.hk/News/HKEX-Calendar?acttype=%7BF4C40F45-DA56-41A7-A740-F86B26DA5349%7D&currenttab=search-result&datefrom=2026%E5%B9%B49%E6%9C%8820%E6%97%A5&dateto=2027%E5%B9%B43%E6%9C%8819%E6%97%A5&defaultdate=2026-09-20&order=asc&sc_lang=zh-HK)]

- PBOC – new liquidity backstop: Governor Pan Gongsheng said the PBOC, financial regulator and CSRC will create a facility for large redemptions at non-bank institutions, covering fund managers, bank wealth-management subsidiaries and their parent institutions. It would activate when redemptions and bond sales form a negative loop, subject to high-grade collateral and macroprudential conditions. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193899924797.html)]

- Tech finance and market opening: Pan also announced plans to expand SLF eligibility, connect the interbank and exchange bond markets and make renminbi purchases and sales easier for overseas financial institutions. An 11-department guideline supports international venture-capital centres in Beijing, Shanghai and the Greater Bay Area and greater use of state and social-security patient capital for emerging industries. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193899924797.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193900262027.html)]

- CSRC – 12-point capital-markets package: Chairman Wu Qing outlined measures including a fund-industry monitoring centre, AI compliance rules for securities firms, advance-compensation guidance, revised refinancing rules and support for broker consolidation. The package also envisages Northbound Stock Connect access for REITs and renminbi trading counters, plus easier QFII operations. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193900060188.html)]

- Active ETFs – first batch approved: Regulators approved 18 actively managed ETFs, split evenly between Shanghai and Shenzhen, with products spanning balanced, value, dividend and growth strategies. Managers may begin publishing sale notices today, adding a new listed format for active equity allocation. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193899972814.html)]

- Export controls – drones and tunnelling equipment: MOFCOM and other departments adjusted controls on specified drone items and imposed controls on shield-tunnelling equipment, while separately revising dredger controls. The shield-machine and dredger measures take effect on 18 November, affecting dual-use machinery, aerospace components and related exporters. [[MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfyrth/art/2026/art_1e581b3d58ff46dabe8c44abab408d6c.html)]

- GCL Technology – lithium-materials acquisition: GCL Technology (3800 HK) signed a non-binding memorandum to acquire about 81.91% of Sichuan GCL Lithium Technology for an estimated RMB2.5bn. The proposed consideration comprises roughly RMB650m cash and RMB1.85bn in new shares, potentially moving GCL further into lithium iron phosphate cathode materials. [[Viewpoint](https://www.guandian.cn/article/20261019/615150.html)] [[Investing.com](https://hk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/article-1704982)]

- Xiechuang Data – RMB37.49bn computing contract: Xiechuang Data’s wholly owned subsidiary signed a cloud-computing service agreement with an undisclosed customer carrying an estimated maximum value of RMB37.49bn. Orders run for 60 months after acceptance, although the company flagged financing, delivery and customer-acceptance risks. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193900108276.html)]

- Hygon Information – semiconductor earnings: Hygon Information reported nine-month revenue of RMB14.96bn, up 57.6%, and attributable profit of RMB2.85bn, up 45.1%. Third-quarter revenue rose 45.5% to RMB5.86bn and profit increased 37.9% to RMB1.05bn, providing a fresh earnings read-through for domestic CPUs and AI accelerators. [[Eastmoney](https://emwap.eastmoney.com/a/202610193900100414.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193900262027.html)]

- Pengding Holdings – PCB buyback: Pengding Holdings approved an RMB300m–500m share repurchase at no more than RMB80 per share, funded with internal or raised funds. The shares will be used for employee ownership or equity incentives, relevant to the Apple-supply-chain and high-end PCB complex. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193900221394.html)]

- Shengyi Electronics – post-close repurchase: Shengyi Electronics plans to buy back RMB100m–200m of shares at no more than RMB179.34 each over 12 months. The stock will be allocated to equity incentives or an employee ownership plan, adding another post-close capital-management catalyst for the AI-server PCB chain. - Three Gorges New Materials – electrolyte acquisition: Three Gorges New Materials plans to acquire 100% of Zhuhai Saiwei Electronic Materials through shares and cash and raise up to RMB1bn from no more than 35 investors. The target produces lithium-ion battery electrolytes; Three Gorges New Materials resumes trading today. [[Eastmoney](https://emwap.eastmoney.com/a/202610193900100414.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610193900262027.html)]
