Morning Market Memo | 15 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 14 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 15 Oct 2026 06:20 HKT research cutoff)

- **High-level opening signal:** Premier Li Qiang said China will further lower market-access barriers, import more high-quality products—particularly higher-value-added goods—and support capable Chinese companies investing overseas. The message at the 140th Canton Fair supports exporters, logistics, cross-border commerce and consumer-import names. [[MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_cd539a3d276d4881bae11bbbfc1b5a2c.html)]

- **PBOC adds six-month liquidity:** The central bank will conduct RMB700bn of six-month outright reverse repos today; against RMB500bn maturing this month, the operation represents a RMB200bn net injection after two months of neutral rollovers. The move supports interbank liquidity as local-government bond issuance and new policy-finance instruments increase funding demand. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610143895735585.html)]

- **Credit growth remained moderate:** September-end aggregate financing stock rose 7.1% year on year to RMB468.17tn, while aggregate financing added during the first nine months was RMB27.46tn, RMB2.63tn less than a year earlier. First-nine-month RMB lending was RMB11.52tn; M2 and M1 grew 7.2% and 3.2%, respectively, keeping attention on banks, brokers and domestic-demand sectors. [[PBOC via Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610143895517426.html)]

- **Anhui backs domestic memory leader:** Anhui Party Secretary Li Lecheng visited ChangXin Memory Technologies and called for stronger support to expand the company, build a broader integrated-circuit supply chain and accelerate critical-core-technology research with universities and institutes. The post-close message is relevant to domestic DRAM, semiconductor equipment and materials suppliers after another weak US memory-chip session. [[Sina](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/2868676035/aafc85c302001yl8s)]

- **Commercial space — Zhuque mission succeeds:** LandSpace’s improved Zhuque-2 Y4 rocket launched at 19:40 and placed ten satellites, including Guoxing Aerospace and optical-communications payloads, into their designated orbits. The fresh mission supports the commercial-launch, satellite-manufacturing and space-based optical-communications chains. [[CCTV via Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610143895636311.html)]

- **High-purity indium and InP capacity:** Wuhan Tianyuan Environmental Protection (301127 CH) plans a roughly RMB500mn base in Chuxiong capable of producing 200 tonnes annually of 6N/7N high-purity indium and 100 tonnes of polycrystalline indium phosphide. The project is relevant to compound semiconductors, optoelectronics and critical-mineral processing. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610143895692215.html)]

- **AI-PCB earnings remain strong:** Wus Printed Circuit (002463 CH) guided first-nine-month net profit to RMB5.45bn–5.70bn, up 100.5%–109.7%, citing high-speed switches, AI servers, high-performance computing and smart vehicles; its Thai unit also moved into profitability. Jinlu Electronics and Suntak Technology separately forecast substantial profit growth, providing fresh read-through for the PCB, copper-foil and AI-server supply chains. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610143895692215.html)]

- **Dongbai abandons optical-chip acquisition:** Dongbai Group (600693 CH) terminated its proposed RMB316mn purchase of a 52.6667% stake in Hangzhou Kuailing Optoelectronics after the parties failed to agree key terms. The reversal came only three days after the proposed cross-sector transaction was announced, removing the planned optical-detection-chip catalyst. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610143895733039.html)]

- **ChaPanda remains suspended:** ChaPanda (2555 HK) said operations remain normal and it is seeking legal advice while cooperating with the Hong Kong SFC over concerns that demand in its IPO may have been artificially created. Hang Seng Indexes will apply a HK$0.0001 calculation price on 22 October and remove the stock from affected index series after that close, effective 23 October. [[NBD via Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610143895815552.html)]

- **Overnight US markets and China ADRs:** The Dow fell 0.09%, the S&P 500 lost 1.11% and the Nasdaq dropped 1.88%; the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index slid 4.3%, with Nvidia and Meta down more than 3%, Micron, Seagate and Western Digital down more than 4%, and CoreWeave off more than 15%. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index fell 1.39%; Bilibili lost 4.74%, NIO 4.21%, Li Auto 4.17%, XPeng 3.38% and Baidu 2.25%, while Alibaba edged up 0.29%. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610153895886595.html)]

- **US inflation revives rate-hike pricing:** September headline CPI rose 3.7% year on year versus 3.6% expected, while core CPI increased 2.6% versus the 2.5% consensus; monthly readings were 0.6% and 0.3%. Futures briefly priced roughly a 44% probability of an October Fed hike, up from about 22% before the release, pressuring growth and long-duration technology shares. [[CLS via Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610143895769615.html)]

- **Cross-assets — oil higher, precious metals sharply lower:** WTI rose 1.09% to US$95.17 a barrel, while COMEX gold fell 1.20% to US$4,127.30 and silver dropped 6.26% to US$55.80. The dollar index gained 0.37% to 102.554; the US ten-year yield ended at 5.1854% and the two-year at 4.7975%, relevant to HK technology valuations, oil producers and precious-metal miners. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610153895886595.html)]

- **Hormuz shipping disruption intensifies:** Two UAE cargo vessels sank after attacks near the Strait of Hormuz during the past 24 hours, an escalation from the vessel damage and fires reported earlier in the conflict. The development keeps crude, tanker rates, marine insurance and airline fuel costs in focus for CNOOC/PetroChina, shipping names and Cathay Pacific. [[CLS via Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610153895880319.html)]

- **Fed Beige Book flags faster price transmission:** Four Federal Reserve districts reported accelerating price increases, with energy, tariffs and insurance costs continuing to move through supply chains. AI and data-centre investment still supported manufacturing and construction, but some companies were adjusting hiring as AI raised productivity—mixed read-through for exporters, AI hardware and consumer names.
