Morning Market Memo | 17 Aug 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 14 Aug 16:00 HKT close → 17 Aug 06:56 HKT research cutoff)

- China credit – household borrowing stays weak: The PBOC said seven-month aggregate financing reached RMB22.25tn, RMB1.74tn below a year earlier, while RMB loans increased RMB10.38tn. Household loans fell RMB827.1bn, versus an RMB11tn increase for corporates; July-end M2 and M1 grew 7.7% and 4.0%, respectively. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608143841758688.html)]

- Cross-border cash pools – nationwide rollout: The PBOC and SAFE expanded unified renminbi and foreign-currency cash-pooling nationwide, allowing multinational groups to centralise collections, payments and internal funding. Applicants generally need annual cross-border receipts and payments of at least RMB700m, or qualifying domestic and overseas revenue; property companies and local-government financing vehicles are excluded. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608143841795687.html)]

- Chips and machine tools – tax deferral: Four ministries said qualifying integrated-circuit and industrial-machine-tool companies may spread gains from non-cash asset exchanges evenly across as many as five years. The measure applies to transactions from 1 January 2026 through 31 December 2028, supporting equipment upgrades and industry consolidation. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608163842551542.html)]

- Hong Kong IPO pipeline: The CSRC issued overseas-listing filing notices after Friday’s close for Transsion and Junxin Environmental, while Longsys, Biwin Storage, SinoCellTech and Autel Intelligent have also submitted Hong Kong applications. Thirty-two A-share companies have listed in Hong Kong this year, raising HK$229.05bn—already above full-year 2025—with 30 subsequently entering Stock Connect. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608173842574476.html)]

- Kweichow Moutai – first profit decline: Moutai reported first-half revenue of RMB90.70bn, up 1.47%, while attributable profit fell 1.95% to RMB44.52bn; second-quarter profit was RMB17.27bn, down 36% sequentially. The print is a fresh read-through for premium baijiu demand and sector pricing. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608153842242593.html)]

- Lithium – Canadian review risk: Hualian Holdings said Canada’s foreign-investment authority may begin a national-security review of its approximately US$175m acquisition of an 80% interest in Argentina’s Arizaro salt-lake project. The company cannot yet assess the impact or whether the deal will receive final clearance. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608163842526052.html)]

- Zhongwei Semiconductor – AI and auto demand: The MCU supplier reported first-half revenue of RMB726m, up 44.01%, and attributable profit of RMB172m, up 98.48%. Management attributed the increase to demand from AI, robotics and automotive electronics alongside larger-customer wins. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608163842526052.html)]

- Satellite Chemical – earnings acceleration: First-half revenue rose 30.92% to RMB30.71bn and attributable profit increased 126.94% to RMB6.23bn. The result provides a fresh earnings benchmark for the chemicals and energy-materials complex. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608163842551542.html)]

- Unigroup Guoxin – stronger second quarter: The security-chip and semiconductor company reported first-half revenue of RMB3.44bn, up 13%, and attributable profit of RMB798m, up 15.29%. Implied second-quarter profit rose about 38% sequentially to RMB463m. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608163842551542.html)]

- Optics – companies temper market expectations: Focuslight said optical-communications and consumer-electronics businesses still represent a relatively small share of group revenue, while Yangtze Optical Electronic said it has few telecom-fibre orders and no newly added major communications customers. The disclosures are relevant after strong trading in the A-share optical and AI-hardware chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608163842551542.html)]

- Frequency Precision Laser – STAR listing Tuesday: The laser maker will list on 18 August after issuing 10m shares at RMB186.88 each; initial unrestricted float will be about 7.62m shares. The debut adds another high-priced semiconductor and precision-manufacturing name to the STAR pipeline. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608163842526052.html)]

- Nvidia optical switching – mass production: Nvidia said its Spectrum-X Ethernet silicon-photonics switches have entered full production, cutting the number of lasers fourfold and power consumption fivefold while increasing mean time between failures tenfold. The announcement is relevant for China and Hong Kong optical modules, switches and data-centre power suppliers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608163842551542.html)]

- Hormuz – Iran and Oman agree transit plan: Iran’s foreign-ministry spokesman said the two countries had agreed a navigation arrangement for the Strait of Hormuz despite US objections. The implementation details remain unclear, but the development is directly relevant to oil, shipping and airline names. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608153842447012.html)]

- US consumer – July retail sales miss: Retail sales fell 0.6% month on month against expectations for a 0.1% increase, after a revised 0.2% gain previously. The weak print raises demand concerns for exporters and consumer-facing China ADRs, although it also reduced expectations for additional Federal Reserve tightening.
