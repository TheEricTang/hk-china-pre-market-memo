- **Oil – Iran talks trigger sharp reversal:** WTI and Brent futures were down about 6% in early Asian trading after President Trump said the US and Iran would hold talks on 3 August. OPEC+ separately agreed to raise September production quotas by 188,000 barrels a day, adding pressure on oil producers while lowering near-term fuel-cost risk for airlines and transport names. - **Overnight US markets:** The Dow rose 0.53%, the S&P 500 gained 0.70% and the Nasdaq added 1.00%; Amazon jumped more than 15% after earnings while Apple fell more than 7%. COMEX gold lost 1.49% to US$4,098.60/oz, the US 10-year yield rose to 4.7347% and the DXY closed at 99.917; US index futures were up 0.4%–0.8% by the Asian morning. A verified same-session Golden Dragon close was unavailable in the opened wraps and is omitted rather than estimated. [[AP](https://apnews.com/article/wall-street-stocks-dow-nasdaq-37d8d182f02f0fcdcf9f7db67e6dfadd)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608023829175248.html)]

- **State Council – domestic demand and infrastructure:** Premier Li Qiang chaired a post-close State Council meeting calling for full use of existing policies and timely, practical incremental measures to expand domestic demand. The meeting prioritised new power-grid and logistics networks and approved a draft widening housing-provident-fund withdrawal and use, relevant to grid equipment, logistics and property consumption. [[Xinhua](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313828498511.html)]

- **Nuclear power – eight new units approved:** The four projects approved by the State Council cover eight reactors in Zhejiang, Guangdong, Liaoning and Shandong, with estimated combined investment above RMB170bn. The approvals support the nuclear-equipment, engineering, construction and power-operator supply chains. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313828500821.html)]

- **PBOC – moderately loose policy retained:** The central bank’s second-half work meeting pledged ample liquidity, low aggregate financing costs and timely adjustment of monetary-policy tools. It also said the two capital-market support facilities would remain in use and reiterated support for Hong Kong’s role as an offshore renminbi hub. [[PBOC](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608023829117083.html)]

- **Financial institutions – four regulators issue governance rules:** The financial regulator, PBOC, CSRC and Ministry of Finance jointly released 22 measures intended to establish clearer responsibilities, stronger risk controls and more effective incentives by 2029. The framework applies across banks, insurers and securities firms. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313828300239.html)]

- **SAFE – further opening and external-shock controls:** The foreign-exchange regulator’s second-half meeting called for steadily expanding institutional opening while strengthening the economy’s defences against external shocks. SAFE also prioritised tighter foreign-exchange supervision and improved reserve-management capabilities. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608023829127720.html)]

- **US entity list – MOFCOM protests new restrictions:** China’s Commerce Ministry criticised Washington’s addition of more than 40 Chinese entities to the Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act list, calling the move unsupported economic coercion. The additions create fresh supply-chain and export risks for affected industrial companies. [[MOFCOM](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608013828990530.html)]

- **Solar – regulator targets below-cost competition:** The State Administration for Market Regulation instructed 27 photovoltaic companies to strengthen cost accounting, conduct compliance reviews and resist malicious low-price competition. Authorities warned that companies ignoring guidance or seriously disrupting pricing would face enforcement, reinforcing the industry’s anti-involution drive. [[SAMR](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313828442372.html)]

- **Drug procurement – 265 companies directly qualify:** The first-round results of China’s 12th national centralised drug procurement awarded proposed selection status to 265 companies, with unsuccessful bidders offered a second quotation round. The outcome is relevant to generic-drug pricing, hospital access and volume expectations across mainland pharmaceutical names. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313828406652.html)]

- **Telecom distribution – third-party SIM sales halted:** China Mobile, China Telecom and China Unicom said that, from 1 August, online SIM-card orders may be processed only through operators’ official apps and websites. Third-party internet channels can no longer handle applications, shifting customer acquisition toward the three carriers’ controlled platforms. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608023829175248.html)]

- **AMEC – five-year equipment expansion plan:** AMEC chairman Gerald Yin said the company aims to cover at least 60% of high-end semiconductor-equipment categories within five years, spanning more than 100 products. The company plans to expand total production space to 900,000 square metres, with capacity of at least RMB70bn. [[The Paper](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608023829087865.html)]

- **GigaDevice – RMB1bn–2bn cancellation buyback:** GigaDevice proposed repurchasing shares at no more than RMB750 each, with all purchased stock to be cancelled and registered capital reduced. Zhongchuang Zhiling, Yabao Pharmaceutical and Beijing Junzheng also announced or received proposals for sizeable repurchases, extending the shareholder-return theme. [[STCN](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608023829088775.html)]

- **July EV deliveries – Leapmotor crosses 100,000:** BYD reported approximately 419,000 July vehicle sales, while Leapmotor delivered 101,267 vehicles, its first month above 100,000; NIO, XPeng and Li Auto each exceeded 30,000. The weekend disclosures provide fresh volume comparisons for today’s HK-listed auto open. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608033829219922.html)]

- **Huaxin Cement – Philippines acquisition:** Huaxin Cement plans an initial US$527m purchase giving it 67.62% of Holcim Philippines, based on an enterprise value of US$780m. A second-stage option covering another 31.38% has a minimum consideration of US$280m, expanding Huaxin’s Southeast Asian cement footprint. [[STCN](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608023829152414.html)]

- **HKEX – five-year China government bond futures launch:** Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing begins trading five-year Chinese government bond futures today. The contract is intended to complement Bond Connect and Swap Connect and broaden offshore renminbi interest-rate hedging tools. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608033829195383.html)]

- **Morgan Stanley – AI focus shifts toward applications and resources:** China chief economist Robin Xing described AI investing as entering a mid-cycle pause rather than suffering fundamental deterioration. He expects attention to rotate from upstream computing hardware toward revenue-producing AI applications and “HALO” resources including energy, storage and strategic raw materials. [[STCN](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608023829176875.html)]
