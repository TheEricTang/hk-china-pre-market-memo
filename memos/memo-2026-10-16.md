The volume is less than one day of global diesel consumption, leaving refining margins, transport costs and oil-linked inflation in focus. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610163897348317.html)]

- **Alibaba options trading:** Cboe Options Exchange will add early-morning and post-close trading sessions for selected equity and ETF options from Monday. Alibaba, TSMC, Nvidia, Apple and Tesla are among the covered names, extending price discovery around Asian trading hours. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610163897348317.html)]

- **Apple–China engagement:** Commerce Minister Wang Wentao met Apple executive chairman Tim Cook after the close to discuss China–US trade, artificial intelligence and Apple’s China operations. Cook said Apple remained positive on China’s prospects and would further expand its business in the country; the meeting is relevant to Apple suppliers including Luxshare Precision and AAC Technologies. - **Premium EV safety review:** MIIT, the Cyberspace Administration and the State Administration for Market Regulation formed a joint team to investigate online reports of a brake-pedal bracket fracture in the Maextro V800. The authorities said the matter would be handled under applicable rules, putting the vehicle and component supply chain under scrutiny. [[Xinhua](https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/20261015/ee3d7afa4df74e2186f25601a17e4ae7/c.html)]

- **Eoptolink buyback proposal:** Eoptolink Technology’s controlling shareholder and chairman proposed an RMB800mn–1bn A-share repurchase, funded with company or borrowed resources. The proposed programme would run for 12 months after board approval and provides a fresh catalyst for the optical-module chain. [[China News Service](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/5993531560/1653e08a802001ppnu?finpagefr=p_104)]

- **Piotech acquisition and repurchase:** Semiconductor-equipment maker Piotech plans to acquire Wuxi Shangji and two holding vehicles for RMB2.5bn, funded through shares and cash, and raise up to RMB1.82bn in supporting capital. Its chairman separately proposed an RMB200mn–300mn repurchase for incentives, employee ownership or capital reduction. [[National Business Daily](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610153897293314.html)]

- **Domestic semiconductor earnings:** Fudan Microelectronics forecast nine-month net profit of RMB950mn–1.15bn, while Hygon Information guided to RMB2.71bn–2.98bn. Xi’an Yisi estimated third-quarter net profit of about RMB441mn as wafer sales reached roughly 8.3mn units in the first nine months, supporting the domestic FPGA, AI-computing and semiconductor-material chains. - **Lithium-material recovery:** Minmetals New Energy reported nine-month revenue of RMB12.50bn and net profit of RMB462mn, reversing the prior-year loss as orders, utilisation and product margins improved. Management attributed the recovery to expanding battery-material demand and better pass-through of higher raw-material prices, although third-quarter profit moderated sequentially.
