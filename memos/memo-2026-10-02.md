- Fiscal policy – incremental measures under study: Finance Minister Lan Fo’an said the ministry will formulate targeted incremental policies, accelerate spending and focus more on domestic demand. Measures include expanding consumption and investment interest subsidies, using unused local-government debt ceilings and issuing RMB300bn of special treasury bonds to replenish core Tier-1 capital at central financial institutions. - Wuhan housing rules – completed-home sales and funding flexibility: Wuhan’s new implementation rules give priority to completed-home sales on land transferred after 28 August; projects using presales generally must reach structural topping-out. Eligible developers may replace up to 30% of required completion funds with bank guarantees, while deposits on completed homes are generally capped at 5% of the purchase price. [[Wuhan Housing Bureau](https://zgj.wuhan.gov.cn/zwdt/tzgg/202609/t20260930_2856363.shtml)] [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103383600.html)]

- Golden Week travel – rail outperforms overall flows: The transport ministry estimated 334.1m cross-regional trips on 1 October, up 52.3% from the previous day but 0.5% below last year’s comparable day. Rail traffic was expected to reach 24.8m, up 7.2% year on year, while air passenger volume was estimated at 2.51m, up 0.3%; the mix is relevant to airlines, rail operators, hotels and online travel platforms. [[Beijing Daily](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2026/10/01/11980902.shtml)]

- Huawei Mate 90 – flagship launch during the HK holiday: Huawei launched and began selling the Mate 90 series from RMB5,999, with the top 2TB RS model priced at RMB18,999. The range uses Kirin 9030, 9035 and 9050 Pro chips; Huawei also said devices running HarmonyOS 6 or 7 have exceeded 90m, providing a fresh catalyst for the domestic semiconductor, optics and handset-component chains.
