- Capital-market policy: CSRC Chairman Wu Qing said social-security, insurance and pension funds have purchased more than RMB1.5tn of A-shares since 2024, while stocks and bonds accounted for nearly 50% of new aggregate financing in the first nine months. He said new-economy companies now represent more than half of A-share company numbers, revenue and market value. [[CSRC](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c106311/c7670983/content.shtml)]

- Derivatives rules take effect: China’s first CSRC departmental rules dedicated to derivatives trading become effective today, covering swaps, forwards and non-standard options. Securities and futures firms must meet capital, suitability, margin, reporting and risk-control requirements; non-compliant businesses cannot add new transactions and must run off existing positions. [[CSRC](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7632835/content.shtml)]
