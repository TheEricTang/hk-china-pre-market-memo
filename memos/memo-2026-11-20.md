- APEC Shenzhen outcome: Leaders issued the post-close “Shenzhen Outlook”, supporting regional economic integration, trade digitalisation, supply-chain resilience, cross-border data cooperation and responsible AI. The document provides policy support for Chinese logistics, digital-commerce, cloud and AI companies expanding across Asia-Pacific markets. - LPR due at 09:00 HKT: China’s November loan prime rates will be released before the Hong Kong open. Consensus expects the one-year and five-year-plus rates to remain at 3.00% and 3.50%, respectively; any change would immediately affect banks, developers and consumer finance names. - Alibaba results and domestic chips: Alibaba 9988 HK reported fiscal second-quarter revenue of RMB271.52bn, up 10%, while adjusted net profit rose 92% to RMB19.91bn but missed the RMB26.61bn estimate cited by local media. Alibaba Cloud’s external commercial revenue increased 64%, and CEO Eddie Wu said the next-generation T-Head chip should support large-scale model training from the first quarter of 2027. [[NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-19/4653034.html)] [[Alibaba](https://alihome.alibaba-inc.com/zh-HK/document-2060147792046718976)]

Marketing-services revenue grew 12% while transaction-services revenue fell 12%; the ADR closed 11.04% higher after profit beat market expectations. [[PDD](https://pinduoduo.gcs-web.com/static-files/cdb6628b-4704-45fa-88e3-1bc2447e7239)] [[CNMO](https://internet.cnmo.com/finance/822871.html)]
