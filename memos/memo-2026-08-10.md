- China inflation – services offset food weakness: July CPI rose 0.5% year on year and fell 0.1% month on month. Services gained 0.7% year on year, while food declined 1.5% and pork fell 13.3%, leaving a mixed read-through for consumer, restaurant and livestock names. - PBOC gold and reserves: China’s official gold holdings increased for a 21st consecutive month, rising from 75.44m to 76.08m ounces in July. Foreign-exchange reserves edged up US$2.5bn to US$3.4188tn, relevant to gold producers and the renminbi opening tone. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608073835185575.html)]

- Beijing property easing: From Saturday, non-Beijing households need only one year of social-security or tax payments to buy within the Fifth Ring, down from two years. The maximum housing-provident-fund loan for couples was doubled to RMB2.4m for first homes and RMB2m for second homes, with combined policy additions lifting the ceiling to RMB3.4m in qualifying cases. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608083835847895.html)]

- LOF delisting rules: The Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges sought feedback on mandatory delisting arrangements for commodity-futures, QDII and persistently small listed open-ended funds. A small LOF would trigger delisting after 60 consecutive trading days with on-exchange net assets below RMB10m; commodity and QDII LOFs would receive a transition period ending no later than 31 December 2027. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608073835354079.html)]

- New technology ETFs: Sixteen fund managers filed applications on Friday evening for ChiNext computing-infrastructure and financial-technology ETFs. The first product filings tied to the two sector indices may broaden listed funding channels for A-share computing-power and fintech constituents. DeepSeek’s parent, the national social-security fund and several large internet groups feature among pre-IPO or strategic investors, keeping robotics suppliers in focus.
