Morning Market Memo | 08 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 07 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 08 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

- **Overnight US markets:** The Dow fell 0.66%, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq each lost 0.22%. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 1.15%, with TSMC down 2.09%, Qualcomm down 2.16% and optical-equipment names broadly weaker; Micron bucked the move with a 4.06% gain. The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index added 0.12% as JD.com rose 2.00% and NIO gained 1.72%, while Alibaba and Baidu fell 2.07% and 2.01%. WTI declined 0.56% to US$88.94, COMEX gold fell 1.20% and silver dropped 2.50%. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610083889028945.html)]

- **Fed minutes – inflation still elevated:** Minutes from the Federal Reserve’s September meeting showed officials unanimously backed the 25-basis-point rate increase to 3.75%–4.00%, citing resilient spending and inflation above the 2% target. The firmer policy backdrop remains relevant for HK growth valuations, although overnight losses in US equities were contained. [[Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcminutes20260916.htm)]

- **Holiday consumption – smart products led demand:** Ministry of Commerce data showed embodied-intelligence robot and smart-glasses sales on monitored platforms rose 1.3 times and 1.0 times year on year respectively during 1–6 October. Consumer trade-in programmes reached 6.392 million users and generated RMB33.48 billion of sales, including RMB12.34 billion of appliances and RMB8.68 billion of digital and smart products, supporting appliance, electronics and robotics names. [[Xinhua](https://www.xinhuanet.com/20261007/7dad035689d349ea907e599a79d01767/c.html)]

- **Holiday travel – nationwide mobility reached 2.144 billion trips:** The Ministry of Transport estimated average daily cross-regional passenger movements rose 0.7% year on year to 306 million during the seven-day break. More than 1.96 billion trips were made by road, while new-energy vehicle traffic increased 35%, providing a steady read-through for toll-road, railway, airline, tourism and charging-infrastructure operators. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610073888963669.html)]

- **AI hardware – high-end demand outperformed:** Suning reported a 50% year-on-year increase in core-store traffic and more than 40% growth in AI-appliance sales during the holiday. JD.com saw transaction value for premium smartphones priced above RMB6,000 rise more than 30%, while Taobao said AI-toy sales increased over 150%; the data favour high-end handsets, AI PCs, smart appliances and consumer-robotics supply chains over low-end devices. [[Securities Daily](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610083889015687.html)]

- **Beijing holiday spending:** Beijing received 23.45 million visitors, generating RMB29.49 billion in tourism spending; its 70 monitored shopping districts separately recorded 60.35 million visits and RMB15.68 billion of consumption. Performances, sporting events and suburban tourism were highlighted as incremental demand drivers for travel platforms, hotels, restaurants and retailers. [[Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/20261007/970da97e688942688410aafa2c163d71/c.html)]

- **Tencent buyback:** Tencent (700 HK) disclosed that it repurchased 238,000 shares for HK$100.3 million after Wednesday’s session, at HK$419.40–425.80 per share. The shares are intended for cancellation, extending the company’s regular capital-return support following recent weakness in the stock. [[Futu](https://news.futunn.com/post/1000650021/tencent-00700-repurchased-238000-shares-for-hk-100-3-million)]

- **Huahai Pharmaceutical – profit warning sharply positive:** Huahai (600521 CH) expects nine-month net profit of RMB1.028–1.103 billion, up approximately 170%–190%, with recurring profit rising 145%–175%. Growth came from APIs, domestic formulations and a turnaround in US formulations, alongside arbitration, disposal and research-cost effects; the company also recorded roughly RMB130 million of foreign-exchange losses. [[Sina](https://vip.stock.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12631049&stockid=600521)]

- **Allist Pharmaceuticals – lung-cancer trial missed endpoint:** Allist said a Phase III study of furmonertinib monotherapy in untreated EGFR exon-20 insertion non-small-cell lung cancer failed to meet its primary progression-free-survival endpoint. Management and partner ArriVent are reviewing the full data; separately, the chairman proposed a share repurchase, partially offsetting the negative clinical read-through. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610073888917970.html)]

- **Asieris – European licensing deal:** Asieris Pharmaceuticals (688176 CH) granted Theramex exclusive rights to cervical-lesion therapy APL-1702 across Europe, Australia, New Zealand and Turkey. Asieris will receive a US$15 million upfront payment and a US$11 million near-term registration milestone, with total potential consideration exceeding US$250 million plus tiered royalties. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610073888917970.html)]

- **Post-close restructurings:** Beibu Gulf Port plans to buy 100% of Henggang Terminal using shares and cash, while Sunway plans to acquire control of industrial-gas supplier Qiyuan Gas; both stocks will suspend trading from today. Rongfeng Holding will also halt for up to two sessions while its controlling shareholder considers a transaction that could change control, putting port assets, industrial gases and small-cap restructurings in focus. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610073888917970.html)]

- **Computing infrastructure orders:** Roman Technology’s controlled subsidiary signed a RMB277 million procurement framework for computing equipment and related technical services. Honglu Steel separately won RMB278 million of steel-structure work linked to a data-centre project and a lithium-battery anode-material facility, offering fresh order read-through for computing infrastructure and specialist construction suppliers. Garden Bio also signed an agreement with Guangzhou Laboratory to establish a company developing and commercialising broad-spectrum antiviral candidates based on 25-hydroxycholesterol, with Garden holding at least 51%.
