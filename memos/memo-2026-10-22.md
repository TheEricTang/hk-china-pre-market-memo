Morning Market Memo | 22 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 21 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 22 Oct 2026 06:36 HKT research cutoff)

- **Technology–industry integration:** China’s central and state council offices issued a 17-point plan to accelerate commercialisation of research. Priorities include breakthroughs in integrated circuits, industrial machinery, advanced materials and biomanufacturing; development of low-altitude aviation, embodied AI, brain–computer interfaces and 6G; broader “AI+” adoption; and government venture funds investing earlier and for longer in hard technology. [[MOST](https://www.most.gov.cn/xxgk/xinxifenlei/fdzdgknr/fgzc/gfxwj/gfxwj2026/202610/t20261021_197540.html)]

- **China–Russia cooperation:** Premier Li Qiang and Russian Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin agreed to advance investment, energy, transport, aerospace, finance and industrial cooperation, while developing AI, biotechnology and digital-economy projects. The premiers signed a joint communiqué and oversaw additional agreements covering trade, space, technology and education. [[Foreign Ministry](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/wjb_673085/zzjg_673183/xws_674681/xgxw_674683/202610/t20261021_12044564.shtml)]

- **Active ETFs:** The Shanghai Stock Exchange convened the nine managers behind China’s first active ETFs and seven brokerages to prepare for orderly launches and insurance-fund participation. SSE-listed ETFs totalled 943 products and RMB3.35tn at end-September, with long-term investors holding nearly RMB1.7tn; the new products add an exchange-traded active-equity channel. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223903047816.html)]

- **Beijing Stock Exchange reform:** The CSRC said it will steadily implement market-value-based allocation for BSE IPOs and improve the exchange’s issuance, trading, refinancing and M&A arrangements. The measures are intended to broaden institutional participation and financing access for innovative small and medium-sized companies. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610223903054856.html)]

- **Bank wealth management:** Outstanding bank wealth-management products reached RMB35.37tn at end-September, up 10.08% year on year and RMB1.71tn from June. Wealth-management subsidiaries accounted for RMB32.92tn, while the industry generated RMB141.7bn of investor returns during the third quarter and increased its allocation to public funds. [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4230725.html)] [[CLS](https://m.cls.cn/detail/2511155)]

- **CATL buyback:** CATL (300750 CH/3750 HK) repurchased 3.49m A-shares on Wednesday for about RMB800m at an average RMB229.32. Cumulative purchases under its RMB20bn–40bn cancellation programme reached RMB4.61bn, providing a fresh signal after investor concern about near-term battery demand and margins. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610213903034352.html)]

- **China Life restructuring:** China Life (2628 HK/601628 CH) plans to sell a 33.73% stake in China Life Pension to its parent for RMB4.146bn, while its asset-management unit will sell another 3.53% for RMB434m. China Life expects an approximately RMB2.57bn gain and will remain the pension company’s second-largest shareholder with 37.01%. [[RTHK](https://gbcode.rthk.hk/TuniS/news.rthk.hk/rthk/ch/component/k2/1874813-20261021.htm)]

- **Shanshan battery-material expansion:** Shanshan (600884 CH) proposed a RMB4bn private placement fully subscribed by controlling shareholder Conch Group. RMB2.5bn will fund a 150,000-tonne annual lithium-battery anode-material complex, including 50,000 tonnes of graphitisation capacity, with RMB1.5bn allocated to working capital. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610213903013742.html)]

- **Xiaomi buyback:** Xiaomi (1810 HK) bought back 1.9m shares for HK$50.95m at HK$26.76–26.84 per share. It has repurchased approximately 138m shares, or 0.53% of the share base at the authorisation date, under the current mandate. [[Zhitong](https://cn.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/article-3619001)]

- **Optical components earnings:** Shijia Photons (688313 CH) reported third-quarter revenue of RMB1.23bn, up 116.06%, and net profit of RMB271m, up 226.19%. The company attributed the acceleration to AI-computing demand and stronger data-communications orders, relevant to the optical-module and datacentre supply chains. [[Eastmoney](https://data.eastmoney.com/notices/detail/688313/AN202610211830763307.html)]

- **Semiconductor testing equipment:** Hangzhou Changchuan Technology (300604 CH) forecast nine-month net profit of RMB1.715bn–1.815bn, up 98.17%–109.73%. Management cited strong semiconductor demand, full orders and scale benefits, providing another positive read-through for domestic testing and packaging equipment suppliers. [[Sina](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12653220&stockid=300604)]

- **Cross-border payments:** Zhejiang China Commodities City (600415 CH) said Yiwu Pay processed more than US$7.51bn during the first nine months, up 98%. Chinagoods added over 580,000 registered buyers, although group third-quarter net profit fell 16.14%, leaving payments and digital trade as the main incremental indicators. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610213902680294.html)]

- **Broker call – China Merchants Bank:** JPMorgan raised China Merchants Bank’s (3968 HK) target price to HK$63 from HK$62 and maintained an overweight rating. It trimmed its 2026 earnings estimate by 0.6% but raised the 2027 forecast by 1.1%, reflecting a broadly stable medium-term outlook. [[Sina](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-10-21/doc-inivysyn8045738.shtml)]
