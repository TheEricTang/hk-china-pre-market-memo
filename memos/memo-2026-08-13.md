- PBOC signals further support: The second-quarter monetary-policy report said the central bank will prepare practical incremental measures, strengthen counter-cyclical adjustment and keep liquidity ample. It highlighted a RMB1tn relending facility for private enterprises and said June new corporate-loan rates were about 3.0%, roughly 20bp lower year on year. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608123839277092.html)]

- Liquidity operations – tax-period support: The PBOC will offer overnight reverse repos on 14 August and 17–19 August, with a daily ceiling of RMB600bn. The new operations are intended to manage tax-payment pressure and the RMB1tn six-month outright reverse-repo maturity due on Friday. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608123839209213.html)]

- Tencent – revenue tops RMB200bn: Second-quarter revenue rose 11% to RMB204.8bn while attributable profit increased 0.7% to RMB56.0bn. AI infrastructure prepayments contributed to negative free cash flow of RMB13.8bn; excluding those payments, free cash flow was RMB37.6bn, and Tencent said it has substantially increased third-quarter computing-power procurement. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608123839188380.html)]

- Tencent buybacks and user base: The company spent HK$24.4bn repurchasing shares during the first half. Combined WeChat and Weixin monthly active accounts reached 1.439bn at quarter-end, up 2% year on year, supporting the distribution case for its AI products and advertising tools. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608123839188380.html)]

- Pop Mart – Duan Yongping vehicle raises stake: A post-close HKEX disclosure showed H&H International Investment, managed by Duan Yongping, increased its Pop Mart holding from 5.55% to 7.70% on 6 August. - Antong Holdings – control may shift to China Merchants: Sinotrans Container Lines and China Merchants Port have nominated more than half of the company’s proposed board. If shareholders approve the slate, Sinotrans would become the controlling shareholder and China Merchants Group the ultimate controller, providing a fresh restructuring catalyst for shipping and logistics. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608123839346235.html)]

- CSPC Innovation – AstraZeneca payment received: Subsidiary Jushi Biotech received a US$30mn upfront payment from AstraZeneca under their July agreement covering novel extrahepatic-delivery siRNA candidates. - Baofeng Energy – profit and dividend growth: Baofeng Energy (600989 CH) reported first-half net profit of RMB9.73bn, up 70.14%, on revenue of RMB30.20bn, up 32.33%.
