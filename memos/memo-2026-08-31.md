- **Iran–US escalation:** Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said early Monday that it fired missiles at a US base following US strikes on military facilities on Larak Island; separate reports cited launches toward US vessels around the Strait of Hormuz. Oil moved higher and US equity futures weakened in early Asian trading, putting China oil producers, airlines and shipping names in focus. - **Property policy package:** Housing authorities now require presold residential buildings to have completed their main structure, with down payments and mortgage proceeds deposited into supervised accounts; new projects will be encouraged to adopt completed-home sales. Separate financial rules extend maximum personal mortgage terms to 40 years and introduce a project-centred main-bank system for development lending, supporting developers, banks and construction contractors. [[Xinhua](https://zjj.changzhi.gov.cn/xwzx/hyxx/202608/t20260828_3200304.html)] [[NFRA](https://dfjrjgj.hlj.gov.cn/hljjrjd/c113136/202608/c00_31970700.shtml)] [[Suzhou Government](https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/zwgg/202608/07e7fe83327c47beba5f30df6987f651.shtml)]

- **Property capital-market support:** The CSRC issued 12 measures supporting listed developers’ refinancing and acquisitions using shares, directional convertible bonds and cash, alongside company bonds, commercial mortgage-backed securities and property ABS. The framework says reasonable financing needs should be met regardless of ownership type, broadening funding options for private and state-owned developers. [[CSRC](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7655025/content.shtml)]

- **Restricted-share tax rules tightened:** The Ministry of Finance, State Taxation Administration and CSRC retained the 20% individual-income-tax rate on gains from restricted shares but expanded coverage to bonus and transferred shares generated after lock-up expiry. Taxpayers must complete final reconciliation by June 30 of the following year, while the previous deemed-cost treatment is being tightened. [[State Taxation Administration](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5252027/content.html)] [[Sina](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-08-28/doc-inipwnxp4453282.shtml)]

- **Token economy and data labelling:** National Data Administration chief Liu Liehong said tokens are becoming a pricing and settlement unit linking data, models and computing power, and called for work on commercial models, industry applications and supporting rules. The administration will also deploy a second round of data-labelling pilots in 32 cities, taking the total to 39 and benefiting AI-data, cloud and model-service providers. [[National Data Administration](https://www.nda.gov.cn/sjj/jgsz/jld/llh/llhldhd/0829/20260829183133599713651_mobile.html)] [[Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/20260830/642ef01dd4a440d38f8c913105c80043/c.html)]

- **ChangXin LPDDR6 enters mass production:** ChangXin Memory said its LPDDR6 memory has entered mass production and will debut in Xiaomi’s 18 Fold, due in September; Xiaomi’s in-house Xring O3 processor will be the first chip supporting the product. The device offers peak speed of 12,800Mbps and capacity of up to 16GB, a fresh catalyst for ChangXin Technology 688825 CH, Xiaomi 1810 HK and the domestic memory chain. [[Securities Times](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608293859181167.html)] [[The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33971751)]

- **Liquid-cooling orders remain strong:** CCTV reported that domestic liquid-cooling server and component producers are operating at high utilisation, with one CDU production line booked through December and a Guangdong supplier’s orders, output and deliveries all nearly doubling year on year. The report supports AI-server cooling, pumps, heat exchangers and data-centre infrastructure suppliers. [[CCTV](https://news.cctv.com/2026/08/30/ARTIxqWvgl3K96neJhiwzBTf260830.shtml)]
