- **Copper – LME price reaches record:** LME copper traded above US$14,530 a tonne for the first time and finished near US$14,510, extending the rally on supply constraints. The move keeps HK/A copper miners and smelters in focus, alongside margin pressure for downstream cable, appliance and equipment producers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609073867033752.html)]

- **Southbound flows – modest net buying:** Southbound investors recorded approximately HK$1.3bn of net purchases on Monday, with Xiaomi attracting buying while Kingboard Laminates and Hua Hong Semiconductor saw reductions. Southbound turnover was about HK$91.4bn, equivalent to more than 40% of Hong Kong’s main-board turnover. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609073866802190.html)]

- **PBOC – gold purchases accelerate:** China’s official gold holdings rose by 650,000 ounces in August to 76.73m ounces, marking a 22nd consecutive monthly increase. Foreign-exchange reserves increased US$19.55bn to US$3.438tn, supporting gold-sector interest and signalling continued reserve diversification. [[Yicai](https://m.yicai.com/news/103354561.html)]

- **Auto supply chain – stricter payment rules:** MIIT and the market regulator ordered automakers to clarify when supplier payment periods begin, complete routine parts inspections within three working days and pay overdue interest. The guidance encourages payment to small suppliers within 30 days and requires completion within 60 days, with joint interviews and rectification possible for automakers that deliberately extend terms. [[MIIT](https://wap.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/zbys/wjfb/art/2026/art_6043f55908f44a71a3e51236f663356b.html)]

- **Semiconductor materials – Japanese DCS deposits start today:** MOFCOM’s provisional anti-dumping measures on Japanese dichlorosilane imports take effect on September 8, after the ministry found dumping and material injury to the domestic industry. Importers must provide deposits of 80.8%–99.2%; DCS is used in thin-film deposition for logic, memory, analogue and silicon-carbide chips. [[MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/gpmy/art/2026/art_b72896ae235242c09ad2e07be51df03c.html)]

- **Coal supply – NDRC targets higher Xinjiang shipments:** The NDRC convened Xinjiang officials and railway operators to stabilise coal production, strengthen transport capacity and raise outbound coal volumes. The initiative is aimed at national supply security and is relevant to coal pricing, rail freight and Xinjiang producers. [[NDRC](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fzggw/jgsj/yxj/sjdt/202609/t20260907_1407458.html)]

- **Iron ore – exchange tightens trading controls:** Dalian Commodity Exchange will cap non-hedging daily opening positions in each iron-ore contract at 5,000 lots from the September 8 night session. It will also reduce the price limit from 9% to 6% and the exchange margin from 11% to 8% after September 9 settlement, a mixed package designed to curb speculative turnover while normalising risk parameters. [[Mysteel](https://news.mysteel.com/a/26090716/9953285BE3D08F72.html)]

- **CICC – three-way brokerage merger approved:** CICC said the CSRC approved its share-swap absorption of Dongxing Securities and China Securities, clearing the principal regulatory hurdle for the sector’s major consolidation. CICC’s A shares will suspend from September 15 for dissenting-shareholder procedures, while the two target companies will remain suspended until delisting. [[NBD](https://m.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-09-08/4575202.html)]

- **Huawei – flagship Kirin chip returns:** Huawei launched the Mate XT 2 tri-fold handset at RMB19,999 and above, featuring the new Kirin 9050 Pro and HarmonyOS 7. The first flagship Kirin launch at a Huawei event in six years renews focus on domestic mobile chips, foldable displays, hinges, cooling and optical components. [[Sina](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/mobile/n/n/2026-09-07/doc-iniqyzxu6397792.shtml)]

- **Xiaomi – new foldable and in-house chips:** Xiaomi introduced the 18 Fold, its highest-positioned handset, starting at RMB10,999 and shipping from September 10. The device debuts Xiaomi’s Xring O3 processor and domestically produced LPDDR6 memory, while the company also presented its O100 AI accelerator and D100 autonomous-driving chip. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609073866876271.html)]

- **Xiaomi Auto – range-extender line priced below presale:** Xiaomi launched four Pengcheng SUVs at RMB209,900–299,900, including N70 Pro/Max and N90 Max variants. Final prices for the N70 Max and N90 Max were RMB20,000 and RMB30,000 below their respective presale levels, intensifying competition in the large family-SUV segment. [[NBD](https://www.mrjjxw.com/articles/2026-09-07/4575132.html)]

- **Yakang – RMB1.19bn computing contract:** Yakang subsidiary Yakang Intelligent Computing signed a 60-month high-performance cloud-computing agreement worth RMB1.188bn including tax with an unnamed Anhui customer. The contract uses fixed closed pricing and provides a sizeable order for the AI infrastructure-services company. [[Sina](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/aigc/zdht/2026-09-07/doc-iniqyzxu6409441.shtml)]

The related-party transaction extends the company downstream into heat-exchange systems and adds customers in power equipment, oilfields and rail transit. [[Sina](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-09-07/doc-iniqznpq6273069.shtml)]

- **Tencent – HK$100m daily buyback continues:** Tencent repurchased 228,000 shares at HK$438.40–442.40 for HK$100.2m on Monday, its 16th consecutive trading day of repurchases. - **ASR Microelectronics – Alibaba trims stake:** Alibaba Network sold 5.283m shares in ASR Microelectronics between August 17 and September 7 through block and market trades.
