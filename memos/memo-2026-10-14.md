- Hong Kong IPO enforcement – The SFC said targeted action over suspected manipulation of IPO subscription demand at ChaPanda and another issuer, whose shares were suspended, should reinforce rather than undermine market confidence. It pledged further proportionate action with HKEX against serious misconduct. [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4215004.html)]

- Shanghai green-development plan – Shanghai’s new 15th Five-Year energy-efficiency plan targets 3GW of new-energy storage by 2030 and more than 5GW of additional solar capacity during 2026–30. It also caps the PUE of new computing facilities at 1.2 and supports liquid cooling, subsea data centres, virtual power plants and hydrogen, green-methanol and sustainable-aviation-fuel trading platforms. [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4215101.html)]

- Embodied-AI deployment – Hangzhou’s national embodied-intelligence pilot base signed agreements with governments in Yunnan to test robots and related systems in real border-patrol environments. The partners aim to move from initial trials toward broader, scaled deployment, adding a fresh application case for the robotics supply chain. [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4215086.html)]

- CATL industry fund – CATL (300750 CH) will commit RMB400m to a roughly RMB1.50bn Shanghai venture fund, giving it a 26.64% interest. The fund will target new and future energy, AI and digital technologies, power systems and other emerging industries. [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4215054.html)]

- China Power Construction overseas order – PowerChina (601669 CH) signed a €1.621bn, or approximately RMB12.53bn, contract for part of Belgrade Metro Line 1. The package covers design and construction, with a 60-month project period and two-year warranty, supporting the overseas infrastructure backlog. [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4215152.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610133894298526.html)]

- Shenhao Technology wins first computing lease – Shenhao Technology (300853 CH) secured a RMB336m central-SOE contract covering 468 PFlops of dense GPU computing capacity and maintenance over 60 months. The award marks the company’s first computing-leasing order and expands its business beyond power-grid inspection robots. [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4215194.html)]

- Transsion H-share pricing – Transsion Holdings set its Hong Kong offer price at HK$35.30 per H share, the bottom of the marketed HK$35.30–38.80 range.
