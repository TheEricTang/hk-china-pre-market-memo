Morning Market Memo | 21 Oct 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 20 Oct 2026 16:00 HKT close → 21 Oct 2026 06:52 HKT research cutoff)

- **National Data Group launched:** China inaugurated the centrally administered National Data Group in Xiongan, taking the number of central SOEs to 100. Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang said it should develop data services, infrastructure and core technologies; the group will also supply high-quality datasets and corpora for AI development. [[Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20261020/2039459dfaeb4c10872705a3f7b845cf/c.html)]

- **DeepSeek barred from US federal civilian systems:** The US Department of Homeland Security ordered civilian agencies to stop procuring DeepSeek products and remove them from information systems. MOFCOM called the restriction discriminatory and said China would take all necessary measures against actions that materially damage Chinese interests, keeping AI platforms and cloud-related names in focus. [[Xinhua](https://www3.xinhuanet.com/world/20261020/9298b67514c74756b2604e433cc752c6/c.html)]

- **US sanctions five Chinese technology companies:** Washington imposed measures on five Chinese companies under legislation covering alleged theft of US trade secrets. MOFCOM rejected the accusation, said Chinese AI development resulted from domestic research and innovation, and warned that China would respond if the measures were not corrected. [[Xinhua](https://www.xinhuanet.com/fortune/20261020/bb055507c17d4d149e5e0b4fad3d4767/c.html)]

- **CATL results – revenue growth outpaced profit:** CATL (300750 CH) reported third-quarter revenue of RMB165.22bn, up 58.58%, and net profit of RMB21.25bn, up 14.54%. Nine-month revenue rose 56.19% to RMB442.14bn and profit increased 31.60% to RMB64.53bn, while operating cash flow fell 23.60% and inventories rose 53.15% from year-end. [[Eastmoney](https://data.eastmoney.com/notices/detail/300750/AN202610201830724821.html)]

- **Tianqi Lithium flags sharp profit recovery:** Tianqi Lithium (002466 CH) expects nine-month net profit of RMB5.1bn–6.0bn, up 2,735%–3,235%. The company attributed the improvement to higher selling prices for its main lithium products and stronger downstream new-energy demand, providing a fresh read-through for the lithium and battery-material chains. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203901483959.html)]

- **Montage Technology – DDR5 drives earnings:** Montage Technology (688008 CH) guided for third-quarter net profit of RMB1.2bn–1.3bn, representing 154%–175% year-on-year growth. Higher shipments of DDR5 memory-interface and module-support chips, including newer generations of registered clock-driver products, were the main contributors. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203901483959.html)]

- **Yunnan Germanium – AI optics demand lifts results:** Yunnan Germanium (002428 CH) reported nine-month net profit of RMB176m, up 870%, including RMB102m in the third quarter. The company cited stronger demand for indium-phosphide compound-semiconductor material from AI data centres and optical communications, alongside higher germanium-product volumes and prices. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203901483959.html)]

- **Robot components – Fulin Precision adds capacity:** Fulin Precision (300432 CH) agreed with the Mianyang Fucheng district government to invest RMB500m in intelligent robotic electric joints and core components. First-phase investment is RMB250m, with planned annual capacity of two million joint sets, adding a post-close catalyst for the humanoid-robot supply chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203901483959.html)]

- **AI hardware – Meishi Technology orders GPU cards:** Meishi Technology (001229 CH) said a wholly owned subsidiary signed an RMB839m contract to purchase GPU accelerator products. The equipment will be used for AI model training, inference deployment and large-scale visual-data processing; execution and funding remain the main disclosed risks. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203901483959.html)]

- **PCB maker plans Hong Kong listing:** Aoshikang (002913 CH) approved preparations to issue H shares and seek a Main Board listing in Hong Kong. The company said the transaction is intended to support its international expansion and overseas business development, adding another electronics manufacturer to the HK IPO pipeline. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203901483959.html)]

- **Qinhuangdao Port plans asset consolidation:** Qinhuangdao Port (601326 CH) is considering issuing A shares and paying cash to acquire minority stakes in four Caofeidian port businesses from its controlling shareholder, alongside a possible supporting fundraise. Its A shares will suspend trading from today for up to ten trading days; the assets cover bulk and coal-handling infrastructure. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203901483959.html)]

- **China Mobile – profit declines:** China Mobile reported nine-month revenue of RMB780.3bn, down 1.8%, and shareholder profit of RMB106.2bn, down 7.9%. Mobile customers reached 1.014bn after a net addition of 8.89m, while broadband connections rose by 13.05m to 342m; the earnings decline is the main read-through for 941 HK and telecom peers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610203901483959.html)]
