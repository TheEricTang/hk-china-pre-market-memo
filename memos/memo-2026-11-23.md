Morning Market Memo | 23 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open
(covers 20 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 23 Nov 2026 06:15 HKT research cutoff)

- **Margin financing:** Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing exchanges lowered the minimum financing-margin ratio from 100% to 80%, effective 30 November. Brokers may also recalculate available collateral on outstanding contracts, supporting A-share liquidity and securities names. [[SSE](https://www.sse.com.cn/aboutus/mediacenter/hotandd/c/c_20261122_10838649.shtml)]

- **Capital-market fee cuts:** Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges will waive trading-unit usage fees and extend existing listing-fee and bond-transaction-fee exemptions through 2028. ChinaClear will also continue waiving specified service and settlement fees for green, technology-innovation and private-enterprise bonds during 2027–28. [[Xinhua](https://www2.xinhuanet.com/20261122/4c02cea391004b3cb64e1e28710604fc/c.html)]

- **ChiNext market makers:** Shenzhen Stock Exchange opened consultation on introducing market makers to ChiNext, covering broker registration, quoting obligations, performance assessment and risk controls. Feedback closes 6 December; the mechanism is intended to improve liquidity, pricing efficiency and market stability. [[SZSE](https://www.szse.cn/www/aboutus/trends/news/t20261122_623984.html)]

- **Active ETFs:** China’s first 18 active ETFs list in Shanghai and Shenzhen today after all reached their RMB2bn fundraising caps, raising RMB36bn in aggregate. The launch adds a transparent, exchange-traded active-equity channel and brings incremental demand for holdings including Kweichow Moutai, Midea and CATL. [[NBD](https://m.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-22/4654960.html)] [[Beijing Daily](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2026/11/22/12039624.shtml)]

- **Hang Seng Tech expansion:** The index will expand from 30 to 50 constituents, adding CATL, Luxshare, Zhongji Innolight, Victory Giant, Hesai, Biren, ASMPT and UBTech, among others. Changes take effect on 7 December; MiniMax and Zhipu join the HSCEI while Postal Savings Bank and Shenzhou International exit. [[Hang Seng Indexes](https://www.hsi.com.hk/content/dam/wpb/hsil/en_hk/press_releases/content/20261120T174500.pdf)] [[RTHK](https://gbcode.rthk.hk/TuniS/news.rthk.hk/rthk/ch/component/k2/1878630-20261120.htm)]

- **Fiscal and property pulse:** January–October general public-budget revenue rose 4.9% to RMB19.57tn, while securities transaction stamp-tax receipts increased 54.1% to RMB251.1bn. Land-sale revenue fell 25.8% to RMB1.85tn, maintaining pressure on local-government and property-linked finances. [[Ministry of Finance](https://bgt.mof.gov.cn/zhuantilanmu/rdwyh/czszsj/202611/t20261119_4004986.htm)] [[NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-20/4653960.html)]

- **China–Australia cooperation:** A post-close joint statement established a bilateral artificial-intelligence dialogue and included cooperation on clean-energy transition, trade and investment. The countries also designated 2027 as the China–Australia Tourism Year, relevant to commodities, EVs, renewables, airlines and travel platforms. [[Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/world/20261120/f395ae4a1c714a138bf4b758d2c6fffc/c.html)]

- **US telecom restrictions:** The FCC voted through rules intended to bar China Mobile, China Telecom and China Unicom from directly obtaining US internet-telephone numbering resources. MOFCOM urged an immediate withdrawal and said China would take necessary measures to protect its interests. [[MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/sjfzrfb/art/2026/art_a8a703480a3a4720a247c584d9f77eeb.html)]

All named perpetrators received administrative punishment, signalling tighter scrutiny of AI-generated and social-media market claims. [[Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/legal/20261120/cc6e58c4eeec4c2eb766038162244b6d/c.html)]

- **AI-server pricing:** Dell, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro are reportedly seeking 25%–45% price increases on some Nvidia-based AI-server orders, including contracts signed months earlier, as memory and component costs rise. The report is relevant to Lenovo 992 HK and the storage, PCB and data-centre supply chains. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611223936967073.html)] [[Intelwire](https://intelwire.uk/zh/alerts/2026-11-22/2038/)]
