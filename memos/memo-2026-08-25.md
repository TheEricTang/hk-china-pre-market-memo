- **PBOC – month-end liquidity support:** The central bank will conduct RMB500bn of one-year MLF on 25 August, a RMB100bn net withdrawal against maturities. It also scheduled overnight reverse repos from 27 August to 1 September, capped at RMB600bn daily; combined with August buyout-repo operations, medium-term liquidity still shows a RMB100bn net injection for the month. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608253851681428.html)]

- **Shanghai – “15th Five-Year” industrial blueprint:** Shanghai targets industrial value-added at more than 20% of GDP and strategic emerging industries at over 50% of large industrial output by 2030. The plan prioritises integrated circuits, biopharma and AI, while seeking more than RMB100bn of incremental output from future industries including quantum technology, brain-computer interfaces, fusion, 6G, silicon photonics and fourth-generation semiconductors. [[Shanghai Government](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/nw12344/20260824/0c052ef0042c4a09bcae2adfe2babe62.html)] [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608253851680681.html)]

- **Beijing property – central-government provident-fund easing:** Dual-contributor households can now obtain up to RMB240m for a first home, with policy uplifts taking the maximum to RMB340m for qualifying families. The changes also broaden home-status recognition, permit mortgaged-home transfers and add a renovation-withdrawal facility, supporting Beijing housing demand and brokers’ property names. [[Government Offices Administration](https://www.ggj.gov.cn/xwzx/ggjxw/202608/t20260824_49667.htm)]

- **IPO policy – tightening rumour rejected:** An authoritative source said exchanges have asked sponsors to improve project quality and avoid a rush of weak filings, but have not imposed a new blanket restriction on loss-making applicants. The clarification removes part of the overnight uncertainty around the A-share IPO pipeline and broker underwriting businesses. [[China Financial Information Network](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-08-24/doc-inipmnpq6037807.shtml)]
