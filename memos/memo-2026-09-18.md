Wang called for communication, cooperation and management of differences on the basis of equality, respect and mutual benefit; the two sides also discussed the Middle East. [[MFA](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/wjbzhd/202609/t20260917_12024847.shtml)]

- **Online fund marketing rules:** Shanghai’s securities regulator issued six implementation requirements ahead of the Financial Product Online Marketing Measures taking effect on 30 September. Fund managers and distributors must not use influencers or other parties outside approved third-party platforms, while platform fees cannot be linked to account openings, trading volume, fees or client assets. [[NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-09-17/4584634.html)]

- **Kuang-Chi Technology – RMB907m contracts:** Subsidiary Kuang-Chi Advanced signed three mass-production contracts for metamaterial products totalling RMB906.89m, including RMB861.60m of structural products for one customer. The company expects limited 2026 earnings impact but a positive contribution in 2027, subject to execution. [[Sina](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12609580&stockid=002625)] [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4189593.html)]

- **Fulongma–Huawei Cloud autonomous sanitation:** Fulongma signed a three-year, RMB200m contract under which Huawei Cloud will exclusively develop and deploy autonomous-driving systems for three-tonne and six-tonne sanitation vehicles. The cooperation covers sweeping and washing scenarios and is intended to support commercial deployment of unmanned sanitation equipment. [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103368572.html)]

- **Hengrui Pharma starts new buyback:** Hengrui repurchased 335,000 A-shares for RMB14.50m at RMB43.10–43.58 per share, the first purchase under its new RMB1bn–RMB2bn programme. The shares are intended for an employee ownership plan; the programme runs through 18 August 2027. [[STCN](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609173877529589.html)]

- **Tencent buyback:** Tencent spent HK$100m to repurchase 235,000 shares on Thursday at HK$425.20–431.00 each. The disclosure provides a fresh support signal for 700 HK after the stock fell 1.71% in the prior session. [[Zhitong](https://cn.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/article-3571292)]

- **Shaanxi Construction – energy storage EPC:** A subsidiary won the EPC contract for a 200MW/800MWh grid-forming independent energy-storage station in Bazhou. The winning bid is RMB1.05bn, although the company cautioned that implementation remains subject to final project arrangements. [[NBD](https://m.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-09-17/4584430.html)]

- **Zhejiang Construction – Hong Kong housing contract:** Subsidiary China Overseas Building Construction received the award for a public-housing development at the former Wang Cheong Factory Estate in Cheung Sha Wan, Kowloon. The contract value is approximately HK$1.5bn with a 1,096-day construction period; the formal agreement has not yet been signed. [[Guandian](https://www.guandian.cn/m/show/603280)]

- **Woer Heat-Shrinkable Material – RMB800m expansion:** A wholly owned subsidiary plans to invest up to RMB800m in an electronic-materials production base at Zhejiang Dushan Port, including around RMB600m of fixed assets. The project will make heat-shrink materials and insulation sleeves used in automotive wiring and piping and still requires shareholder approval. [[Zhitong](https://hk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/article-1662246)]

- **Zenner Metering buyback proposal:** Chairman and president Li Shihua proposed an RMB80m–RMB160m A-share repurchase at no more than RMB20 per share. The estimated four million to eight million shares, or 0.98%–1.96% of capital, would be used for incentives, employee ownership or convertible-bond conversion, subject to approval. [[NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-09-17/4584398.html)]

- **Xianghe Industrial – railway components:** The company signed an RMB178m contract to supply railway fastening-system components, equivalent to approximately 21.32% of its 2025 revenue. - **Cosunter Pharmaceutical – innovation-drug funding:** Cosunter plans to inject RMB587m into a controlled subsidiary, with the full amount earmarked for innovative-drug research and development. The capital commitment is relevant to domestic biotech development and clinical-service demand. [[STCN](https://www.stcn.com/article/detail/4189593.html)]

- **Mankun Technology – Thailand AI-server PCB capacity:** Mankun said its Thailand factory is expected to begin phased production in 2027. The overseas base is designed partly to serve demand for high-layer PCBs used in AI-server power systems, although the precise commissioning schedule remains subject to construction progress. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609173877644294.html)]
