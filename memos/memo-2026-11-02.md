- Capital-market support tools: Media reports say the PBOC and CSRC will meet securities and fund firms at around 09:00 HKT today to discuss capital-market support tools. - PBOC policy-rate anchor shifts overnight: From today, the central bank will conduct overnight reverse repos every working day through fixed-rate, quantity tenders, with the current rate at 1.25%. Seven-day operations become fixed-quantity, multi-price tenders mainly used around tax periods and month-end, making the overnight rate the clearer short-end policy anchor for banks and bonds. [[PBOC via Eastmoney](https://fund.eastmoney.com/a/202610303916569143.html)]
