Morning Market Memo | 09 Nov 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 06 Nov 2026 16:00 HKT close → 09 Nov 2026 06:10 HKT research cutoff)

- **State Council – domestic demand and market stability:** Premier Li Qiang’s Friday meeting called for faster implementation of major projects and the “six networks,” support for equipment and intangible-asset investment, and fuller use of policy funds. It also ordered improvements to capital-market stabilisation mechanisms and continued resolution of local-government debt and small-financial-institution risks. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611063925205337.html)]

- **China–US economic talks:** Vice Premier He Lifeng held a Friday-night video call with US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Trade Representative Jamieson Greer. China raised serious concerns over recent US economic restrictions; both sides agreed to keep using the bilateral consultation mechanism, strengthen cooperation and manage differences. [[Foreign Ministry](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/web/wjdt_674879/gjldrhd_674881/202611/t20261107_12055300.shtml)]

- **US sanctions – Commerce Ministry response:** MOFCOM opposed Washington’s sanctions on two Chinese companies over alleged Cuba links, saying unilateral measures involving third countries violated international law and damaged Chinese corporate rights. Beijing urged the US to reverse the measures and reserved the right to take necessary action. [[MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfyrth/art/2026/art_0a686e2464da49a58a076d9eab42187f.html)]

- **Private-sector policy consultation:** NDRC chairman Zheng Shanjie met executives from Hengyi, Yulong Petrochemical, TCL, Twins Group and Sino-Crystal Diamond to discuss year-end implementation and 2027 policy planning. Companies sought stronger funding and energy support, better execution of major-project and equipment-renewal programmes, and tighter control of disorderly competition. [[The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_34423116)]

- **Stabilisation funds entered equities:** CITIC Securities, Guotai Haitong and CICC completed initial stock purchases under the third Securities, Funds and Insurance Companies Swap Facility; Galaxy, China Merchants and Orient Securities also disclosed purchases. The underlying PBOC operation was RMB100bn, with 31 securities and fund firms participating at a 10bp winning fee. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611063924929925.html)]

- **ChiNext Index methodology:** Shenzhen Stock Exchange will gradually lower the maximum weight for a single ChiNext Index constituent to 15%, using a staged mechanism that only reduces the cap. The revision takes effect with the 14 December periodic adjustment and should progressively broaden passive-fund exposure beyond the largest constituent. [[SZSE](https://www.szse.cn/disclosure/notice/t20261106_623796.html)]

- **China reserves:** October foreign-exchange reserves rose US$4.9bn to US$3.4052tn. Official gold holdings increased by 830,000 ounces to 78.30m ounces, marking a 24th consecutive monthly increase and supporting the domestic precious-metals theme despite Friday’s decline in global bullion. [[People’s Daily](https://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/1107/c1004-40829675.html)]

- **Time Interconnect – HK$500m buyback:** Time Interconnect Technology (1729 HK) approved open-market share repurchases of up to HK$500m over 24 months, funded from internal resources. The board said the current share price did not reflect the company’s intrinsic value; the announcement is relevant to the HK-listed data-centre and cable supply chain. [[GMT Eight](https://www.gmt8press.com/content/detail/449364)]

- **CMOC – A-share repurchase:** CMOC Group (3993 HK/603993 CH) approved a RMB1bn–2bn A-share buyback over three months at no more than RMB22.78 per share. The estimated 43.9m–87.8m shares equal 0.21%–0.41% of issued capital and may later be sold or cancelled under the stated conditions. [[China Securities Journal](https://www.cs.com.cn/ssgs/01/2026/11/06/detail_2026110610050414.html)]

- **Wus Printed Circuit – buyback proposal:** Wus Printed Circuit (002463 CH) received a chairman’s proposal for a RMB500m–1bn A-share repurchase. The shares could be used for employee incentives, capital reduction or value maintenance, providing a fresh catalyst for the PCB and AI-server hardware chain. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://www.cnstock.com/commonDetail/825439)]

- **Guangzhi Technology – Coherent supply plan:** Guangzhi Technology’s (300489 CH) subsidiary signed a letter of intent to supply six-inch indium-phosphide substrates to Coherent’s Swiss and Swedish plants during 2027–2029, with indicated value of at least US$601m. Final contracts and an export licence are still required, but the agreement is material for optical components and data-centre connectivity. [[NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-11-06/4640936.html)]

- **Jiechuang Intelligence – computing contract:** Jiechuang Intelligence (301248 CH) signed a 60-month computing-services contract with an undisclosed “H1” company, provisionally valued at RMB4.915bn including tax. Services will be delivered in batches and settled monthly; the company cautioned that the provisional total is not an earnings forecast and requires substantial upfront investment. [[NBD](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-11-06/doc-inixvfew8825109.shtml)]

- **CPIC Fiberglass – electronic-cloth capacity:** International Composite Materials (301526 CH) plans to invest RMB1.158bn in a Chongqing production line with annual capacity of 111.3m metres of electronic-grade glass-fibre cloth. Construction is scheduled from November 2026 to September 2027, adding supply for PCB, high-frequency communications and AI-server materials. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611063925052785.html)]
