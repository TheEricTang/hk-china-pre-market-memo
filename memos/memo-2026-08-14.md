MIIT’s 6G coordination programme aims to form a batch of domestically developed technical solutions by 2029; telecom equipment, optical-fibre and satellite-internet names are relevant. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608133840674693.html)]

- **Indian optical fibre:** MOFCOM will continue anti-dumping duties on single-mode optical fibre imported from India for another five years from today. The measure supports domestic fibre pricing and capacity utilisation across China’s optical-communications supply chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608133840806943.html)]

- **US drone tariffs:** President Trump signed tariffs of 10%–100% on imported drones and components, including a 100% rate for drones above 25kg or with thermal-imaging functions and 25% for certain smaller products. Most drone tariffs take effect in 21 days, with some non-sensitive component duties delayed 180 days; Chinese drone exporters and suppliers face the clearest read-through. [[CCTV](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608143840872285.html)]

DeepSeek V4 Pro peak output pricing rises to RMB27 per million tokens from RMB6, while ordinary web and app users remain free; the change directly affects enterprise AI deployment costs and domestic cloud demand. [[Yicai](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608133840744175.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608133840806943.html)]
