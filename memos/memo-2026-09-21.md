Beijing opposed unilateral and secondary sanctions, said normal trade should not be subject to third-party coercion and reserved the right to take necessary measures. - **Iran offers terms to end the conflict:** Iran said it had submitted conditions to the US through Qatar, including an end to hostilities, the release of frozen Iranian assets and termination of the maritime blockade. Washington’s response remains pending; the development is relevant to oil, airlines, shipping and chemicals after recent energy-price volatility. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609203879461881.html)]

- **State Council targets silver-economy and healthcare capacity:** Premier Li Qiang’s Friday-evening State Council meeting called for more inclusive elderly-care services, new consumption scenarios for older people and stronger links between home, community and institutional care. It also ordered an expansion of medical rehabilitation and nursing capacity, support for smart rehabilitation equipment, more high-quality sporting events and stronger protection for traditional Chinese medicine knowledge. [[Gov.cn](https://big5.www.gov.cn/gate/big5/www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202609/content_7081464.htm)]

- **Regulators intensify action against low-price competition:** The State Administration for Market Regulation said authorities had opened 29,000 cases involving pricing, competition, quality and certification violations under the campaign against low-quality, low-price practices. The regulator wants competition to shift from price-cutting toward quality and value, with implications for consumer goods, autos, internet platforms and industrial supply chains. [[SAMR](https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/zj/art/2026/art_165abf69d1a5422d9372e6d204159270.html)] [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609213879492670.html)]

A 24GB LPDDR5X product based on the platform is also in production for mainstream flagship phones, providing a fresh catalyst for the domestic memory, packaging and equipment chain. [[STCN](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609213879494140.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609203879461881.html)]

Nine measures cover product quality, registration and scrappage, driving tests, road access and parking, campground construction, tourism products, rentals, financing and promotional activity, benefiting auto aftermarket, tourism and campsite operators. [[Ministry of Culture and Tourism](https://www.mct.gov.cn/whzx/whyw/202609/t20260918_967191.htm)]
