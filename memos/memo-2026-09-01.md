Morning Market Memo | 01 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 31 Aug 2026 16:00 HKT close → 01 Sep 2026 09:29 HKT research cutoff)

- State Council – investment rules and urban infrastructure: Premier Li Qiang chaired a meeting calling for clearer encouraged and prohibited conduct in local-government investment promotion, consistent with the national unified-market drive. The meeting also ordered faster, better-coordinated upgrades of gas, water, drainage, sewage and heating networks, and approved a draft revision of the Tax Collection Administration Law for submission to the NPC Standing Committee. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608313860229937.html)]

- Consumption – RMB60tn retail-sales target: MOFCOM and six other departments set a target for total retail sales of consumer goods to reach around RMB60tn by 2030, with green, smart and health consumption each forming markets measured in the trillions. The plan supports easing unreasonable car-purchase restrictions, expanding charging infrastructure, promoting AI devices and robots, and increasing financial support for autos, appliances and smart homes. [[MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_702002863efa4530bfc54b6fd835f12a.html)]

- Battery tax takes effect today: China begins levying 2% consumption tax on lithium primary batteries, lithium-ion batteries and vanadium-flow batteries, rising to 4% from September 2027. Sodium-ion, solid-state and fuel cells, plus perovskite, tandem and gallium-arsenide solar cells, are exempt through end-2028; the tax authority clarified that semi-solid batteries do not qualify as solid-state products. [[Ministry of Finance](https://szs.mof.gov.cn/zhengcefabu/202607/t20260717_3993743.htm)] [[State Taxation Administration](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/c102414/c5252006/content.html)]

- Bank and insurer wealth products – disclosure rules effective: New information-disclosure rules for asset-management products issued by banks and insurers take effect today, unifying core principles, responsibilities, common disclosure content and internal controls across three product categories. The change raises compliance requirements for bank wealth-management, insurance-asset-management and trust-linked product providers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609013860747475.html)]

- Basic-drug catalogue expands: The 2026 National Essential Medicines List takes effect today with 794 medicines and four domestically developed Class 1 innovative drugs included. The broader list improves access through public hospitals and primary-care channels, benefiting selected innovative-drug, chronic-disease and traditional Chinese medicine suppliers, although companies cautioned that near-term sales effects remain uncertain. [[The Paper](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609013860569178.html)]

- Data assets and robotics – new standards: Two national standards covering data-asset classification, coding and registration take effect today, alongside the new general architecture standard for intelligent robot control systems. The measures provide more consistent operating frameworks for data exchanges, enterprise data accounting and industrial-robot system design. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609013860747475.html)]

- Huawei – R&D rises despite profit pressure: Huawei reported first-half revenue of RMB467.82bn, up 9.55%, while R&D spending reached a record RMB121.38bn, equivalent to 25.94% of revenue and up 25.2%. The spending remains supportive for domestic semiconductors, computing infrastructure, optical communications and Huawei supply-chain names. [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103341147.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://fund.eastmoney.com/a/202608313860441745.html)]

- Enflame Technology IPO priced: Domestic AI-chip developer Enflame Technology set its STAR Market IPO price at RMB142.18 per share for 43.04m shares, implying gross proceeds of about RMB6.12bn. The still-loss-making company is valued at 61.8 times 2025 sales on a diluted basis; subscriptions open on September 2, providing a fresh benchmark for the domestic AI-accelerator chain. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/news/1345,202608313860221271.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://fund.eastmoney.com/a/202608313860441745.html)]

- Zhongji Innolight – large buyback: Zhongji Innolight (300308 CH) proposed spending RMB4bn–8bn to repurchase shares at no more than RMB1,200 each. At the upper funding limit, the company estimates buying about 6.67m shares, or 0.57% of outstanding stock, providing a fresh post-close signal for the optical-module and CPO complex. [[Eastmoney](https://fund.eastmoney.com/a/202608313860441745.html)]

- International Composites – RMB5bn electronic-materials raise: International Composites (301526 CH) plans a private placement of up to RMB5bn for high-frequency, high-speed electronic fibre cloth, equipment upgrades and pilot development of ultra-low-loss Q-cloth for AI applications. The project adds capacity ambition upstream of copper-clad laminates, PCBs and AI-server hardware. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/news/1345,202608313860249076.html)]

- China Jushi – electronic-cloth price increase: China Jushi confirmed September price increases of 15% for thick electronic cloth and 20% for thin cloth, with mainstream prices above RMB10 per metre. The company said supply remains tight and inventories low; its first-half electronic-cloth volume rose more than 12% to 544m metres, relevant to CCL and PCB suppliers. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/news/1345,202608313860324639.html)]

- Copper and gold – major Anhui discovery: The Ministry of Natural Resources reported a major exploration breakthrough at the Chating copper-gold deposit in Xuancheng, with potential to form a large new resource base in eastern China. Further drilling is continuing because the deep ore body has not been fully penetrated; the project could eventually improve nearby feedstock availability for Yangtze River Delta smelters and processors. [[CCTV](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609013860745754.html)]

- Goldman Sachs – AI capacity still constrained: Goldman’s TMT research head said AI infrastructure supply and demand may not balance until the first half of 2028, implying continued hyperscaler capital expenditure and pressure on memory, chip and data-centre capacity. The call supports the medium-term demand case for HK/A-share optical modules, servers, cooling, memory and power-equipment suppliers. [[CLS](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609013860759732.html)]

- Oil and Middle East: WTI October futures rose 2.83% to US$85.76 and Brent November futures gained 2.71% to US$90.49 after President Trump said the US would respond to an attack on its base in Jordan. US energy shares advanced, while airlines weakened; the move is relevant to PetroChina/CNOOC/Sinopec, airlines and downstream chemical producers.
