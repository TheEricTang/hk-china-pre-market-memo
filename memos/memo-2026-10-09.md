- **PBOC – exchange-rate stance:** The central bank said market supply and demand will remain decisive in setting the renminbi, with no preset exchange-rate target and no intention to seek trade advantage through competitive depreciation. It retains macroprudential tools and direct intervention for extreme, disorderly moves, making the statement relevant to exporters, banks and other currency-sensitive names.
