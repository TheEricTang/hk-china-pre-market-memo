- **Hormuz remains closed – oil settles higher:** An Iranian senior official said the Strait of Hormuz would remain closed unless the US ended the war, released frozen Iranian funds and met other conditions. WTI settled 1.34% higher at US$83.23 a barrel; spot gold fell 0.50% to US$4,368.15 an ounce, keeping energy, airlines and precious-metals names sensitive to further headlines. - **Shanghai software plan – RMB4tn industry target:** Shanghai’s economic and information technology authority issued its 15th Five-Year software and information-services plan, targeting industry scale of RMB4tn and value added above RMB1.1tn by 2030. It also targets 35 companies with annual revenue exceeding RMB10bn, with AI, critical software, digital content and intelligent services identified as priority areas. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608123838282597.html)]

- **Industrial Fulian – AI demand drives record first half:** Industrial Fulian reported first-half revenue of RMB557.86bn, up 54.6%, and net profit of RMB23.74bn, up 96%. Cloud-computing revenue rose 75.7%; AI-server revenue for cloud customers increased 2.3 times and GPU AI-rack shipments rose 3.2 times, providing a fresh benchmark for the mainland AI-server, liquid-cooling, networking and optical supply chains. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608113837925896.html)]

- **SK Hynix – Dalian NAND expansion restarted:** SK Hynix’s Solidigm has resumed construction of its second Dalian NAND factory, with equipment installation expected from November and production targeted for the first half of 2027. The new line is planned for roughly 50,000 wafers per month, increasing the group’s Dalian capacity by about 50% and reinforcing demand for China-based semiconductor equipment and materials. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608113838065090.html)]

- **Optical and liquid-cooling capacity – two post-close funding plans:** Yuanjie Technology (688498 CH) plans to invest about RMB4.27bn in a semiconductor industrial park containing laser-chip production lines. Qiangrui Technology (301128 CH) separately proposed raising up to RMB1.05bn for AI-server liquid-cooling components, automated server assembly and high-end semiconductor-equipment parts. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608113838063575.html)]

- **MCU pricing – Guomin Technology announces increase:** Guomin Technology said it will raise prices for some products by 10%–20% from 1 October, citing stronger AI-related demand, changing supply-chain costs and tighter MCU supply. The notice is a fresh pricing signal for domestic microcontroller and analogue-chip peers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608113838063575.html)]

- **Battery materials – major phosphate expansion:** Titanium Energy Chemical (002145 CH) plans a two-phase RMB5.46bn project with annual capacity for 600,000 tonnes of lithium-iron-phosphate or sodium-ion-battery precursor material. It also plans to increase investment in a subsidiary developing 400,000 tonnes of titanium dioxide and 1.4mn tonnes of ferrous sulphate capacity. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608113838063575.html)]

- **Storage-company capital returns – Jiangbolong and Biwin:** Jiangbolong (301308 CH) secured a bank commitment for up to RMB720mn of dedicated share-repurchase financing with a term of no more than three years. Biwin Storage (688525 CH) separately proposed a RMB200mn–RMB250mn buyback for cancellation, adding to capital-return activity across the strongly performing domestic memory chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608113838063575.html)]

- **Shipbuilding – RMB2.8bn–RMB3.3bn European order:** Runbang Heavy Industry (002483 CH) said its marine subsidiary signed contracts with a European shipowner to build six live-fish carrier vessels, including optional ships. The aggregate contract value is approximately RMB2.8bn–RMB3.3bn, offering fresh order-book support for specialised shipbuilding and marine-equipment suppliers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608113838063575.html)]

- **Nvidia – reported trillion-parameter open model:** Nvidia is reportedly developing the Nemotron 4 open-model family, with its largest version expected to contain at least one trillion parameters and potentially be ready in late autumn. The company also released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning and an open-source model-routing library aimed at lowering enterprise token costs, relevant to China’s open-model and inference-computing competition. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608113838259260.html)]

- **GPU costs – CME plans compute futures:** CME Group and Silicon Data plan to launch H100 and B200 GPU-rental-index futures on 5 October, subject to regulatory approval. The contracts would track hourly rental costs and give AI developers and cloud operators a hedging benchmark, formalising compute capacity as a tradeable input for the global AI infrastructure market.
