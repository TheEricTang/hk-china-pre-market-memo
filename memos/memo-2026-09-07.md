Morning Market Memo | 07 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 04 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 07 Sep 2026 08:21 HKT research cutoff)

- **Central financial firms – RMB360bn capital injection:** Eight state financial institutions announced capital-raising plans on Sunday. ICBC and Agricultural Bank of China will raise up to RMB100bn and RMB160bn respectively through targeted A-share issues, while PICC, China Life, China Taiping, China Re and two policy institutions will also receive capital; the Finance Ministry will fund RMB300bn through special government bonds. [[Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/20260906/691d81b74d574947bc9fdde91be6e683/c.html)] [[People’s Daily](https://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0906/c1004-40793361.html)]

- **PBOC – RMB500bn outright reverse repo today:** The central bank will conduct a three-month, 89-day outright reverse-repurchase operation on Monday to maintain ample banking-system liquidity. The operation matches RMB500bn maturing this month, leaving the outstanding balance unchanged. [[People’s Daily](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrbhwb/pc/content/202609/05/content_30179302.html)] [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103349361.html)]

- **Private funds – first comprehensive fundraising rule proposed:** The CSRC issued a seven-chapter, 45-article consultation draft covering qualified-investor tests, fundraising accounts, risk disclosures and manager or distributor responsibilities. It also bans guaranteed-return claims, misleading promotion and fundraising through unqualified intermediaries, raising compliance requirements across the private-fund distribution chain. [[CSRC](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7656676/content.shtml)] [[21st Century Business Herald](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-09-04/doc-iniqskun4054796.shtml)]

- **Data centres – seven ministries set green-computing roadmap:** The Cyberspace Administration and six other agencies released a 2026–30 digital-green transition plan after Friday’s close. Priorities include liquid cooling, waste-heat recovery, low-power chips, 800-volt direct-current architecture, higher-density server racks and increased renewable-power use by computing facilities, relevant to data-centre cooling, power equipment and grid suppliers. [[CAC](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-09/04/c_1790271981702331.htm)]

- **Baidu – southbound trading begins today:** Baidu’s 9888 HK shares enter Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect on 7 September following its conversion to a dual-primary Hong Kong listing. The adjustment gives eligible mainland investors direct access to the shares and adds a fresh southbound-liquidity channel. [[Baidu](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-upcoming-inclusion-its-class-ordinary-shares)] [[SSE](https://www.sse.com.cn/services/hkexsc/disclo/announ/c/c_20260904_10831331.shtml)]

- **Bilibili – US$700m convertible and Tencent transactions:** Bilibili 9626 HK priced zero-coupon convertible notes due 2031 at an initial conversion price of HK$155.79, a 28.3% premium to Friday’s close; Tencent will subscribe for US$200m. The package also includes concurrent placements and repurchases, with up to US$300m authorised for the special buyback programme, while proceeds will partly support AI-driven growth. [[Bilibili](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/04/3356588/0/en/bilibili-inc-announces-pricing-of-offerings-of-us-700-million-convertible-senior-notes-concurrent-equity-placement-and-concurrent-share-repurchases.html)]

Projects still at the planning stage and not yet started are reportedly being paused, while registered projects already under construction remain unaffected; watch CATL 300750 CH, EVE Energy 300014 CH and other storage suppliers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609063866133945.html)]

- **China State Construction – vice-president removed:** China State Construction 601668 CH said vice-president Chen Yong left his position on 5 September because he is under disciplinary review and supervisory investigation for suspected serious violations. The company said responsibilities have been reassigned and normal operations are unaffected. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609063866110630.html)]

- **Yubang New Materials – control change and Monday resumption:** Suzhou Deling agreed to acquire 29.99% of Yubang New Materials 301266 CH from its controlling shareholders and plans a partial tender offer for another 5%. - **Fosun Pharma – trims Gland Pharma holding:** Fosun Pharma 2196 HK/600196 CH sold 9.897m Gland Pharma shares, approximately 6% of the Indian company, through block and market trades. Its holding falls to about 45.76%, but Gland Pharma remains a controlled and consolidated subsidiary. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609063866110630.html)]

- **Zelgen – thyroid-cancer application accepted:** China’s drug regulator accepted Zelgen Biopharmaceuticals’ 688266 CH application for a new indication of recombinant human thyroid-stimulating hormone. The proposed use is alongside radioactive iodine treatment following thyroid removal in differentiated thyroid-cancer patients without distant metastasis. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609063866110630.html)]

- **Aistar – robotics expectations tempered:** After two consecutive limit-up sessions, Aistar 002403 CH said its embodied-intelligence robotics cooperation remains at an early stage and is not expected to materially affect this year’s financial results. - **Space industry – international cislunar constellation launched:** China announced the formal launch of an international cislunar CubeSat scientific programme at the Tiandu deep-space exploration conference. The proposed satellite network will support space-environment monitoring, gamma-ray-burst observation and lunar-resource exploration, providing a fresh policy catalyst for satellite manufacturing, payloads and ground systems. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609063866133945.html)]
