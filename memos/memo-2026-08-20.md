- NDRC – “six networks” coordination: The National Development and Reform Commission established a “2+3+N” coordination mechanism linking two power-grid groups, three telecom operators and computing-related companies to accelerate computing, next-generation communications and new-power-grid projects. The announcement is relevant to data centres, optical networks, grid equipment and telecom infrastructure. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608193846670183.html)]

- JD.com – China blocks assistance with EU subsidy probe: The Ministry of Justice, working with MOFCOM and other agencies, ruled that cross-border measures used under the EU Foreign Subsidies Regulation in its JD.com investigation constitute improper extraterritorial jurisdiction. Organisations and individuals are prohibited from implementing or assisting with the measures, providing a fresh legal response around JD.com’s proposed European acquisition. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608193846670183.html)]

- Medical insurance – support for innovative drugs: The National Healthcare Security Administration’s 15th Five-Year Plan sets 26 measures across eight areas, including procedures to add eligible innovative drugs to reimbursement lists and improve commercial-insurance coverage. It also proposes a launch-pricing mechanism that recognises clinical value and allows highly innovative products to maintain relatively stable initial pricing, relevant to the HK/A biotech and medical-device sectors. [[China Securities Journal](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608203846754345.html)]
