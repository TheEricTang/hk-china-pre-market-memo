- Shanghai strategic industries plan: Shanghai set a 2030 target of RMB2.1tn in strategic-emerging-industry value added, with the sector exceeding 50% of large industrial output and the three lead industries—integrated circuits, biomedicine and AI—growing manufacturing output by at least 10% annually. The plan covers 12 emerging-industry tracks and 10 frontier technologies, including advanced packaging, innovative drugs, robotics, satellite internet, 6G, quantum technology and brain–computer interfaces.
