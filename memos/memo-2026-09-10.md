- **Financial-policy briefing today:** The State Council Information Office will hold a 15:00 HKT briefing on implementing the 15th Five-Year Plan and building a stronger financial system. Speakers include PBOC Deputy Governor Lu Lei, NFRA Deputy Director Cong Lin, CSRC Vice Chairman Li Chao and SAFE Deputy Administrator Li Bin, putting banks, brokers and the renminbi in focus. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103869718992.html)]

- **China–US AI dispute:** MOFCOM rejected claims by three US security agencies that six Chinese AI companies conducted unauthorised “industrial-scale” model distillation, calling distillation a normal industry practice. The ministry said China would take countermeasures if the US uses the allegations to suppress Chinese AI companies. [[MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfyrth/art/2026/art_1439afbf24d941bfaefba078aacf340c.html)]

- **Beijing high-tech plan:** Beijing released its 2026–30 high-tech industries plan, calling for reusable-rocket and high-thrust engine breakthroughs, greater satellite and component mass-production capacity and participation in national constellations. The plan also backs advanced chips, new materials and six future-industry clusters, supporting the commercial-space and semiconductor supply chains. [[Beijing Government](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/202609/t20260909_4856726.html)] [[CLS](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2478236)]

- **Beijing digital-economy targets:** A separate 2026–30 plan targets roughly 8% annual growth in Beijing’s digital economy and a share above 50% of local GDP by 2030. It calls for domestic computing clusters, commercial deployment of embodied intelligence, more than 100,000 robots across manufacturing and public-service scenarios, and 100% green-power use at new intelligent-computing centres. [[Mysteel](https://m.mysteel.com/a/26090916/477AA6E1041AE046_abc.html)]

- **Medical-consumables procurement:** China’s seventh national bulk procurement round for medical consumables opened in Tianjin at 07:30 HKT. It covers 23 digestive-intervention products used mainly in endoscopic surgery, making submitted prices and winning suppliers relevant for domestic device makers and hospital suppliers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103869945929.html)]

- **Apple enters foldables:** Apple launched the iPhone Duo, its first foldable handset, at RMB15,999 for the base model and RMB26,499 for the top 2TB version, with sales starting October 23. The company also unveiled the 2nm A20 Pro-powered iPhone 18 Pro range, providing a fresh read-through for HK/A-share display, hinge, casing, optics and component suppliers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103869727256.html)]

- **Enflame Technology listing:** Chinese cloud-AI chip developer Enflame Technology (688801 CH) confirmed it will list on Shanghai’s STAR Market on Friday at RMB142.18 per share. The IPO comprises 43.04 million shares, while only 17.90 million shares, or 4.16% of post-issue capital, will be freely tradable initially. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609093869520751.html)]

- **Shenzhen Kaifa advanced packaging:** Shenzhen Kaifa Technology (000021 CH) approved a RMB1.85bn expansion of high-end memory-chip packaging and testing. The plan includes facilities capable of handling 90,000 conventional wafers and 10,000 2.5D wafers per month, plus a 2.5D/3DS advanced-packaging research line. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609093869482382.html)]

- **Hand Enterprise Solutions drops H-share plan:** Hand Enterprise Solutions (300170 CH) terminated its proposed H-share issuance and Hong Kong listing, citing the macro environment, capital-market conditions and its development plans. The company said the decision would not affect normal operations, but it removes a prospective HK IPO from the pipeline. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609093869482382.html)]

- **Optical and copper theme warnings:** PCB maker Xunjiexing (688655 CH) said optical modules are not a major revenue source and its micron-scale products remain at the technical-discussion and sample stage. Huamai Technology (603042 CH) said data-centre and computing-related revenue is small, while Jingyi (002295 CH) warned that higher copper prices increase working-capital requirements rather than directly benefiting earnings.
