- **Alibaba – founder and management buy shares:** Yicai reported, citing sources, that Jack Ma bought more than HK$600mn of Alibaba 9988 HK shares over several days. Together with purchases by chairman Joe Tsai and CEO Eddie Wu, recent management buying exceeded HK$800mn, following the group’s HK$80bn AI-focused placement. [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103333253.html)]

- **Infrastructure funding – NDRC studies “six networks” support:** Vice chairman Yue Xiuhu chaired a post-close meeting on financing mechanisms for the six-network programme. Officials discussed combining fiscal, financial, investment and pricing support to accelerate projects spanning computing, power, communications and other strategic infrastructure. [[36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/newsflashes/3954822230457733)]

- **Shanghai trade plan – metals, autos and offshore business:** Shanghai’s new 2026–30 international trade-centre plan calls for larger copper and aluminium trading markets and new lithium, cobalt and nickel futures products. It also supports auto-consumption reform, integrated-circuit and AI exports, foreign-company reinvestment and further offshore-trade pilots; the city targets more than US$3tn of goods trade during the five years. [[Shanghai Government](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/nw12344/20260825/7909324866ab4a89b6a56e7197762bce.html)]

- **Agricultural distribution – 2030 market-system target:** The Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs issued its 15th Five-Year Plan for agricultural wholesale and origin markets. By 2030 it aims to improve market layouts, cold-chain and logistics facilities, monitoring systems and links between producers and domestic and international buyers. [[Jiemian](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14988254.html)]

- **Nuclear fusion – Yangtze River Delta magnet consortium:** China Fusion Energy and eight partners will jointly develop high-temperature superconducting magnets for the China Circulation-4 tokamak. The programme targets a 25-tesla magnet development and testing line before 2028 and completion of a prototype magnet before 2030, relevant to listed fusion-material and superconducting-equipment suppliers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608253853413855.html)]

- **North Huachuang – semiconductor-equipment revenue up 25%:** North Huachuang 002371 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB20.16bn, up 24.9%, and net profit of RMB3.37bn, up 5.1%. Its new 12-inch high-end ICP etching tool has completed customer validation and entered the mass-production introduction stage, while equipment coverage expanded across etching, deposition, cleaning and ion implantation. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608253853440276.html)]

- **Hudian – AI PCB mix lifts margins:** Hudian 002463 CH reported first-half revenue of RMB13.69bn, up 61.2%, and net profit of RMB2.92bn, up 73.7%. Revenue from PCB products with 32 or more layers rose 190.8%, helping PCB gross margin increase about 4.1 percentage points to 40.5%; the read-through is positive for high-layer PCB and copper-clad-laminate suppliers. [[China Securities Journal](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608253853343193.html)]

- **Kuaishou – another HK buyback:** Kuaishou 1024 HK repurchased 899,000 shares for HK$29.98mn on Tuesday at HK$33.24–33.46 per share. Purchases since the June mandate reached 28.54mn shares, equivalent to about 0.66% of the share base at the mandate date. [[jrj](https://m.jrj.com.cn/madapter/finance/2026/08/25190858232753.shtml)]

- **Nongfu Spring – tea drives double-digit growth:** Nongfu Spring 9633 HK reported first-half revenue of RMB29.72bn, up 16.0%, and net profit of RMB8.89bn, up 16.6%. Tea-drink revenue increased 30.1% to RMB13.12bn, while packaged-water revenue rose 2.1% to RMB9.64bn, leaving the growth mix led by non-water beverages. [[HSTong](https://www.hstong.com/news/detail/26082519331002770)]

- **WuXi Biologics – adjusted profit outpaces reported earnings:** WuXi Biologics 2269 HK posted first-half revenue of RMB11.79bn, up 18.4%, and attributable profit of RMB2.44bn, up 4.3%. Adjusted net profit rose 38.4% to RMB3.31bn, with pre-IND service revenue up 27.2%; no interim dividend was declared. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608253852970767.html)]

- **China Taiping – investment result reverses to profit:** China Taiping 966 HK reported first-half attributable profit of HK$12.87bn, up 90.3%, as insurance-service earnings improved and net investment results swung to a HK$9.69bn profit. [[Hong Kong Commercial Daily](https://www.hkcd.com.hk/hkcdweb/content/2026/08/25/content_8771613.html)]
