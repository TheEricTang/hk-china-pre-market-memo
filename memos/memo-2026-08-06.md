- China–US countermeasures: MOFCOM announced a package responding to US restrictions immediately after Wednesday’s close. Measures include tighter case-by-case controls on US-bound drones and key components, sanctions on seven US entities, suspension of certain US factory inspections under China’s CCC system and a national-security investigation into imported printing and copying equipment; MOFCOM warned that further US restrictions would trigger additional retaliation. - Critical-minerals recycling: The US will later this month impose a one-year export ban on tungsten scrap and lithium-ion battery “black mass,” requiring the materials to be sold domestically unless exporters obtain exemptions. The move may tighten overseas access to recycled tungsten, lithium, nickel and cobalt feedstock and is relevant to China’s battery-recycling and strategic-metals chains.
