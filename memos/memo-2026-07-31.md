- Central Politburo – incremental policy and market support: The Politburo called for more proactive fiscal policy and moderately loose monetary policy, with existing measures fully deployed and practical incremental policies prepared in time. It also called for stabilising property, deepening capital-market financing reforms and improving market resilience and confidence. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313827154315.html)]

- Domestic demand, infrastructure and AI: The meeting called for stronger service consumption, fiscal-financial coordination to support demand and faster construction under the “six networks” programme. It also ordered deeper implementation of the “AI+” initiative, development of new intelligent-economy formats and an improved AI governance framework, relevant to consumption, computing, communications, grid and infrastructure names. [[Shanghai Securities News](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313827154315.html)]

- China–US trade communication: Vice Premier He Lifeng held an evening video call with US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Trade Representative Jamieson Greer. The two sides discussed maintaining stable economic ties and expanding practical cooperation; China raised serious concerns about recent US trade restrictions, while both agreed to keep using the bilateral consultation mechanism. [[Xinhua](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303827081471.html)]

- Unitree IPO schedule: Unitree Robotics will conduct preliminary price consultation on 5 August and online and offline subscriptions on 10 August. The STAR Market offering comprises 40.45 million shares, or 10% of post-IPO capital, providing a fresh catalyst for the A/H humanoid-robotics supply chain. [[Jiemian](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826993516.html)] [[Securities Times](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303827079993.html)]

- Rongbai – large sodium-battery investment: Rongbai Technology (688005 CH) plans to invest RMB4.72bn in a 300,000-tonne-per-year integrated sodium-battery cathode-material project in Xiantao. Capacity will be developed in three phases of 50,000, 100,000 and 150,000 tonnes, supporting the sodium-ion battery equipment and materials chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- Youyan New Materials – advanced chip targets: Youyan New Materials (600206 CH) approved a RMB486m second-phase expansion for high-purity semiconductor sputtering targets. The project targets 30,000 additional copper, copper-alloy, tantalum and titanium targets annually and is intended to address materials requirements for processes below 7nm. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- Chifeng Gold – Laos resource upgrade: Chifeng Gold (600988 CH) said the estimated gold-equivalent metal resource at its SND gold-copper project in Laos rose from 107 tonnes to 260 tonnes after second-phase drilling, an increase of about 143%. The update is relevant to the gold and copper complex after overnight precious-metals gains. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- Hairong Technology–Hema transaction: Hairong Technology (300915 CH) will pay RMB130m for 55% of food supplier Tanghe Foods and signed a long-term cooperation agreement with Hema. Subject to full performance, Hema and its affiliates will place at least RMB210m of annual orders through 2030, giving the food-ingredients company a defined customer pipeline. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- Guiguan Power – storage and overseas solar: Guiguan Power (600236 CH) plans a 101MW/200MWh storage project in Weifang with total investment of about RMB235m. It will also take an 80% interest in a joint venture developing a 500MW solar project in Laos, with total project investment of RMB1.62bn. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- Tianwei Electronics – satellite-processing acquisition: Tianwei Electronics (688511 CH) will invest RMB90m to acquire 60% of Xiuwei Technology. The target adds general signal processing, satellite baseband processing and digital beamforming products, extending Tianwei beyond its existing protection-device business into higher-end defence information equipment. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- East Sunshine – matching buyback and shareholder increase: East Sunshine (600673 CH) received a proposal for a RMB300m–600m company share repurchase. Its controlling shareholder separately plans to buy RMB300m–600m of shares, making this the largest fresh shareholder-return announcement in Thursday evening’s A-share batch. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- Nord – copper-foil turnaround: Nord (600110 CH) reported first-half revenue of RMB6.43bn, up 113.3%, and net profit of RMB103m versus a RMB72m loss a year earlier. The company said battery and storage demand, slower capacity additions and removal of low-end supply helped lift copper-foil processing fees from their trough. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- Dongpeng Beverage – earnings and dividend: Dongpeng Beverage (605499 CH) reported first-half revenue of RMB12.44bn, up 15.9%, and net profit of RMB2.87bn, up 20.7%. It proposed a RMB3-per-share dividend, with growth attributed to broader nationwide distribution and higher sales across energy drinks, hydration products and fruit tea. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607303826900452.html)]

- Google expands into whole-body robotics: Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2, designed to combine reasoning with walking, crouching and object manipulation by humanoid robots. The release reinforces the global shift from standalone robot hardware toward embodied-AI models, relevant to Unitree and the broader Chinese robot-component chain. [[CLS](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313827155667.html)]

- Amazon – AWS growth beats: Amazon rose more than 10% after hours after second-quarter sales reached US$200.6bn versus US$197.0bn expected. AWS revenue rose 37% excluding currency effects to US$42.23bn, with backlog at US$496bn, a positive read-through for cloud computing, optical components and AI infrastructure. [[CLS](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313827171137.html)]

- Apple – guidance disappoints despite quarterly beat: Apple fell more than 8% after hours after forecasting fourth-quarter revenue growth of 9%–11%, below the 12.1% market expectation, and warning that supply constraints would intensify significantly. Third-quarter revenue and earnings nevertheless exceeded forecasts; the weaker guidance is the key read-through for Apple supply-chain stocks. [[CLS](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202607313827166057.html)]

The Nasdaq Golden Dragon China Index gained 1.05%, with Tencent ADR and Baidu up more than 2% and Alibaba above 1%; WTI fell 0.59%, while COMEX gold and silver gained 1.61% and 2.02%, respectively.
