Morning Market Memo | 11 Sep 2026 | HK/China Pre-Open  
(covers 10 Sep 2026 16:00 HKT close → 11 Sep 2026 08:34 HKT research cutoff)

- SME payments – State Council tightens rules: The State Council ordered sector-specific payment standards and encouraged large companies to pay SME suppliers in cash within 60 days. Central SOEs must use cash for all SME payments, while listed companies with large payables will face expanded disclosure requirements; electronic payment certificates will be capped at six months. [[Xinhua](https://www.xinhuanet.com/20260910/8ff70cba6fb048a2806d553adf3d3cb4/c.html)] [[China News](https://www.chinanews.com/cj/2026/09-10/10694085.shtml)]

- PBOC – overnight liquidity operations: The central bank will conduct overnight reverse repos from 14–17 September through fixed-rate quantity tenders, with daily operations capped at RMB600bn. The operations are intended to meet short-term funding demand around the mid-month tax-payment period. [[Eastmoney](https://qhweb.eastmoney.com/news/202609103870630951.html)] [[Xinhua](https://www1.xinhuanet.com/20260910/2200f891a85a4f4f9481584f8fd69766/c.html)]

- Industrial pricing – anti-involution rules detailed: The NDRC and market regulator issued cost-accounting rules for industrial products affected by disorderly low-price competition. Authorities may warn operators and launch cost investigations, using individual production costs or adjusted industry averages as the benchmark; the move is relevant to solar, batteries, chemicals, steel and other sectors facing price wars. [[NDRC](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xxgk/jd/jd/202609/t20260910_1407523.html)] [[Sina](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/2299163722/890a744a00102dr06)]

- Medical consumables – seventh national tender results: The latest high-value consumables procurement produced proposed winners across 23 gastrointestinal intervention products, with 148 companies and 517 products qualifying. The products include gastrointestinal dilation catheters, stents and guidewires, with hospital use expected from around January 2027; pricing pressure remains the key read-through for domestic device makers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103870749626.html)]

- Hengrui Pharma – two new indications approved: The NMPA approved two additional indications for Hengrui’s innovative oral thrombopoietin-receptor agonist hetrombopag, covering adult chemotherapy-induced thrombocytopenia and certain children aged six or above with chronic primary immune thrombocytopenia. The company said cumulative R&D spending on the product was about RMB658mn. [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103359728.html)] [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103870889835.html)]

- Youcai Resources – state-owned change of control: The existing controllers and an affiliated vehicle agreed to transfer about 22.72% of the company to Jiangyin Environmental Protection Group, which will also subscribe for up to 33mn new shares for no more than RMB300mn. The buyer is expected to own about 29.29%, making the Jiangyin municipal state-assets office the ultimate controller; trading resumes today. [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103359728.html)]

- Yuanwanggu – optical-equipment acquisition: Yuanwanggu (002161 CH) signed an intention agreement to acquire 100% of Guangtai Communication using internal and/or raised funds. The target supplies automated coupling equipment, testing instruments and testing solutions for optical-device and optical-module manufacturers, giving the deal relevance to the AI optical-interconnect chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103870719357.html)] [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103359728.html)]

- Jiuliang – move into commercial energy storage: Jiuliang (300808 CH) plans to acquire at least 51% of Haidi New Energy for no more than RMB275.4mn, a transaction expected to constitute a major asset restructuring. Haidi develops lithium-battery power and storage systems, extending Jiuliang beyond portable products into commercial and industrial storage. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103870719357.html)]

- Dingtong Technology – liquid-cooling expansion: Dingtong (688668 CH) will invest RMB100mn to establish a wholly owned liquid-cooling and thermal-management subsidiary in Xinyang. The company framed the investment as an expansion into cooling infrastructure required by AI computing systems and data centres. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103870719357.html)] [[Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103359728.html)]

- Jinshi Resources – mine restarts today: Jinshi Resources (603505 CH) said its Changshan Jinshi underground mine completed required safety rectification and regulatory acceptance and will resume production on 11 September. The company is continuing work to restart its other Zhejiang subsidiaries in sequence, relevant to domestic fluorspar supply. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103870719357.html)]

- Capital returns – buyback and insider buying: Aidi Precision (603638 CH) proposed a RMB100mn–200mn share buyback at no more than RMB28 per share for employee ownership or incentives. Separately, directors and senior executives of Huihuang Technology (002296 CH) plan to purchase RMB22.3mn–36.3mn of shares over six months. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103870719357.html)]

- Sell-side – AI infrastructure remains preferred: China Securities maintained its positive view on both North American and domestic AI-computing supply chains, citing sustained cloud-provider capital expenditure and demand across optical modules, GPUs, ASICs, testing equipment and fibre. The call supports continued attention on HK/A-listed optical and data-centre infrastructure names despite overnight semiconductor weakness. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609113871026349.html)]

- US inflation – PPI slightly above forecast: US August producer prices rose 0.4% month on month and 5.4% year on year, versus a 5.3% consensus for the annual rate; energy prices rose 4.2% and diesel jumped 24.1%. Core PPI excluding food and energy rose a softer 0.2% month on month, but Treasury yields climbed as markets maintained expectations for further Fed tightening. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609103870757564.html)]

The Nasdaq Golden Dragon Index fell 0.66%; NIO lost 3.24%, Bilibili 2.77%, Li Auto 2.18%, Baidu 1.56% and Alibaba 0.77%. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202609113870998212.html)]
