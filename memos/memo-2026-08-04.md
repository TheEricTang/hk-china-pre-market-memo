- Integrated-circuit IP protection: Premier Li Qiang signed the revised Integrated Circuit Layout Design Protection Regulations, effective 15 October. The 54-article framework expands protection to integrated photonic and quantum-function circuits, tightens application procedures and strengthens infringement compensation, relevant to Chinese chip designers, foundries and semiconductor-IP owners. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608033829852972.html)]

- Alphamab Oncology – cervical-cancer trial: Alphamab Oncology (9966 HK) said China’s CDE agreed to a Phase III trial of JSKN033 for recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer following failure of platinum chemotherapy and PD-1/PD-L1 treatment. The company describes JSKN033 as the first high-concentration subcutaneous combination of a HER2 bispecific ADC and a PD-L1 inhibitor, adding a fresh clinical catalyst for the HK biotech sector. [[AAStocks](https://iis.aastocks.com/20260803/12269728-0.PDF)]

- Sungrow – RMB1bn buyback ceiling: Sungrow Power Supply (300274 CH) approved an RMB500m–1bn share-repurchase programme, with a maximum purchase price of RMB188 per share and a 12-month implementation period. Repurchased shares are intended for an employee ownership or equity-incentive plan, providing a fresh capital-return signal for the solar-inverter and energy-storage complex. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608033829939625.html)]

- Battery materials – RMB2.81bn expansion: Shida Shenghua (603026 CH) announced three projects totalling RMB2.805bn: a 230,000-tonne annual liquid-lithium-salt facility, a 12,000-tonne additives project and a 200,000-tonne electrolyte plant. The plans expand upstream and midstream capacity across the lithium-battery electrolyte chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608033829939625.html)]

- Wind and solar – Yunda project: Windey Energy Technology (300772 CH) plans to invest RMB1.45bn in the first phase of a green-power project in Fengzhen. The approved 264.5MW capacity comprises 214.5MW of wind and 50MW of solar, supporting equipment and engineering demand across the renewable-power chain. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608033829939625.html)]

- Sunshine Co – new computing centre: Sunshine Co (000608 CH) said its controlled subsidiary plans to invest up to RMB980m in a computing-power centre in Xingning, Guangdong. The post-close announcement adds another domestic data-centre project for servers, storage, cooling, power equipment and network-infrastructure suppliers. [[Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202608033829939625.html)]

- Gaole – five-year computing contract: Gaole (002348 CH) said a wholly owned subsidiary signed a five-year computing-services contract worth RMB3.195bn including tax. The company estimates roughly RMB200m of 2026 revenue if implementation proceeds as planned, but still expects to remain loss-making this year after accounting for equity-incentive costs.
